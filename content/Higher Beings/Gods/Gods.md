Those who hold power over the mortals of the world of What Lies West. Their power is not to be tested- their wisdom even less.

They are as follows:

[[Y'zrold Acktraz, The Mage-Eater]]: God of Purgation and Hunger, patron deity of the [[Sons of Y'zrold]].

[[Raknarous The Bloody]]: Goddess of Bloodshed, Spiders and The Harvest- Patron deity of the [[Hands of Raknarous]].

[[Orpheus]]: God of light, the sun, and hope. Patron deity of the [[Church of Orpheus]] and the entire [[Humans|Human]] race, at least mostly.

[[Generous Harush]]: The very world itself, Harush is the Patron deity of the [[Catfolk]], and also is the namesake of their home- [[Harush]].

[[Whallad The Whimsical]]: God of delight, joy, and comedy- Whallad is not widely worshipped, though still widely recognized as a being who just wants to make folk smile... sometimes at the [[Heckling 101|expense of others]].
