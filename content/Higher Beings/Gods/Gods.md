The rulers of magic- the guiders of mortals- the makers of the world itself. The gods are beyond strength or wisdom, they are the apex of anything possible, and many things that are not.

# [[Y'zrold Acktraz, The Mage-Eater]]:
 God of Purgation and Hunger, patron deity of the [[Sons of Y'zrold]].

# [[Raknarous The Bloody]]: 
Goddess of Bloodshed, Spiders and The Harvest- Patron deity of the [[Hands of Raknarous]].

# [[Orpheus]]: 
God of light, the sun, and hope. Patron deity of the [[Church of Orpheus]] and the entire [[Humans|Human]] race, at least mostly. Orpheus is viewed as in direct opposition to [[Mortacious, Father of Curses]].

# [[Generous Harush]]: 
The very world itself, Harush is the Patron deity of the [[Catfolk]], and also is the namesake of their home- [[Harush]]. The [[Centaurs]] often worship Harush in the form of [[Themest]], or perhaps its the other way around?

# [[Whallad The Whimsical]]: 
God of delight, joy, and comedy- Whallad is not widely worshipped, though still widely recognized as a being who just wants to make folk smile... sometimes at the [[Heckling 101|expense of others]].

# [[Mortacious, Father of Curses]]: 
The god of arcane curses, Mortacious is a vilified god that mortals see as innately evil- and those who worship him, willingly or not, as damned. Mortacious is also sometimes seen as the god of darkness- being in direct opposition to [[Orpheus]].

# [[Houll The Stormbearer]]: 
The goddess of weather, Houll is a dwarven deity that according to their faith- pushed her kind into the earth with powerful winds, guiding them to their true home under the rock and stone.

# [[GRUZZ]]: 
The chief deity of the [[Orcs|Orcish]] people, GRUZZ- always fully capitalized- is the god of pride, combat and companionship. GRUZZ is a powerful god known for leading the Orcs out of their chains, and forcing them to never doubt themselves- for pride is power- power that should not ever be vain or false.
