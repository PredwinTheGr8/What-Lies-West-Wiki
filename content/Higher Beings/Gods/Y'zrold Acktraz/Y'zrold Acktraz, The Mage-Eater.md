The [[Gods|God]] of Purgation and Hunger- Y'zrold Acktraz is the patron deity of all [[Witch Hunters]]- particularly his most devoted followers the [[Sons of Y'zrold|Sons of Y'zrold]]. Y'zrold is said to be a harsh and demanding deity that demands a watchful eye of his followers to destroy any who have fallen for the temptations of [[Magic|magic]]. Those who are slain by his templars are said to be fed directly into his maw.
