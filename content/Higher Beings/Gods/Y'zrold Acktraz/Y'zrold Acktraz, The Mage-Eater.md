The [[Gods|God]] of Purgation and Hunger- Y'zrold Acktraz is the patron deity of all [[Witch Hunters]]- particularly his most devoted followers the [[Sons of Y'zrold|Sons of Y'zrold]]. Y'zrold is said to be a harsh and demanding deity that demands a watchful eye of his followers to destroy any who have fallen for the temptations of [[Magic|magic]]. Those who are slain by his templars are said to be fed directly into his maw.

Y'zrold is the youngest of all gods, being born from the minds of the [[Dwarves]] who sought to destroy magic several centuries ago. Their malice to the arcane brought forth Y'zrold from [[The Aether]], a new deity made to fulfill their desires. The Sons claim this means that their purpose is true, and that magic is a blight on the world that must be exterminated.

Y'zrold is said to commune with followers in their dreams as a great, raging artifice of steel and cogs. A dial at its center is the god's voice, and deciphering it is no easy task.
