Harush, for short, is the Patron [[Gods|God]] of the [[Catfolk]], though he sees over, according to the Catfolk, everyone, everywhere. For you see, Harush is the very world itself, he is the land, he is the sea, he is the trees and grass. As one might guess, the country of [[Harush]] is named after Generous Harush, and thankfully it is a simple story.

The Catfolk simply named the entire world 'Harush' , and when they made contact with the [[Dwarves|Dwarven]] emissaries and they asked for the name for their country, they had no true answer. To save time the Dwarves asked what they called their land, and the Catfolk responded "Ahh, Harush of course!"

To this day, the Catfolk are not pleased with this misrepresentation of their God being a mere desert.
