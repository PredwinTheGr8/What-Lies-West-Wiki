Harush is the god of The World, its denizens, and the use of its resources. He is the sprouting of trees, the roiling boil of a pot of soup, the bounty of the boars and deer. The [[Catfolk]] name him the undeniable ruler of all Gods- their creator, the reason they have mortals to rule over. Harush's name is also what the Catfolk call the entire world- there are no continents or countries, only Harush.

Harush receives food as offerings- proof from his followers that they use all he gives and uses it as best as can be. Lavish, hearty soups, thick cuts of smoked meat and tender steamed vegies are all proper offerings for the god of the world. Festivals are thrown yearly to appease him- to show gratitude for his ever gracious bounty. Nothing is ever the fault of Harush, all is as he ordains- you must simply make sure he is on your side in everything.

Harush is known to commune to his followers with impossible nature- the gust of wind in a closed room, the howling of a wolf in a cave, the amassing of locusts in winter. Harush makes his will clear- punishing those who wrong him with weak crops and sparse hunts- and rewarding his faithful with bounties that would make a ruler of worlds jealous- if only they valued food instead of coin.
