The Primal Goddess of bloodshed, Spiders, and The Harvest- Raknarous is the patron deity of her [[Hands of Raknarous]], who worship her as a provider. Raknarous is also the ruler of [[Hivewaste]], which no mortal, even her worshippers, are allowed to enter. Raknarous uses hands as her religious symbol, hence the name for her worshippers as they place this sigil all over their bodies using branding irons. Raknarous is sometimes also considered a god of pride, as to hide your symbol of devotion to her you are essentially admitting guilt or shame to worshipping her, which can sometimes be seen as heresy, though it is never an extreme offence.

Raknarous is also fond of tradition and a more primal lifestyle, though that can be saved for [[Hands of Raknarous|another day]].
