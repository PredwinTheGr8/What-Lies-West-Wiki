"Glory be to all who would seek the light of his majesty! From most ignoble of goblin brigands to most supreme of living saints! But alas for even them! They are not born his creatures! The ones who know his warmth, who never leave it even in darkness! Who possess the power to fly unto the very site of heliomancy itself! To fly into the Sun!"

-[[Vicar Friedrich Von Müller]], Vicar of [[Church of Orpheus|The Church of Orpheus]].

Light seekers, commonly known as 'moths', are believed by myself, and others of the faith of [[Orpheus]], to be his angels. Moths flit through the land basking in his light during the day, and flit around the effigies of his majesty during twilight- that being lanterns and torches of course. As such, in their constant vigilance of light and endless pursuit of it, many of the heliomantic faith believe of them as representatives of Orpheus' presence.

There are many kinds of moth, just as there are many kinds of mortal. And yet, despite all of their differences, despite all of their features- from horns of flesh to massive gleaming wings- all moths are guided by his light and constantly seek it. They are an example to us all- to unite in purpose and virtue. Glory be to his light seekers, to his moths.
