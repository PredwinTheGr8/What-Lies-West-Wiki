Orpheus is the [[Gods|god]] of light, hope, and perseverance. He was once a mortal [[Humans|human]] who through great toil banished the wretched [[The Old Ones|Old Ones]] from the mortal realm and back into [[The Deep]]. Orpheus' [[Church of Orpheus|Church]] is the most vast and devoted of all worship to any god. Orpheus' story has long since been heavily altered, and no cannon version can be truly identified- creating great rifts between worshippers.

Orpheus' power of light is in direct contrast to the master of the moon- [[Mortacious, Father of Curses|Mortacious, The Curse Father]]. The two gods, in some versions of the book of Orpheus, are in direct opposition, and in others are twin forces that work to shape mortals to become stronger- one way or another. Some... drastic books also claim the two a pairing of more than mere acquaintances, but such is faith- all up to interpretation.

Orpheus communes with his followers through dreams- though his are the hardest to recognize, and even harder to find the meaning of. [[Vicar Friedrich Von Müller]] blames this on the many different interpretations of his will and history- conflicting faith hampering his might.
