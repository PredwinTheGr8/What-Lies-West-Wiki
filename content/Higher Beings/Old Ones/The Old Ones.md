Creatures beyond time, that have existed before it was even a law of nature. Their number is unknown but a handful of them have made themselves known in attempts to gather worshippers. While most of them aren't hostile, their very nature is dangerous to witness, being more than enough to drive a mortal mad with only a glimpse of their aberrant form. The power they exude is known as [[Eldritch Energy]].

The list of known Old Ones is as follows:

[[Ol'moordurad]]: Great Old One of Solitude. Seen as the loner's deity, as an old one the act of being lonely, or isolated locations that are forgotten/ underknown directly empower Ol'moordurad. Patron deity of [[The Marked Ones]].
