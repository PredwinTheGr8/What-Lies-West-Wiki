The primal [[Gods|goddess]] of bloodshed, spiders, and harvest- Raknarous is the patron deity of her [[Hands of Raknarous]], who worship her as a provider. Raknarous is also the ruler of [[Hivewaste]], which no mortal, even her worshippers, are allowed to enter. Raknarous uses hands as her religious symbol, hence the name for her worshippers as they place this sigil all over their bodies using branding irons. Raknarous is sometimes also considered a god of pride, as to hide your symbol of devotion to her you are essentially admitting guilt or shame to worshipping her, which can sometimes be seen as heresy, though it is never an extreme offence.

Raknarous is not inherently 'evil' per say, despite assumptions. Raknarous favors its followers, but does not express scorn for those who follow other gods. Raknarous' followers look upon her as a giver, not a taker of life- as all things must die for others to be born. From plants to cattle every corpse makes a body, sometimes more than one. To live to fruition, die and create new life with your carcass is the highest calling of Raknaran worshippers.

Raknarous is said to commune with the living by controlling insects such as ants or spiders- making them write out sentences in the tongue of gods. Perhaps being the easiest of any god to take orders from.
