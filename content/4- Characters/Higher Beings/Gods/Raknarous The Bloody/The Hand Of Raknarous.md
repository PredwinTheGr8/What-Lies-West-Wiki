([[Build Togethers|340]], [[What Lies West]]?)

"There are many of us. Those who have accidentally invoked the favor of her, of [[Raknarous The Bloody|Raknarous, The Bloody]]. Bloodshed can come from anything, and if you feel just a bit too passionate, you get a hand- her mark- to let everyone know what kind of person you are."

-An unnamed man on death row for murder.

The 'hand' of [[Raknarous The Bloody|Raknarous]] is one of many physical attributes one might acquire from worshipping a [[Gods|god]]. Those who worship [[Orpheus]] develop bright, yellowish skin. worshippers of [[Harush]] become more outgoing and adventurous, and also develop extra body hair. But those who worship Raknarous, whether purposefully or on accident- they are branded for their actions to mark them as hers, and hers alone.

The hand of [[Raknarous The Bloody|Raknarous]] is a bleach white, floating 'tattoo' that no matter how many layers of clothing you try to obscure it with, it will always present itself. The hand floats just an inch away from the skin on the upper bicep. It is impossible to hide such a mark without enacting regular rituals to dispel it. 

Many psychopathic murderers have been found out because in their mirth of slaughter, the mark appeared on their shoulder. For a time, before the discovery of [[Hivewaste]], it was believed that the mark was from [[Orpheus]], a form of punishment from those who would hide from his light. But as The East was mapped out, a tribe of [[Humans]], and some other peoples, were found bordering the hellish realm of [[Raknarous The Bloody|Raknarous]]. These folk all carried the white hand with them, and named themselves thus- [[The Hand Of Raknarous]].

For a time, the explorers and knights dared not approach them, but to keep things short they were eventually invited into the diplomatic world of [[Koross]]. With their discovery, a new discovery was found- that the hand was of course not from [[Orpheus]] but from [[Raknarous The Bloody|Raknarous]]. The mark appears when someone kills something- anything- numerous times while enjoying it greatly. Farmers, butchers, monster hunters. Any of these professions could lead to becoming marked by Raknarous, and that doesn't necessarily make them villains, though still eerie.

It is rare nowadays to be unwillingly marked by [[Raknarous The Bloody|The Bloody One]], with most understanding the rules with which Raknarous chooses her property now. Most folk don't enjoy killing either- at least the act of it- anymore. They fight for survival, or when they kill something they might simply feel indifferent to it. [[The Hand Of Raknarous]] may all posses the mark, but they are much more ritualistic in their slaughter- making monthly offerings to Raknarous with livestock or criminals within their midst. Overall, people don't run in fear anymore when someone possesses the Hand of [[Raknarous The Bloody]] any more, its just a part of life in [[The City of Wyrms]] and beyond.
