GRUZZ, always capitalized, is the loud and proud god of war. GRUZZ is seen by most as the patron deity of [[Orcs|Orc]] kind, with many depictions in the seldom pieces of art painting him as a great green behemoth. The name 'GRUZZ' in fact comes from Orcs themselves, wi8th the word simply meaning 'charge' or 'onwards' in their tongue. The deity once went by a previous name, but despite that being less than a millennia ago it has somehow become lost- even to [[Elves]] who invoked the same god during their battles against the [[Lizardfolk]] at the same time as [[Orc Revolution|the revolution]].

GRUZZ has become vilified since the revolution by [[Humans|Humanity]] and by extension many of their allies- such as [[Goblins]]. The [[Church of Orpheus]] and it's denizens in particular have grown wary and fearful of even the uttering of GRUZZ'S name, with a belief that saying it is to evoke the god of war like one may summon [[Whallad The Whimsical]]. Nowadays, GRUZZ has fewer followers than even [[Mortacious, Father of Curses|Mortacious]]- who's true devoted are only in the thousands. GRUZZ has begun to fade into history, and the war god has done little to counter such a fate.

GRUZZ does not commune with followers, he expects all who worship him to earn their might and steel their pride. 'Only cowards are guided by those stronger' his War Priest once said to me, a bitter sentence to swallow to be sure. Regardless, those who follow GRUZZ cannot ever expect to be gratified by their god for their work, and instead are only ever granted audience if they have angered him- resulting in death. The [[Orcs]] themselves have almost all moved to the worship of other [[Gods]] like [[Orpheus]], [[Houll The Stormbearer]] or even sometimes [[Raknarous The Bloody]] as a different kind of blood spilling God.
