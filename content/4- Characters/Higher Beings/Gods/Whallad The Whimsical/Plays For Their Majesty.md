([[Build Togethers|478]], [[index|What Lies West?]])

Sometimes a cruel man dies, a dictator is brought low, or a madman is caught by the law. For some of these events, they are simply forgotten. For others, remembered with distain. But, for a choice few, those events of historical importance or just a good roll of the dice, a different kind of memory may be formed by the peasantry and playwrights. From the death of the cruel [[Iradeen Thaddeus]], to the fall of grace that befell Ragor Verneren- the most devoted followers of [[Whallad The Whimsical|Whallad]] desire to turn tragedy and fear into laughs and smiles.

There are many such plays and interpretations of them all throughout both [[Koross]] and [[Balphora]]. With puppets shows in [[The City of Wyrms]], and in depth, multi dozens of gold worth acts in [[Luminarch, The City of Lanterns|Luminarch]]. All of this is largely conducted by the willing, or even unwitting, followers of Whallad The Whimsical. Efforts that most often in vain, attempt to earn the chuckling god's favor. But what does this favor look like? Well, most often it is represented as even better ideas for comedies and dramas, though I admit I don't see the attraction in working for the right to work harder next time around, at least that's what it seems to be.
