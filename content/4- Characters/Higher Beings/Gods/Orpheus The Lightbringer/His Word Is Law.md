"And for his followers; they who would face his foe without fear and with conviction, there is only the greatest of paradises waiting for them in death. Become unto yourself what he would make you, do what he would ask, think what he would allow. Only then, when you accept his will into yourself will you become pure."

-Excerpt from the Book of [[Orpheus]], Sanctum verse 4:44.

The Lightbringer is salvation in darkness. He is the lantern at the bow of a ship, the beacon on top of a tower, the port in a storm. When others ask if there is a king or queen of gods, they wonder only of Orpheus for there is no other answer. What other god would heal the sick? Who else would shield a peasant from [[Mutants|mutation]]? Who else has the power to defy even death for any with faith in he who lights the world?

None.

For there is none else as benevolent as he. None else as giving, cheerful, and merciful as he. None else who would look upon their followers and think of them not as a resource, but as precious things. For he, and only he, was once a man! From the 'world' god [[Harush]] to the primordial horror of [[Raknarous The Bloody|Raknarous]] none else have lived as we have! None else have died as we will! Orpheus and Orpheus *alone* knows how we have lived and died and suffered! He alone can console us in our darkest hour!

So then, faithful chaff. You are a diamond in the rough for your faith alone, a prize any other deity would be jealous and covetous of. How many other gods have had defectors flock to them instead of Orpheus- rather than the other way around? You may hear whispers from drunk men suggesting that even Orpheus' faithful can waver. They are lying scoundrels, who's lives are over for their lack of belief. So do not falter, not even for a moment. For a second may undo centuries of devotion, and a minute may forever damn oneself to [[The Deep]].
