"When you walk where you shouldn't, where one could not, where one will never- between the seams and curtains of the material and the border of the absurd- the impossible. That time and that time alone is when any mortal would ever be the closest to [[The Four]]- at least physically, if such laws of reality even exist within such a place. Dripping thoughts falling from a ceiling of the mind of the unfathomable- careening down and down and down unto and into and throughout you. 'Sallow Shadows in The Shadows, Risen Withered and Unbidden.' Remember this."

-A thought I did not think.

The Deep. The place where [[The Four]] wait and wait, their weight pressing upon history and future. Orpheus annihilated their kingdom among the physical, so they made one that cannot be entered nor left, that they and only they may rule it. The Deep is the realm of the eldritch, the fathomless, the incomprehensible. Just as our [[Ocean Magnamous]], The Deep has no bottom, there are unknowable horrors lurking within- though at least our horrors can be slaughtered with knife. No matter how large a Kraken, no matter how numerous the sharks, no matter how cunning the leviathan- we can kill them. Harpoons on rope, pitch to burn the waves, muskets to coat the depths. But the things within The Deep, the other realm, they cannot be wounded, they cannot be even gazed upon lest the mind will be rended asunder.

The creatures born of or twisted by The Deep are known as "Spawn" by [[Adventurers|adventurer]], [[Scholars|scholar]] and [[Recorders|recorder]] alike. We fear them, and rightfully so for they are the bump in the night, the demons in the folktales, the men that knock at midnight. They ask to enter homes they will never leave. They bind to souls they will never vacate. They are hateful of us, all of them- from smallest thoughtform to most monolithic [[The Old Ones|Old One]] they despise us in flesh and blood and mind and soul.

The things spawned from The Deep are just that- created, not born. No doubt the [[Mutants]] born of [[Eldritch Energy|eldritch energy]] are not themselves present in their bodies, their flesh has simply been replaced, their position in our world swapped out with something from the other side.

I visited it, you know? I survived it. It spoke with me yet I could not understand. It drug me out 'twards [[The True Desert|the desert]] and told me something I could not possibly know. If these... things are what rules over The West, then how can we possibly conquer it? How could we ever hope to build a city there? Or to carve aquifers into the mountains? The Deep, the realm of the eldritch is already here I feel. It's just hiding.
