Creatures beyond time, that have existed before it was even a law of nature. Their number is unknown but a handful of them have made themselves known in attempts to gather worshippers. While most of them aren't hostile, their very nature is dangerous to witness, being more than enough to drive a mortal mad with only a glimpse of their aberrant form. The power they exude is known as [[Eldritch Energy]].

The list of known Old Ones is as follows:

[[Ol'moordurad]]: Great Old One of Solitude. Seen as the loner's deity, as an old one the act of being lonely, or isolated locations that are forgotten/ underknown directly empower Ol'moordurad. Patron deity of [[The Marked Ones]].

[[Az'rulgath]]: The ever hunger, the starved, the furious. Az'rulgath is, even more than [[Y'zrold Acktraz, The Mage-Eater|Y'zrold Acktraz]], ever starving, ever craving the flesh of mortal. Az'rulgath is perhaps the most innately 'evil' of the Old Ones, at least from a mortals definition.

[[Sc'tharra]]: The maker of mutants, the bringer of doom, the creator of agony. Sc'tharra is a silent creature that hides itself in the furthest reaches of [[The Deep]]. Sc'tharra is the most enigmatic, alien of all Old Ones. It is the reason flesh boils under eldritch light, why muscles writhe in the dead, why people scream from within their puppeted corpses.

[[Aie]]: The sea, the drowning thousands, the ever suffocating. Aie is the calmest and most agreeable of all Old Ones, but wronging it will result in a doomed voyage across the [[Ocean Magnamous]]. Aie is the most widely worshiped of the old ones- mostly out fear for the depths below the deck of a ship.
