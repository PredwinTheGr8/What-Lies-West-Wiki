"Flesh falls, mind falters, bone screams. Shadows will whisper, demons will flee, life will fear. The wind will scourge, the earth will scorn, the sun will weep. Such is its power- Maker of Mutants be amused upon your sloughing, slowing, stopping heart- reanimated to life after death to mutilate, maim, *mutate.* Fears to be against hateful Sc'tharra."

-Excerpt from "Titans, Unfathomable" by Ermal Von [[The Conrad Family|Conrad]]

The Maker of Mutants, the Despiser of consistency, the devourer of the flesh. Sc'tharra is the [[The Old Ones|Great Old One]] that controls mutation, eldritch energy, and negative change. The Unmaker of Men is the most widely despised and vilified of all the eldritch powers- being the entity associated with the creation of [[The Annihilator]]. [[Scholars]] and [[adventurers]] alike interact with the direct and indirect powers of Sc'tharra every day, with creatures such as [[Mutants]] and even pure eldritch spawn such as [[The Things in The Dark, Null Figures|Null Figures]] being notable consistent interactions between Sc'tharra and mortal kind.

With the constant battle between the mind and [[The Deep]] when facing the might of Sc'tharra, and the frequency with which its power appears, Sc'tharra is a very real and permeable threat within [[The West]] and even [[The Wider World]], with stories in [[Koross]] going back centuries involving the twisting of flesh, the snapping of bone, and the sloughing of the soul as what was once a person becomes anything but. 

Ermal Von Conrad coined the name "Maker of Mutants" for Sc'tharra, as part of her effort to slowly allow mortal kind to forget the very existence of the Old Ones- in hopes that forgetting them is akin to killing them so to speak. Though her book; "Titans, Unfathomable" sold rather poorly, even for an academic work, it still did small wonders for bringing a great deal of understanding to the nature and potential weaknesses thereof the dwellers of The Deep.

Other Old ones often seem to be maleficent to Sc'tharra, with [[Aie]] being the only true 'potential' ally Sc'tharra may possess. In the cultist underworld of [[Koross]] and [[Balphora]], cultists of [[Az'rulgath]] and of Sc'tharra are at constant odds, directed by their masters to pursue after each other's lives. Followers of [[Ol'moordurad]]- most often [[The Marked Ones]]- also are often commanded to stem any Sc'tharran plans. The worshippers of Sc'tharra- called "Vess," an elven word for 'Rot'- commonly find themselves at the mercy of the law and other cultists, as their constant mutations are almost never beneficial, and frequently fatal. The Followers of Sc'tharra are the fewest in number among any religion, a good thing I reckon given the nature of Sc'tharra's goals.
