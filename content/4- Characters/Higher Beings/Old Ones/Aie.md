"When the storms rage above the fathomless depths, and the lighting crashes against the mast of a ship. When the krakens rise and the leviathans hunger. When thousands are drowned and forgotten; thus is the anger of The Prince Of Pressure- the anger of Aie."

-Excerpt of "Titans, Unfathomable" by Ermal Von [[The Conrad Family|Conrad]]

Aie is the [[The Old Ones|Old One]] that rules over the sea. With its might and surveillance Aie ensures all pay their toll when crossing The [[Ocean Magnamous]], lest they be tested with a storm- or worse, a deep-dwelling creature of immense size. For eons, even afore the time of [[Balphora]], denizens of [[Koross]] on the beaches of The Ocean Magnamous paid their tributes to "generous" Aie for its permission to live against it's skin of the deep blue. These worshippers, whether zealous devotees or just travelers who know better than to anger Aie, are called "Ins" for the Dwarven term "Enz," or "to drown."

For as long as mortals have fished the seas, just as long have they given prayer, sacrifice and caution to "The Prince of Pressure" as renowned author Ermal Von [[The Conrad Family|Conrad]] coins Aie to avoid speaking or writing its true name. There are tales of captains who finally get fed up with giving tribute, only to be swept away deep- *deep* into the blue of the world, their crew cursing them in their deaths and manifesting great vessels of ghostly blue light manned by ghosts driven by hate. Whether true or not, these tales make for haunting thoughts, and further reinforce mortal kinds' efforts to please Generous Aie.

Ties with the other three of [[The Four]] are strange. With only one interaction visible with [[Sc'tharra]] who's power can be found twisting the deepest-dwelling fauna of the oceans- [[Ol'moordurad]] and [[Az'rulgath]] both abstaining from contact with Aie. Whether or not this interaction between Sc'tharra and Aie is one of hostility or cooperation is unknown, but given that both still remain 'alive' after their eons of existence, it is likely that Aie does not mind, or rather does not care.
