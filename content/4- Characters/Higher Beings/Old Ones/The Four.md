"The world moaned. The stars laughed. The seas drowned. The skin faltered, the soul shattered, the mind broke. If stories are to be believed [[Orpheus]] stopped the utter end of the world with his quest. Yet his foe was never slain- not all of them. They are beyond such things as death."

-Excerpt from the Book of Orpheus- Litanies 8:12

"The Four" is the name given to the combined force of the last remaining [[The Old Ones|Old Ones]] after the quest of Orpheus- [[Aie]], [[Ol'moordurad]], [[Sc'tharra]] and [[Az'rulgath]]. These four creatures have hid in the darkness for millennia, regaining their strength for yet another strike against the mortal world. Some cults have come to worship them, the largest of which being the elven kingdom of [[The Marked Ones]], who worship Ol'moordurad as a god. Fools.

Other small cults worship Az'rulgath as a doom bringer, they crave the death of all and would use Az'rulgath to cause it. Sc'tharra is the beast that made room in nature for things such as [[Mutants|mutation]] to be possible, hiding away in its impossible fortress at the precipice of [[The Deep]]. Finally, Aie drowns colony ships in hurricanes, drags thousands to the depths below and belches back up their bloated carcasses after its done sowing its carnage.

Orpheus was right to fell as many of these creatures as he could, that only four of them remain is a testament to his might. Ever since their doom, the number four has become associated with their presence, their watchful eye. The more the number repeats the greater their presence. With four hundred and forty four meaning their absolute, undivided attention. Or so it is said.
