([[Build Togethers|540]], [[index|What Lies West?]])

"But me friends drop the whole 'Von Conrad' part."
-Seamus The Alchemist? 102nd in line to inheriting the Conrad Fortune

Seamus [[The Conrad Family|Von Conrad]], though he doesn't use that full name, is currently the premiere alchemist within [[The City of Wyrms]], Seamus has spent a great deal of his life after disowning himself from his family working to understand the 'chemistry' of The West, from the makeup of the magic within, to the uses of flora inside. That latter point is his passion; Seamus is keen on finding a purpose for every leave, splinter of bark, and mycelial flesh he can get his hands on.

Seamus is to be attributed to many concoctions used regularly by adventurers; [[Wretch]], [[Healing Potions]], [[Skarnical]], [[Draughts of Earthly Focus]]- and many, many more. While many, I know, may be apprehensive to working with a Von Conrad I would like to stress that Seamus is one only in title. He has done his best to distance himself from the family name, and hopes to remarry and take the house name of his spose to "bury it like a dead cat."

Veteran [[Adventurers]] regularly visit "Seamus' Cauldron" on the corner of Breggan Street and Onyx Arch Way, a wonderful spot to have a shop, really. The location does wonders for Seamus' business, with him being able to fully staff his shop and still afford to purchase hundreds of pounds of rare ingredients if the need arises.

Seamus remains so popular in the City that, at one point he was fully pardoned for Murder. Another Conrad inheritor had come to insult Seamus for his behavior regarding the family fortune. Seamus, for his part, refused to converse with his cousin or distant sister or whomever it was. This persisted until eventually the Conrad brat struck one of Seamus' workers with a potion flask containing Liquid [[Viridisum]], liquifying the outer layers of the worker's skin. Seamus immediately responded with shooting the noble in the stomach with a pistol, and repeatedly slamming a cast iron skillet into her cranium.

Many patrons were in the shop at the time, around three dozen of them. Once Seamus got back up to take a breath, everyone carried on about their business and Seamus tended to his employee, allowing her to take some time off whilst she recovered.

While the Conrad Families attorneys tried to convict Seamus, with a few favors called in Seamus had Three Minotaurs standing outside his door constantly. Needless to say, a few wimpy lawyers didn't have the brawn or balls to dare enter and speak with Seamus. Ever since, the City has only trusted Seamus more and more as 'not a Conrad,' though there is a certain slang they used instead that I will refrain from using here.

Either way, current day Seamus is one of the wealthiest and most influential vendors within [[The City of Wyrms]]. He has a voice in politics, and a vote in policy decisions, a power he shares with the actual, genuine leader of [[The Conrad Family]]- [[Vernan Von Conrad]]. Seamus is known to consistently grin at Vernan when voicing his votes against policies the Lord Conrad suggests.
