"Wars were fought. Cities were burned. Thousands were hung. Even more saw their own corpse as their head flew away after a guillotine slash. Countries folded, castles crumbled and lords kneeled. Thus was the rule of Iradeen. Cruel."

-[[The Khrough Chart|Khrough]], Son of Orrin, [[Scholars|scholar]].

Iradeen [[House Thaddeus|Thaddeus]] was once the indisputable ruler over two thirds of [[Koross]]. His family was on the brink of conquering the entire continent before the flames of the [[Orc Revolution]] brought it all to a halt. The earliest records of his life start in 648 of the Orphidian calendar, as of current writing that is well over one hundred and ninety years ago. Iradeen began his life as a mere servant to his own grandfather- Gregor Thaddeus, widely regarded as one of the greatest and most just rulers in history. Iradeen was recorded as being a most dutiful servant. He was noted as being the most active and professional of Gregor's thirty eight grandchildren, which eventually earned him a place within the true royal family.

His days of cleaning windowsills and dusting the tops of cabinets was long over now, and he began his days of tutelage at the age of sixteen. He excelled with top grades in political sciences, etiquette, swordsmanship and mathematics. It was noted though at this time that Iradeen began treating those beneath him more and more harshly- being accused, but never proven of maiming an elven servant girl when she missed a speck on a window. Ira was accused of pushing the girls face against the window and breaking it, then slamming her forehead onto the broken glass. While the injuries were confirmed and treated, Iradeen got away without any formal trial or even repercussions.

Gregor had begun personally visiting Iradeen though, as his grades showed fantastic potential. Personal lectures with his grandfather filled up every evening Ira had, and his days were spent patrolling both the manor and its castle-town beneath. Ira was tasked numerous times with leaving a good impression on the local townsfolk who he might one day rule. Further records emphasize that Ira was nothing short of a terror upon local shops, smithies and artisan stalls. He demanded discounts, free items and threatened any who refused with the hangman's knot. Still, despite his failures in this department Gregor still kept tutoring him and trying to improve his attitude towards commoners.

Then, on the thirteenth day of Callen in the year of 671, on Ira's twentieth birthday, he broke into Gregor's bedchamber with a knife. It is suspected that Ira threatened his grandfather at knife-point to sign the papers making Ira his rightful heir. While nothing was proven directly, the bloodstained transfer of will and the slit throat of Gregor told the story well enough. But the power now wielded, rightfully or not, by Ira was absolute to any who would oppose him. He knew well enough that nobody would respect him out of love, so he chose to rule with fear. Guards were hung for questioning orders. Servants were tossed out of windows for failing in their duties, and all of Gregor's other children and grandchildren were burned at the stake.

Iradeen's rule was without doubt now. Nary a harsh breath was huffed in his presence. Not a speck of dirt remained in his manor. Nary a pile of dung or dirt befouled the streets of his city- any horse or cow who would dare besmirch him thus was beheaded and hung from the same wall as the others.

Gregor's territory was large, make no doubt. But by the end of Ira's rule it had nearly sextupled in size. What was once a riverside kingdom on the [[River Fallow]] turned into a near complete conquering of all of the eastern nation. Ira led armies of steel legions, commanded summoned [[Dreadnoughts]] in the dozens, and bribed the northern high elves of [[Moradesh]] into his marches. Magic rained from the heavens, steel constructs crashed through walls and hundreds of soldiers followed behind them. Twas' a tide of blood that followed Iradeen in his wake.

Even other lineages were not safe from his desire for more. The [[Orcs]] were shackled. The [[Kobolds]] caged and brought to homes as pets, the [[Goblins]] sent to the mines to toil away. The only peoples that managed to keep Ira away from their homes were the stout [[Dwarves]], who were forced into the same treaty as the elves regardless of their fortifications, and the eastern dwelling [[Lizardfolk]], who's grasp over nature completely thwarted Ira's conquest of their [[The Great Swamp|Great Swamp]]- saving the unknown [[Catfolk]] and [[Centaurs]] from his wrath on complete accident as well no doubt.

The [[Minotaurs]] were walled, locked inside of [[The Northern Wastes]] under the hateful gaze of the [[Phrazmollograggra Mountains]]. By the time he was eighty Ira ruled almost every human nation, the only two that escaped his wrath being [[Church of Orpheus|The Church of Orpheus]] and [[Cresthram]], with the Wall of [[Luminarch, The City of Lanterns|Luminarch]] keeping both groups safe against Ira's wrath.

Finally, he seemed to be content with his conquest. Iradeen found himself a wife in Miranda Harrow, who was, regrettably, forced away from her own marriage to produce an heir for Ira. Ira had thirteen children- eleven of which were found 'unfit' to live after they reached puberty. The only two who survived to adulthood are [[Maximillian Thaddeus]] and [[Amelia Thaddeus]]. By the time The [[Orc Revolution]] came around, Ira had turned ninety eight. While we all know the result of the Revolution, Ira still lived on to the ripe age of one hundred and two years old, before passing on and leaving his fallen kingdom to his son, Maximillian. His twenty three year old daughter went on to become the Adventurer Amelia Harrow, who now resides within Harold Green's party.

To this day, the very utterance of Ira's name sends chills down folks spines. The very name is frowned upon- a symbol of a tyrant. No child has been named Iradeen ever since his uprising, and likely never will.
