([[Build Togethers|541]], [[index|What Lies West?]])

Emmerick's mother was an [[adventurers|adventurer]], who regularly visited [[The West]]. In one of her expeditions, Maeve Von Conrad conceived Emmerick within [[The Forlorn Graveyard]] during a particularly powerful night when [[The Many Named Moon]] was full. during her pregnancy, Maeve struggled with Emmerick's development. She contracted extreme magic sickness and almost perished several times as Emmerick both sapped and exuded magic through her body with the waning and waxing of The Moon.

When Emmerick was born, after a notably long pregnancy- 13 months- he was seething with raw power. Maeve nearly perished during his birth, receiving powerful burns that would have cooked her alive were it not for the presence of an Orphean priest who consistently healed her throughout the process- which in and of itself made birth via surgery impossible.

Emmerick's eyes burned bright with the might of [[Maernos]], quite literally in fact. Emmerick spent his first decade of life without eyes, but not blind. It was said he could see objects because the [[Magic]] in the air stopped against them- meaning of course that he could, or rather still can, physically see the presence of magic in the air. As he grew, and learned to control his power, Emmerick eventually reconstituted his eyes after forcing the power to heal away from mighty [[Orpheus]]. This has made him rather... vilified to say the least within the lighted faith. Thus far he is the only mage to ever force a gift from a [[Gods|god]].

Emmerick went on to earn his place at the upper hierarchy of [[The Conrad Family]]. And though he climbed his way up the inheritance ladder through his efforts he would eventually become completely disinherited. Emmerick's attitude towards authority, or rather his complete disregard of it as his power grew only served to distance himself and his relatives. Emmerick, in the words of his younger sister Vernonia, eventually began treating those around him coldly when he reached adulthood at twenty- eventually separating himself from the Lord Conrad's will entirely and settling in [[Three Purses]] with a small following of loyal servants.

Today, Emmerick has made efforts to acquire arcane artifacts from both [[The West]] and [[Koross]]' old history. Whatever he plans to do with such instruments is unknown, and its not as if anyone has the power to stop him.
