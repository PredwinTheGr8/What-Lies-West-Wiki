The name given to the strange, [[Magic|arcane magics]] that drive mortal kind to go to [[The West]]. The Calling suddenly afflicted almost every mortal, particularly [[Humans]], [[Elves]], [[Dwarves]] and [[Goblins]] and gave them a great desire to cross the [[Ocean Magnamous]] to what is now called [[Balphora]].
