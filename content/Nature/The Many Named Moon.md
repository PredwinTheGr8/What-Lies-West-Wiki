The moon of the world, who's occupants could not agree upon a name and thus it was named as such. The [[Catfolk]] called it... [[Generous Harush|Harush]] as they doo all things natural. The [[Humans]] named it Eurydice, and the [[Elves]] coined it Aesha. For those who don't care, or those who converse with other peoples, it is simply called The Many Named Moon to save time.
