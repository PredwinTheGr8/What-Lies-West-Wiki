Magic is the lifeblood of the world. It is a complex energy that primarily enters the material plane through so called [[Sites of Power]], the largest sites of power in the world being the northern and southern [[Polar Gates]]. Magic is thought to be originative of a distant realm known as [[The Fae Wild]]. A formless place similar to [[The Deep]], The Fae Wild is instead a home for the arcane rather than the eldritch, obviously meaning that its gets a better opinion than the former domain.
