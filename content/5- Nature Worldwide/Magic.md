The lifeblood of the world, magic is everywhere mortal kind has wandered, though it is the strongest in [[Hivewaste]] and [[The West]]. Magic's origins cannot be traced, as it has always been here- always existed. Those who research it, [[Wizards]], are known for their obsession with the resource.

Magic is generated by [[Sites of Power]]- arcane lands or structures that emanate the arcane, and let it flow on the wind like spores. These places are identifiable from magic that is so dense, it becomes visible as a vibrant fog that shifts in color and smells of peace. Peoples who live their lives close to sites of power become reliant on magic to sustain themselves, becoming weak if they venture far away from sites of power, though with time they may recover their strength. One notable group that is consistently close to a site of power are the [[Lizardfolk]], who's ancient temple within [[The Great Swamp]] emanates massive amounts of mana into their ecosystem, booming its strength against rots and in some cases, intruders.
