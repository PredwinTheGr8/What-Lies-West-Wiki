The Yawning Gates, the Roiling Clouds, the Quaking Mountains. All is as it should be, and that is why everything will wither and bloom and wither again. Make the plunge, drop the doll, swing the blade. **Commit the sacrifices**."

-[[Seamsters|Seamster]] "Ivan"

The Sites of Power are integral to our world. Without them, all kinds of ills would befall our home. Despite their importance to our livelihood, The Sites Of Power within [[Koross]] remain the most dangerous places our mortal feet may tread. From the immolating heat of The [[Phrazmollograggra Mountains]], to the blood thinning toxicity of the heart of [[The Great Swamp|Moulugg]], walking in the apex- or the outskirts of one, of any Site of Power within The East will almost certainly result in death.

The burning heat of the mountains would strip skin from bone in lacerating, hellish winds. The arc-flashes of [[Mt. Vellz]] would make bone into ash, the poisonous winds of [[The Widow's Willow]] would send a man to his knees before his blood runs from every pore and orifice. [[Pyromancy]], [[Dendromancy]]- [[Electromancy]] ALL magic is not without peril, not without danger at the slightest touch. Or so we thought.

When that group of orc Laborers saw the [[Wyrmscale Reaches]] two centuries ago everything changed. Make no doubt, [[The Calling]] had not yet struck the world, and nary a scholar's apprentice would ever step foot in [[Balphora]] for another ninety years- but yet even those with the mildest temperament for the arcane felt something off the further [[The West|West]] they went. The magics of The West are kinder. Far, far kinder than that of the East's.

In fact, all over the Western Continent Sites of Power, small as a puddle to large as a kingdom can be found. No matter how chaotic some should be, they remain calm. Eyes in a storm. [[The Voltaic Monolith]], for instance, produces more arcane power than [[Mt. Vellz]], The Maelstrom and The Titan Tree combined- yet comparatively to the others its lethal radius is roughly one *tenth* as large. The magic of The West is weaker in potency, and that is what even allows [[Mutants|mutation]] to be possible. Other sources just outwrite kill whoever treads in their domain. BUT WHY? I cannot for the life of me fathom an answer to this question!

The only Site of Power in The West that *remains* dangerous is the massive stretch of magma that is [[The Warbling]]- I'm sure you can guess why. BECAUSE PYROMANCY IS HOT! That's the only reason! If you were immune to heat the magic would take actual MONTHS to kill you there! Mutation is the ONLY danger provided by the Sites of Power in The West!

I do not jest I have spent years theorizing and thinking in sleepless nights. There is no reason that all these monolithic sites would be weaker than those outside The West. Some adventurers have even claimed the very *[[Polar Gates]]* are weaker in this scientific null-zone! Damnit! It already has me thinking again!
