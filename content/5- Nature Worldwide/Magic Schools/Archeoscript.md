([[Build Togethers|538]], [[index|What Lies West?]])

"Callipse. Verun. Soran. Vaeth.

Egon. Malou. Sepheth. Cain.

Vor. Ins. Ser. Kli."

-Every primary word in Archeoscript


Until we reached [[The West]], [[magic]] was as simple to us as changing clothes- we understood it inside and out- or so we thought. Magic was once simple like mathematics in a way- predictability, certainty, definable, real outcomes based on the factors within. Each 'word' of a spell is subconsciously said by the 'soul' of a being. This silent reading is known as Ley-Whisper, and very few can be trained to actually 'hear' the sound of the soul that is this strange language. Still, understanding the silent vocal components of a spell let us do wonders three hundred years ago. While some, like [[Sons of Y'zrold|The Sons of Y'zrold]]. vilify all magic there were still many who began using magic as a facet of daily life.

Cargo lifts, indoor lighting, war. Magic had found a use, though crude as it was it could never work on-masse, not profitably at least. Then, like so many stories of change start, we went west. It took decades for folk to realize something incredible was hiding in the shadows of [[The Forlorn Graveyard]]- a hidden secret that would change how we perceived magic forever.

Archeoscript.

Twelve words- that is the entire script. Whereas pyromancy has eighty three, and cryomancy one hundred and two- so on and so forth- this new school of magic discovered in The Graveyard held a measly twelve. Only three are actually, really understood. Callipse- to shrink, Ser- to empower, and lastly Kli- to kill. The other nine hide their purpose well. There is theory that Verun, Vaeth and Vor remain closely tied to another, all starting with the letter 'v'. And while only speculation, patterns in magic; even small ones, are almost always important. Natural magic is about repeating geometric patterns. Artificial magic is the simple refinement and control of the repetition within.

So yes, even something as small as starting with the same letter when all others do not means that is is likely of extreme importance.

Archeoscript is the force that keeps the entire west moving. Obviously mana-tech runs off the stuff, but even things that we thought we already understood were flipped on their head when we looked closer.

For instance, "[[The Calling]]." We know it- the spell that summons us occidental. The white particles seen floating through the sky during the apex of the annual spell have been examined before, but now we know that it contains three words of the script.

"Callipse, Malou, Ins."

Over and over this pattern repeats in every last grain of magic. Callipse is to shrink, or to make shrink- or so 'collapse', but the other two? Who knows? Malou, some claims brings to mind the Malediction family and their own, personal researches into Archeoscript these days. Ins, though, is similar to the Dwarven word "Enz:" "to drown." Similarities are not enough, though, and despite our endless efforts to decode anything whatsoever it remains a mystery as to the purpose of each individual script word. Regardless, they show up everywhere in The West, repetitively, endlessly, infinitely. From [[Wyrmscale Reaches|The Wyrmscale Reaches]] to the derelict machines of [[The Fracking Plant]], Archeoscript is ever-present. The only question is how?

Mayhap... mayhap then, I should finally ask it something? The thing out in the sands. It told me to ask it anything... I think. Perhaps it would tell me if I-

. . .

No.

No, a fool, I.
