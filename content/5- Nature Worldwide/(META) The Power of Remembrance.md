([[Build Togethers|616]], [[index|What Lies West?]])

*This entry is not knowledge that can be found in-world within WhLW, it is meta-info that contains minimal spoilers.*

In [[index|What Lies West]], there are certain moments where you may look at a timeline and think, how is this guy alive still? Such as with [[Iradeen Thaddeus]] and his son, namely Maximillian has lived for roughly 140 years.

In Maernos there are some things I keep thinking about to myself but not discussing with the "audience" and my players simply because either a prompt hasn't been relevant or because there isn't a way to naturally write about it. One such mechanic to it's world is "Remembrance" or "Renown."

Simply put, the more well known a person(s) is, the longer they live. It does not matter if they're remembered fondly so long as they are known of. Iradeen earned his long life by conquering, and Maximillian his from his legacy. While Maximillian hasn't started any wars... yet, his father left a scar on the world that meant Maximillian was already known by most of the planet before he was thirty. Whereas Iradeen became known late into his years, Max has been infamous since much earlier in his life, and that means he will live longer than Ira despite not having quite as much of a 'following' as his father.

Some other Characters earn their long lives through other means. Amelia "Thaddeus" Harrow is a blessed Cleric of [[Orpheus]]. Her connection to the god of light gives her a longer lifespan. [[Immolesh]], The Fire Tamer, became a demi god by bathing in magma. He will likely live forever unless struck down, and his renown on top of that as the leader of [[The Herd of Immolesh|His Herd]] means that even if he was mortal he would still live for quite a long time.


To answer the real question though, probably Wyverns, I think they can live theoretically forever.
