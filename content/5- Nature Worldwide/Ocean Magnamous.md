The Ocean Magnamous is the name granted to the largest body of water ever discovered- a massive saltwater span that connects [[Koross]] and [[Balphora]] with a five month sea journey. The Ocean Magnamous is the largest [[Magic|mana]] well in the world, places where magic is essentially destroyed- ensuring that the substance never completely consumes the world.

Many peoples of the world have made their homes permanently on the many islands within the Ocean Magnamous- taking residence in a place where the deadliest thing they'll ever have to contend with is a shark or stinging conch, rather than a monster the size of a building. The waters hold a great bounty of food for those who know how to extort it and live off of its ecosystem. Bizarrely, the folk who live on the beaches of the world could not see, or feel, [[The Unfeathering]] and therefore do not feel [[The Calling]]. Though if they are caught far from their ocean home when The Unfeathering occurs again they will become affected.

It seems that the vast ocean absorbs the magic of [[The Unfeathering]] faster than it can be perceived, and though [[The Calling]] isn't deadly, some wish they were on the beaches when it occurred, as the constant nagging feeling to uproot their lives is quite and annoyance to some people.
