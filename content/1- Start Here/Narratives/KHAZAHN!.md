Blood on the dunes. Scorched ancient plates. The char of seared flesh. What has transpired here? Hrikahl looms over the carnage before him with sorrow in his heart. In the center of the ashes is a sight he never wished to see- two figures, and adult and child, one trying to shield the other from the flames... to no avail.

He walks over, planting his spear into the ground as he kneels down to examine the bodies. The sand 'round them is glassed into a black smear of death. Their skin is ash, clumped together by sheer force of pressure. How they, or any other were not immolated to complete dust is a mystery only the venerable ones could have solved. Under the glass, a faint glint sheens through. With his bulk Hrikahl slams a fist down, shattering the tempered quartz into shards to dig down and retrieve the item.

It's her charm that he made all those years ago. His S'vrayn. His wife and daughter.

##    "Vaeth."
Her name; 'To Sing' in Bath'kahn. He utters it against the wind of an incoming sandstorm. Looking north, a trail of glass and soot disappears over the dunes.
##    "Rest, my loves. I will bury your ashes in the hides of your killer."

Dragging his spear out from the ground, Hrikahl lumbers into the distance. The evidence left by their killer is clear- an unmissable path of destruction. A **K'ng** is their murderer, a hardy foe- an impossible foe some may say. If he is to fell it and avenge them... he will need assistance, and not from his own kind. He is wise enough to know it takes the small to fool the large. He will need the **Iboki**- the *small ones.* They are easy enough to find- they stumble around like lost babes without a beacon to guide them. Hrikahl, though reluctantly, must be that beacon.

His trek is long. The heat of the great fire hammers away at his nulled skin. He unfurls his crest on his back, keeping the heat at bay- though it still bites away at him. He follows the trail of devastation left by the **K'ng**, taking shelter in sandstorms and sweeping thunder clouds. A small **Scoar**, or as the Iboki call them: 'inverter' tried to drag him under the sands. Too small a predator for too large a meal. Hrikahl enjoyed the protein.

Eventually though, he arrives in a place of remembrance. The scolding heat of the great flame dies away with the clouds as he enters the oasis. The Iboki call this place 'the basin'. It shames him to admit they may know more about his own history than even his entire people, they are much better off to enter the small buildings full of old lore- an annoying prospect in retrospect.

Many critters scurry away as he rounds corners. His bulk makes sand into stone beneath his feet, or so the Iboki say. Though the Destroyer had come through recently, its likely that the Ibok never saw or even heard it- they are ignorant like that. Waltzing through the basin, Hrikahl stands out like the paint on a bison. If any 'adventurers' are nearby, they will see him before he notices them. Only the brave or foolish will not hide from him- they are who he wishes for, though.

Hrikahl has known many Iboki in his time. Many are incompetent sloths who trek the dunes only for fame and fortune. But among the masses are diamonds in the rough- Iboki who have themselves slaughtered a K'ng, or have witnessed the Eye of Doom and lived to tell the tale. This place, this 'basin' is a convergence point for them. He has come and gone aplenty, and every time he sees several groups, sometimes not even a mile away from each other. While many try to hide from him, others know well enough the traditions of The Bath'kah. They are not traveling the ancestral grounds here, there is no tithe to be paid- no tribute to be demanded. This place is free- and those who know do not run, for they know nothing is to be feared.

These ones, the ones who know Bath'kah law- they are perhaps adorable, in a way. Like the scab of an Im'roh, or the pup of a warp beast. They have an attraction to them in a way- there is humble beauty and strength in learning of others and not turning them away- thus is the way of Kayne, the rite of survival.

Eventually, as he wades through the tepid waters and saunters over sands he finds what he searches for.

A campfire in the twilight hours. A confident provocation. And that he has grown so close to it and it remains aflame? A promising proposition indeed. Eager, Hrikahl speeds his step up- both to be quick and to intimidate them further. His rapid steps shake the earth with pulsing tremors, closing in on the campsite he sees them- Iboki.

There are... many- too many to count quickly. A sizable group is a strong group, a point in their favor. Looking at him are three individuals, the rest give not a care in the world. Good, another point. The three are... an... an *orc*, a *lizardfolk* and a-.... a *human*. Ancients above they are hard to tell apart. The orc braces a maul no bigger than Hrikahl's finger, the lizardfolk... woman? Woman. The lizardfolk woman brandishes a great sword, as does the manling. Other faces turn to Hrikahl as he slowly walks forwards, his thundering steps putting out the fire as sand is kicked onto it.

   "We don't speak Bath'kahn. Do you understand Korosian?"
The human speaks up. Even in rest, he remains in full-plate armor. Hrikahl remarks at how wealthy he must be among humans for such embossed armor- if only he himself were not so large, perhaps he could have procured something similar.

##    "This one is quite fluent. You are brazen to keep a flame in this place. Is this not where the Eye is most accounted of?"

   "The Annihilator would find us no matter how hard we hid. There's no hiding from the
omniscient. Tell me, mighty Bath'kah. What brings you to us? Have the territories expanded? I fear we must leave if so, this hunt has not been at all successful."

Hrikahl sets himself down gently, pulling a bright-stone chunk from his satchel and giving it to their firekeeper with a light flick. The Ibok nods and flints the stone, setting the campfire back ablaze.
##   "No, Iboki. This one comes seeking hunters itself. It requires your cunning- IF you prove to be cunning, that is. I hunt a creature that has killed my S'vrayn and K'vash- my wife and daughter, you would call these words."

   "What kind of monster would prompt a great Bath'kah to call upon the assistance of the small
ones? A Wyrmling? A Dunewaker?"

##   "A K'ng. This one recalls the Iboki calling them 'Chosen Destroyers.'-"

   "Chrozhran... That is a troublesome foe, venerable giant. We cannot simply agree to help cull
such a beast without reward."
The man looks worried. He is intelligent enough to barter- Hrikahl would have it no other way.

##    "This one is capable of granting several treasures. A black mark, for one."
A black mark is the very least that Hrikahl could do for them if successful. A charm that grants any Iboki toll-free passage through the ancestral grounds
##   "The meat and bones of the Destroyer are yours as well, I only wish for the beast's hide, that I may use it for burial of my dead."

The party look at each other for several minutes, speaking at their own volume far too quite for Hrikahl to hear. They pass a whisper between ears, conniving betwixt one another for their plans to be.

   "You're sure you want us teenies to tag 'long with ya?"
The orcish man speaks up.
   "If'n we're going with you, it'd be faster ta carry all o' us, can you do that? Or is that offensive or
some crap?"

##    "Mmm."
Hrikahl laughs in his thoughts, a whimsical proposition. Another of his ilk may be enraged by such a provocation

##    "This would be most celeritous of an action. This one will carry you and yours onward."
Hrikahl knows the orc speaks truth well enough. They walk slow- annoyingly slow for such short lived creatures. It would be best to carry them.
##    "We shall depart at first light, for now, rest- all of you. Nothing will come with this one looming."

The Iboki nod amongst each other, curling up in their rolls and drifting off in minutes.
