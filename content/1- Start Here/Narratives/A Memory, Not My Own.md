

I dreamt of a memory last night. A memory that I don't own, it doesn't belong to me.


It started as "I" woke. It was loud and dark. A blaring, ringing noise I don't know the source of. I was laying down on some sort of cloth sheet held taught by metal bars. I rose up fast to the noise on instinct, not fear. I hurried to put on complex shoes with laces and straps held together by a hissing fabric all while in complete darkness. I fumbled for something as  I stood and felt a small lever no larger than a finger. Flicking it, a lighting fixture was instantly powered. Others in the room were moving as well out of their beds in panic. Some didn't move at all, with sheets covering their faces and the smell of rot coming from their corners of the room.

I ran out the door into a large hallway, grabbing what looked to me like a rifle, only far more complex than the one's I've held in my hands. It proved to be thus later. Running down the ramshackle hallway something distant and thrumming began to shake my body. And horrific whispers flooded into my mind the further along the hallway I got. Images flashed into my head as other people dressed like myself ran out of rooms in the dozens. They shouted in an elven dialect I could barely parse, but their messages were not good.

"Is he here? Is it go time?" I asked what was likely to be an officer of sorts.

"That alarm doesn't go off for no reason! Get to your-" A few words here I do not understand, but they are similar to the words that would eventually become the modern day words for "rifle"  or perhaps simply "musket."

"Yes sir." I respond with haste. I shove my way past him as a burning sensation takes over my legs. I look down to see glyphs burning my calves and sending me forwards at great speed. I knock over a man coming from his room and hear him thud against the wooden walls before cursing me out and running behind me.

I breach the tunnel quick. Darkness everywhere above and to my left. But to my right a city defends against invisible foes.

"CODE, SERAPHIM! SQAUD, GO, FORWADS, 500 FEET!" A loud voice is carried over the hills to my right and I see what can only be a Dawnhammer crest the hill with a platoon of soldiers all screaming in terror and thirst for battle. They take to defended positions as the Dawnhammer charges past. I run as fast as I can, the Dawnhammer misses me by a few shy inches as it turns to avoid collision.

I look left, now. Thousands of white lights fly out into the darkness and explode over a pockmarked battlefield, like a meteor shower of manmade destruction. Unknowable things scream beyond the darkness as the shrapnel makes contact with whatever it is out there. I run to a foxhole like those used during the revolution and stumble down as my legs give out. With adrenaline still flowing I crawl to a machine crewed by several others.

"WE DON'T NEED YOU HERE! GO FURTHER DOWN THE-"

The woman is cut off as her fellows head is removed from his body.

"GET OVER HERE!"

I crawl forwards as rain hammers down. I shove the dead man out of the way and grab a sash of advanced munitions.

"ECO, FOUR, FOUR, FOUR, FOUR! ALL SQAUDRONS, ENGAGE, LETHAL. MAGES, CAST, PROVINICTION."
The Dawnhammer shouts out commands and the hidden mages in the ranks of the defense obliged. A blinding torrent of light follows the incantations of hundreds. Flares of holy light bring sight to the battlefield.

Millions. Millions of shapes given clarity. Shambling infinities as our towers of steel rained hell upon them. A crying noise of hatred came from the clouds as both man made and real Wyrms pelted the battlefield in fiery inferno. More Dawnhammers, More infantry, more everything piled up and up as those ringing sounds kept going and the bullets kept spewing and the men and woman of this memory kept laughing and crying in pain and adrenaline. My hands burn from the spent casing landing on me but I keep pushing ammo in and in and in, without yielding.

A man goes past me on a wheeled contraption praying with healing for all over and over and as he glances past me my legs are granted feeling and the burn blisters disappear, so I burn my hands again.

Clusters of explosives rack the battlefield and even titans begin to charge from behind us. The night rain pours colder and faster as things that looks like the drawing of the Bath'kah I've seen roar and charge in full plated armor wielding axes and cannons larger than longships.

Eventually so many soldiers amass and so many weapons fire that the light makes it impossible to see the enemy yet again. There is an overwhelming torrent of destruction and obliteration that could not be matched today even if every last mortal on this planet worked to achieve it.

I scream out in horror as the woman aiming the machine's torso is devastated by aberrant magic. I shove her corpse aside and scream at another passing soldier to take her place. He runs over and bashes his nose when he dives down to duck a bolt of hatred coming from the enemy.

The tide shifts slowly after that. One by one minute or hour something important goes down. A cannon down the line, a Bath'kah look-alike in the battlefield, a soldier not even twenty feet from me. The bullets stop flowing, the Dawnhammers stop moving. One by one everything falls to hell.

And then. It all goes quiet.

The gun I feed jams. The air goes silent too as the rain cowers in fear.

Anticipation hits like a horse-drawn cart. Even the enemy has stopped moving.

A light slowly, slowly gleams in the distance. Then grows closer.

A verdant, green eye with black holes behind the iris lights up the night. A golden gleaming glow returns vision to everything, everyone, everywhere. It looks down at all of us, then looks all the way to my right. A beam of light comes from it as it starts to slowly look left, and I am annihilated.
