I, [[Andrich Cain Hyresh]], have born witness to numerous things I'd rather have never gotten sight of. I have heard things best left unspoken. I have touched things previously intangible, and I have smelled the scent of entropy and creation. I have interacted with things that defy everything and I cannot for the sake of my health remove the memory of such atrocities to the material plane... to [[Maernos]] itself from my head.

It was during my expedition into [[The West]]. I was deep inside of [[The Forlorn Graveyard]] with Harold Green's party, and I had gotten separated from them. A [[Monster Group Names|Glimpse]] of [[Grolles]] had cut me off from them, and I was forced to flee lest I be consumed by the aberrations. I remember the fear I felt- the primal, terrifying fear that coursed through me as three of the creatures ran me down, their horrid pig-like screaming echoing behind me as I got distance on them.

I recall climbing up a threshold and holding myself in place with pure adrenaline as they ran underneath of me and I finally heard their howling grow distant. I... I dropped down and fell onto my hands and knees- and when I looked back up... When I looked back up I was not where I had fallen. What was once a simple two-door hallway had become a platform suspended above an endless sea of darkness. Cracks of light shone down from holes in the ceiling as specks of sand fell down into a hole with no bottom. I turned around and looked for a means of egress from this place but only saw the umbral darkness surrounding my entire being.

I paced edge to edge for a time. It had to at least have been thirty minutes... I think. Time felt wrong, I took glimpses back up to the surface and the lights went from that of The Tyrant's to [[The Many Named Moon]]'s own pale glow in but blinks of time between gazing up at the ceiling. I eventually started to hear a rumbling noise- but it sounded wrong. It was like down-pitched speech slowed down to a snail's crawl. Like something trying to act out the words of the entire Book of [[Orpheus]] in one breath. It was undeniably something vaster than the sky speaking to something else. Me? I would find out.

I brought myself to the edge from which the noise was loudest, and I shout out.

"Hello?" I questioned the darkness' occupant. I had hoped it was all but something in my mind- the wild thoughts of a trapped man. I was wrong.
