#### The air is cold, the ruins' ambient, distant noises echo throughout the desolate hallways. Ervis, a small, red-scaled kobold, stares distantly into a pitch black corridor. Around him is carnage. His fellows lay strewn across the ground in piles of flesh and blood. An eye rolls from it's socket and comes to a rest as it collides with Ervis' foot. He doesn't take his eyes from the door.

*drip*
#### He grimaces slightly, his back is to a wall and the only way back out is through the threshold that he- a kobold- cannot see past. Distantly, constantly, something wet drips down to the floor. *drip, drip, drip*: every other second or so. The air keeps getting colder and colder. Ervis blinks one eye at a time as, with great hesitation, he braces to move.

# *drip*
#### He freezes again, though. The sound... the dripping noise... Its behind him now. He can barely see now as the shroud of darkness envelopes him. A shudder goes through both he and the room around him. The walls and floor flicker like a torch, brief visions of something... unknowable. He doesn't move his eyes, but still, he sees. The walls give way for brief seconds to a distant landscape- to show a structure without rhyme or rime.

*hfff*

#### A breath from behind. Ervis stays as still as he can. He closes his eyes as dissonant thoughts mingle with his own. Something moves, then, at that moment- he tries to hide behind his closed eyes. Something heavy, moving like it weighed only a feather. The presence now stood directly in front of him, heaving with weary breaths barely audible above the distant rumblings of the flickering ruins. Then, someone gags on blood inside the room. The sound sends waves of fear down Ervis' spine.

#### "Ervis." Cayden spoke, but it wasn't him. His voice sounded wrong, like something from another world spoke just behind his words. An aberrant puppeteer that made his voice sound metallic and warbled- though so quietly hidden behind the words it would go unnoticed by anything less than a kobold or Catfolk.

#### "Ervis you have to..." The voice behind struggled to find the right words, mumbling for a moment as it regained its composure. "You have to help me. You have to help me... hnnngggg... please."

#### Ervis dared not open his eyes. He knew what happened to Cayden. What the thing did to him. The voice had no mouth to come out of, no head to track the source to.

#### "You have to open your eyes. Please! Bring me the kit.... bring me... ... ... Bring...   br-"

#### The voice behind grew quiet. A wet thud echoed in the room as Cayden's hand slapped onto the concrete floor. The thing moved again, soft cracks in the floor as it silently glided with thousand-ton steps. For a moment, Ervis thought it was leaving- getting bored of this farce. It wasn't.

## *drip

#### Something is touching him now- a million pinions coated in dry acid. His skin bubbles on contact in painless waves of agony. A feeling creeps up his arm where the thing caresses him- a crawling sensation like a dozen centipedes making their way to his face.

#### Everything start flickering again, only it was his thoughts this time instead of this wretched ruin. He sees visions of a chromatic field of grass, sights of a limitless city moving and reorganizing itself in the clouds- an ageless titan sitting on a precipice of reality.

#### The creature breathes on him again- it sounds like metal grinding on metal; bit-crushed breaths from a creature without lungs. It drug its feathered fingers across his chest- left to right. More waves of rippling flesh bounce through his body.

## "Open your eyes. I want to show you something. Something beautiful."

#### The voice is trying to hide again, but not in a voice Ervis knows. Its making one up, trying again to assure Ervis that its safe now. The new voice is feminine, but cold. It tries using charisma but its all so hollow, so charmless that it could be mistaken for some sort of automaton.

"No!" Ervis finally shouts into the room. "Leave me alone! Go away!" Tears make their way up his face, flying upwards into the air as the room shifts.

## *drip 
#### The monster stands still for moment, but Ervis feels it looming nearby. It breathes in again, before moving to the threshold opposite Ervis in the room.
