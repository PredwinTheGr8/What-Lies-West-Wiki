### "What Lies West?"

A question that has plagued generations- an arcane calling to adventure, riches or intrigue. The continent of [[Balphora]] is home to thousands of folk who have left behind their old lives to do one simple thing: **Go West**. Most return empty handed- some maddened by the [[Cardinal Sickness]], some not at all. But there are a handful who return with riches unbound- piles of gold, diamonds and sculptures made from platinum. [[All Major Factions|Massive guilds]] with sponsors from all over the East control everything in [[The City of Wyrms|The City of Wyrms]], from who goes to who leaves.

This land calls to people from [[Koross]]- decades of explorers, smiths, artisans or just people looking for change being called by an arcane desire to **go west**. Ships come in on the dozen every day, dropping off more people, shipping more goods, but almost nothing ever, ever leaves Balphora. Some have left, choosing to return to family or friends, but the people who have come here are usually the ones with nothing to lose in the first place, eager, or desperate, to get away from their past lives across the [[Ocean Magnamous]].

The cities withing Balphora are built by ruffians, brigands or massive noble families all with conflicting interests surrounding relics and riches found deep in the desert, sometimes managing to be brought back through what horrors people describe to be out in the wastes- Monsters. 
 
Monsters are found elsewhere, of course, but the ones within The West are different beasts. Intelligent, conniving and malicious beyond anything previously catalogued across the ocean. There are countless [[All Narratives|stories]] to be told about these beasts.
