([[Build Togethers|470]], [[index|What Lies West?]])

"*Huhhhhh*"
Gk'road Who Fells Beasts takes a deep sigh as the battering light of The Tyrannical Sun hammers down onto his scales.

"Thou art doing well, oh mighty way finder one?"
Ashkram, Gk'road's recorder and primary spellcaster asks.
"Thou looks to be lost, both in thought and in direction way finder."

"I'm fine, just- my feet are really, really starting to hurt. The suns one thing, but this horrid sand is cooking through my soles. I feel like my feet are actually burning at the moment."
Gk'road looks to Ashkram from the other side of the cart moving between them.

"Ahh, then lucky be us that The City is within a days travel, at least according to Makkar Vericrad's work. With luck, by suns death we shall arrive, sound of mind and flesh once more."

Gk'road grins slightly, though its difficult to tell with his anatomy. Other Lizardfolk don't really do grins- Gk'road must be hanging around humans and the like too much. He turns to look at the carriage for a moment.

If the solid silver construction wasn't obvious to its contents, then the purity seals and prayers of of safety from Orpheus would certainly give away the contents within. One male Arcanosaur, thirteen feet long, nine feet tall, and a quarter of a metric ton in weight. The beast is sleepless, even after ancients know how many days of this. Constant thrashing, roaring, screeching and potentially even a few fireballs if the seals break. It was a right pain in the rear to pin the thing down, luckily Ashkram is competent with a magic hand.

"I know this is real- real late to ask now Ashkram-"

"Hmm?"

"-but what exactly does Emmerick want with this thing? It cannot in the slightest be anything good. That Conrad spawn EVEN 'stooped low' enough to ask us, *Lizardfolk* to get one for him."

"Would that I and you be permitted to know, way finder mine. The Verdant Mage paid a particular premium for our discretion on the matter- and even more a-filled our coin purse asking for us not to ask such things- questions."
Ashkram slows his speed slightly to fall into line with the carriage.
"Fortunate, then that he cannot hear us- and we may partake in the wonder of speculation, than be dulled with boredom for 'rest of our trek. What wouldst say you, Storm-bringer?"
Ashkram turns to the carriages driver, Merill- a Catfolk woman, and looks expectingly.

"*Tsk.* War, control, the burning of witches? Who knows. Conrad brats are always up to something and Emmerick is always up to even more than they're. If I had to place a bet though, the former most option. Cocksuckers probably planning on conquering the north and earning his sweet pa-pa's forgiveness for whatever it is he did."
Merill looks done with the conversation. Her fur does nothing to shield her from the heat, let alone the heat of the Tyrant Sun above.

Ashkram straightens up a bit before he talks;
"Would that I had more information. But your theory has evidence. Circumstantial evidence, that is."
Ashkram smiles as he looks at Merill. She returns with a frustrated frown.
"Mayhap the Verdant Mage wouldst only like for a companion? A fool, him, for thinking then that an Ignis Lacerta would make a fine pet- but alas I am not coffered to offer advice, only a prize. Perchance then that the exiled noble only wishes for an exotic meal?"

Gk'road scoffs.
"No way! He's paying us several hundred silvers just to bring him dinner? I think Merills got the right mindset- no way us giving him this will do anything good for Balphora."

The Arcanosaur within the carriage hits a wall again, wailing in pain as the seals light up and burn the creature... again and again. When will it learn that it isn't going anywhere?
It keeps thrashing for several minutes afore going silent again- if only to repeat the process in half an hour.

Time passes on. The Tyrant sun is near what could only be described as noon now, near the center of the sky- though every blink shifts its coordinates by miles. The creature has roused numerous times today. It seems to be concerned with its proximity to The City of Wyrms.

"Ahh!"
Ashkram lights up with relief at the sight on the Horizon, The Wyrmscale Reaches.
"Alas, we have been a-roving for some time- to be within sight of the sky breakers is to be relieved- to be at ease."

A short, quick hissing noise comes from up ahead. Gk'road groans slightly as he unsheathes his sword. Staring at a patch of sand, he sees several beady black orbs peeking shyly out from the dunes.
"Reaper."
Gk'road holds his hand out to stop Merill and the horses.
"I'll deal with the little pest."

With a quiet movement, a large scorpion-like creature ascends from the sands. Gk'road twirls his falchion in his hand and strides confidently forwards.

...

Great. Now Gk'road has Ironback blood all over his drawers... sticky, smells like copper. Perfect- its in his right boot now.
"Surprised it got up in the first place. Carrying around an Arcanosaur you'd be inclined to assume we weren't gonna see anything stupid enough to get in our way. What's that reaper make, twelve now?"

Merill chuckles.
"Fourteen if we were to count that Mire Hulk that tried dragging you to safety Gk'road."

"Hey. You said you wouldn't talk about that. I'm hurt, truly."

"Hmm"
Ashkram thinks for a moment.
"T'would seem that instead of a predator the beasts of this land would instead sense a trapped piece of prey. Verily, they seem eager to attempt at its demise. Recall then, that Wyrmling that followed us twixt even the mighty boughs of the titan trees?"

"Maybe. Though I'm sure a Wyrmling would easily make a meal out of even a healthy Arcanosaur."

"True though that may be way finder, nevertheless it found us time and time again when otherwise it could not have. The Arcanosaur is hidden in scent, and it could not have heard it time and time again thus. Mayhap then that The West wants not for us to take its precious denizen."

"The West isn't alive Ashkram. Not in a sapient way at the very least. If anything's to blame for us being harassed it would have to be the Annihi-"

"SHUT!"
Merill shouts out, throwing her metal flask at Gk'road.
"No invoking that fiend while I'm with you fork-tongue! I'll have none of that thank you very much, Harush protect me and all that music."

"Please"
Gk'road responds.
"Its not omniscient. Only the ancients and maybe the God's are. If you're so scared why'd you even come? It's not exactly a secret that the thing shows up every other day now."

"Girl's gotta get paid right? I can't afford the ferry back to Koross and I'm not gonna stoop back into crime. Though maybe once we deliver this pretty little prince maybe I can afford to sit on my ass for a month or two. A lucky investment and I can afford to leave you two to your monster hunting."

"Ow, my feelings. I thought we were friends Merill?"

"Of course we are, but I'm not foolish enough to keep coming back out here until I die to some horrid freak or massive flying leviathan. Let alone the sun scourge itself."

"The sun-scourge? You mean... The *Annihilator*?"

"Hey! Not funny!"

"Annihilator."

"Stop!"

"Annihilator."

"Cut it!-"

"Halt."
Ashkram speaks above their childish banter. The other two, and their entourage do as he says without so much as a peep.
"Doth thou feel the pressure now in the air? The sallow words beneath the soil? The acidic thoughts in the gaps between gales?"

"What!? What are you talking about Ashkram!?"
Merill places her hands at her sides, ready to spring off if something happens.

Gk'road chuckles slightly, before the others join in as well.

"What?"
Merill looks around confused.
"Oh. Ha ha."

Ashkram stifles a laugh too, before the group away to the City on the Horizon.
