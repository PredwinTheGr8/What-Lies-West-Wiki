([[Build Togethers|396]], [[What Lies West]])

It was cold.

The blood pooled onto the ground at his back. Girtz heard his allies shouting foggy commands as the Abomination roared in the distance. Harold ran over and stood above him, Girtz's Human leader stared down with a frown before shouting incoherently at the backline.

The Abomination roared again, though inaudible as it was Girtz still felt its bellow shake the tiles on the floor under him. He barely managed to keep his eyes open long enough to see Amelia running up to him, glowing golden light accumulating in her hands... But the black was brighter.

Like a sudden tug, Girtz found himself standing in a large field. Quiet wind thrashed the knee-high grass and flowing dandelion seeds flitted through the air. There was still no sound.

Girtz took a look around him, spinning in a slow circle as he took in his surroundings. Nothing but prairie grass and distant trees as far as the eye could see. As he looked out, he saw his shadow- looking at it for but a moment, his heart skipped a beat.

Suddenly, faster than the blink of an eye, a silent movement occurred behind him. His shadow was drowned out by a far larger one- one that blocked out many tens of feet in darkness. Girtz turned to look, and found that suddenly, without explanation, a large gateway had appeared directly behind him.

As soon as he laid eyes upon it, sound returned. The winds blew with great speed, the grass scraped against itself, and the massive gate held within it muffled chimes.

"Hello?" Girtz stared up at the gate. Taking a few steps back to process just how massive this thing truly was. Finally, after several agonizing seconds of wait, the Gate went ajar. Girtz felt an overwhelming pressure it him- a wave of dark, invisible miasma that punched him in the soul itself. He wanted to run, but his legs no longer moved.

Each chime of the bell inside, he felt closer and closer to death. Or was he already dead? Did Amelia's spell not work? Was this-

The light became brighter. The Gate disappeared. His eyes opened. Screams of commands and attacking strikes echoed again within the dark chamber. Amelia stood over him, hands covered in blood, eyes watering as she looked towards the ongoing battle. She looked down to see Girtz looking back up at her.

'You're alive!" She smiled slightly as she hurried to give Girtz his hammer.

"Indeed I am!" Girtz let out a manic smile as he got up. He surveyed the situation. Looked winnable, no one else was on the ground.

"Right then. COME AND GET ME YAH MALFORMED TESTICLE WITH TEETH!"
