[[Build Togethers|335]], [[index|What Lies West?]]

*The vet and the rookie sit at a tavern table, enjoying a cool pint of Bunker's finest. The vet looks at their leader- "The Phoenix" and speaks up to his fresh colleague.*

'She's not the same anymore. She always seems distant, alienated from us. I suppose that's just a side effect of seeing the other side.'
'The other side?'
'Yeah, the afterlife- what comes after death. You don't know?'
'Know what? She's... she's died before?'
'Heh... I guess you haven't been with us long eh? Yeah. Yes she has, she doesn't talk about it, I understand why... I **think** at least.'

*The vet adjusts his seat, scooting forward and leaning in, taking a large swig of liquid gold before continuing.*

'It was nigh on a year ago, we'd been in The West for several months straight, drinkin' the blood of the thicket and feasting on the flesh of ironbacks or anything else that was good for eats out there. As was regular, we didn't go out with a destination in mind, the desert doesn't care none about where you want to go, only where it wants you to go. Now, we had found ourselves in the Forlorn Graveyard-'
'What's that place like?'
'We haven't gone there with you yet? By Orpheus we need to get you out there more often man... uh its sort of... sad? The 'grave' part of the name is from all the giant spires coming from the ground and reaching into the sky. The place was obviously important, once, but now whatever structures were built there have been swallowed by the sand- foundations lost forever. Sometimes one of the spires has a window and you can get inside, good loot in those ancient halls.'
'Has anyone ever gotten trapped?'
'Nah, but your getting me off track, keep your questions for once my stories done kiddo.'
'Oh, sorry sir, of course.'

*The vet takes another hearty drink from his flagon and spreads his hands out, pointing around himself wildly*

'We were inside one of the aforementioned spires, 'Melia- er, **A**melia was doin a good job keeping us all alive with her magic, and of course, I was good for keeping the wee crawlies from the important folk with my hammer.'

*The vet pats his massive great hammer on the pommel before taking a deep breath and continuing*

'No siree, didn't matter what foul beastie I faced my might was always the better- until it wasn't.'
'What do you mean?'
'I'm getting to it young'un! Don't interrupt a recounter when he's setting up the drama!'
'Sorry...'
'Good! *ahem*. We found a large chamber- now we had seen em before, there was always a big statue in the middle, but it always seemed lifeless- never gave any thoughts to it being able to move. But this time, when me and the other vanguard walked in we all paused. The statue was there, as it always was, but this one was... off.'

*The rookie looks on with baited breath as, once again, the vet pauses to drink*

'It had... an air of authority to it, a regality like that of a king- not a shoddy human one, but like an ancient dwarven lord- bold, wise, knowing of all. We told the group such, that we could feel its eyes looking at us. But despite our repetitive correctness with such matters the rest of the group didn't believe us, didn't help that Mickey's nervous laughter made them think we was joshing 'em.'
'What happened?'
'They went forward, pushing past us. Luckily I managed to grab Amelia afore she walked forwards more and held her back just in case. I told her to stay put as I rushed to take up the front, though I kept assuring them the bloody thing would spring to life any moment of course.
'So we stepped forward, I lifted my hammer and the rest of must have finally realized we were serious because they stopped moving too- to late though. The eyes of the statue lit up with a foul light, the kind that sent shivers down your spine.'
'What color?'
'Ya know, that's the damn question isn't it? I remember red, Mickey remembers a sickly green and Amelia says they were definitely purple. Anyways, the stone beasty took a heaving step forward- off of its pedestal towards us. I'll be honest, I didn't rightly know what I was supposed to do, it was massive- taller than the average building 'round these parts and made of solid stone to boot. Ivan, our old mage before you, well he started panicking, flinging spells at it splashing against its armored body like water on treated silk.'
'Wow.'
'Wow indeed lil fella. I had to dodge out the way from its path of movement- it walked straight past me despite my rampant hootering and hollering trying to taunt it. No, it ignored all us melee fighters like it knew- well I suppose that's not unique among monsters out there, they always try and get at the backline at the soonest opportunity. Amelia screamed and started running, but Jane stood her ground, she fired arrow after arrow up at its face as we tried to distract it. It reached down to swipe at her but she barely managed to roll out of the way. The statue held its hand up to its face- expecting to see her like when ya swipe at a fly.
'It stood there for a moment while us warriors bashed at its feet. Amelia was far in the back panicking just like Ivan- throwing bolts of holy light at the face of the statue to no avail. I remember the look on Mickeys face when it made another swing at Jane. There was a crunch and a spray of blood as she barely mustered a scream. The guardian, sentinel, whatever it was lifted her up to its eyes, looking at her die like a curious toddler, coking its head to the side quizica- quizim... questioningly sorry that's a hard word for me.'
'Keep talking!'
'Yeah, yeah yeah- I looked up at Jane and saw the life flee from her eyes as blood pooled out from the hand of the statue, splattering the ground with a canvas of crimson. We all stood shellshocked for a moment. Sure, we had lost folk before but it always felt like it was because we made a mistake- because there was a way to turn things around... but this time, no. No from the moment it woke there was nothing we could do. It felt... beautiful in a way, at least to me but everyone calls me mad so maybe the others didn't feel that way.'
'Yeah, your insane man.'
'Oi! Shut your gob! Anyhow, the Statue didn't move either, it just kept looking at her for nigh on half a minute. It felt like an eternity before Amelia let out a scream of terror that echoed throughout the whole dungeon, and that's when the statue flinched. It opened up its palm and looked down at Jane like it didn't mean to kill her. It looked at all the blood on the floor with its blank expression and trembled. It looked down at the rest of us like it didn't know what to do, before looking back to Jane with some sort of epiphany. It reached out with its empty hand and that same malicious glow, again the color is very argued upon, flew like locusts into Janes mouth. It set her down on the ground, and walked around us like caltrops back to its pedestal and went dormant.'

*The vet pauses to take another drink but finds his flagon empty- he slams it down on the table and the barkeep walks over with a fresh pint for him*

'I walked over to her with Mickey and the gang, and saw that she looked... well completely fine. Her armor had taken some damage but she didn't have no cuts or broken bones. I poked at her and, wouldn't you believe it she gasped back to life. She looked around like a scared fawn, back up to the unmoving stone-man and looked absolutely terrified of... well everything. She saw the afterlife and to this day she won't talk about it.'
'So, what happened after?'
'Why we pillaged the ruins and made bank of course! We wouldn't be so shooketh to our core as to not get our dosh! What kind of adventurers would we be if a single death that became a questioning of the concept of mortality stopped us from doing our job, eh!? Am I right Jane!?'

*The vet raised his flagon to The Phoenix, she returned with a half-assed toast herself with a slight smile*

'Maybe you'll die next eh, and you'll get a cool story to tell all the ladies!'
'Uhh, no thanks man, I'm good.'
