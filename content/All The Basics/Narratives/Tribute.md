[[Build Togethers|348+349]], [[index|What Lies West?]]

The harsh, bitter winds of The Void Dunes rattle the weary bones of a group of adventurers- they have been travelling for a long while, searching specifically for this place. Among their number is a rare sight in the wastes of The West, a scholar of the Explorator's Guild, hoping to have a conversation with the locals of The Void Dunes.

The group of thirteen slog their way through the dense dunes, trudging past a massive herd of beasts that graze on the small, hair like Umber Grass poking just a finger's length through the sand.

'We're sure this is a good idea? It took a long time to get here in the first place, we're already short on rations- can we really afford the tribute?' The group's Elf, Virani looks concerned-like to their leader, Gk'road Who Fells Beasts, a Lizardman.

'Yes, our *client*'- Gk'road pauses to shoot daggers at the scholar-' Doesn't want to wait any longer.'

'Be angry at me all you wish,' Ammon the scholar retorts, 'I paid you to find the Bath'kah in this expedition, you're the ones who agreed to my "absurd" terms.'

The others remain silent, but aware. They constantly look out to the herd on their right, seeing the red paint over the Void Bisons, marking them as the property of the giants that call this place home. Eyes shift between each silhouette, checking to make sure that one of the gray-skinned marauders isn't stalking them from the herd's midst.

'Remind me, Virani, was it? How *big* are the Bath'kah?' Ammon asks.

'However big you think they are, probably bigger. Some of the chieftains I've seen could swallow a man in a single gulp, I'm sure of it.'

The scholar suddenly looks much less sure of himself.

Finally, as if to break the tension, three hulking figures appear from the sandstorm, looming in front of the party. Hides hang from their shoulders, massive metal weapons rest in their palms, heads buried in the dark blue sand. They tower above any of the largest Ogres back home in Koross, at least sixteen, maybe eighteen feet tall.

### 'Rauk mol ibok.'
'The toll, small one.' One of the behemoths speaks with the weight of the world- a deep, rumbling voice that shakes bones.

'Of course, mighty Bath'kah.' Gk'road gestures back to the rearguard, ushering them to unveil the cart they've been forced to drag for several days after a blight of Grolles ate their mule. The guard nod and pull back the red carpet- revealing dried meats, spices, ale, butter and sugary treats, all made as big as possible for the mighty giants in front of them. The three giants look amongst each other and smile.

## 'Mmmm, very well done, small one. Come, the chieftain would meet with such... magnanimous guests.'

The trek was long- The Bath'kah titans that guided them seemed very, *very* annoyed at the slow walking pace of the party. Every ten steps or so, the giants halted and waited tiredly for the adventurers to catch up. Eventually, whether out of genuine curiosity, or to quench boredom, one of the goliaths starts conversation with Gk'road.

## 'What brings you here, small one? I have not seen one of your kind before. Are you ill?'
The Bath'kah looks down to the Lizardfolk, his massive face blotting out the barely visible sun that shyly pierces the storm.

'No, no I am not ill. I come from a place full of water far from this place, like others you may have met. But my home is covered by mighty trees, though not as mighty as you, of course. I am what the common tongue names a Lizardfolk.'

## 'Hm. Lizardfolk. I will remember this for the next time I meet one of you're... ilk.'

'Ilk? A noble's word, truly you are very well learned, mighty Bath'kah.'

## 'Indeed I am- But you forgot part of my question, small one.'

'Ah, of course, oh inexorable titan. You see this man in blue robes?' Gk'road gestures to Ammon, who stares with awe at the Bath'kah. 'He is a man of intellect as well, you see he has heard a great deal of the mighty Bath'kah and would love nothing more than to learn of your ilk.'

## '*Why?*'
The giant's eye's narrow, addressing Ammon now.

'Oh! Well you see, my kind like books a great deal- specifically the books that relate to history- to the past. I come to ask the great, and well versed Bath'kah of their own history. Are there any rememberers within your clan?'

## 'Rememberers?'

'Poets, novelists, story tellers?'

The giant looks off into the distance, staring far away at the outline of The Colossal Rift, barely peaking through the sandstorm. He thinks for several seconds, before answering.

## 'The Chieftain, Ulgrek. He is old, the oldest. He is big too, the biggest. He can answer any question, I think.'

'Ulgrek, I see. And what of you and yours, mighty Bath'kah? What are your names?'

Before long, the storm comes to a halt at the sight of two great wooden pillars. Glyphs of magic glow and dispel the wind surrounding the Bath'kah camp. The group of twelve shrink in dread, barring Gk'road, who walks with confidence. There are at least fifty of them here, from infants the size of a man to ancient, pockmarked elders that stand as tall as a bastion- all staring at the newcomers. Some with curiosity, others with dismissal, but some of them... with the hope that their toll was not good enough. They look disappointingly, though, as the group is walked to the biggest, most colorful teepee in the camp- for they know that it was a most generous toll.

Inside the tent, a monolithic, twenty five foot tall at least Bath'kah male stands. His hair is long and grey, trailing down his back and stopping just below his hips. His hands are pale, but well defined with muscle. His attention is drawn to a canvas of solid linen- a gift from a previous group of adventurers. He places hefty strokes, each movement showing off his massive muscles- not the kind just given to a Bath'kah, though. No, these were earned by picking very heavy things up, and placing them back down.

He pauses, placing one more blot of paint before finally turning to his audience.

# 'Welcome, oh honored Adventurers|adventurers. This must be a rich week for The City of Wyrms, mustn't it? What with you all coming to my sands and giving me such generous gifts, that is. I assume this is because of a most generous gift and not something more... unfortunate?'

The larger Bath'kah among their guides steps forwards, and places his hand over his eyes in respect.

## 'No, wise Ulgrek! These small ones have brought the greatest delights. We only ask for a portion of their gifts in exchange for guarding them on their journey.'

# 'Hm. Very well, present my toll to me, children of the East.'

Gk'road nods, and gestures once again to the rearguard to unveil the payload.

# 'Ah! Very good! Very good indeed!'
The colossus kneels down to the cart, and though the portions are clearly far too small, his face still gives way to a childlike glee.
# 'Meats! Cheeses! Oh, and of course the greatest of delicacies!'
Ulgrek reaches down into the cart and picks up the largest of a the gifts- a keg of dwarven ale, 5 tons of it, enchanted with glyphs to lighten it's load significantly.
# 'A-hah! Most generous of you, East-born. It even fits in my hand! Hah! Tell unto me, small ones, why have you come all this way with such dangerous prizes for the beasts to smell?'

Ammon steps forward, and clears his throat while he nervously shakes. 'Well, mighty Ulgrek, I have come to ask you of your peoples' history. I wanted to ensure that I gave the most appealing gifts as I could, in exchange for such a valuable prize, at least to my people that is.'

# 'Indeed? That is all? Very well, very well! My sons of the winds, go and fetch some small chairs for my guests!'

## 'Of course, mighty Ulgrek. But-'

# 'Yes, yes bring some for yourselves as well, It would be glutenous of me to partake of this bounty alone.'

The guards smile before departing. Returning some time later with a handful of tiny stools, dusty and under-used- as well as with some far larger boulders, fresh from the dunes. Everyone takes their seats, grabs their snacks and grog- and hold their breath as the chieftain leans in to speak.

# 'Eons ago, when the Bath'kah were not called that name, we ruled over a portion of a great kingdom. Mighty stone walls, taller than a beast thrice my size- and yet, we were that size. Yes, we were taller than the clouds!... Or so it is said. No, I think that we may have been a good bit taller, but the clouds are very, very tall. You know this, you have looked up.'

Ammon nods with dumb awe.

# 'We ruled as proxies for a great king, and his kingdom. We served him for generations under his eternal guidance. We waged war when he asked. We sowed fields when he commanded. We build cities when he deigned! It was purposeful! But, one year, the King went silent. He hid away in his citadel of bone and spoke with us, and all of his disciples no more.

# 'Our ancestors waited, and waited. But as time went on, other things seemed to stop. The rivers stopped flowing, the crops stop growing, the fields stopped sowing. Grass withered and died, trees dried away, and rivers sunk into the earth. Then, when all was thought to be at its worst- it happened. The crawling sand came. The Bath'kah were mighty, but armies cannot fight time- nor can any number of anything stop the march of the inexorable.

# 'Our ancestors did not run, though. We were the Bath'kah! We were the strongest! But strength does not have even the slightest chance against an infinite foe that cannot bleed, nor think. The others fled, if the stories are to be believed. But we stayed, and we shrunk, and we fought for territory. Now, what was once hundreds of thousands is but hundreds. The Bath'kah are defeated, and their death is slow.'

Ulgrek looks down for a moment, before taking a swig of amber mead.

'Amazing!' Ammon shouts. 'Thank you, thank you so, so much! Oh the others won't ever laugh at me again, hah! Come on come on! We have to get back to the city! Come on!

The party sighs with weary hearts as they heave themselves up. Gk'road looks at the wagon, then back to Ulgrek. 'You can keep this, if you like. Would make for a good source of kindle, I assume.'

# 'Hah! Or an excellent toy for the youth. Walk with swiftness, son of Moulugg.'

Gk'road looks up at Ulgrek with a shocked expression, but smiles for a moment before he makes his leave behind the others.

## 'We used to be bigger?'
The large guard asks.

# 'Hah! I don't know, I just made it up to make the sniveler happy!'

## 'Oh that's rich, boss.'
