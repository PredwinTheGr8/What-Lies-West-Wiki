[[Iradeen Thaddeus|Ira]] looks down with a grin on his face to the letter in his hands.

*'I report that, on this day, the '[[Orc Revolution|orc revolution]]' and its effects on [[Koross]] have been successfully put to an end. I will report in person to secure my promised reward for the [[Minotaurs|Minotaur]] peoples, expect my arrival within the hour this letter arrives.'

"Father?" [[Maximillian Thaddeus|Max]] speaks up withing the silent office. He looks at Ira expecting to hear the letter's contents.

"They've done it boy. The beef-men have brought the Orcs to heel, at long last." Ira takes a sip from his wine and smacks his lips slightly. "Bring Amelia here will you? I have other promises to fulfill."

"Marriage? Isn't it a bit too-"

"Silence. I will do as I want, child. Bring her here. Now." Ira stares at Maximillian with fury in his eyes, anger at the very idea that his son would question him.

"I- Very well father. I shall bring her here post haste."

Max leaves the room with a rapid near-jog, almost slamming the door behind him if it weren't for its massive size.

Ira stares deeply at more documents, the contracts made with the so called 'demi god' [[Immolesh]]. After seeing the Minotaurs effect on the war, it would be very, *very* unwise to refuse their demands. Ira has less than two thousand soldiers left, a far cry from the previous twenty three thousand, five hundred and thirteen- give or take an extra thousand or so. With a finger to his temple Ira dips his pen and signs the papers.

*It's not right.* He thinks. *What could have been done, though? If only those damned knife ears hadn't gone and pissed off the hornets nest in that mire we would have been fine!*

The knocker on his office rings out three times.

"Enter." Ira says just loud enough for it to carry through the mighty mahogany door. A small Kobold heaves open the door just barely, straining his legs just to crack it ajar.

"L-Lord Thaddeus! The F-Fire Tamer is here to s-see you, urgently he says! Shall I-"

"Send him in, and don't dawdle, Dawdle." Ira stacks the papers neatly and prepares to place his wax seal onto the pile. He looks up to see the hulking figure of Immolesh pushing open the door fully.

Two other Minotaurs enter, fitting nicely through the threshold, but Immolesh places his hand on the top side of the frame, cracking the wall slightly with his might as he leans down to enter. Dawdle shirks away the moment Immolesh's ashen hoof enters the room. He skitters off to fulfill some other task and look to busy to bother. Immolesh's advisors walk silently despite their immense size compared to a man. Their cloaks betray no signs of movement as they glide to either side of the room. Immolesh, though, shakes the room slightly with each careful step. He is covered head to toe in silver plate- shin guards and a mighty armored skirt covering his loin. A cuirass adorned with black steel and gold embroidery rests on his torso. He removes and now holds a helm the size of a normal mans chest, it has hinges on it which allow it to be efficiently be dawned and doffed, despite the unusual shape of a Minotaurs head.

In his other hand, a mighty black halberd clinks against the floor with each step. Small ashes fall from its haft with each light impact against the vibrant yellow rug, staining it with small specks of sallow grey.

Ira grimaces slightly at the seen, he just had that carpet made last week and nobody but him was allowed to walk directly on it... not that he would dare tell Immolesh that. Outwardly Ira remains calm, but inside his thoughts betray his true feelings. Immolesh is haunting to him. The very thought that Immolesh could incinerate him in a moment, or cleave his head off, or rip him in twain, or-... No, its best not to think such things.

Immolesh stops just before Ira's desk. He looks down at the small platter of meats and cheeses with wine and frowns slightly.

### "You look comfortable, Thaddeus. Enjoying your victory?" 
Immolesh's words are like acid in the air, his breath is near-molten, his speech laced with sparks.

"I am." Ira barely manages to look at him as Immolesh talks, squinting his eyes like you would when looking at a campfire. "I have prepared everything, you and your people are granted permanent citizenship within the borders of the now perfect kingdom of [[Koross]]."

## "Perfect? Immaculate, you say? Nary a man in rags on a corner? A sick man in a hospice? A hungry man day dreaming food on his table?"
Immolesh hides strikes in his words no doubt. He means to belittle Ira by comparing his kingdom to its occupants.

## "Who will make them houses now? Who will forge you new weapons? Who will starve in their place?"
Immolesh looks down, more than ever, at Iradeen. His eyes squint narrowly- small specks of red light with a dim glow coming from his eyelids.

"A ridiculous question no doubt. Now that you've brought them to heel it will be the greenskins, as it has been for centuries." Ira grins as if he aced some sort of quiz.

## "Unfortunate, Ira. I had hoped this debacle would have made you realize something very, very important." 
Immolesh looks at his attendants for a moment before continuing.
## "You will not be receiving more slaves. Not now, not ever."
The demi-god's grip tightens around his halberd.

Ira's face goes through a slew of different emotions. Confusion- realization- finally landing on anger.

"Wha-! Who do you think you are!? You would deny me my property!? Do you know how hard I have worked my entire life to get to where I am now you damnable whoreson!?" Ira stands up rapidly, pointing his fork up at Immolesh. "If you did not do as I asked so perfectly perhaps it would have been *you* on this plate you half-spawned waste crawler! You will bring me my trophies! You will drag them to me in *CHAINS* do you hear me!?"

Ira took deep breaths as he stared angrily at the Minotaur. For a moment nothing but his anger filled his mind, but after three, maybe four seconds he realizes just what he has done. He makes and attempt to back up.

Immolesh lets out a sigh, placing his helm down onto the table before reaching over it and grabbing Ira by the chest. Ira winces at the impact and constriction of Immolesh's fingers, struggling in vain as he is raised up to meet the Minotaur eye-to-eye.

## "You disgust me."
Immolesh looks at Ira's twisted face with a frown. He takes in a deep breath before continuing.
## "Have you learned nothing, old man? Is your brain as mottled as your skin? As bare of wrinkles as your head is of hair? Your wits as gone as your virility? Look!"
Immolesh carries Ira out to his massive stained glass window. Below, his city stretches on to the horizon, three glorious miles of civilization. Or it would be.
## "Do you see? Not a single smoke column from a fireplace! Nary a child in the streets! Your 'perfect' kingdom is on its last legs! Its crumbling to pieces because of YOUR mistakes Ira! This is YOUR fault! You will- WILL- release the orcs from their bondage. Maybe, just maybe I can convince some to remain but even Orpheus would know that you are nothing but scum of the earth. Tell me, Ira, where is your wife? Sorry, your thirteenth wife, right? How desperate are you for love that failure after failure you seek it out just to fail again? Do you? Feel love, I mean?"
Immolesh turns and slams Ira onto his desk. He pulls a pistol from its brace and puts it against Ira's head.
## "Does your son have hobbies? Does your Daughter draw art? What about the others that could have been? Would Kalli have ever dreamt of riding a horse? Would Samwell ever had begged for a broadsword on his name day? Do you know why Max hasn't come back with his SEVEN YEAR OLD SISTER? Because she isn't here! All of your efforts to conquer and you will STILL die alone! All of this bloodshed and yet it is you will bleed a slow and painful end! Perhaps, then, I should end it for you? I'm sure Max would be MORE than happy to sing s simple piece of paper to save his own hide! What do you say Ira? Should I ruin your 'families' victory with the end of their leader? No, that's impossible, it would only make it better wouldn't it?"

"On-"

## "SPEAK UP COWARD!"

"On second thought... I believe I will sign that paper."
