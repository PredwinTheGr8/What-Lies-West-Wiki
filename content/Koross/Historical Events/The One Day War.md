"GATHER ROUND! BROTHERS AND SISTERS OF [[Ramradga|RAMRADGA]]! TODAY, WE BREAK OUR RULERS ON OUR KNEES! FOR [[The Great One|THE GREAT ONE]]!"

-The rousing speech of an unnamed [[Goblins|Goblin]] at the beginning of The One Day War.

The 'One Day War', is a name given to a brief, near-bloodless conflict between the at the time vassalized Goblin kingdom of Ramradga and their rulers, [[House Thaddeus]] and by extension their other vassals. House Thaddeus had just suffered massive losses, both in population and income, from the [[Orc Revolution]]. Over 20-30% of their population was lost to the war- with most of them being conscripted farmers and blacksmiths. Even discounting the losses from the freedom of the [[Orcs]], the extreme number of artisanal and provisional workers who lost their lives meant that [[Koross]] was in for a long recovery, if it ever would at all.

To make up for population losses, [[House Thaddeus]] reluctantly opened up their borders to their vassalized neighbors, who could freely move into Koross and embed themselves into their society. Among these immigrants were thousands, *thousands* of [[Goblins]]. Their [[The Great One|Great One]], the leader of Goblinkind, told every last goblin to leave of a date- a day when they would rise up and declare war for their freedom from the tyrannical rule of [[Iradeen Thaddeus]].

This day was mere weeks after Koross had opened its borders, and all at once, across their empire, goblins took to the streets as The Great One declared a war of freedom against House Thaddeus.

There was an hour or so as soldiers reported the assaults and defended keeps, but the vast numbers of Goblins and other rioters overwhelmed their own number. Reports came in hours after the war started to Iradeen, who saw the writing on the wall. The doom of his House would be in the ashes of another conflict.

And so, a ceasefire was called and the two rulers met- the leader of [[House Thaddeus]] and the King of [[Ramradga]]. A treaty was signed- in exchange for ending the war Ramradga would be granted extra land and be officially recognized as its own nation separate of Koross. [[Iradeen Thaddeus|Iradeen]] took a sigh of relief, but it was short a short lived levity.

Koross' other vassals- the [[Ratfolk]] and other [[Humans|Human]] noble houses- took the opportunity to declare their own sovereignty. Today, 'Koross', the kingdom, has shrunk to 16% of its original size, though House Thaddeus still has a great amount of coin in their coffers to plot further dominion it seems that they may be doomed to fade away.
