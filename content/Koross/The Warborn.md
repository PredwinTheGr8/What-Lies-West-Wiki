A term, like [[The Plain Three]] and [[The Beastpact]], used to describe the apparent allegiance with the[[Minotaurs]], [[Ogres]], [[Orcs]] and [[Centaurs]]. Other lineages fear their capability for destruction, and promote this name sometimes as truth to their nature- a form of prejudice the larger peoples do not appreciate.
