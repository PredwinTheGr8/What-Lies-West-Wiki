A scorched, desolate region that now lies completely empty, but once was home to the [[Minotaurs]]. Their ancient bastions dot the [[Phrazmollograggra Mountains]], and their empty villages lay scorched across the lands. Sometimes the Minotaurs of [[The Herd of Immolesh]] will send out warbands to pillage their own civilizations and reclaim their lost weapons.
