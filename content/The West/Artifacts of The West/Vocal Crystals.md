([[Build Togethers|347]], [[What Lies West]]?)

[[The Old Empire of The West]] is perhaps the most curious civilization in the world. It has left behind many an arcane curio, from ancient weapons with no keys, to monolithic golems with no life in them, the ancients that inhabited this land before the known world was even drawn were quite the inventors.

One of their ancient creations that can still be found to this day are what is known as 'Vocal Crystals'. These small, inconspicuous gemstones are laden with ancient script- the alien language of the Old Empire. Select scholars, those who have studied [[The West]] the longest, have managed to decipher parts of the language used in The West. The writing placed upon these Vocal Crystals is the command word to activate them- and though it took some time to figure out the spoken word, as that is an entirely different challenge, eventually, one of these Vocal Crystals was activated.

The story told by these crystals is, rather unfortunately, completely undecipherable. The words and tales within have been eroded by sand and time- coming out as a garbled mess of sounds and growls. To this day, not a single crystal has been in any way even remotely coherent, but that wont stop merchants from buying them up for a pretty platinum.
