([[Build Togethers|360]], [[What Lies West]]?)

"Woah. That's the first thing you'll say, or think when you see them for the first time. Calling them 'mountains' might just be the greatest insult in the world- they're not mountains, their impassable diamond stones that choke the sky- they block the clouds, they smite the sun. The [[Phrazmollograggra Mountains]] are the tallest mountains only because these aren't such a petty thing as a 'mountain."

-Rakrak The Mouse

The Wyrmscale Reaches are not mountains, they are monoliths of diamond rock- a solid, immutable surface resistant to the weathering of time itself. The Wyrmscale Reaches climb far past the clouds- so high that the snow on their surface is choked away, a layer of ice and sleet disappearing away as the very air itself fails to climb the side of the bladed structure.

The Wyrmscale Reaches are visible for many more miles than any other geological feature within [[Balphora]], [[Koross]] and perhaps even [[The Wider World|beyond]]. Whoever... whatever made these, for no amount of time could ever form something like this, they left no clue as to their purpose, not alone at least.

The [[Bath'kah]] of [[The Void Dunes]] speak of part of the ancient calamity- the so called 'crawling sands' of even further west that ate the world and left it bleeding. It seems, if their beliefs hold true, that the Wyrmscales were made to stop such a disastrous anomaly from consuming the entire world... but whether or not it truly guards the entire globe or not is unknown, perhaps the west is closer than all think, slinking inwards from the north and south, or the east even.
