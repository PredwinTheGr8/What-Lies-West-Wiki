"The whole place feels off. The silent, purple monoliths barely holding on by a thread keeping the isles aloft. The quiet gales raging beneath the rock howling distantly. The stray pebble falling down from impossible heights. And lastly, that ominous bridge from the ground- ascending up and up into the fog above. The creatures take the cake too. Hollow specters wailing in rocky prisons. Scurrying black voids with shells clambering at your feet. Flying glyphs of angry magic. The Shattered Divide is as far from natural as any other place in the west- hell, I'd feel safer crawling around the intestines of some ancient horror in [[Ossuary Reach|The Reach]]."

-Ezlan, [[Adventurers|adventurer]]

The Shattered Divide is a [[All Regions of The West|region]]of [[The West]] characterized by countless floating islands suspended above the clouds. These isles vary in size from barely a man's width to well over twenty miles acrost. The islands are kept aloft by numerous [[Graviturgy]] sigils embedded on obelisks throughout the region. The largest of these obelisks was The Gleaming Bastion, though nowadays it has been annihilated. The Islands are often connected via large, rickety bridges that sometimes strain themselves as the isles float apart. If they break, an island is liable to drift off for a ways before exiting the [[Sites of Power]] that the divide sits on, causing the hunk of earth to fall into the sands below.

[[Adventurers]] feel uneasy within The Divide, it always smells as if about to rain- a facet of Graviturgy's presence- and the altitude can sometimes mess with the headspace of a person. Many claim the place itself is cursed, that walking on its rock is to invite suffering of the soul. The creatures of the region help none in alleviating stress. From the innocent [[Goodfellows]] to the harrowing [[Whgoggs]] all of the creatures within The Divide are completely, and utterly unnatural. Some have mouths and digestive tracts, sure, but a great majority are specters and ghosts, at least in appearance.

The place is, in truth, healthy for the soul. The magic produced, or rather refined, by the obelisks is of the highest quality- which is what allows fae creatures like Goodfellows to exist here in the first place. This alone means that The Divide is currently the only region safe to remain within indefinitely. Another, mild fact is that ever since [[The Annihilator]] destroyed the Gleaming Bastion, it has not been seen here again- though perhaps its finally learned how to hide effectively.

As said above, the creatures of The Shattered Divide are strange and almost all ethereal, at least partially. While there may be some discrepancy on permanent residents and even whether or not some Monsters actually visit this region, the list below is- to my knowledge- completely correct.

[[Goodfellows]]: Native. Found hiding in crevices waiting for a passing group of adventurers worthy of their 'quest.'
[[Arba-barba]]: Native. Found flitting about in and out of the rocks eating particles of magic. Dwarven for 'little flyer'.
[[Ckrazza]]: Native. Often spied distantly staring at adventurers with their many eyes. Dwarven for "Pervert."
[[Spectotums]]: Native, invasive in other regions. Encountered in violent battles to the death, wailing and flailing with their ghastly bodies in a vain attempt to quash their thirst for blood.
[[Whgoggs]]: Native. Harrowingly seen crawling about with shaky muscles and flaking skin. Their acidic saliva drips into the earth and feeds the viscous flora in their wake. Named by a Kobold whom misspelled the word "Scary"... By a lot as you can see.
[[Wyrmlings]]: Perhaps Native, but they nest in other regions as well. Seen flying above or crashing down to battle adventurers. [[Goodfellows]] often lead adventurers to these beasts' dens.
[[Umbral Whimseys]]: Native. Seen aimlessly flying around like a sea jelly. Morphs into a persons deepest fear for a moment when touched- though it is only a visible change.
[[Carna-Charrakaa]]: Native. Often seen at the last moment before it attempts to take the head off an adventurer. Dwarven for "Flesh Reaper".
[[Immolators]]: Non native and incredibly invasive. Spawn of [[The Annihilator]] that's soul purpose is to destroy, rarely seen burning out [[Goodfellows|Goodfellow]] cairns and melting down Obelisks. Kill on sight.
[[Babblin' Brooks]]: Native. Seen harmlessly floating in mid air, their bubbly bodies making a constant bubbling noise, hence their name. Agued upon whether or not it is in fact a living being or just a byproduct of immense Graviturgy.
[[Rancor Glyphs]]: Elemental, present wherever Graviturgy has a high presence itself. A hostile mote of purple light that approaches adventurers silents before ripping them apart at the limbs. Impossible to kill, impossible to deter. Avoid at all costs.
