"Fire, ash and ruin- like the [[Phrazmollograggra Mountains]] back home. Though the heat here is constant- never wavering agony. You'll sweat out rivers, the wrong rock will set your pants on fire, and the beasts wandering the lakes of fire would make a meal out of you in an instant. But the same dangers are profitable- gems of concentrated mana, the cores of [[Lava Elementals]], and the echoing, desolate halls of ancient buildings teetering on the edge of magma oceans."

-R'mdrahl, [[Adventurers|adventurer]]

The Warbling is a [[All Regions of The West|region]] of [[The West]] that is characterized by its intense heat from rivers of lava and the constant patrols of its conflagratory denizens. The Warbling is a rather small region, with most of it being visible from afar so long as you've got the high ground. The Warbling is perhaps the most dangerous region of The West, with the heat of the area getting so extreme it can melt flesh from the bone in seconds.

Indeed, all it takes is a single misplaced step to break a thin layer of rock and fall feet first into molten lava. While the danger is immense, the riches that can be earned here are still more than enough to keep any visitors that stumble their way here. Ancient hoards of gold, the parts of monsters within, and even the very earth itself- with massive ore veins running across the surface.

As for the origins of The Warbling, the name itself obviously stems from the phenomena known as 'warbling', and the region itself likely just as simple. An influx of magic, or a simple seismic event, caused a massive rift to the inner earth of the world resulted in a massive amount of magma flowing to the surface. The sudden influx of heat created a [[Sites of Power|Site of Power]] that further heated the region. To this day, however many centuries later, The Warbling maintains its heat.

The creatures found within The Warbling are extremist lifeforms that can survive in a hellish realm, and are not to be underestimated-

- [[Arcanosaurs]] (Rarely)
- [[Grolles]] (Rarely)
- [[Lava Elementals]] (Unique)
- [[Corpse-Thicket]] (Rarely)
- [[Ash Devils]] (Unique)
- [[Aberrant Isopods]] (Unique)

There is a possibility that more [[Monsters]] hide in the cracks and crevasses of The Warbling, but without proper expeditions it's likely that they won't be found soon, or ever at all.
