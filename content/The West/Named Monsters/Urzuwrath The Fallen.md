([[Build Togethers|448]], [[index|What Lies West?]])

"The red paint, the skull markers, the stones covered in sun baked blood. That cave mouth has been found by many, and all of them have warned about its occupant, the Last [[Wyrms|Wyrm]]."

-Harold Green, [[Adventurers|adventurer]]

The Wyrms have long since been gone. Tales of their might existed in [[Koross]] but until that group of Explorators found [[The City of Wyrms]] for the first time they were only legend. More tales of their power came about for the hundred years the City underwent renovation, yet some began to think that if all the Wyrms had died, then surely they could not have been so powerful? Those who found study in their bones though knew that it would have taken immense power to slay one, just look at their descendants for an example of their previous might. [[Chrozhran Destroyers]] turn dirt to glass in the wake of their fire, and [[True Wyverns]] wipe out parties in mere moments. The might it takes to fell them alone is no easy feat either, with a mere three Destroyers having been felled in the last forty years and not a single True Wyvern being slain by any mortal.

So then, what could have slain them all? There is only one who would know this answer, and its mind is long since lost. Urzuwrath the Fallen- combining both the [[Humans|Human]] and [[Dwarves|Dwarven]] words for 'wrath'. Urzuwrath has, for however long [[The Old Empire of The West|The Old Empire]] has ended, been rotting away within a massive cavern within [[Ossuary Reach|The Ossuary Reach]]. If one were to approach the entrance to this cave, they would find dozens of different warnings from the last 'brave' idiots to delve into its home. Red 'x's', words of warning, the skulls and rotting heads of the fallen, even traps to hinder any who approach.

Urzuwrath's cave is regularly encountered by Adventurers, likely out of the same strange magic that is used by [[Seamsters]], though Urzuwrath seems to be doing it on accident. Harold Green's party has made several attempts to slay Urzuwrath, but all have failed horribly- though thanks to the power of Amelia Harrow none have permanently lost their lives. Harold and his fellows describe a haunting monstrosity, though.

"**Black, fetid sails for wings. Drooping, loose skin with the scales barely hanging on. Hollow eyes with dim green flame darting around like fake irises. The sickly smell of posies, the smell of [[Necromancy]]. It's gargantuan, it towers above any other living thing in the west- only shorter than a [[Colossi]] by a hundred feet or so. When we strike it, its just barely at the ankles, and our blades cut through like an inferno through tinder- but it isn't enough. Its flesh is like putty, connecting together even through cauterized wounds. The beast is immortal, utterly and truly, unless something, somewhere keeps it alive that we can destroy or shut off, it will never fall.**"

-Harold Green, [[Adventurers|adventurer]]

The cavern is allegedly full to the brim with gold and jewelry, with Urzuwrath being seen idle on top of a massive pile larger than the combined coffers of all the kingdoms of [[Koross]]. As can be expected then, many Nobles constantly send out [[Contracts]] for the monolith of fetid flesh's head, that the coin may be claimed. 

Onto the fate that befell The Fallen one then. The answer is... completely unknown. I know I know, how original of [[The West]] to pose another unanswered question. Speculations include the massive amount of wealth underneath Urzuwrath, believing that when the Other [[Wyrms]] fled, for whatever reason Urzuwrath remained with its hoard. Perhaps it is just a tale of greed then, with the Wyrm falling to starvation, and perhaps the stone of its dwelling prevented its soul from leaving, trapping it in its own carcass for eternity. Or perhaps some other truth may be discovered, something more cruel, or more benevolent. Though, 'perhaps' is a rather slim chance, isn't it?
