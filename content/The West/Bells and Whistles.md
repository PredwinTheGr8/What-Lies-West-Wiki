(298, WLW)

When venturing [[The West]] one might find themselves in numerous deadly situations- running from a monster, hiding from raiders, or simple and notorious dehydration. There are hundreds of ways to die in The West, but barely any of them can *truly* be predicted reliably long before they occur. But one among these 'signs' is bizarrely obvious, antitheses to every other omen that could possibly present itself to an adventurer- The Bells and Whistles.

In the distance, faintly audible- the chimes of a church and the whistling of an angry beast, like the machinations of dwarves. Hearing this for the first time, an adventurer might simply ignore it given how far away the sound is... for now. No, no this sound does not hide- it presents itself with gusto to those who would dare hear it. The bells begin to shake the earth, and the screaming whistles boil blood. The closer, the louder it gets the more agonizing it is. From this point, all reports of The Bells and Whistles is from those who have fled to [[The City of Wyrms|The City of Wyrms]] instead of facing whatever the sound comes from. Whether ALL who have heard it have fled, or those who faced it have always died, The Bells and Whistles remain an ill omen of damnation for all who hear it- unless they return East to [[Balphora]].
