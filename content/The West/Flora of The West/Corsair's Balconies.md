### [[Visual Appearance]]-

- Large Shelf Mushroom
- Regularly has [[Wander Moss]] growing on its underside

Corsair's Balconies are a mycelia of [[The West]]- found within the damp forest of [[Sorrowed Weald|The Sorrowed Weald]]. Corsair's Balconies posses the largest sporocarps of any mushrooms in the known world. Corsair's Balconies can grow upwards of 30 feet across at their widest points as they wrap around the trunks of the [[Weeping Titans]]. The topside of a Balcony is shaped like a saucer, used to trap water which is infused with spores as the mushrooms contents flood out, spreading to the roots of other Weeping Titans and climbing out of the bark once more.

Corsair's Balconies are edible, though it takes extreme effort to prepare them as their flesh is incredibly dense. Its better to look for other sources of nutrients within [[Sorrowed Weald|The Sorrowed Weald]] instead of putting hours of effort into a lackluster meal from these blue-hued behemoths. However, a Corsair's Balcony is not useless- their undersides are often the driest parts of the forest, and their 'nectar', an orange sap-like liquid, can be used to treat wounds and prevent infection.
