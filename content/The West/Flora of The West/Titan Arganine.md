## [[Visual Appearance]]-

- Large, spiraling branches that combine into a large, bright orange fruit with 'teats'
- Smells of alcohol
- Attracts many pollinators
- Pulsing, white to yellow leaves
- Makes constant creaking sounds

Arganine, both the name of the plant and the drink made from it, is a rare fruit bearing tree of [[The West]], found within both [[Sorrowed Weald|The Sorrowed Weald]] and [[Creeping Peninsula|The Creeping Peninsula]]. Some smaller variants, called [[Verdant Arganine]], can be found inside of the grassy, plant filled chambers of [[Forlorn Graveyard|The Forlorn Graveyard]]. Titan Arganine, the one we will discuss, is a strange specimen of flora. The plant seemingly targets [[Adventurers|adventurers]]- but we'll talk more on that later.

The life cycle of a Arganine, titanic and verdant, starts with the planting of a spore. The spore takes root and begins to grow, slowly taking over the soil content of its surroundings. Titan Arganine are powerful disruptors of local plant life- often clearing an area of ten feet around them of any roots. Arganine in close proximity to [[Weeping Titans]] will even contest their monolithic roots, with at least one Weeping Titan being killed by three Arganine that grew around the beastly tree. Once fully grown, the Arganine will produce a large, orange-like fruit as its branches spiral around to cradle it. This fruit starts out quite hollow, but slowly fills up with a sugary liquid that, in time, will ferment.

Once fermented, the fruit will grow several udder-like protrusions that dangle to the ground below. These "teats" can be... suckled from.
