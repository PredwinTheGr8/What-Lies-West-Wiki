### [[Visual Appearance]]-

- Large, spherical sporocarp
- Faint red, and solid yellow spots
- White coloration
- Numerous, gaping holes, often housing small creatures
- Upwards of five feet tall, and 4 feet across

Marrow Bushes are a species of 'flora' from[[The West]], though calling it a plant may be incorrect, if only slightly. Marrow Bushes are semi-large, upwards of five feet tall, spherical fungi that dot the desolate corpses of [[Ossuary Reach|The Ossuary Reach]]. They are primarily a bleached, bone-white color with small speckles of yellow and faint reds.

Marrow Bushes also possess numerous, upwards of 300, one-inch holes across their fruiting bodies. These chambers lead into the internal chamber of the mushroom, opening up into a larger chamber where [[Pricks]] make their nests. The problem, however, is that these Marrow Bushes are among the only sources of food within [[Ossuary Reach|The Ossuary Reach]], leading many an adventurer to anger the bush's occupants.

Leaving the specifics for the archival of the [[Pricks]] themselves, Marrow Bushes also act as food sources for many other creatures that wander [[Ossuary Reach|The Reach]]- such as the odd [[Arcanosaurs|Arcanosaur]], or true natives like [[Bone Dogs]] and smaller [[Ossuary Titans]]. The strange, almost alien part of a Marrow Bush is the question of how they sustain themselves in [[The West]]. In fact, thus far, Marrow Bushes are some of the only large mushrooms found within The West, every other example of mushrooms being mats of mycelium, [[Vampire Weed]], in [[Crimson Expanse|The Crimson Expanse]], or the monolithic [[Corsair's Balconies| Corsair's Balcony]] shelf mushrooms of [[Sorrowed Weald|The Sorrowed Weald]] that grow on the [[Weeping Titans]]. But both of these mushrooms are directly next to, or are capable of finding sources of moisture.

The Vampire Weed taps into the soles of creatures that walk over it- siphoning out their flood with razor sharp 'fangs' as small as grains of sand. The Corsair's Balcony are located in The Sorrowed Weald, needless to say they have enough water. But the Marrow Bushes are in [[Ossuary Reach|The Ossuary Reach]], where the only true sources of water ARE the Marrow Bushes. Some creatures can metabolize water from the blood of their prey, but Marrow Bushes are like balloons of liquid. Cutting into one will cause it to spill water out like a tap, thus many creatures in The Ossuary Reach source their water directly from these mushrooms.

However Marrow Bushes source their fluids, they remain as a second crux for the ecosystem of [[Ossuary Reach|The Ossuary Reach]], just behind the massive carcasses they grow from.
