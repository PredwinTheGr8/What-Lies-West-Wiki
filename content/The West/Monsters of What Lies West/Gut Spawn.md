### [[Monster Callsigns|Callsigns]]-

- Eggs present on cured meat
- Weakness
- Strange cravings
- Desire to defecate near carcasses

Gut spawn are parasitic [[Monsters|monsters]] of [[The West]] that reside in any region that supports large monster life. Individually, a gut spawn is a two inch long worm with a silvery, metallic exterior. Gut Spawn start as eggs on the prime flesh of a corpse- which has been cured by the saliva of their parents to preserve both the meat and the eggs until another monster comes and consumes the carrion.

Once consumed, the eggs will hatch and begin to influence their host in several ways. Appetite will increase, cravings for strange food will appear, and repetitive spells of weakness may plague larger individuals, but smaller peoples such as [[Kobolds]] and [[Goblins]] may be outright consumed by the colony without proper intervention.

Gut Spawn are incredibly resistant to heat, needing a sustained roiling boil to properly kill off the parasites hiding in flesh, though a hearty dose of salt serves very well to purge the outermost layer of parasites. Infected flesh will also usually take on a whiter- silver like color, though it is quite faint and not entirely reliable as some monsters, such as [[Sky-Rippers]], already posses white flesh.

To properly combat an infestation, drinking a large amount of salt water and fasting for several days immediately afterwards serves as the quickest and least invasive form of cleansing that one can enact. If you experience extreme stomach pain during this period, drink more salt water. Remember to consume extra clean water to ensure you do not dehydrate.
