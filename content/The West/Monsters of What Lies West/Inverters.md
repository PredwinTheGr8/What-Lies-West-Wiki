### [[Monster Callsigns|Callsigns]]-

- Flat sand patterns- no waves or bumps
- A rhythmic tremor
- Smell of rot
- Anywhere with sand- ANYWHERE.

Inverters are [[Monsters|monsters]] of [[The West]]. Small creatures believed to be the spawn of Inverters are pentaradially symmetrical creatures. They possess five stump limbs that connect at a central point where the mouth of the creature is. The creatures prefer to move in sand, where they will remain upside down using small, hair like extensions to move underneath the dunes. When they sense tremors, they will rise to the surface, leaving only a small bit of their bodies exposed- namely the edge of their mouth.

Once a prey animal, such as a Jerboa or Crow, has walked on top of the Inverter's mouth, the monster will rapidly extend a fleshy balloon covered in a highly adhesive acid. This will likely result in the prey animal becoming stuck to the protrusion, believed to be the stomach of the monster, resulting in the growth retracting back into the Inverter, dragging the animal in with it.

Adult Inverters are rare- so rare that is seems that life in [[The West]], even monstrous life, is unaccustomed to them. The only living monsters that seem aware of the presence of an Inverter are [[Arcanosaurs]], and even then they seemingly aren't always 'in the know' about these sand leviathans.

An adult inverter will often attract the attention of Buzzards and crows, as their slow digestive process leads to their food rotting while still inside them, a side effect of the adhesive properties to their stomach acid, seemingly resulting a less effective potency, though it is merely a speculation. The smell of rot is rare in the desert, thus when one catches a whiff of something foul, its best to proceed with caution.

If Inverters have a weakness, it has not yet been found. It's best to avoid them entirely.

Inverters prey on anything and everything they can fit in their mouth- [[Maws]], [[Arcanosaurs]] even the odd [[Wyverns|Wyvern]]. The only creatures seen eating an adult Inverter are [[Tarrasques]].
