The Bath'kah, as they call themselves, are a [[Monsters|Monstrous]] group of 'people' from [[The West]], and its [[All Regions of The West|region]], [[Void Dunes|The Void Dunes]]. The Bath'kah are monolithic, grey-skinned humanoids, upwards of eighteen feet tall. They travel in large 'herds' throughout The Void Dunes hunting the other, massive [[Monsters]] in the region, or to keep their tamed Monsters fed. They are charismatic, though innately chaotic creatures that can easily go from cracking jokes to smashing skulls.

The Bath'kah are capable of speech, commerce, and learning. Some among their number have learned one or more languages of [[Koross]] just from written books, which of course they taught themselves to read. Despite their inherit, extremely high intellect, the Bath'kah are driven by their emotions, seeming to be the driving force of their civilization, whether they like it or not. 

The first [[Adventurers|adventurers]] to come back, alive at least, from a Bath'kah camp reported that the only thing to 'save their hides' was their dwarven ally's personal alcohol stash, which the Bath'kah thoroughly enjoyed before letting the party go on their way. Ever since, most [[Adventurers|adventurers]] going out into [[The West]] will at least keep a flask of whiskey on their person, in case the desert decides to send them into [[Void Dunes|The Void Dunes]]. Though some call them brutish, others recognize the existence of the Bath'kah as something extraordinary- an opportunity to truly find the answer to the age old question- [[What Lies West|What Lies West?]]

If one is to find themselves in combat against one or more Bath'kah, evasive attacks and ranged weaponry will serve the best to avoiding instant death at the hands of their warclubs, or just their normal hands, really.

The Bath'kah keep domestic [[Void Bisons]] for food, and hunt down [[Behemoths]] on occasion. The Bath'kah are rarely preyed upon, as most creatures in [[Void Dunes|The Void Dunes]] fear them, though if any is to be the most likely to kill a Bath'kah it would be a Behemoth.
