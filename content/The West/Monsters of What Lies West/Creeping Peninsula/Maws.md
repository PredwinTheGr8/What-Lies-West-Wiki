### [[Monster Callsigns|Callsigns]]-

- Blue saliva marking rocks and trees
- Metallic howling
- Tracks
- Lack of common wildlife- [[Ossuary Leeches]], [[Weeping Sprites]] etcetera
- Detect Good and Evil- perhaps insinuating faeish origins

Maws are [[Monsters|monsters]] of [[The West]] that previously only inhabited [[Creeping Peninsula|The Creeping Peninsula]], but after last years [[The Unfeathering|Unfeathering]] they have begun spreading throughout as many [[All Regions of The West|regions]] as they can- consuming vast amounts of endemic life. Maws, at a distance, resemble large dogs- though at close distances they are better compared to a monitor lizard with their massive scales and hissing growls. Maws average at half a ton in weight- averaging ten feet long and 7 feet tall at the shoulder, with black scales, glowing blue eyes and tongues, giant spined quills on their backs and a red underside.

Maws earn their name from their massive jaws- capable of engulfing and swallowing any [[Adventurers|adventurer]] save for a [[Minotaurs|Minotaur]] or [[Centaurs|Centaur]] whole. Their mouths are lined with multiple rows of backwards-facing teeth that keep prey from escaping their gullet. The teeth are so prevalent that the Maw's tongue has developed a chitinous armor on its underside to prevent itself from lacerating against the fangs. A Maw has two esophagi, one leading to their stomach, and the other leading to a throat sac filled with paralyzing liquid. a Maw that is already fed will often place their additional prey within this pouch for storage.

The sack can carry many creatures at once- according to adventurer Harold Green and his party, one Maw swallowed three of their number at once and stored all of them within the sack- though the beast was felled and their comrades saved. On that note- Maws seem almost unable to pass up an opportunity to fill their throat sack- greedily slurping up as many creatures, or adventurers, as possible before sluggishly jogging away to their den.

As if to make things worse, Maws are pack hunters- hunting in groups of up to six. The morale of a pack is built upon their alpha- often clearly different form other Maws with a white streak across their spines- and if it is slain the pack will rapidly disperse. In combat with multiple Maws, creating a kill circle and shielding smaller members of a party will help avoid losing members one by one. Maws show a weakness to silver as well, with the metal singing their skin on contact- suggesting they may be [[Eldritch Energy|eldritch]] or demonic in origin, though there are creatures outside of these categories still weak to the metal.

Maws are at near the middle of the food chain within [[Creeping Peninsula|The Creeping Peninsula]], but their invasion of other regions has resulted in them destabilizing ecosystems. Though [[The West]] is certainly an evil realm, it is the livelihood of thousands- and if it were to fall their would be an economic crash built to end kingdoms. Maws are hunted by [[Fog Men]] in their natural habitat, but outside of it may face predation from [[Behemoths]], [[Bath'kah]], [[Arcanosaurs]], [[Inverters]], [[Warp Beasts]], [[Wyverns]] of all kinds and many more large monsters.
