Ironback Reapers are [[Monsters]] of [[The West]] that typically reside close to [[The City of Wyrms|The City of Wyrms']] Western gates, hiding out in the dunes waiting for rookie [[Adventurers|adventurers]] to make a wrong step. Ironback Reapers get their name from their metallic sheen on the tops of their carapaces, and their long, sickle-shaped stinger that faces inward, rather than outward like a normal scorpion.
Ironback are prized for their innards, particularly their tomalleys which are used to make a concoction that boosts the resilience of those who plan on long treks in The West. This concoction is referred to as [[Tomalley 'Tea']], a sarcastic slight at just how vile the brew looks.

Ironback Reapers have two primary means of offense- their claws and their stinger. These natural weapons can easily end the life of anyone who doesn't use enough caution when facing an Ironback.  Luckily, they aren't too large- capping out at around 5 feet long, tail not included. Their flat bodies make for easy targets and a quick jab to the brain can drop them in no time at all. It should still be noted that they are designed for ambushes, and will hide in sand pits though these are easily recognizable.

Ironbacks show several weaknesses that can be exploited by those smart enough to recognize them:

- Aversion to floral scents.
- The stinger is weak and can be cut off.
- Weakness to all magic.
  
 Ironbacks will naturally prey upon [[Scollifs]] and Jerboa, and may not be hungry enough to hunt anything [[Dwarves|Dwarf]] sized or bigger.
