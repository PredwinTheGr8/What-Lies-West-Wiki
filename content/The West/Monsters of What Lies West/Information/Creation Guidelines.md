Non-Cannon Document, contains soft spoilers.

When I make a monster for WhLW, I currently prioritize empty niches in a region that a creature would evolve to fill. Is there a high amount of a certain resource- animal, plant, fungi? Does something consume/use that resource in some way? If not, why? There are a lot of questions I ask myself to create a monster.

- Ecological purpose
- Interactions with other monsters
- Tier on the food chain
- Herbivore, carnivore, omnivore
- Violent, peaceful, defensive
- Size

Finally, there are five separate in-universe monster categories that a creature needs to properly fit into-

## Eldritch Spawn-

Monsters created by the presence of [[Eldritch Energy]]- like [[The Knights in Yellow]], [[Grolles]] and [[Abominations]]. Eldritch spawn are the most alien of the monsters- either because they defy physics itself or don't really exist, not in a traditional way at least. They are often harmful to look at or be in the presence of as their power is antithetical to the laws of the universe as the inhabitants of WhLW know them.
## Arcane Beasts-

Monsters previously mutated by [[Magic]], but over time their mutations have stabilized into a coherent form. Many monsters of [[The West]] fall under this category- [[Arcanosaurs]], [[Ossuary Titans]], [[Wyverns]] and [[Scollifs]]. An arcane beast must be able to USE magic in some way to either sustain themselves or as a tool to hunt or defend themself. Creatures that do not use magic fall under the next category.
## Mundane Beasts-

Monsters, or normal animals such as Jerboa, that do not posses an aptitude for magic. [[Ironback Reapers]], [[Mire Hulks]], [[Void Bisons]] and [[Bone Dogs]] are excellent examples. Even though they may look alien, their only defense or offense comes from natural weapons like teeth and claws. These monsters are often the safest to consume as well, as they regularly lack magic in their bloodstreams which can greatly hasten the effects of the [[Cardinal Sickness]].
## Mutants-

Mortals that have fallen to the [[Cardinal Sickness]] and mutated beyond recognition. [[Killfreaks]], [[Gnolls]] and [[Slimes]] are prime examples of mutants. Their mutations often vary, but based on environmental factors certain mutations are more prevalent. Mutants are often easy to dispatch, though, as mortals are the weakest creatures to walk the wastes, even when empowered by arcane forces.
## Automata-

Ancient constructs left behind by [[The Old Empire of The West]]. The only current example are the [[Chromatic Sentinels]]. Some are mechanical, some arcane, but all are artificial and irreproducible. They have a finite number that will never increase, as the facilities to make them are long lost.
