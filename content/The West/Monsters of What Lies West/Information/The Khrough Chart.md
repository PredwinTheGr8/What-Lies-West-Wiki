([[Build Togethers|399]], [[What Lies West]]?)

[[Monsters]] are the lifeblood of both [[adventurers]] and the very [[The West|West]] itself. While many adventurers learn from experience which ones are easy prey and which are unkillable terrors, its not altogether necessary. The [[Scholam Wyrmras]] and its [[Scholars|Blue-Cloaks]] have created what is known as The Khrough Chart.

The Khrough chart, named after the scholar of the same name, is a system used to judge the power of monsters and the danger they may pose. The system has its flaws, though, namely that it currently remains a private document- meaning that no one outside of the Scholam has ever seen it, not without taking a payment of silence at least...

Therefore, I hope to bring it more recognition to the public. Lets hope I don't get fired for this.

## Keyla-

Named directly after Khrough's daughter.

Absolutely zero threat to a trained adventurer. This doesn't necessarily mean that its harmless to the average person though, so don't go sticking a hand out to pet anything within this rank. Most of the [[monsters]] here are indeed harmless though, or at least can't maim someone with ease. [[Ossuary Leeches]] are a good example of a monster that isn't particularly dangerous that can still kill without precautions. On the other hand, [[Mire Hulks]] could definitely rip a man apart, but still find their place in this rank because they aren't innately hostile to adventurers.

## Headhunt-

As far as I can tell, this is named after them being dangerous enough to warrant drawing a sword? Maybe?

Threatening, if only partly. A [[monsters|monster]] that sees adventurers as food or fodder, but isn't particularly dangerous to a skilled group of adventurers. Just another day of work for heavily armed folk. The monsters within this rank, again, aren't a massive threat to adventurers, even rookie ones. [[Ironback Reapers]] in particular, which are in this rank, act as a first challenge to many adventurers leaving [[The City of Wyrms]] for the first time. Confidence and a deft hand are more than enough to fell a creature in this category.

## Bulwark-

Perhaps named for the recommended tactics of defense over offense?

Tread with caution. If the callsigns suggest the presence of such a [[Monsters|monster]], its best to acknowledge it and remain in a tight group. These monsters are where battle tactics start to make a major difference- sloppy groups will lose a few members, or perhaps all perish if incautious enough. [[Arcanosaurs]] are an excellent example, as their fire breath and immunity to magic are a major skill checkpoint for adventurers. They have the highest confirmed kill count of any monster in [[The West]]. Still, keeping your wits about you and not running away at the earliest sign of death will keep you and those around you alive.

## Themest-

Named after the [[Centaurs]] god of the hunt, their own interpretation of [[Generous Harush]]. Though I have no idea how this plays into the tactics surrounding the monsters in this rank.

Defensive tactics are completely necessary. Surround your archers, gunners and mages with vanguard members. Never leave the kill circle under any circumstance until the threat is gone. [[The Knights in Yellow]], [[Killfreaks]], [[Fog Men]] and other larger [[monsters]] are common in this class. Though they don't possess the greatest of kill counts, that's only because of either their rarity or reputation. Adventurers may be wise to simply flee any monsters in this rank. Still, even more than a Bulwark rank monster, slaying one of these is sure to be a sign of great success in the future, as you have conquered what has wrought fear unto many others.

## Overlord-

I'm not sure really. Maybe Khrough thinks of these as the 'rulers' of monster-kind, as if they all fall under an invisible hierarchy? Nevertheless, they certainly have the power to destroy cities alone.

If a fight is unavoidable, a loose formation and diversionary tactics work the best. Some Overlord rank monsters do not fight alone, though, and at least two vanguards should remain in the backline to protect casters and rangers. These monsters typically posses great size and power, being nearly unkillable by normal men, but have still been slain in past. [[Abominations]], [[Death Knights]] and [[Behemoths]] are excellent examples of Overlords.

## Flee-

This one is more obvious- you should run away from anything within this rank, fighting is impossible.

Destroyers of continents- impossible foes that cannot be slain by mortal hands. There are few beasts in this ranking, though there may be many still left undocumented, as is their nature as slaughterers. [[True Wyverns]], [[Ossuary Titans]] and [[The Annihilator]] itself are some of the few beasts within this most infamous rank.
