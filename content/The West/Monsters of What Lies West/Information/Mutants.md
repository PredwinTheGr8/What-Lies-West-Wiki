There are two kinds of mutation that can occur on an expedition to [[The West]]. [[Eldritch Energy]] mutation and [[Magic|Magical]] Mutation. Eldritch mutation occurs when the power of [[The Deep]] bleeds into the flesh of a mortal, often from the physical presence of an eldritch entity such as a [[Grolles|Grolle]] or [[The Things in The Dark, Null Figures|Null Figure]]. Eldritch mutations are often the more grotesque. Arcane mutation occurs when a mortal remains sessile in an area of extreme arcane power, such as a [[Sites of Power|Site of Power]] like [[Phrazmollograggra Mountains|The Phrazmollograggra Mountains]] or [[Voltaic Monolith|The Voltaic Monolith]]. Mutants born from arcane mutation are more likely to passingly resemble their former selves.

Mutants are a common and saddening truth of [[Adventurers|adventuring]]. Many explorers go out of their way to mercy kill their fellows if they can, as it's one of the only ways to end their suffering.
