### [[Monster Callsigns|Callsigns]]-

- Arcanosaur tracks
- Arcanosaur dung
- Seared Carcasses
- Desert, Ruins, [[The Warbling]] Locales


Arcanosaurs are ancient [[Monsters]] of [[The West]], being nine foot tall reptilian creatures, like the Raptors of [[The Great Swamp|Moulugg]]. Arcanosaurs, as their names let off, are able to utilize [[Magic|magic]] in their hunts- though more importantly are immune to its effects completely, or at least to an extent not yet tested. An Arcanosaur hunts by itself, prowling the sands for their preferred preys (see addendum) but will hunt [[Adventurers|adventurers]] if they're desperate enough for food, or feel threatened. When face to face with an Arcanosaur, making yourself look big can discourage the beast from attacking you, and remaining in a group can ensure your safety.

Using magic on an Arcanosaur can result in the creature healing depending on the strength of the spell, and will empower their physical traits such as endurance and strength. An Arcanosaur's body is incredibly hot, preventing any parasites or diseases from forming within the body of the creature- meaning that from the moment one is slain it is completely safe to eat- though the meat is tough and stewing it may help to increase the meals enjoyability. The scales of an Arcanosaur are also very valuable to armor smiths- as the scales can be made into magic resistant mail or shields.

Arcanosaurs have few weaknesses, it is best to avoid them but if you must fight one, never use magic on it in any way-

- Aversion to lilacs.
- Cold sensitivity.
- A good punch to the nose.

Arcanosaurs prey on anything smaller than them- namely [[Ironback Reapers]], [[Scollifs]], Jerboas, Snakes and [[Grolles]], and are preyed upon by [[Wyverns]], [[Inverters]] and [[Mimics]]. When in ruins it is not uncommon for them to be found and slain by [[The Knights in Yellow]].
