### [[Monster Callsigns|Callsigns]]

- Blue dust on walls
- Hair standing on end
- Ruins locale
- Occasional minds whispers

Thunderjaws are [[Monsters]] of [[The West]]. They are six foot long, nine foot wide, flat, coin-shaped, scarab-like creatures that inhabit the many ruins of [[The West]], though they are rare beasts to encounter despite their population. When immature, Thunderjaws can be seen skittering around ruins as harmless nymphs. These nymphs are preyed upon by most carnivorous inhabitants of ruins such as [[Grolles]], [[Slimes]] or [[Arcanosaurs]], though this is not a full list. As they mature, Thunderjaws will eventually become some of the most dangerous hunters in their territories- being capable of dropping a fully grown [[Arcanosaurs|Arcanosaur]] like a sack of dirt with lightning fast strikes from their massive mandibles. These mandibles have enough bite force to crush bone in most mortals, and can bisect smaller folk like [[Kobolds]] or [[Goblins]] in the blink of an eye.

Though this is not their only offensive capability- their namesake comes from their ability to manipulate lightning. Thunderjaws are able to shoot bolts of lightning from their mandibles towards their prey- though this action can only be done once an hour or so and is used only as a last resort to defend themselves as it usually drains their energy and leaves them defenseless. Helping them ambush prey or hide from predators, a Thunderjaw's body is very flat, allowing them to position themselves inside the gaps present in most ruins of [[The West]]. Seemingly, these gaps were designed with the Thunderjaws in mind, perhaps suggesting that the monsters were artificially created to inhabit these cracks and perhaps protect the ruins, though their beastial instincts have long since removed such an instinct if it ever existed at all.

Their ability to fit in these gaps allows them to go unnoticed by most adventurers, and while Thunderjaws do not regularly prey on mortals, one should still check every corner to ensure that a Thunderjaw isn't lying in wait as they can easily be startled into attacking mortals.

Thunderjaws show several weaknesses can be used to get an extreme upper hand-

- Low mobility when on the floor
- Weakness to cold
- Fear of loud noises
- Aversion to salt

Adult Thunderjaws prey on [[Arcanosaurs]], [[Grolles]], [[Tappers]], [[Killfreaks]] and other large ruin wildlife, and are preyed upon by the very same monsters. They will often be slain by [[The Knights in Yellow]] as they attempt to move throughout their ruins.
