### [[Monster Callsigns|Callsigns]]-

- Frequent Mind Whispers
- Nosebleeds
- Visions
- Texture warping
- Clumps of finger or toenails
- Ruins, [[The Warbling]], [[Forlorn Graveyard]]

'Grolles', as coined by veteran adventurer Harold Green, are incomprehensible man sized [[Eldritch Energy|Eldritch]] spawn. They posses no visible physical form, though they have the aspects of one- such as taking up a physical space, being vulnerable to conventional weapons, and though they seem alien they require food and water just like any other living creature. Grolles are active hunters- constantly patrolling the desolate halls of Ruins, walking through the steaming columns of [[The Warbling]] and being found wandering the blue corridors of the buried spires within [[Forlorn Graveyard]]. Grolles use what can sometimes be perceived as tentacles to examine their environment, though it is speculated that they have at least one pair of working eyes as they react to visual stimuli from upwards of 120 feet away, or so it is said.

The truth is, the behavior and life cycle of Grolles is completely impossible to identify with scholars' current understanding of, well, everything. There are no other examples of creatures similar to a Grolle that aren't completely alien to the world. Their skin changes textures, they shed human fingernails, and emit a constant wailing noise when they spot prey- like a crying babe. So much about a Grolle is horrific and cosmically terrifying that scholars have all given up out of a need to keep their sanity intact.

Despite their shifting nature, even in death, Grolles are just as susceptible to physical armaments as any other wild animal. A musket ball to the head is enough to instantly kill a Grolle. Even though mortals cannot eat them for fear of... mutation, other [[Monsters]] of [[The West]] often use Grolles as a primary food source, seen in the addendum. Grolle hide can be used to make Cloaks of Invisibility, and thus fetch for a high price... if you can manage to look at it long enough to skin the foul thing- for looking at a Grolle, similarly to other creatures affected by [[Eldritch Energy]] such as [[The Knights in Yellow]] causes damage to a persons thinking, and can result in death with prolonged exposure.

Grolles show several weaknesses that can be exploited to ease along their slaughter-

- Cannot cross salt lines
- Weakness to slashing weapons
- Vulnerable to bright flashes
- Cannot see creatures that remain perfectly still

Grolles will attempt to prey upon anything they find, and are preyed upon by [[Arcanosaurs]], [[Thunderjaws]], [[Killfreaks]], and most other large Monsters. [[The Knights in Yellow]] do not react to the presence of Grolles, nor vise versa- though [[Chromatic Sentinels]] will obliterate any Grolles that enter their chambers.
