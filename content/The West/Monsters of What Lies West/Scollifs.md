"You know, one time, when I was patrolling our camp in the night, I saw a huge group of them- uhm... Scollifs I think, out far away in the sands. Their lights and shifting colors all lighting up the ridge... gods it was beautiful, most peaceful thing I've seen in my life... I want to see it again so bad."

-Harold Green, [[Adventurers|Adventurer]].

### [[Monster Callsigns|Callsigns]]-

- Angelic singing
- Worm-like tracks in the sand
- Desert Locale

Scollifs are peaceful [[Monsters]] of [[The West]] that float across the dunes dragging tentacles across the sand. Why Scollifs do this, and more importantly how they sustain themselves, is completely unknown though some speculate it has something to do with their behaviors with sand. A Scollifs' body is gel-like and see through, with its crystalline organs visible from the outside, and its yellow blood pumping through its veins. Scollifs are known for their high pitch, melodic singing as they trek across the dunes of [[The West]]- with the melody being a great comfort to those who hear it- and even speeding along the healing of wounds.

Scollifs are separated into three different "classes", easily identifiable to any who know how to see. The Drones are the smallest, and posses a blue hue. They average three feet long and one foot across. The Guard are an uncommon, larger variant with a green hue. They average six feet long and four feet wide. Finally is the Queen, who is always accompanied by the Guard. The Queen averages twenty feet long and fifteen feet wide, with it also being the rarest.

The Queen is the only one able to birth more Scollifs, with herds missing a Queen being doomed to die out one by one unless intervened with. Scollifs, despite their peaceful nature, are more than capable of defending themselves. Their tentacles posses a painful sting, that while not outright deadly is more than capable of sending a fully grown [[Minotaurs|Minotaur]] reeling in pain. Do not pet Scollifs, no matter how friendly they appear. A Scollifs may approach you and prod you with its antennae, but do not touch it back lest you risk being stung.

If you absolutely MUST kill a Scollifs, they are incredibly weak creatures, with even a single pierce to their flesh causing them to fall to the ground and suffocate in the sand. Congratulations. Though with the slaying of a Scollifs one may harvest its 'jelly' from its flesh to concoct [[Healing Potions]].

Scollifs are preyed upon by [[Ironback Reapers]] and [[Arcanosaurs]], which are both immune to their stings.
