([[Build Togethers|334]], What Lies West?)

"We had lost her a while back, she wandered away to scout some ruins. We looked for weeks, back and forth from [[The City of Wyrms|The City]]. Finally, we had found a ruin near where she said she'd gone to, inside of it was this awful thorn-covered bramble. We looked deep into those ruins, and we found her- suspended in the air by root-tendrils, a gaunt carcass but it was her- her armor, her patchy, red hair... Then... she looked up at us, hollow eyes filled with green, pulsing tumors. Gods preserve us, I couldn't make it back to the City fast enough, I was too [[Cardinal Sickness|Sick]] to move faster than I had already been. It felt like ages before I was safe, before everything stopped itching."

### [[Monster Callsigns|Callsigns]]-

- Ruins Locale
- Extreme plant growth
- Occasional Mind Whispers
-  Itchy skin

Corpse-Thicket is a [[Monsters|Monster]] of [[The West]]- resembling a patch of dense, thorny vines, though the inner material of the vines contains flesh and blood. Corpse Thicket is a parasitic, highly intelligent creature that can make complex decisions and solve problems. The Thicket's goal is to infect a living host and puppet their carcass to new places for the Thicket to sow seeds and grow. The infection starts slow, thus the physical vines will attempt to incapacitate a host, either by restraining them or killing them if necessary. Eventually, the host will be overtaken by the growths of the Thicket- with the 'plant' taking over their eyes and organs for itself to use, seeing, tasting and feeling the world.

The infection will rapidly deteriorate with distance from the parent Thicket- with a prevalent itching feeling being the only sign that one is infected. As with plague, it seems that physical touch is not necessary to become infected, though it certainly will speed it along if one is lacerated by a vine. The Gates of [[The City of Wyrms]] have ancient runes on them that burn away a Corpse-Thicket infection, making it the only way to be certain an infection has been completely cleansed.

Corpse-Thicket is smart enough to use a victims voice and speak with others, Corpse-Thicket that has found a host will likely not attempt to take on another unless the current vessel has begun to rot, or the host is found close to the parent Thicket. Horrifyingly, Corpse Thicket will have completely normal conversations people it encounters, not behaving at all as their victim, instead referring to themselves as "The Briar", and asking questions about where a person grew up, their hobbies and interests, and otherwise seemingly genuinely interested in the lives of people East of the City of Wyrms- fascinated that the world is much more massive then they had believed. However, if any questions are asked about the Corpse-Thicket's origins, or [[What Lies West]], the host will become aggressive and attempt to infect those around them.

Corpse Thicket seems to be closely tied to [[Eldritch Energy]], though it also possesses an affinity for the arcane, seeming to be a bizarre mix of the two powers. If one runs out of water, the boiled blood of a Corpse-Thicket makes for a good alternative.

Corpse Thicket shows few weaknesses, its best to avoid it altogether- 

- Weakness to cold
- Axe weaponry
- Despite assumptions, Corpse-Thicket is resilient to flames due to a massive amount of moisture stored in the vines' flesh.

Corpse-Thicket is preyed upon by [[Arcanosaurs]], which are immune to its infection.
