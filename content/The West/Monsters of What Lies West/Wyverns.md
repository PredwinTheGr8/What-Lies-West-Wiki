"Smart folk know that there's only one kind of 'wyvern'- and that there's two others that tend to get put in the same pile. That doesn't make the other two any less scary, though- all of em are good at murderin' and maimin'... best to hide, then, when their shadow cloaks the tyrant sun."

-Girtz Ribmangler, [[Adventurers|adventurer]]

'Wyvern' is a blanket term used to describe three separate [[Monsters|monsters]] of [[The West]]- [[Plaguewings]], [[True Wyverns]] and [[Charnel Wyverns]]. These three monsters all look similar when the sun casts their shadow onto the sands, leading to many adventurers simply calling out the word 'wyvern' when their forms splay over the ground.

These monsters all are completely different in their origins- Plaguewings are mundane bat-like flying monsters. True Wyverns are the namesake for the group- scaly, fire breathing lizards with wingspans of up to one hundred and ninety feet. Finally, Charnel Wyverns are [[Eldritch Energy|Eldritch]] Spawn made from the melded carcasses of dozens- some monsters, and some adventurers.

Each of these creatures inhabit the same regions, as their massive wingspan means that only the other two pose a threat to one, as any other threat can simply be flown away from. The only other creature in [[The West]] that could possibly kill or maim a Wyvern are [[Bolide Birds]], though they seem to be rapidly adapting to pursue such a thing in their mutations.
