"Cold- despite what you'd think. They don't care about you, your presence, your attacks on them. They just walk, and walk... and walk. Dragging through sand and glassing it behind them, through forests setting them ablaze- or, just in circles in a pit of lava either they or their predecessors made. So yeah- cold, uncaring, indifferent."

-Markus Krauler, [[Adventurers|adventurer]]

Lava Elementals are, as their name says, elementals that can be found within [[The West]]- inside of [[The Warbling]]- and the molten caverns underneath the [[Phrazmollograggra Mountains]]- though they theoretically can spawn anywhere with enough heat. As elementals, these creatures do not need to eat, sleep, or drink- existing as a perpetual 'lifeform' so long as the [[Sites of Power|Site of Power]] that spawned them remains intact.

Thus far, only three types of elementals have been found to fully manifest- belonging to the powers of Pyro, Hydro, and Cryo. The other powers, Dendro, Electro, Helio, Necro and so on, are either not populous enough or not powerful enough to spawn an elemental naturally or are too weak to perform such a task. The only other font potentially strong enough is Electro, but the only place with massive amounts of lightning would of course be [[Voltaic Monolith|The Voltaic Monolith]]. To this day however, nothing has been proven towards the existence of lightning elementals.

Back to Lava Elementals- which are technically just extra powerful Fire Elementals, or perhaps a mix of fire and earth given the nature of lava- they are incredibly durable. Lava Elementals are powerful combatants simply in their idleness, as any who have gotten close to lava can attest- it is incredibly hot. Still, [[adventurers]] seek out these elementals for their cores made of pure pyromancy, which fetch for a hefty price in markets within [[The City of Wyrms]].

Combat against a Lava Elemental may be perhaps the most mundane of any task- Simply firing at it repetitively with a flintlock weapon or hitting it with cryomancy spells will work the best. Once the elemental 'dies' it will rapidly cool and harden, when it can be mined to find the core.

Lava Elementals are prey to [[True Wyverns]], that will ingest them to increase the power of their mobile Site of Power.
