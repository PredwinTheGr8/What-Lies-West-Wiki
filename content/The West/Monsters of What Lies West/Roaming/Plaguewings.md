### [[Monster Callsigns|Callsigns]]-

- Visible presence
- Intense stench of decay

Plaguewings are False-[[Wyverns|Wyvern]] [[Monsters|monsters]] of [[The West]] that can be found roaming the skies of many [[All Regions of The West|regions]] within. Plaguewings visually resemble massive bats, with a wingspan of sixty to seventy feet and dark brown fur on their undersides- and bleach white fur on their topside.

Plaguewings earn their name from an aura of disease that surrounds them- a non-[[Magic|magical]] malaise of sickness that weakens those who would dare, or more likely are unlucky enough, to face the false-wyvern. The sickness promotes extreme coughing fits, nausea, blurry vision and loss of limb control. Weapons will drop from hands, knees will fall to the ground, and eyes will water with green tears.

The sickness a Plaguewing brings is known, uncreatively, as 'Wingplague'. It's symptoms do not linger long, though, as most mortal bodies do not contain very much magic, which seems to fuel the illness into becoming a far more ravenous disease than normal. Sorcerers are particularly susceptible to the illness, as their magical blood fuels it and allows it to take over their body if it remains untreated.

Wingplague is not a strong illness unless it can take root- chewable tablets made in [[The City of Wyrms]] can be taken easily before an expedition starts and every few days after to prevent the disease from taking hold.

When facing a Plaguewing, the beasts tend to use their mouths or frontal wings to slash and bite foes. Keeping towards the rear or using ranged weaponry works best to dispatch a Plaguewing or force it into fleeing- though if one can fell such a beast a sac in their chest that contains the liquid form of Wingplague can be used to make further antidotes and thus sell for a high price.

Plaguewings prey upon all monsters of [[The West]], and will often attempt to eat each other as adults in an effort to keep their territories. They fight for dominion with the other two 'wyvern' species of the west- [[True Wyverns]] and [[Charnel Wyverns]], and are sometimes, rarely, slain by [[Bolide Birds]].
