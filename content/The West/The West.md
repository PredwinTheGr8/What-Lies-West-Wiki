A vast expanse of desert West of [[Balphora]] that holds numerous ruins full of riches, intelligent [[Monsters|monsters]] and strange, arcane [[Magic|magics]]. People have over the last two centuries been drawn to The West by an arcane desire known as "[[The Calling]]", though not every race is affected. The desert is said to have knowledge beyond fathom- and power for all, or one, to rule the world and topple the very [[Gods]] from their pedestals.

The West is dangerous, not just because of monsters, but for the existence of [[Eldritch Energy]] phenomenon such as the [[Bells and Whistles|Bells and Whistles]] and [[Cardinal Sickness]]. But still, countless adventurers go into the sands to find their fortune, and enough come back alive to encourage more each year to heed The Calling.
