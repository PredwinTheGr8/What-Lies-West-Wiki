([[Build Togethers|338]], [[What Lies West]]?)

Clearly, before [[The Unfeathering]] and [[The Calling]] brought people to [[Balphora]], there was once a glorious kingdom out in the sands, perhaps it wasn't even a desert then. The average person who ventures west is someone who has nothing to lose back across the [[Ocean Magnamous]] in [[Koross]], they have no interest in the logistics and history this ancient empire had. But, among their number are scholars, archivists and professors who all look at the vast desert and say- 'Hmmmm...'

From the various, clearly defined [[All Regions of The West|regions]]- ancient destruction now labeled as a 'biome' or 'locale'- to the vast number of monsters that hide away in the desolate citadels and walk across the scorching dunes, there is much to investigate, much to question. The most notable investigator of [[The West]], [[Ser Mallorin Berns of Harush]], has spent thousands of gold coins on the contents of [[Adventurers|adventurer's]] bags trying to piece together what exactly is happening out there beyond [[The City of Wyrms]].

Though it is impossible to plan an expedition out to [[The West]], as the magics within tell *you* where to go, [[Ser Mallorin Berns of Harush|Mallorin]] believes he's on the verge of cracking the 'code' so to speak on how to block out the magic and move freely. The very spell itself is curious as well, was it made by the ancient empire? By the Wyrms? Or was it the aftermath of whatever calamity wiped out their civilization- save for [[The City of Wyrms]].

And [[The Calling]]! Oh The Calling, what is it? *Why* is it? There are so many spells affecting [[The West]] and [[The Wider World]] that one would expect it to consume all the magic constantly pouring out from the [[Sites of Power]] all across the sands. But no- no no no there is enough to go around even with spells that affect the entire world constantly hungry for mana. And every year there is enough magic to cast that spell again and again.

Whoever... whatever made its home here, they were practically gods, and yet they have been slain.
