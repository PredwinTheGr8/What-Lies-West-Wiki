### [[Monster Callsigns]]-

- Frequent mind whispers
- Uneaten dead- cleaved in half or beheaded
- Other, non-animated suits
- Evil nature- detect evil alerts
- Burning detection glyphs

The Knights in Yellow are [[Legendary Monsters|Legendary Eldritch Spawn]] of the west that reside in any of the sunken ruins found beneath the sands, though they can sometimes be found displaced on the surface. The Knights in Yellow appear to be animated suits of armor meant for larger humanoids, perhaps the [[Bath'kah]] of [[Void Dunes|The Void dunes]]- though if their stories are to be believed the armor is too small for their ancestors to have worn. The suits themselves- most of them mundane- average twenty feet tall, with yellow highlights on white, silver plate. Each suit also possesses a beautiful yellow cape that stops just above the ankle- and ribbons of yellow silk draping across their cuirasses in varying fashion. All suits wield a large, twenty-seven foot long great sword with a flat head- traditionally seen used by executioners.

Most suits, roughly nineteen in twenty, are not animated. The suits that are, however, are completely indistinguishable in their craft, though they are often cleaner than the other suits as their frequent movement displaces dust on their shoulders and clothing. Additionally, [[Eldritch Energy|eldritch]] whispers can be frequently heard when an animated suit is near.

A spell such as detect good and evil can alert one to the presence of an animated suit, as their nature as Eldritch Spawn will set off the spells detection- and are one of very few monsters that can do such. Additionally, dwarven paper-glyphs are sold that will burn upon reaching a thirty foot proximity to an animated suit within [[The City of Wyrms]]. Infamous as they are, The Knights in Yellow are an easy to manage foe.

The Knights in Yellow do not follow linear paths to their target- alternating on the grids of ruins and never moving diagonally across the grids. Additionally, they cannot pass across salt lines allowing a party to section themselves off from the suit and remain in safety while they prepare. In combat, remaining diagonally adjacent to a Knight will ensure it needs to keep moving to attack you- you should be swift on your feet or incredibly durable to dodge or block their strikes. Axe weaponry serves well to puncture the armor of The Knights.

If one strays too far, however, an animated suit may begin firing bolts of eldritch energy to make up for its lack of movement. Finally, being in proximity or looking at an animated suit will cause blood to come from one's tear ducts- causing problems with vision and reducing accuracy. Unless you can fight through the urge to close your eyes you will constantly be at a disadvantage. Combat will be long, but The Knights in Yellow make for a great test for one's progress in [[The West]], felling one means that you're in for success... or so it is said.

The Knights in Yellow are largely dominant within the ruins they inhabit, slaughtering any creatures- save for [[Grolles]]- they find, though some monsters can certainly hold their own or even completely overpower a Knight such as [[Killfreaks]] and [[Abominations]].
