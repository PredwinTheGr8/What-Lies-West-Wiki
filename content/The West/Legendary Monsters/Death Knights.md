### [[Monster Callsigns|Callsigns]]-

- Smell of rot
- Stench of Necromancy- commonly experienced as the smell of posies
- Echoes of dripping liquid
- Muffled groans
- Trails of blood droplets, roughly five inches across
- Bone shards on walls and in corpses
- Arcane dread
- Detect evil
  
Death Knights are [[Legendary Monsters|Legendary Mutants]] of [[The West]] that rarely form on their own, and populate any ruin infested with necrotic energy. Death Knights are incredibly rare, some adventurers claiming that the monsters are completely made up as no evidence of a slain one has been compounded- a fact derived from claims that they disintegrate upon death. Death Knights are so far along their mutation that there is no resemblance of what lineage they may have been. Their bones have overtaken their skin, forming into dense plates of mail that have been enhanced by the magic that mutated them in the first place.

Their skin and muscles beneath the plate is mangled, constantly bleeding through the seems and cracks of the armor and dripping onto the ground in frequent splotches as they travel around the ruins. Growing to sizes of thirteen feet- they barely manage to hunch through the corridors and stand as pale beacons in larger chambers- all while wielding a blade made of bone designed to sever heads from torsos.

Death Knights are fearsome opponents, deftly skilled with their blade and hardy enough to block powerful strikes with only their forearm. Girtz Ribmangler and his party claimed that a Death Knight once 'tanked' an entire fireball spell without so much as a flinch, though the voice inside screamed in agony as the spell made contact. Death Knight encounters often end with at least one death, as their aura of fear cripples response from adventurers approached by the Knights. Usually, after the first vanguard suffers an early execution the other members of a party snap out of their haze and run for their lives- a wise decision.

Backtracking slightly, the 'voice' inside of a Death Knight acts in contradiction to the actions of the Mutant. Muffled screams begging for forgiveness when the Death Knight is winning and pleas for merciful death when it begins to falter- at least according to the more perceptive adventurers as most only hear muffled screams. The touch of a Death Knights blade is dangerous- not just because of the sharp edge, but because it holds the same mutative powers that corrupted the Death Knight in the first place. Those struck by a Death Knight that survive the encounter are doomed to inevitably become one unless they seek restoration from a priest of [[Orpheus]].

The process, described by the only known infected- Girtz Ribmangler, was "Agonizing- my organs felt as if shards of glass pierced them. It took constant healing spells on the way back to the [[The City of Wyrms]] just to keep me from bleeding out from my arse."

If one cannot escape a Death Knight, or perhaps has been tasked with slaying one, successful kills report that nothing short of overwhelming attacks will bring down a Death Knight. A weakness somewhere in the armor or perhaps to a certain school of magic is speculated, but with the rarity of encounters and the deadliness of their blade many adventurers simply choose to run.

Death Knights only target adventurers and do not appear to hunt anything else according to observation. Passively walking past [[Grolles]], [[Thunderjaws]], Animated [[The Knights in Yellow|Knights in Yellow]] and other ruin fauna. [[Chromatic Sentinels]], as well as [[Abominations]] however will immediately attack a Death Knight, with the former being likely to succeed and the latter often becoming an even worse beast after being struck with the bone blade- or just meeting their end against the only creature that can match their brawn in the dungeon.
