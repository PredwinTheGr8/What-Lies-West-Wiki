## [[Monster Callsigns|Callsigns]]-

- Loud footsteps- enough to carry over several miles
- Earth-shaking roars
- Increased wind pressure, speed and arcane taint
- Thunder clouds
- Carcasses showing signs of both claw and bludgeoning attacks, only offal consumed- all other meat remains
- No scavengers on dead prey
- Changed [[Void Bisons|Void Bison]] behavior- tighter formations, frequent danger calls
- Dead [[Bath'kah]] patrols or camps
  
Behemoths are [[Legendary Monsters]] of [[The West]] found naturally within [[Void Dunes|The Void Dunes]], though as of two years ago they have been seen in other [[All Regions of The West|regions]] such as [[Blasted Basin|The Basin]] and other, more hospitable regions. Behemoths are near the top of the entire food chain of The West, being among the largest carnivores present in the desert and being capable of manipulating magic at will. Behemoths have earned a great deal of infamy among adventurers- with many caravans destined for the [[Bath'kah]] being taken or destroyed by a wayward Behemoth. 

Visually, Behemoths do not resemble any other living creature, though perhaps being passingly com-parable to the wooly rhinos of [[Balphora]]. Behemoths are twenty foot tall, sextapedal mammalians with pitch black fur that drags down to their feet. This fur is incredibly dense and can absorb a great deal of physical and metaphysical force- notably deflecting any electromancy right back at the attacker. The fur covers most of the Behemoths body, save for their face and their tail. The head of a Behemoth is comprised of numerous circular patterns- with eight eyes, three nostrils, and one octagonally symmetrical mouth at the center. This mouth is a large sphincter that unfurls to reveal a massive appendage covered in spiny teeth, and can further extend to reveal two rows of canines and incisors. The first layer of teeth is used to bludgeon and lacerate prey, while the second layer is used to consume the stomach and then other offal of their victim.

A Behemoth is a very territorial creature- unlike other monsters their size they will happily go out of their way to slaughter and consume adventurers. The territory of a singular Behemoth can be massive, while measurement is impossible to a perfect degree in The West, competent adventurers claim that they see signs of a lone Behemoth for days of travel. Behemoths mark their territory by combining their harsh saliva with the sand of their home, binding the sand into a concrete-like substance that is then shaped into a pillar and then urinated on. Their urine is noted to smell highly of [[Electromancy|electromancy]]- commonly described as the smell of dried insects.

The [[Bath'kah]] call Behemoths "Im'roh", which in their tongue translates to "Venerable One". The Bath'kah believe that Behemoths are the result of their own ancient scholars tampering with magics in an attempt to stop The Crawling Sand, one of the many disasters that struck The West at its fall. The Bath'kah believe that these ancient mages, despite knowing the risks, gave their lives away to stop- or at least slow- the crawling sand. As such, many Bath'kah tribes venerate the Behemoths in one way or another- some tribes may build statues of them as signs of respect, others may hunt them to receive their strength in consumption, and even perhaps their wisdom.

A Behemoth's ability to manipulate the weather at will lends some credence to this, with their control of local magic being near absolute. This alone makes Behemoths a difficult foe, needless to say that their might makes it even harder a challenge to bring one down.

Despite this, one Bath'kah chieftain- named 'Rgan' is famous for taming a Behemoth 'scab', the name given to their young- an apt description for the reproductive process of a Behemoth. This Behemoth, named 'Kasal'vren Incar' is well behaved and seems to love Rgan a great deal. As one might expect, [[The Conrad Family]] and other powers that be would very much love to get their own scab to raise and use in war or whatever deplorable acts they wish to commit.

Onto what I'm sure raised your interest- at least hopefully- the reproductive cycle of a Behemoth. Behemoths are asexual, self replicating beasts. They grow several 'scab' like growths underneath their fur. These growths are the external presence of a perfect clone growing underneath the skin of the host Behemoth. The gestation process takes, according to Rgan, seven months. After which time one or several scabs will fall off. If the Behemoth is not willing to raise a child it will then eat the scab and continue on until the next pops off. An infantile Behemoth weighs one metric ton at birth, and they are quite useless unless assisted by another creature like their parent.

Finally, if one faces a Behemoth in combat and escape is not an option a loose formation works best to avoid an instant 'party wipe'. Behemoths are experts at crowd control, and grouping together only makes their job easier. Successful hunts report that getting underneath a Behemoths fur is the best way to deal with them, as the skin of their belly is the weakest and is susceptible to cuts and slashes. A wounded Behemoth will enter a rage-like state, attacking with great ferocity. It should be noted that Behemoths will never- NEVER flee. They will fight to their last breath if needs must. Keeping away from their front and rear will keep you alive, but their thick fur means that eventually you will need to approach one of their faces to land a strike.

Behemoths prey upon [[Void Bisons]], [[Warp Beasts]], [[Bath'kah]] and anything else unlucky enough to cross their path. [[True Wyverns]] have a poor matchup against Behemoths, as the Behemoth will often knock the Wyvern from the sky with lightning and then bludgeon them to death when they hit the ground. The only Monster that can win more often than the Wyvern will. [[Chrozhran Destroyers]] will incinerate a Behemoth then eat the charred remains, and [[Bath'kah]] will organize hunts to slay any troublesome Behemoths in their territory.
