# Artifice;

It remains unclear where artifice ends and the arcane begins, but for now we must discus technology that uses absolutely no pieces of the arcane. One such tool that may come to mind is the black powder firearms of the [[Minotaurs|Minotaur]] peoples. Black powder weapons function by simply sparking and igniting a propellant that then slings a metal or other projectile at great speed. This uses none of the arcane as fuel, energy or otherwise. A step up may be the utilizations of some magic patterns in tools- manufactured uses of magic that while not incanting the realm of the arcane still draw it in. Some argue this is still 'mundane' technology because the actual mechanism contains little to no actual glyphs or sum circles. Those who propose this might only do so to dissuade those of a certain fanatical purgation such as [[Sons of Y'zrold|The Sons of Y'zrold]]. In my own eyes, I believe that "technology" stops as soon as a reliance or utilization of any magic is involved.

One of my fellow writers of this article, Rakrak The Mouse, believes that whatever the case may be that intrinsically the Arcane will influence the artificial, and that one cannot advance without the other. There is an argument to be made that some sources of electromancy- such as lightning storms over the [[Ocean Magnamous]]- do not possess arcane components within, and may hold a future for "pure" technology.

Another, Ashkram Who Speaks Strange, claims that technology is only an extension of the arcane, that it cannot exist at all without it, and that the arcane and the spirits commanding it are of the highest possible power achievable in this realm.
