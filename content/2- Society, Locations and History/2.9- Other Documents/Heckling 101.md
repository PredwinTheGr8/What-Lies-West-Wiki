([[Build Togethers|331]], What Lies West?)

[[Koross]] is no stranger to political speakers, carnival performances and parades- while all of these things demand attention, not everyone is good at enthralling their audiences. Some might have a few walk away mid sentence, others get an object or two thrown in their direction- such as a tomato or a chair. But for those who truly, *truly* disappoint those around them there is a cruel fate waiting them indeed.

### **Invoking [[Whallad The Whimsical|Whallad]]-

Whallad, The Whimsical one, is the [[Gods|God]] of jokes, mirth and all things comedy. While few directly worship Whallad, almost all who live in the true territories of [[Koross]] know her... his... their name. When someone disappoints a crowd of onlookers, they may be forced to watch as what starts as only a handful of people chanting "Whallad!" over and over turns into the whole gathering, all pointing at the speaker repeatedly- Invoking Whallad's presence.

Now, for the average crowd their voice is not loud enough to draw the attention of a god, and even if Whallad noticed they may not act upon it unless it suites their fancy. But, for large gatherings, upwards of hundreds, maybe thousands of people, Whallad has no choice but to give the people what they want.

A "blessing" of Whallad will be given to the speaker- and oh how many blessings there are:

- A giant red nose that turns blue when they lie.
- Clown shoes that can never be taken off, and yes they start to reek.
- A very unflattering cowlick that flings off any garments that would hide it.
- Fish breath.
- A ridiculous accent.
- Shoe laces that cannot be tied.
- An urge to others to throw pie at you.
- Being unable to speak outside of self deprecating humor.
- Transforming into a hilarious, impish creature.
- And so much more.

Luckily, these "blessings" are not permanent, and will fade eventually, depending on just how much Whallad wanted to jest with someone. Still, it serves well enough for many speakers to practice time and time again, for fear of a harmless prank.
