([[Build Togethers|336]], [[What Lies West]]?)

"Ah, to be an adventurer- to wander the wastes and to plunder its bounties for your own. There's no greater life! The intrigue! The danger! The clout! hah-ha ha! Oh man, its a real blast when everything goes right."

-Logan Rauff, [[Adventurers|adventurer]]

Since the very first of [[The Unfeathering|The Unfeatherings]], people from all across [[Koross]], [[The Great Swamp|Moulugg]] and [[Harush]] have been [[The Calling|called]] to [[The West]]. The average person cannot uproot their lives so frivolously though, they look on as ship after ship of 'free' vagabonds and marauders, soldiers and mercenaries, nobles and knights all venture out to 'The West', the land of riches and promises of wealth.

Sure, life on the [[River Fallow]] is good, there is rich soil to plant wheat and carrots and onions, and thus plenty of profit to be made, but people crave adventure, and with the constant itch in the back of their minds to go west... its enough to drive some people mad. There have been numerous occurrences of farmhands and castle guards finally snapping and abandoning their lives, hitchhiking onto a ship only to find more of the same- more farmland, more monotonous labor to do.

No, its not so easy as 'getting to [[Balphora]]' to become an [[Adventurers|adventurer]]. You must walk your way to [[The City of Wyrms]] and hope that you have enough money left over to jumpstart your career. You have to spend money to make money, after all. And ow how you'll have to empty your coffers if you want even a slim chance at surviving.

But it must be worth it, right? There are stories of [[Adventurers]] making their ways back east, to [[Koross]] and becoming dukes just from their sheer wealth alone! If only you could get your claim, your lucky break- an ancient vault, a decrepit mausoleum of a king! Your only one expedition away from striking it big! Don't give up now! What do you have to lose anyways? Your job? Your family? Yeah right, if you've come to Balphora you've already left those things far behind. What could you possibly have to lose?

**Your life. You could lose your life.**

But alas, thus is the life of an adventurer, looting, hunting and dying! So what are you waiting for? Go! Go and find out-
