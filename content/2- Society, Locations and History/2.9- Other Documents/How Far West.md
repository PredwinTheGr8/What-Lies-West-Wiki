What lies west doesn't seem that important right now, really. It could be gold, it could be power, it could be a slumbering demigod. What I think is important- something folk think about but never dwell on, is exactly *how* far west, is [[The West]]? Where does the desert end? Where does is truly 'start,' at least where its effects are concerned. Scrying magic cannot perceive within, clairvoyance offers no guidance, even the psionic power of the far-marked cannot pierce the veil. But how? Why?

# [[How Far West|How Far West?]]

I think I know, at least partially. Many who attempt to measure the west try to use mile long ropes, that are lost in thought as they lay forgotten between regions. Others drag their wagons with weighted wheels, but the winds always cover their tracks. But me? Makkar? I believe I've found the only REAL way to calculate distance travelled in [[The West]]!

It all started on one of my sponsored expeditions with Markus Krauler's party. I had noticed that, consistently between chance visits between [[Ossuary Reach|The Ossuary Reach]] and [[Devastated Mesas|The Devastated Mesas]], that I was always rather thin and in early stages of starvation. So, I had an idea! I would take a started weight, and then simulate the environment of The West with magic! Then, by eating to roughly twenty stone's worth of weight, I could measure that weight as time spent traveling- or even by using accurate measurements, the ACTUAL distance between to certain points.

If my calculations are to be believed, and this information is indeed accurate, The Ossuary Reach and The Devastated Mesas are roughly twenty eight to thirty miles apart! This could be revolutionary- though complicated of course. If I manage to keep this system up, and take my own weight measurements between numerous region jumps... Well I do believe I can paint a vague map of The West. I need to get everything ready for my next trip West, but after that, I think the information will be extraordinarily prevalent in future academic explorations into the vastness of The West.

I go out two nights from now. [[Ernin]] preserve me.
