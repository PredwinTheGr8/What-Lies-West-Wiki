Today was a sunny day. Nary a cloud in the sky, a cool mist flowing through the streets, parasols extended and awnings stretched to the road.

I thought that today would be a good break from all this writing, and perhaps all of these... thoughts forced upon me as of late. A walk would have been good for me, I thought. But imagination and reality can be different in extremes, even when so fiction-less as hoping for a good day.

My stroll brought myself and Dave through Breggan Street on our way to the Sluicegate- a local pub in [[The City of Wyrms]]- to get something that would give me heartaches later. As we neared, I noticed a construction team working on some repairs after some idiotic [[Adventurers|adventurers]] dropped a [[Storm Batteries|storm battery]]. I could tell from the blue scorch marks what had happened. That and the burned out husks of the four imbeciles caught in the explosion still smoldering under the sun. There was probably an enterprising cleric of [[Orpheus]] on their way to revive them already.

Since our way forward was blocked, I had considered just turning around and getting food elsewhere, but in my foolish desire I imagined the glistening, juicy tavern burger I had daydreamed about afore. Orpheus..  Divines forgive my greed.

So, we went down an alleyway hoping to get around the construction. That's when it all got... sallow. Coughs, retching, splattering vomit. The poisoned backroads were occupied by the unfortunate.

I passed a man- I think it was a man at least- covered head to toe in teeth and tendrils. He looked up at me with a head covered in so many teeth you could barely see the meat beneath. His hair barely came out in clumps, and his eyes had but mere slits to see through. His right arm had completely been overtaken, covered in at least thirty pounds of pure enamel. Even as he just moved to look at me, he lacerated himself and bled from between the crevices.

I walked past him and saw a kobold, or maybe it was once a goblin. A scaly creature regardless. It was hunched over in a fetal position next to another, long dead kobold. The breathing one was melded into the pathway, black ichor seeping out from its mouth as it corroded the stonework with every drip. It looked up at me, and extended out a hand- begging to me. It shames me to admit I only hastened my steps away from the creature, or person rather.

I continued through, keeping to the right so that I did not lose my way. I passed some less horrific spawn-touched unfortunates, others who's bodies could not be described with words on paper. But one final... mass barred my path. I saw... I don't know what it possibly could have been once. Maybe it was once multiple, or it could have been a child perhaps. Whatever it was, now it had become a mass of rolling flesh and popping boils. Within the flesh a voice groaned in agony. Dave had flew far away now, perhaps I should have followed him.

I pressed on, something within me knew I should be quiet passing this... thing. The ground around it was littered with its own refuse and the bones of what was undeniably humanoids. The skull of a kobold, the ribs of a dwarf, the festering green flesh of a goblin. Why on earth did I keep going? Why did so many enter its grasp as well? Clearly it had some sort of power over the mind to assure any who approached that they should keep going, there is no other explanation for such foolishness on my part. Surely not.

As I kept my eyes on the blubbery mound, I knocked my foot against a femur bone. In a moment, the creature roused awake, seven eyes at least opening up to look right at me.

I didn't hesitate even for a moment, I shot it. It squealed like a hog. I stepped out of its way as it tried to grab me. I reloaded, I shot again, and again and again. I didn't know I could load a flintlock that fast. But eventually, It got its hand on me and I... I went feral. I remember biting its flesh and ripping chunks out of it and... and when it let go I lunged back at it. I can still taste that horrid blood in the back of my throat.
