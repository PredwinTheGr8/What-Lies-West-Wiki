I simply have some research to say in regards to this note, and the answer to the "why" of their weaknesses. Its rather... simple really. The Eldritch is the unstable, the chaotic and unpredictable. While technically speaking, if you took enough swings at a Spawn you'd eventually hit it, it is much easier for everyone if that attack is *predictably* able to land if you would have otherwise connected. The Spawn live of such spontaneity- they are defined by it.

Iron, as a materiel, is incredibly stable. We don't know the specifics as is such with our limits, but Iron is nonreactive to many sources that would have interactions with other metals. Iron cannot be enchanted whereas something like copper or bronze can be thus. This is likely the reason why Steel does not work despite simply being carbonized Iron, as Steel CAN be enchanted and therefore something deep within the composition of the steel is fundamentally different.

The stability and nonreactive nature of Iron is conceptually anathema to Spawn on a near complete level, with only a few Spawn being immune. This essentially causes the 'skin' of a Spawn to become *real* on contact, a phenomenon and concept that essentially cannot happen and the fabric of our world responds to by deleting that matter from its plane. Each hit takes off chunks of a spawn's form and though they are alien in nature and form they are still made of a certain amount of energy that when depleted renders them 'dead.'

Salt is the greatest extreme to use against a Spawn, though. Salt, as a primary alchemical reagent, is used for transmutations. By containing Iron itself, salt momentarily creates existing flesh on contact, which the salt then infuses immediately with. When the chunk of flesh disappears some salt is then caught in the 'portal' that brings the substance into the eldritch plane of [[The Deep]]. Here, the salt transmutes the affected Spawn constantly into physical matter which CANNOT exist within The Deep, causing the same mathematical solution that [[Maernos]] uses to destroy eldritch matter to occur within The Deep. Essentially, you make a Spawn allergic to its home realm on an extreme realm, causing the entity to "freak out" and flicker between existing within real space on a full level- which kills it slowly- or exist in its home plane- which again, kills it slowly. This will persist until the salt looses alchemical charge and the Spawn can stabilize. When this happens, apply more salt and strike the entity with more iron implements.
