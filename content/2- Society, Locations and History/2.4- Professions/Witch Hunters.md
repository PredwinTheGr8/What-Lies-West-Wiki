Mortals who seek out those who fell for the temptation or curse of [[Magic|magic]], the largest group of these folk being the [[Sons of Y'zrold|Sons of Y'zrold]]. Witch Hunters have only been around as long as the enslavement of the [[Orcs]], and have died out significantly since the discovery that the Orcs were in fact, not corrupted by magic.
