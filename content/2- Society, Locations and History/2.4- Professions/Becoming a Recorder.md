"I remember my first day. It was a lot of work- writing, drawing, dissections. It's tedious sometimes, painful others- like when you accidentally stick yourself with a quill or something. But still, despite its flaws, its my passion. I wouldn't trade it for the world."

-Marie Northup, [[Recorders|Recorder]]

Recorders are a sub-profession of official [[Scholam Wyrmras]] [[Scholars]]- with the two groups being distinguished from one another via their separately colored robes. Scholars are known for their deep blue scarves and cloaks, while Recorders are instead recognized for their bright green capes and garments.

Becoming a Recorder is hard work, but its a very desirable position for many. The pay is good, the days are easy- though they drag on- and the work itself is often what draws in so many. A rigorous interview process is done first. Massive qualifications must be met- history with surgery, wound treatment- essentially asking for a glorified surgeon that knows how to cast spells and is good with a notepad.

Many Scholars themselves tried to become Recorders, but their interview either went wrong or was forgotten as more and more piled up. Many blue-cloaks hold envy or distain for those who "took their position" from them. Despite assumptions, then, Recorders and Scholars don't get along very often, though some good friendships are still made.
