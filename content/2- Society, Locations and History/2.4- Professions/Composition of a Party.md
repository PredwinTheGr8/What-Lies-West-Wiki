[[Build Togethers|405]], [[index|What Lies West?]]

"It's important. Maybe the most important. You need someone light on their feet to check ahead, at least one behind them- the vanguard, and behind them is the smaller, lighter fighters. Finally, at the absolute rear is the mages and their escort. Simple as can be, but one mistake could be the end of everyone there."

-Harold Green, [[Adventurers|adventurer]]

The following discussion is a transcript of my talks with Harold Green, for simplification his dialogue will be slanted and mine will remain mundane. My assistant for the day, Screek Whisper-Whisker is to be credited for his assistance in writing our talk down.

*"Well it is. It's easy to die out there, and it's even easier to make it certain in some situations."*

"How do you mean?"

*"Ok, lets say you've found a dungeon right? How many entrances are there? What do they look like? Are the callsigns suggesting anything that might be too strong for your group? An [[Abominations|Abomination]]? [[The Knights in Yellow]]?"*

"And you check every time?"

*"Not me personally, our scout does. Akk-ak is small, light footed and has good eyes. He may be a bit dull but he knows how to read- and he also loves his pattern recognition. He's real good at recognizing frequent callsigns to tell if there's something big ahead, or even if its already in the room with us."*

"How important would you say Akk-ak's job is, exactly? Has he ever failed in his duties?"

*"Incredibly important. Maybe even more important than Amelia's- she's our healer of course. Without him we would stumble into traps and get hurt, then Amelia would burn out her [[Magic]] early and then we're certainly in for a world of hurt later when, again, something big shows up. Something big ***always** shows up. As for him messing up? Once. The room had a bright arcane chandelier in it and he couldn't see the glyph on the ground. Girtz is tough though- he walked it off just fine."*

"And Girtz, he's one of your vanguards I'm led to understand?" 

*"Yes- him, Traila and myself act as our party's vanguard. We're the ones that soak up all the attention and keep beasts from getting to the backline. Akk-ak will let us know first what kind of [[Monsters|monsters]] waiting around the corner and we'll act accordingly."

"Does anyone else directly rely on Akk-ak?"

*"Our commander, which used to be me, relies a lot on Akk-ak. Ezlan- our commander- treasures Akk-ak's information gathering. Our commander usually tries to stay in the middle of the group. He doesn't fight much but he is absolutely invaluable. When you're stuck in you can lose your wider attention- you might not see a gremlin or some other little beast creeping up just out of your field of vision- especially if you wear armor like me. Ezlan shouts out commands and updates us on monster positions and actions. His job might be the second most important- Akk-ak is still the one who lets him know what we're even up against. Still, that shouldn't discount how much harder Ezlan's job is. He has to remember an entire manual's worth of information- which might I add he quite favors your work in archiving monsters."

"Thank you, I try my best. There are still many left though, its tedious work and information gathering takes time."

*"You know we could expediate the process a bit if you wanted to come out with us once. We're used to guarding unarmed [[Scholars|blue-cloaks]]."

"Heh, no thank you I'm quite comfortable in my study. Ezlan's commands though- they help out a great deal?"

*"Without a doubt. I can't count how many times I've nearly been blind sided by a freak only for Ezlan to brace me with a quick, concise order. It's second nature at this point- I hear him say ' Harold; wide left!' and I just ***do**. Instinct will keep you alive, and when your fellows can exploit your instinct to the benefit of the whole you know you're a good match."

"Incredible. I assume that the battlefield gets loud, then? It must with all the shouting."

*"Sometimes. Ezlan usually doesn't have to do much work for smaller skirmishes, and the rest of us don't talk a lot once we start swinging. We save our 'thank you's' and 'you're welcomes' till after our blades our sheathed. The real noise comes from the backline."*

"How do you mean?"

*"Incantations. Mages are loud- the vocal component of many spells need to be shouted or at least said at good volume. Casting also amps up their adrenaline a lot. After a fireball or two our rookie mage gets this frothing look on his face and he takes a bit to calm down- even after the fight ends. Amelia though- the spells of Heliomancy themselves are insanely loud. Chiming bells, humming bolts of energy, radiant pulses of light. Fireball is near silent but a healing spell makes you wanna cover your ears."

"Does it ever get to be too much? Volume, I mean."

*"Occasionally. I remember getting really mad at her once because I took a [[Gnolls|Gnoll]] spear to the gut and she ran up to heal me. The bell tolling was incredibly... disruptive and I had some choice words for her. She made sure I regretted my words though. The fight was over so she could take as long as she wanted before stitching me up. Hah ha."*

"And their escorts- you said the casters needed one?"

*"Right you are. The 'escort' is usually one or two melee fighters and maybe a gunman. They keep the monsters off of the weaker folk so they don't need to worry. You've probably heard this a lot, and written it down even more- but monsters KNOW who the threat is. They go after the mages as soon as they can- [[Arcanosaurs]], [[Fog Men]]- any number of heavy hitters might just waltz right through the Vanguard and make off with a caster- and that's what the escort are meant to stop. they're the last line of defense for mages and without them they wouldn't stand a chance- and without mages we don't stand one either."*

"So, everyone has their part to play, and nobody is less important than another- at least not by much?"

*"Bingo. Without any one of the roles we fill up the whole party would suffer. Though, I think if I HAD to pick one member that seems to do the most work- its Girtz. He's always on top of the biggest pile of bodies- always the one holding up the rubble over Amelia, always the first one to grab your hand and help you back up. He's our backbone, Akk-ak is our eyes, Amelia is the heart and Ezlan is the brain. The others are important too, but if any one of them goes down we suffer the hardest- namely Amelia, though."*
*"Amelia has never once died. The others have- I have too. But Amelia has never so much as gotten a major bleed. Sure, she's been hit once or twice, but if she ever goes unconscious, or worse- dies, that might just be curtains on us. She is the only one that can revivify us. The only one who can wave a broken bone away. Without her we'd be proper screwed."*

"I see. Well Harold, thank you for coming- I really appreciate it. I have some other works to get to but Screek wrote all this down and we can get it summarized up or left as is. I'll be sure the guild sees it and makes sure all the rookies do too."

*"Thank you, I'm grateful."*

"Of course, until next time- Orpheus willing there will be one."
