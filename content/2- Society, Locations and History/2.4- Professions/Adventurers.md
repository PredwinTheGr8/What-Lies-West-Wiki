The 'brave' or just plain greedy folk who venture out into [[The West]] in search of the source of [[The Calling]] or just to make a quick buck. Adventurers have been around since [[The City of Wyrms]] was found- with their origins as hired mercenaries that cleared the city of all the monsters that made the city home such as [[Arcanosaurs]] and [[Grolles]].

Since then, the term 'adventurer' simply refers to those who get make their living directly from The West and selling the resources within.
