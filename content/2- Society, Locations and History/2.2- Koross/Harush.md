The second furthest anyone has ever gone to the east in [[Koross]]. There are likely more peoples out there, beyond even [[Hivewaste]], but the attention of all ilk has been focused on [[The West]]. Harush is a temperate desert and rainforest region with rolling sand dunes sporadically interrupted with towering green forests. The [[Catfolk]]'s history begins here, though some among their number theorize they may have come from even further east.

Harush has little in the way of food, here, resources must be pooled into communal meals which encourages unity and cooking skills- hence the cause for the Catfolks' tendency to cook well ahead of time and make plenty of comrades who might share the burden of hunting, though such times are long since passed.
