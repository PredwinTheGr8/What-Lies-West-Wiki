A term used to refer to the "primary" three races of [[The Wider World|The East]]- [[Humans]] [[Elves]] and [[Dwarves]]. This term is used because... well because they all look the same as each other- at least to the other, more unique races of the World. They are often recognized as the rulers of [[Koross]], though nowadays that hardly means anything.
