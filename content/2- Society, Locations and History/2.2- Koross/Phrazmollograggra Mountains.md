([[Build Togethers|330]], What Lies West?)

The Phrazmollograggra Mountains- the tallest, though not largest, mountains in the known world. The "Firemouths" are the most volcanically active mountains in [[Koross]], with a near constant stream of ash and soot blighting the skies of [[The Northern Wastes]], leaving it a ruined region with soil so stricken with destruction no plants can grow that aren't themselves corrupted by the [[Magic]] so deeply engrained in the earth it plagues it. Yet, though the mountains are destructive, their existence is essential to the magic of all of [[Koross]] and beyond.

The Phrazmollograggras are a [[Sites of Power|Site of Power]]- places where magic is created, and dissipated throughout the world. Without them, the arcane would cease to exist, and the [[Eldritch Energy|eldritch energies]] of [[The Old Ones]] would drown the world in darkness. Though, that is not the only good quality of magic. Magic, in healthy quantities, promotes the growth of flora, and the health of fauna- it holds back disease and strengthens the very land itself. But, as with The Northern Wastes, a massive amount of magic is nothing but bad for a region- luckily there are ways to dilute it.

[[The Great Swamp]] starts with a large series of rivers that feed into it from The Northern Wastes, the water lessens the potency of the magic, and trickles it into the basins of the swamp, feeding the plant life and by extension, the creatures therein. The ash from the mountains also helps to feed the plants as well, with it also keeping the waters relatively clean as it clings to bacteria and forces it to the bottom of the water. While it isn't completely safe to drink unfiltered water in the swamp, you likely wont catch anything horribly dangerous for you.

And, even further east in [[Harush]], the winds carry the magics to the sands, allowing essential plant life to grow even in the scorching heat- plants that both wildlife and the [[Catfolk]] feed on to survive. It is suspected by scholars that the reason [[The Plain Three]] are so, well, similar to one another is because their homes are west of the Mountains, and therefore less charged with mana. The lack of magic makes for a lack of positive mutations in the peoples in the region, though that isn't necessarily a negative thing.
