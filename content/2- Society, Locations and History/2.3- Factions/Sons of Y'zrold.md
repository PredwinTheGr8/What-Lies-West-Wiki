(301, What Lies West?)

In [[Koross]], numerous factions squabble over access to ports and ships to send their faithful to [[Balphora]], one such faction being an ancient order of [[Dwarves|Dwarven]] Templars who worship [[Y'zrold Acktraz, The Mage-Eater|Y'zrold Acktraz]], also known as **The Mage Eater**. These templars, The Sons of Y'zrold, view mages of all kinds as forces of danger- that must be slaughtered if the world is to ever be safe. They see magic as a plague on the world, that has made it reliant on the arcane far too much. As such all magic is to be hated- except runes of course, for that is not magic- at least according to these dwarves.

The Sons of Y'zrold have governed massive chunks of the East for a very, very long time- perhaps even before man awoke. Still, luckily for most mages, as dwarves they don't leave their holds much, going on crusades only every hundred years or so to cleanse the lands around their holds and nothing more.

Though, with the boom in mages and other spellcasters going to [[The West]], the Sons of Y'zrold fear that they may create a heretical empire of wizards that will never be capable of destruction. Thus, in their wisdom they have begun what is known as "[[The Great Feast]]", an event to fill the stomach of The Mage Eater with the souls of hundreds, perhaps thousands of cowardly witches. They sponsor raids into The West as well, to root out and destroy any ancient arcane artifacts they find so that they can never fall into "the wrong hands". [[The Conrad Family|The Conrad Family]] tolerates and even directly works with the Sons of Y'zrold, preferring to have the three thousand or so angry templars on their side, rather than as a foe.

The Sons of Y'zrold, as one may suspect are in direct opposition to the Scholars of the Northwest, with the two factions fighting a proxy war with mercenaries while they plot for a true battle to shake the world. They are also actively at war with [[The Waywards|the Wayward]] squatters hiding in [[K'bass Vould]], with their number putting in great personal effort into retaking the land surrounding the [[Emruld Resvrassik]].
