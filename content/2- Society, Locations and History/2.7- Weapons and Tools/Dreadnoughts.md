"Not all monsters are born of [[The West]]. Many are of course, but some- some are made by man, set loose on hundreds by man, and then discarded to rot by man. Dread those whose war horns drown out musket fire most- for their fire burns hottest."

-Markus Krauler, [[Adventurers|adventurer]]

Dreadnoughts are, or rather were, constructs designed by the [[Elves]] of [[Moradesh]]. Dreadnoughts are infamous for their deployment during [[Iradeen Thaddeus]]' war to conquer [[Koross]], during which times they crashed through castle walls and cut apart hundreds of defenders. While their use during the war was short, the destruction they caused tor apart hundreds, perhaps thousands of families- and paved the path for Iradeen's road to utter domination.

Dreadnoughts are comprised of a dense metallic shell capable of deflecting even musket fire. At their core rests a pyromantic furnace that allows them to become animated. Complex gears give them articulation, and a mind of cunning directives gives them purpose. The Dreadnoughts served as Iradeen's frontline shock troop, soaking up damage so his weaker infantry could make it behind enemy lines during a charge.

Three quarters of the way through the War, Iradeen was forced to de-militarize many Dreadnoughts, as the cost of their upkeep far outweighed the gains of his war by the end. In its final days, only thirteen Dreadnoughts were kept up and running, before all being scrapped to recover resource losses after the war.

When the [[Orc Revolution]] began, and the [[Cresthram|Crestranites]] and [[Ogres]] joined the war, Iradeen ordered the construction of thirty Dreadnoughts to deal with the Cresthran threat. The Dreadnoughts were said to have destroyed nearly everything within their borders, with many walled cities reduced to cinders when the lone Dreadnoughts passed through. Even after the Revolutions end, without the ability to track and disable them, these same Dreadnoughts continued to operate off the fuel they claimed by burning carcasses and homes in their furnaces. To this day, well over a hundred years later, Crestranites fear that at any moment a lone iron beast will come to their home and rend it unto ashes.
