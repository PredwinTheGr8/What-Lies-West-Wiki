A mighty valley made from the [[K'bass Kaulldour]], holding within it four dwarven holds and the five great lakes, or "dwakes" in the dwarven tongue- [[Dwake Mull]], [[Dwake Dwar]] [[Tzad'dwake Moragrund]], [[Dwake G'zrada]] and the [[Emruld Resvrassik]]. The region itself and even more land to the west of the [[K'bass Kaulldour]] is blighted with the constant ash of the active volcanoes within the mountain, making survival harsh and brutal.
