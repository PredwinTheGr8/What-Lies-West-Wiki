A range of [[Dwarves|Dwarven]] mountains in the mid-northern segment of mapped [[Balphora]]. The range is noteworthy for its massive number of active volcanoes constantly spewing forth ash and smog to the west, polluting the area heavily. This range also holds in its valley- the [[K'bass Vould]], lies the 5 great Dwakes- [[Emruld Resvrassik|Emruld Resvrassik]], [[Dwake G'zrada]], [[Dwake Dwar]], [[Dwake Mull]] and [[Tzad'dwake Moragrund]]. These are the largest freshwater lakes in the known world.
