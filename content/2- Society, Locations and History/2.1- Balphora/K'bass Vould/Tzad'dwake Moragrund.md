The largest and least corrupted of the algae bearing great lakes in the [[K'bass Vould]]. Tzad'dwake Moragrund has massive wrecks on its shores from a previous, ancient [[Dwarves|Dwarven]] fishing hotspot, though the algae has smothered the surface of the water and nothing lives in it save for the smallest of guppies.
