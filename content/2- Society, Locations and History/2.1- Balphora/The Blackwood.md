The north-most region of [[Balphora]], the Blackwoods mark the beginning of uncharted territory. The Blackwoods span from [[Wyrmscale Reaches|The Wyrmscale Reaches]] to the mid-eastern section of [[The Lawless North]]. The Blackwoods are dominated mostly by large fir trees that block a great deal of sunlight, hence the forest's name. While no villages have been built within the Blackwoods, as those who have come to Balphora have come for [[The West]] and to answer [[The Calling]], there are plenty of border-towns that harvest the bounty from within the woods to fill their pantries.
