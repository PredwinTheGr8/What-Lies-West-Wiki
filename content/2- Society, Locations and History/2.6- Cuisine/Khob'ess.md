([[Build Togethers|323]], What Lies West?)

"Sing! Cry out for [[Generous Harush]], that he may know our gratitude! Bring the mead! bring the bread! Bring the Khob'ess!"

-The Celebratory words of a Man partying for [[Akmaban]].

During the Celebration of Akmaban, the [[Catfolk]] of [[Harush]] compile their cooking knowledge and make all sorts of delicious foods- stews, meat pies, desserts and kebabs- but among all the dishes native to Harush, Khob'ess is perhaps the most widely known even to outsiders.

In its most basic form, Khob'ess is a sweet made by cooking down cane sugars and coating the produced mixture onto a hard, bread like cracker that itself is sweetened by sugars. Sometimes the Khob'ess is given edible decorations, and the bread may be softer, but never must it NOT be a sweet thing to consume.

Khob'ess is believed to be the greatest gift given from Harush to the Catfolk people, and while it is to be shared, it is never to be "reimagined." Human chefs that get their hands on it and try to change out the sugar may face the religious wrath of their furry coworkers, and even worse if they call something that is definitely NOT a Khob'ess a Khob'ess... The Catfolk have killed for less.

Like a mafia of critics, Catfolk, or others that have fallen into their religion, patrol inns and taverns that serve "Khob'ess" and ensure it meets the standards of their zealous treat- and luckily due to the relatively low cost of sugar within [[Koross]] and the ease of creating the basic dish, most get a pat on the back- but others may find themselves in the wrong kind of spotlight from [[The Beastpact]] as a whole.
