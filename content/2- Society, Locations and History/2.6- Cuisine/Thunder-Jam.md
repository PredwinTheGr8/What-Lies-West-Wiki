([[Build Togethers|337]], [[What Lies West]]?)

"Its barely edible on its own, that's certain. But when your out in the middle of the sands and you haven't had even a fingernails height worth of something sweet for weeks, maybe months... you start craving things that would make a man vomit back East."

-Harold Green, [[Adventurers|adventurer]]

Cane sugar- a hot commodity that grows all across the [[River Fallow]] and in the sparse jungles of [[Harush]]. Despite its abundance, with nobles constantly buying it all up its a rare treat for the average man- though not entirely unaffordable for the average wage. Even across the [[Ocean Magnamous]], in [[Balphora]] the substance is grown and sold, with even more of the average peoples getting their hands on it thanks to a lower number of nobles in the area. [[The City of Wyrms]] is full of sweets- buns cakes and other small pastries- that the adventurers have grown accustomed to, perhaps to a fault.

For in [[The West]] there are no plants with enough sugar to satiate a regular person, and even the lack of it in dietary sources in the dunes can hamper a body. There are few true sources of sugar in the wastes, but the most accessible, and least vile, comes from a [[Monsters|monster]].

#### [[Thunderjaws]]

Thunderjaws are large- scarab adjacent- monsters that reside in the ruins of The West. Hiding their flat bodies inside large crevasses and clinging to the shadow-stricken ceilings out of sight from incautious adventurers below. Thunderjaws feed off of ambient mana to supplement their diets, mana that congeals in their bowels into a red paste that lines their intestines.

This "Thunder-Jam", as those daring enough to consume it refer to it as, is a prized 'treat' in the desert. The substance is comparable to vanilla extract in its potency- thus in smaller doses it makes for a serviceable sweetener, and when combined with other ingredients within the ruins can be made into a cake if one has enough skill. More often, the substance is added into teas and other hot drinks to sweeten them up and give a boost to the energy and vitality of those who imbibe it, similarly to the effects of [[Tomalley 'Tea']].

Adventurers who repetitively expose themselves to the substance show an extreme increase in disease resistance- though the rarity of [[Thunderjaws]] and the low shelf life of [[Thunder-Jam]] means that most never make it back to [[The City of Wyrms]] with an intact container of the substance- and even then the absolutely vile smell of the 'jam' often leads to guards confiscating it and disposing of it.

This smell is described by those not accustomed to it as "the most atrocious thing to assail their nostrils they've ever had the displeasure of experiencing" or "like someone combined the potency of smelling salts and the stench of manure." Its certainly something you need to get used to, if you dare even try.
