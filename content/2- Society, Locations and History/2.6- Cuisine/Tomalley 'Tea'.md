([[Build Togethers|30]], What Lies West?)

"No, no no no NO way am I drinking that man! Oh [[Raknarous The Bloody|Raknarous]] shield me... screw it!"

-A man having his first sip of Tomalley 'Tea'

In [[The West]], prolonged expeditions from aren't just common, their expected. Most groups return after 3 weeks on average, though some have come back long after folks thought them dead- months, even a year once. Sometimes you just get some good momentum, but sometimes you need to start rolling- and one way many, many [[Adventurers|adventurers]] start their expeditions is with a piping hot cup of Tomalley 'Tea'.

In The West all sorts of nasty bugs hide in the dunes- one such beast is the [[Ironback Reapers|Ironback Reaper]], a large scorpion that although intimidating, their numerous appearances and disappointingly weak chitin make them easy to fell in battle- the first challenge for any would be adventurer in the wastes of The West, in strength of arms and... stomach.

Fresh from the beasts carcass, an experienced adventurer will gather the scorpions liver, cut it open and spill out the scalding hot green liquid into cups- this is the 'tomalley' part of the so called 'tea'. The dense... chunky water is popular among veterans as it boosts immune systems, grants immense energy and can even allow a man to overcome his natural limits by tapping into adrenaline with enough concentration.

The 'drink' gives people the strength to go deep into the sands and survive the trek back, and has thus earned its place as a popular, if not infamous, brew of bile.
