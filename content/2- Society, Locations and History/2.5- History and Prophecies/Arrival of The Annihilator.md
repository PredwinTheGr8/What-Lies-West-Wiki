([[Build Togethers|464]], [[index|What Lies West?]])

I remember that day well, like seeing through a window. The sun was near rising, the clouds vacant from the sky. It was going to be a beautiful day, I think. Dogs were barking in the streets as they chased around laughing children, food stalls started grilling meats, music started playing from beggars on street corners. The morning life of [[The City of Wyrms]] was just starting to rouse.

Then, all was bright. The sun had risen rapidly, coming in over the [[Wyrmscale Reaches]] in moments. We all looked up, or out of our windows and saw it- a glowing ball of light, absolutely monolithic in size. It was far, far away, but even still it covered the sky with its bulk as its bottom hung below the horizon. How something could ever have hidden itself is the first mystery that had no clues to an answer.

For a while, it waited there. People came out of their homes to come and look at the massive orb of light. Some began chanting that it was the arrival of the lord and savior [[Orpheus]]. Others' survival instincts guided them away to their homes, barring doors and blotting out windows. Then, as many people gathered in the town's square- a massive, verdant eye opened in the center of the being. And, like lightning striking the earth, it loosed destruction.

Cauterizing beams of light glassed the earth- buildings crumbled away into dust, people struck by it turned to solid statues of liquid water. I remember bracing myself under my desk for the rest of the assault, I dared not take another look from my window.

With time, the event ended. I sheepishly looked back out my window to see every building around mine scorched to ash. I and I alone was an island in a sea of molten earth. Some, to this day, call me a devil tamer. They think that that thing- [[The Annihilator]] has a deal with me, that I led it here. How could I, though? I would never do such a thing! Never! Alas, I am still guilty of surviving when over three thousand did not, if that census is even correct.

According to the academics, forty to fifty percent of [[The City of Wyrms]] was levelled. That's when I first felt them- those thoughts in my head, the Sallow Shadows. Whispers. Dissonant whispers. Gods. Orpheus- whoever is listening, shelter me. Please.

But, regardless- even with the danger presented by something so apocalyptic we stayed, we rebuilt. Even the scummy [[The Conrad Family|Conrads]] pitched their coffers into the efforts. For the first time, for a fleeting three weeks, we were all united in purpose. Hells, even the [[Sons of Y'zrold]] took a blind eye to the mages using magic to reconstruct walls and fabricate buildings. But no good deed goes unpunished, and it seems that we're still waiting for our sentence.
