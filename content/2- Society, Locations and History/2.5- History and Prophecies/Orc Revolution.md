([[Build Togethers|375]],[[What Lies West]])

"The bloodiest war in history. 'Revolution', 'rebellion', the greenskins called it. No, it was slaughter- the hanging of hundreds, the slaughter of innocents, the burning of homes and hospitals. In defying their masters, the [[Orcs]] became what everyone else thought of them as- monsters."

-Skriek Whisper-Whisker, [[Scholars|scholar]]

The Orc Revolution was a two year long conflict, where the orcs, currently slaves, fought to gain their freedom from the wicked grasp of [[Iradeen Thaddeus]] and his vassals in the [[Koross|Korosian]] empire. The conflict was extremely one-sided, as the [[Orcs]] gained the favor of both the [[Ogres]] and [[Catfolk]], allowing them to devastate the frontlines with their larger cousins and send silent assassins to wreak havoc in the cities of man.

For the first year and a half, Orcish revolutionaries dominated the battlefield- pushing human and elven soldiers back mile by mile each day until their foe was forced to make a desperate choice in [[The Northern Wastes]]. The [[Humans]] and [[Elves]] both made a treaty with the [[Minotaurs]], who vowed to fight on their behalf in exchange for citizenship and gold.

From the moment the cow-folk stepped foot on the battlefield, everything changed. Plate armor is good, but not good enough to deflect musket balls and the impact of a massive great club, and the [[Ogres]] fell one after the other in the span of hours. The Minotaurs could smell a [[Catfolk]] from hundreds of feet away, and their generals were almost completely impossible to assassinate. The [[Orcs]] themselves were strong, but Minotaurs were twice their size and more heavily armed.

The tides of war flipped near-instantly, with [[The Herd of Immolesh]] crashing through their trenches and bastions like natural disasters. Within a quarter of a year, the Orcs were forced into surrender, though their story did not end with enslavement. [[Ser Mallorin Berns of Harush]], a Catfolk 'noble' petitioned to Immolesh to simply disarm the greenskins, and force Koross to free them. Immolesh, knowing the prejudice and confinement the orcs had faced personally, agreed to Mallorin's plea.
