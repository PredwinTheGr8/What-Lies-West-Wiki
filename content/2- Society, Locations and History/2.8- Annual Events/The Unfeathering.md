([[Build Togethers|332]], What Lies West?)

"My grandfather told me of the first one- the first unfeathering. It was several years after the [[Orc Revolution]] had ended, people were trying to get along, the Dwarves started to come out of their holds. Then, like every other year, like threads of silk the magics of [[The West]] flowed out in the skies, writhing, beautiful tendrils- and the beginning of [[The Calling]]. It happens every year, now, but It still amazes me just how... *new* it is to our world. Its only a century or so old, [[Elves]] live longer than that, hell so do [[Kobolds]], hah."

-Unnamed man during the 142nd Unfeathering

The Unfeathering is a yearly event where, according to scholars, 'The Magics of The West and its many [[Sites of Power]] swell up and boom forth into the skies.' This event seems to boost the aggression and reproduction rates of many of the [[Monsters|monsters]] from The West, and even generates new ones out of thin air- such as [[The Knights in Yellow]].

Most [[Adventurers|adventurers]] steer clear of the deserts do the days leading up to The Unfeathering each year, but most of them will celebrate its arrival- for the monster population increasing means more work for them, and more materials to sell to the guild. So many tools adventurers use are exclusive to The West- [[Healing Potions]] for instance can ONLY be made from the fluids found inside a    [[Scollifs]], and their population booming with the coming of The Unfeathering is nothing but good for adventurers. But of course, it is not all good.

Certain monsters will become 'supercharged' by the event- The Knights in Yellow, for instance, move much faster and behave far more aggressively than even other [[Hale Ones]]. [[Arcanosaurs]] grow larger and expand their territories thrice their original size. Some ancient creatures that slumbered for eons might awaken as well- creatures like the [[Tarrasques]] who are nearly impossible to deal with, though luckily they stay within the bounds of [[The Ossuary Reach]].

Whether or not adventurers take advantage of the boom in resources though, the day is celebrated over [[Balphora]] as a reminder for the source of their good fortune.
