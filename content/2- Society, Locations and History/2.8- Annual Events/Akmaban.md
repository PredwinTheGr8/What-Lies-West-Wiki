([[Build Togethers|325]], What Lies West?)

The Eclipse, when [[The Many Named Moon]] blacks out the sky for an hour every year. Many groups in [[Koross]] see this as a time of darkness- of evil [[Magic|magics]] when [[Monsters|monsters]] spawn in the night- others are indifferent, like the [[Lizardfolk]] who's [[The Great Swamp|swamp]] is already constantly dark thanks to the monolithic trees in the area. But one group of peoples- the [[Catfolk]] parade in their cities with revelry when The Eclipse begins, for it marks the start of **Akmaban**!

Akmaban is a celebration of the good will from [[Generous Harush]], the [[Gods|God]] of the Land, where those who worship him, namely the Catfolk, light their streets up so brightly you'd think it was day with lanterns, massive campfires, string lights made of magic crystals. But the most important part of the festival is the food- Every home, every street corner is overcome with the smell of spiced meats, roasted stews and grilled veggies. Many foods are only eaten during this time of celebration. specialty cuisine to give thanks to Harush for providing for their people.

Some are eaten outside of the festival, like [[Khob'ess]], but are elevated with more effort during the eclipse and the days after. The chaos of these festivals drives foreigners nuts, as the partying, music and cheering persists for the entire four days of the festival, though some have learned to embrace it.
