Weeks went by and she only got worse- she started pulling out hairs, eating at her nails, scratching her eyelids raw... Then, one time she never showed up for our distribution of coins. We went to her hovel and found her outside... poor girl."

### [[Visual Appearance]]-

- Three~ foot tall bush, emerald green luminescent leaves
- Crimson red roots
- Prolonged proximity will cause auditory hallucinations, often described as "trumpets."

Madman's Requiem is a hazardous plant of [[The West]] that can be found naturally within [[Sorrowed Weald|The Sorrowed Weald]], though the museum of paranormal flora also holds several specimens in their own terrarium. Madman's Requiem is a very dangerous bush plant averaging between two and five feet tall. The leaves of the bush are large, diamond- as in the card symbol- shaped, and glow a faint green color that is only perceivable in the dark. Though on that latter bit, the Sorrowed Weald is always dark. This alone doesn't particularly make Madman's Requiem easy to spot, as many other plants within [[Sorrowed Weald|The Sorrowed Weald]] also glow faintly, or even very brightly. 

The danger of a Requiem lies in its spores, which normally would influence the behavior of the regions [[Monsters]] to wander about aimlessly for several hours, instead brutally torments mortals. Scrapping open skin on the leaves of a Requiem will cause an immediate "sense of loss" in the afflicted. Survivors, those who sought immediate treatment via restoration spells, recount that the feeling was similar to losing a loved one- a solemn feeling that only persisted for a short while.

The symptoms will progress though, becoming a distant chorus of instruments, most often described as "off-tune trumpets." The music will get louder and louder, until the hallucination completely deafens the afflicted from all outside sounds. This becomes absolutely maddening after only a short while, roughly three weeks after becoming afflicted. At this point, a restoration spell will not cure the individual, instead, a twice-a-week dispel curse must be used for roughly a month, a very expensive treatment make no doubt. If symptoms progress, sleep becomes impossible, and the afflicted will begin to show signs of extreme mental degradation.

Most cases without treatment end with grim conclusions. Don't make the same mistake as others- every scrape in [[Sorrowed Weald|The Sorrowed Weald]] may be the worst torture you'll never live through. Always restore yourself after leaving the Weald, its for your own good- and for that of your loved ones.
