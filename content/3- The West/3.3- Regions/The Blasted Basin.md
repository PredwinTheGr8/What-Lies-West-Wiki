"It's a rather nice break, really. Other places are hot, some are acidic, some are festering... but the Basin? The Blasted Basin is a literal oasis in a desert. Plant life, pollinating insects, and really only one or two things that want to eat you. Even the largest of endemic beasts in the Basin, the [[Mire Hulks]], don't wanna chew on you. So take a deep breath, and lower your guard just a little, this place isn't all that bad."

-Harold Green, [[Adventurers|adventurer]]

The Blasted Basin is a [[All Regions of The West|region]] of [[The West]] characterized by being the only region with bodies of water within. Long ago, [[The Old Empire of The West]] saw fit to make many aquifers and reservoirs in the area. While all of the structures have been located and marked with paint nowadays, they still remain vast and expansive structures. The Blasted Basin is perhaps the easiest region to navigate effectively, despite the direction distorting power of The West. Near the regions 'center' was once an ancient dam, named "The Hollow Citadel" by adventurers. This dam was constantly present in the horizon while inside of the region- a gigantic structure only comparable to the even larger [[The Voltaic Monolith]].

This allows adventurers to treat The Hollow Citadel as a waypoint in the region, using its presence as a visual guide to their exact location inside of the area. There have been plans to eventually start construction of a smaller outpost within the area, as the water quantity in the region, as well as the numerous prey animals make it a good spot to set up a physical presence in The West. The Citadel's insides have been thoroughly explored- with every dead end charted and the interior being mapped out in its entirety. This has marked significant progress in understanding the strange, paranormal force that prevents traditional navigation throughout the wasteland.

Scholars believe that, with enough time and effort, similar structures may be hand made in the other Regions to allow them to be fully explored as well. Eventually triangulating the 'source' of the disturbance perhaps, and putting a permanent stop to it.

All of this seemed possible, for a long time. But, half a year ago multiple adventuring parties witnessed something truly grave- [[The Annihilator]] utterly destroyed The Hollow Citadel in its entirety. The creature has gotten more and more active as of late- with many fearing its preparing for another siege on [[The City of Wyrms]].

Adventurers have begun reports that after this event, a new species of monster was found in the area, the [[Kharahziaizi, Psycho Mantises|Kharahziaizi]]. This significantly upped the danger in the region, though it seems that even though the Psycho Mantises are good hunters, the pre-existing monsters in the region do not take kindly to their presence.

The monsters found within the Blasted Basin are often territorial, but not predatory- with only a few active and ambush predators making their homes here:

- [[Mire Hulks]], unique to the region, found inside of the brackish waterways, using their [[Mire Weed]] growths to draw in prey.
- [[Quilbeasts]], sometimes found inside of [[The Creeping Peninsula|The Creeping Peninsula]] as well, suggesting close proximity between the two regions. Found subsisting off of [[Mire Weed]].
- [[Vibrant Whimseys|Vibrant]] and [[Crimson Whimseys]], unique to the region. found fluting in and out of existence above the water and between the palms.
- [[Scollifs]], non-native but not invasive, often seen in the outskirts of the region sifting mana from the dunes.
- [[Chitterbacks]], unique to the region, found grazing on grasses in small herds.
- [[Wyverns]] of all kinds, though notably [[Charnel Wyverns]] do not go near the ruins of The Hollow Citadel.
- [[Plaguewings]], make their nests here, suggesting this is their natural habitat.
- [[Mimics]], found often inside the ancient spill gate buildings impersonating those they've consumed and walking through the palms looking for victims.
- [[Arcanosaurs]], non-native and invasive. Found digging up and eating Mire Hulk eggs- often being the end of them if the parent is around.
- [[Inverters]], found in the outskirts hoping to get the upper hand on any migrating monsters hoping for water. Some dead Inverters can be seen blighting the water, suggesting that at some point the water was lower but has returned.
- [[Sky-Rippers]], non-native but non impactful to the environment, they are rarely seen basking in the lakes and reservoirs of the region.
- [[Mithrilback Reapers]], unique to the region, often spied idly sunbathing between hunts.
- [[Aberrant Jerboa]], non-native but non impactful to the environment, they can be seen darting between bushes and quickly drinking water before returning back to [[The Crimson Expanse|The Crimson Expanse]], suggesting close proximity between the two regions.
- [[Blightwater]], unique, 'seen' infecting the water and dissolving any who drink it.
- [[Ossuary Titans]], sometimes seen wandering through the region, soaking up the moonlight and sometimes taking a pause in deeper waters to cool down.
- [[Mutants]] are not common here, though [[Ghouls]] can only be spawned from [[Blightwater]] poisoning.
