"Musical. Clanging iron pipes, slamming pumps. Its a wonder of engineering too, definitely [[Dwarves|Dwarf-Made]] no doubt about it. But it's new to us, has to be. I can't imagine a place that massive has been hiding for forty plus years without so much as a glimpse of it from [[adventurers]]. It's unsettling to me, how something so big can hide for so long."

-Harold Green, [[adventurers|adventurer]]

The Fracking Plant is a [[All Regions of The West|region]] of[[The West]], characterized as a massive swath of rocky sediment with numerous, massive machines acrost its surface. These "Frackers" as [[Dwarves|Dwarven]] engineers call them, are highly complex machines used to extract gaseous or liquid substances from great depths. The ones within this region seems to have at one point been after deep-mantle oil reserves- according to ancient dwarven documents found within some derelict buildings report that multiple of the Frackers "extractors" go down twenty to thirty miles into the earth.

These machines have persisted in an activated state for so long that whatever they were previously mining has long since run dry, though some pockets deep below contain an altogether completely new gas-based material. This bright, glowing blue cloud is known by adventurers as "[[The Haze]]". A virulent cerulean cloud that kills in seconds, mask or not. The cloud maintains a highly compact formation, creatures that enter, or rather become surrounded by the cloud are crushed under extreme pressure, instantly liquifying most creatures- adventurers included. This mass of flesh is then disintegrated, or perhaps 'digested' by the cloud, before the Haze moves on. To see more information regarding The Haze, please locate my document on the very matter.

These harvesting machines have been thoroughly examined and even reverse engineered to a degree, but without the tools to locate deep mantle resources they currently serve zero purpose. As with many of the extremely high-tech relics left behind by [[The Old Empire of The West]], mortal kind remains incapable of using any of it.

The Fracking plant has a high concentration of surface minerals and sulfuric vents as well, taking the idea of resource wealth to an extreme. Copper veins bleed into the air, poisoning it. Methane gas vents choke out mineshafts, sulfuric geysers burn off layers of skin. While there is much to be gained from access to The Fracking Plant, thus far it has never been the "first stop" of any adventurers, suggesting that like [[The Colossal Rift]] it exists further away than the other regions by an extreme margin. Still, smaller pockets of less extreme minerals can be gathered and taken back to [[The City of Wyrms]] for a hefty profit. Never get greedy though, as some of the unique [[monsters]] within The Fracking Plant's bounds do not take kindly to overexploitation of their home.

The Fracking Plant is perhaps the most hostile to life of any inhabited region. While places like [[The Swallowed City]] are dominated by one single life form- making them hostile to all other forms of it, The Fracking Plant is acidic, poisonous and has no plant life whatsoever. The sheer number of gaseous poisons is enough to kill a hundred million men thrice over. But, that same chemical imbalance is what allows extremophile monsters to be born and created here-

**[[Revenants]]**: Native- Mutants that got a whiff of [[The Haze]] and were let go by the cloud- leaving them hollow mortals with glowing blue eyes and hulking, spindly forms.
**[[Bri'talla]]**: Native- Hulking masses of flesh and stone that can be found looming over sulfuric vents to collect energy. Apex "herbivores" of the region, at adult size they are untouchable by anything else in The West- though that only comes from their home. Dwarven for "Brittle."
**[[Drymund Klaws]]**: Native, pack hunter no larger than a horse. A granite-based layer of armor protects soft innards that while edible, taste rotten. Their claws are almost entirely made of compressed carbon- also known as diamond. Dwarven for "Diamond Claw."
**[[Tanracks]]**: Native- migrating hunter, found near sulfuric vents in a hibernating state covered with the skin of other monsters. Since the regions discovery last month, Tanracks have been seen in other regions hunting large monsters for their hides- for as of yet unknown reasons. Make notes when seen, non threat to mortals smaller than a [[Minotaurs|Minotaur]].
**[[Terracyns]]**: Elemental, found wherever Geomancy is most potent. Harmless wanderers on their own, though based on their composition they may be poisonous to remain near.
[[Arcanosaurs]]: Non-Native, sometimes seen at the outer edges of the region searching for [[Aberrant Jerboa]] that may be in the vicinity.
[[Killfreaks]]: Mutant, found walking through the desolate halls of the structures above and below the earth here. Mercy kill at convenience.
**[[Cerulean Whimseys]]**: Native, seen floating above or inside of [[The Haze|Haze]] pockets. When touched they warp to resemble an adventurers preferred weapon for a brief moment before reforming.
[[Aberrant Jerboa]]: Non-Native, can be glimpsed darting rock to rock in search of [[Gallium Worms]] to eat.
[[Gallium Worms]]: Native, small invertebrates that make up the lowermost layer of the ecosystem within The Fracking Plant. Speculated to metamorphize into the [[Bri'talla]] or other large lifeforms within The Plant.
[[Exterminators]]: [[Automata]], produced locally. Towering metal monstrosities once meant for pest control, now purpose built for slaughter. Nothing save for the [[Bri'talla]] can withstand their blows and noxious poisons.
[[Glass Slugs]]: Non Native, invasive. After hitching a ride on some adventurers the slugs have begun infecting the local monster population. Many argue that this is a good thing.
The [[Bath'kah]]: Come and go to harvest ores, allegedly knowing of this place far longer than [[The City of Wyrms]] has.
