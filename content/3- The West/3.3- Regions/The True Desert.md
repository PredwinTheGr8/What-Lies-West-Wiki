"This is what you think of when you imagine [[The West]]. Rolling sand dunes, towering sandstone pillars every thousand feet or so, great shaking dunes with the breath of [[Inverters]]. Its beautiful I s'pose, in its own way. A nice contrast to the abstract lunacy of everywhere else in the desert."

-Markus Krauler, [[Adventurers|adventurer]]

The True Desert is a [[All Regions of The West|region]] of [[The West]] characterized by rolling sand dunes, small ruins peaking from its depths, and the occasional storm raging acrost its surface. From Haboobs to twisters, the True Desert is nearly constantly assailed by harsh weather during the apex of each day- noon. These regular storms offer a necessary, though still annoying, break from the immense heat of The Tyrant Sun. With constant storms, the dunes of The West are constantly disturbed and sometimes turned inside out when particularly powerful twisters and tornados roll through- meaning that many submerged points of interest come and go as the silicate is moved over and over again.

The Creatures within the True Desert constantly keep themselves submerged during the day- other than the obvious outliers such as [[True Wyverns]] and other large [[Monsters]] like [[Ossuary Titans]]. This constant burrowing behavior is the most common biological trait among The West, being shared between numerous species, from predators to prey. The largest example of these are the [[Inverters]], who's adult forms are speculated to reach upwards of 60 tons. The smallest of which- at adult form at least because inverter youth would also take this spot- are Jerboa, curious rat-like creatures with comedically long legs.

While there isn't much to look at within the True Desert- lacking many of the breathtaking vistas that can be found elsewhere- the region itself evokes a feeling of nostalgia and safety for veteran adventurers. Without a doubt the True Desert is the safest region- though finding food and water is still difficult. This means that with repetitive expeditions, [[Adventurers]] learn to eventually 'trust' the True Desert for its sheer predictability and largely vacant sands. Despite Inverters being such a terrifying thought to encounter- their population, and that of any other monster within the desert, is incredibly sparse. For the most part, this means that the desert is the safest region, though the most comfortable is certainly [[The Blasted Basin|The Blasted Basin]].

"Vorren Vash Ishven" the [[Elves]] say- "Dead dry (and) artistic"- and perhaps it is. It is simple in its beauty, though still it remains a vista worth sitting down and simply... *seeing* for oneself. I haven't been to the other [[All Regions of The West|regions]] of [[The West]], but I see the True Desert every day when I look out my kitchen window. I've walked in its sands when... when that ***thing*** spoke to me. The sands may be hot, the winds sharp, and the sun unforgiving- but with [[Orpheus]]' protection there is naught to fear. Even though the Tyrant Sun is heinous, his own light will always protect. *Always*. You mustn't have fear for things out of your control. You mustn't.

The creatures. Monsters rather I suppose. The ones here are over adapted to survive the immense heat of the sun during the day- though not as hot as the glassed surface of [[The Devastated Mesas|The Devastated Mesas]]. Though, as a transient region between most if not all others, large amounts of migratory monsters can be found here. I will only go into the most infamous and famous of such creatures to save on ink and paper.

[[Ironback Reapers]]- Native, waits in ambush feet below the surface with periscopic eyes that take brief but frequent scans of the surface.
[[Scollifs]]- Native, though found in other regions that can support them like [[The Blasted Basin|The Blasted Basin]]. Spied at night time glowing under moonlight as they siphon [[Pyromancy|pyromancy]] from the dunes below.
[[Dunewakers]]- Native, though invasive in other soft-soil regions such as [[The Blasted Basin|The Blasted Basin]] and [[The Creeping Peninsula]]. Adventurers try their best to hide when one rolls by- carried by the winds and sand they awaken to serve them.
[[Inverters]]- Undetermined, though the Desert is the most suitable region for them to live inside of. Never seen until the last moment- when someone springs the trap and the pit is revealed.
[[Shamblers]]- Native, seen amassing outside [[The City of Wyrms]] preparing for war. Kill on sight.
[[Skrin]]- Native, Heard howling in the night as they crawl out from their holes to hunt their fellow man. [[Ratfolk|Ratfolk's 'M'raaskarask']] for 'Burned'.
[[Boozants]]- Native, found in large mounds or in the pouches of alcoholic adventurers. Tastes of spiked apple cider.
[[Gnolls]]- Roamer, often found dead from dehydration as they attempt to migrate to [[The Ossuary Reach|The Ossuary Reach]] form wherever they were [[Mutants|mutated]].
[[Death Knights]]- Rare Roamer- Only found within the ruins beneath the sands by those unfortunate enough to be stuck with such a monster.
[[Abominations]]- Rare Roamer- Only found within the ruins beneath the sands by those unfortunate enough to be stuck with such a monster. Oh, I seem to be repeating myself. Rather, they are a bigger problem than the above Death Knight. Coffee time.
[[Wyverns]]- of the [[Plaguewings|Plaguewing]] and [[True Wyverns|True]] categories. Roamers often seen but luckily not engaged by adventurers- not the lucky ones at least.
[[Chrozhran Destroyers]]- Exceedingly rare Roamer, seen far far away in the distance as their smoky columns of fire trail behind them. Their trail of devastation can often be followed to either [[The Devastated Mesas|The Devastated Mesas]], their natural habitat- or back to where they previously were. The direct path the Destroyers take means that less rations are spent during travel than otherwise.
[[Arcanosaurs]]- Roamer, invasive. Seen stalking adventurer parties solo or in groups of up to three. Posses a threat to the Scollifs' population, cull at convenience.
[[Beasts of Nightmares]]- Roamer, found when adventurers wake to find one sleeping on their chest. Named by Markus Krauler, who thought it was ironic and funny.
[[Ossuary Titans]]- Roamer, seen silhouetting themselves against the sun at the twilight hours of the day. Their titanic forms always face further west when idle, or so it is said.
[[Warp Beasts]], [[Void Bisons]] and [[Bath'kah]]- Rarely seen in convoys headed to unknown ancestral locations. Adventurers who ask too many questions end up in a sack for the Bath'kah's own tribute to something else.
[[Immolators]]- Spawn of [[The Annihilator]]. Scorches sand into glass beneath their footsteps and burns any wildlife to ash on sight. Absolute kill order under authority of Adventurer's Guild director [[Ser Mallorin Berns of Harush]].
[[Mimics]]- Roamer, found only in dungeons below the sands. Heard crying out for help with their stolen voices. Cannot understand languages other than [[Koross|Korosian]], use non-standard speech to confuse and it force them out of their act.
[[The Things in The Dark, Null Figures|The Things in The Dark]]- Roamer. Pray one does not find you entertaining.
[[Liches]]- 'Roamer' is perhaps the wrong word, but they can be found anywhere in [[The West]]. Their lairs are hidden and nearly impossible to locate.
[[The Annihilator]]- Seen most commonly here. Perhaps a reason to keep visits brief.
