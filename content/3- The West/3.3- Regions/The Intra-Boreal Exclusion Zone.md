"The Artist looks down to her connoisseurs. She asks of them to walk away from The Song of The Eye's greatest musician, and plug their ears forever to it's writ. The man in suit and tie would work art upon the Artist's connoisseurs, art reviled by all."

-Maribel, the [[Seamsters|Seamstress]] that guards the gate-ward into The Ibeks.

The 'Intra-Boreal' Exclusion Zone is the rarest and most dangerous [[All Regions of The West|region]] of [[The West]]. It is a brutal, sleet-ridden hell of cold winds and home to the calculating foes of life. No [[Monsters|monsters]] make their home here, only the truest, darkest foes of mortal kind can live in this place- The Spawn- soldier of [[The Four]]. The "Ibeks" as [[adventurers]] simply call it, is not an ecosystem- not any more at least. It is an ancient and long lost battlefield where [[The Old Empire of The West]] lost a gruesome war against the forces of doom itself, and the enemy still plots within today.

'Maribel', a particularly massive [[Seamsters|Seamstress]], stands guard outside a massive shimmering barrier of [[Archeoscript]] that largely keeps The Spawn from ever leaving The Zone. During my one dialogue with her, one I barely understood thanks to The Seamstress' manner of speaking, she illuded to 'The King.' A figure only recorded by [[Seamsters]] and not in any texts or scripture, this King 'smote down and delivered prison-hood onto The Eye.' It is attributed that when Seamsters speak of 'The Eye' they refer to [[The Annihilator]] itself, and such a sentence suggests The Annihilator should be imprisoned.

But clearly it is not. It attacked [[The City of Wyrms]] and has kidnapped me. It stalks [[adventurers]] and creates the [[Immolators]] to do its bidding of destruction. The only thing barring it from destroying all of [[Maernos]] is [[Wyrmscale Reaches]], which somehow stop it from venturing East to [[Balphora]] and then [[Koross]]. Either Maribel is senile, or in this case 'The Eye' is in fact not referring to [[The Annihilator]].

If Maribel grants one entry to The Zone they will immediately begin to freeze. A hostile, arcane cold is omnipresent in the region- a magic cold coming from a large revolving tower in the places center. This tower produces a light that creates a massive and volatile storm that causes immense damage to both the body and the soul. I am of the belief that this light tower's magic isn't even magic, but [[Eldritch Energy]]. The cold disobeys the law of magic, even arcane flame cannot persist here and spells that ward against the cold do nothing. Every time you enter this place you are on a timer before you go unconscious from hypothermia and subsequently die when the frostbite eats away your soul, or one of the many demons of shadow matter finds you defenseless.

The terrain of The Zone is largely comprised of jagged ice spires and winding cave systems. There are massive geodes that leak blood-like liquid, liquid that singes the skin on contact. Harold told me this liquid can be ingested- painfully- to warm back up the body. But the pain is not worth the reward of staying longer in this hell. When I asked what kind of idiot would drink the blood-like water seeping from the earth, he pointed at Girtz. Idiot. No I don't care if he sees this because both he can't read and he deserves it. Fool.

The Spawn of The Zone are particularly strange though. While yes they are each one of them evil entities it cannot be said that all are outright hostile to invaders. I often saw wisps of black smoke hiding behind rocks when we entered a cave, and felt curious eyes peering at me every time I turned around. Harold and his party- one of only two groups Maribel ever lets inside- call these transparent entities 'peepers.' He says Mradden Who Breaks Gods' band call them '[[Victims]]' and for some reason I find that I like that name better, as disturbing as it is.

Inside the caves one can find numerous lost pieces of armor and weapons. Traila Who Wanders West says this is the only reason to ever come here other than searching for answers. These items can be sold for a pretty copper to collectors and smiths, as the items are imbued with the rare and poorly understood Abyssium metal. Some believe this metal might be the so-called 'fifth element' of [[Brygkanite]]. Harold rebutted that there are also ore pockets to mine, and the red liquid may eventually hold alchemical value once further investigated.

According to Harold, the liquid is in fact called '[[Godsblood]]' and is easy enough to collect, only taking a mason jar really. The problem is keeping it stable as it readily turns into a gas at the same temperature as water thaws from ice and bursts into flames around the temperature of [[The True Desert]]'s days. As of yet it is impractical to take it home with them. Rather impossible too given that no expeditions first stop is ever The Zone which suggests that it lies far beyond the speculated 'inner layer' of regions. Using the [[How Far West|Makkar]] method, it seems that The Zone must be at least two to three hundred miles out from The City of Wyrms. Roughly two weeks of on foot travel uninterrupted. It would take a long time moving through The True Desert to reach home, more than enough time for the Godsblood to immolate any who carry it.

As for combat in The Zone, it was fairly regular. Most of our time was spent in the caves to avoid the harsh blast of frost coming from the light tower in The Zone's center. Lesser and greater spawns constantly poured their way towards us in regular waves. The most common foes present being [[Shamblers]] and [[Rejects]], with the latter of the two being a particularly hardy foe. Rejects, with full plate armor comprised of Abyssium, cannot be grappled or pinned down with mortal hands, as the metal reacts with chaos to the flesh of the living. Rejects are surrounded by a dark smoke caused by their aberrant bodies raising the local temperature- despite the effects of The Ibeks' cold.

Girtz was wounded numerous times by even a lone Reject, and seldom appearances from [[Perfections]] meant Girtz and the other vanguard members were regularly cut, stabbed and left to bleed out until Ms. Harrow could use her magic. Speaking of her, that cloak the Seamstress Junibell had made her all that time ago gave her immunity to the malevolent cold air. Lucky her I suppose. But our brief excursion into The Zone was not fruitless despite the bloodshed. We procured numerous valuable pieces of Abyssium in the form of Reject armor and weapons, as well as used a cold spell, courtesy of myself, to finally bring home some [[Godsblood]] which [[Von Conrad, Seamus VI|Seamus]] was quick to purchase.

Whatever is waiting at the base of the light tower nobody can be sure. I for one do not care to find out, and I don't think I'll ever be going back to The Exclusion Zone for at least a hundred years... if we all have that long left.

The enemies of man present in The Intra-Boreal Exclusion Zone are as follows;

- [[The Things in The Dark, Null Figures]]: While non-combatants their presence can distract adventurers or even kill them outright if eye contact is made.
- [[Victims]]: Lost souls, or laughing tormentors? Who can say, but so far none have done so much as look at any adventurers wrong. That's not to say you should ever think of any spawn as a non-threat, only dead men do.
- [[Monoliths]]: Cold shadows of malice that inexorably pursue the living hoping to hijack their bodies. Throw salt at them like one may spray water at a disobedient cat.
- [[Nuumerruth]]: I did not encounter one, but Harold describes them as the largest Spawn in Maernos, barring [[The Annihilator]] itself. Elven for, "of one hundred."
- [[The Knights in Yellow]]: Technically not yellow, just the same spirits inhabiting different suits. Their new Abyssium weapons make them far more dangerous.
- [[Shamblers]]: The spindly, white-armored scoundrels that besiege [[The City of Wyrms]] on the regular. It seems they are made both here in The Ibeks and perhaps inside of [[The Sorrowed Weald]] as well.
- [[Rejects]]: Whereas [[Shamblers]] look organic, Rejects are not in any way thus. They are trained killers, perhaps made from veteran Shamblers to be their true commanders. They are without a doubt only made here in The Ibeks.
- [[The Obelisk]]: Will eventually possess someone who remains here, dragging it away form any civilians that have peaked the creatures interest in [[The City of Wyrms]]. Luckily Girtz was the one possessed and he already knew procedure.
- [[Mindflayers]]: Can be found clinging to cave ceilings waiting to drop down on an unsuspecting fool. Those who are let in are seldom that luckily, and never have we forgotten to look up every ten seconds or so.
- [[Gorepiles]]: I have not the words to describe these monstrosities. Perhaps they are made from the discarded parts of [[Shamblers]]? I care not to theorize though, iron will set them on fire, so use that.

Notably, not one of the parties has reported either [[The Annihilator]] or [[Immolators]] appearing here- reinforcing that some kind of faction separation might in fact be at play. Lucky us either way.
