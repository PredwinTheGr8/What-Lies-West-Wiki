''It's haunting. There, is that what you want? Its a graveyard of ancient monsters older than humanity, older than [[Koross]] itself maybe! Its not a good thought, walking through the ribcages of giants and thinking how they could have died, what could have killed them, if its still around... Some will say the place is beautiful, I suppose their right in a macabre way, but me? I revile the place- The Ossuary Reach is not for mortal souls."

-Markus Krauler, [[Adventurers|adventurer]]

A [[All Regions of The West|region]] of [[The West]]. As with all other regions within The West, it is impossible to plan a trip to The Ossuary Reach, as the confounding [[Magic|magics]] of the desert alter a persons perception of time and space. To find the Ossuary Reach, one must be simply lucky- or unlucky if one hasn't prepared for it.

The Ossuary Reach earns its name from the massive amount of bones in the region- from towering spinal cords piercing the sands to barely visible adder ribs making up the very ground. The [[Monsters]] that make their home here are particularly skilled scavengers- tapping into the ancient marrow of the colossal carcasses to feed, or hunting the creatures whom idly latch onto said bones. Most of the Monsters from The Ossuary Reach are unique to the region- being found nowhere else both making them a 'fun' sight for cataloguers and a prized prey for adventurers. Most of the innards and hides of creatures from The Ossuary Reach go for a high price, being prized as components for elixirs and aphrodisiacs.

The Ossuary Reach is also home to many, many environmental hazards. The giant carcasses beneath the bone-laced dunes of The Reach make for extremely hair-wired pitfalls. Massive underground chambers barely held aloft until some poor sod comes and walks above it- drowning in the white sands as the sinkhole sucks them in. The corpses above the sands aren't safe either- other than the creatures inhabiting them they are often brittle, barely standing features of the landscape. A stray breeze or a loud noise can cause dangling ribs to fall from their places, impaling those below or just crushing them if the beast was large enough.

The nature of the desolate corpses, and what might have caused their end it only speculated without evidence. Some say it was the Wyrms who built [[The City of Wyrms|the city]] of their namesake. Others recant it was the gods who smote such abominations. But those who look upon their carcasses and know of beasts and wild things know- they know whatever killed the giants was a beastly thing- a ravenous monster that knew nothing but hunger... and perhaps it still does.
