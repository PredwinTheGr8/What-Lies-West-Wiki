[[The West]] is massive, its regions and biomes so vast that calling the entire place a 'desert' is an insult.  There are unique [[Monsters]], dangers and treasures within each region, and though it is impossible to plan for one, it is wise to contemplate all.

#### [[True Desert]]-

The 'boring' part of [[The West]], The True Desert is visually the same as any other desert one might stumble across in the world. The [[Monsters]] within this region are perhaps the easiest to combat, though it isn't a certainty with many monsters of other regions sometimes making their way here. Bring a parasol.
#### [[Blasted Basin]]- (**1**)

An ancient, desiccated series of aquifers, reservoirs and dams that have long since been abandoned and subject to the intense sandstorms of [[The West]]. The Blasted Basin, despite its constant assailment at the hands of the weather, is home to the most surface water of any region- allowing life to flourish, and for many territorial [[Monsters]] to make their home here. Watch your footing and double check your drinking water.
#### [[Sorrowed Weald]]- (**2&3**)

A dense, blue-hued rainforest dominated by violent plant life. The Sorrowed Weald is the most dense with life of any of the regions within [[The West]], and also, despite the relative inability to measure such things in The West, the largest of the regions. The Sorrowed Weald is the most commonly visited of the regions, suggesting that the area it covers must be much larger proportionally to the other areas across the dunes, if the rumors that its only up to chance where you go are to be believed at least. Bring a machete.
#### [[Void Dunes]]- (**4**)

A place of darkness, The Void Dunes is a cool break from the scorching heat of nearly everywhere else within [[The West]]. This place is home to the only... 'diplomatic' race of The West, the [[Bath'kah]]. The [[Monsters]] here are brutish hunters that will act as soon as they detect weakness. Bring a shield and a keg of ale... for 'tribute'.
#### [[Ossuary Reach]]- (**5**)

Covered in the carcasses of giant beasts, The Ossuary Reach is home to [[Monsters]] adapted to scavenge from the still-dripping rotted corpses. Though most are but bone, the marrow within is still enough to fuel the many monsters that call the region home- though any marrow will do, its best to keep your distance. Watch your step and check for hitchhikers.
#### [[Forlorn Graveyard]]- (**6**)

A sunken mega-structure swallowed by the sands, only its tallest towers exposed above the dunes of blue sand. Beneath the desert above, a flourishing ecosystem of bio-artificial [[Monsters]], ancient gardens and violent automata battles for dominance. Great arcane lights, drawing power from [[Voltaic Monolith|The Voltaic Monolith]], light the dark corridors and endless hallways. Don't get lost.
#### [[Creeping Peninsula]]- (**7**)

A mundane, foggy savannah biome that hosts many freakish monsters. Despite the lack of powerful magic in the region, the creatures within remain heavily mutated by it. The beasts here are desperate for food, whether it be to satiate themselves or to prevent corruption from taking them.
#### [[Swallowed City]]- (**8**)

A city- comparable to the size of [[The City of Wyrms]], that has been swallowed by [[Corpse-Thicket]]. The large, tumorous mass of flesh-vines has anchored down, building a self-sustaining ecosystem that the parasite feeds off of. Those who venture here rarely return as themselves. Be polite, and don't step on the grass.
#### [[Devastated Mesas]]- (**9**)

A region dominated by monolithic clay mesas, lorded over by some of the largest [[Monsters]] ever seen. The Devastated Mesas' topsides have been blasted by magics and transformed into glass. During the day, temperatures get hot enough to melt a boot- its best to stay in the canyons and passages between the rises.
#### [[Voltaic Monolith]]- (**10**)

A region surrounding a great mountain- a [[Sites of Power|Site of Power]]- that spouts forth countless thousands of lightning bolts a minute. Strangely, despite the wanton destruction caused by the Site of Power, trees and other plants flourish under the rainless storm clouds, sheltering them just enough from the scorching sun to spread their seeds and sow new ground. Massive artificial spires of metal rise from the ground and redirect the voltaic energy, bringing it by wires to [[Forlorn Graveyard|The Forlorn Graveyard]], the only known path between two regions that actually functions as one. Bring a hair brush.
#### [[The Warbling]]- (**11**)

A molten hell of lava geysers and [[Lava Elementals]] that spews forth so much [[Magic|mana]] from its [[Sites of Power]] that to walk in this place is to forsake your flesh. The outer corners of the region are safe, though sweaty, to venture through. The deepest recesses, though, are hot enough to burn demons... or so it is said. Aloe vera might prove useful.
#### [[Shelled Trenches]]- (**12**)

Ancient ravines that layer upon one another in repetitive patterns, like colossal versions of the trenches used during the [[Orc Revolution]]. The Shelled Trenches seem to resemble some sort of bizarre coral reef, with many of the monsters within the region floating as if in water, despite the apparent lack of it. Keep your head down.

#### [[Shattered Divide]]- (**13**)

A broken landscape held aloft by arcane currents of air. Massive metal bridges barely keep the islands connected, straining against thousands of tons of force with each gust of wind. Sometimes, an island gets loose and floats aimlessly to the east before inevitably crashing against the diamond coated [[Wyrmscale Reaches]].
#### [[Crimson Expanse]]- (**14**)

A place heavily corrupted by [[Magic|magic]], The Crimson Expanse is a red-hued, desolate wasteland full of bleeding trees, concertina-like bushes that can shred flesh, and volatile, explosive plants. The [[Monsters]] within this hell are luckily the smallest on average between regions, but that's only so they can hide from the mobile roots that endlessly hunt for nutrients. Brace for goat's heads full of the most painful toxins imaginable.
#### [[Godless Maw]]- (**15**)

A great rift of stone, The 'Godless Maw' is a place that emits arcane dread and terror. It is rare to come across this place, but those who descend within its gaping mouth come back mad- but wealthy. Luck is on your side, but given the order of discovery, with this region being the 13th, it may be best to not put too much hope into such a thing. If one is to dare venture within, there is no documentation for the horrors that lurk inside. A torch may come in handy.
#### [[Lying Veld]]- (**16**)

A vast meadow of flowers and trees, at least when the curse effects you- The Decimated Veld is a cursed prairie, with ancient, nightmarish monsters walking in plan sight so long as the illusion takes hold on you. Was that a bag of coins you picked up, or is it the egg sac of some million-eyed spider? Second guess everything.
#### [[Colossal Rift]]- (**18**)

An [[Magic|arcane]] place dominated by a great eye in the sky- The Colossal Rift is believed to be the furthest West of all regions, at least so far. The Colossal Rift, the namesake of the region, is somewhat present no matter where you are in The West, but always as a faint outline in the distance that changes position every time you look away. But here, in this region, it is blatant, opaque and always staring directly at you- sicking its [[Monsters|monstrous]] minions at you to rend your flesh, or so it is speculated.
