"It gives of a sense of sadness, the Weald, I mean. Sure there's some greenery mixed in with the trees, but its mostly just a blue, dreary place. I've never been to [[The Great Swamp]] but I wouldn't imagine it's anything like the Weald. The forest is just so dreadful, so empty... until it isn't."

-Amelia Harrow, [[Adventurers|adventurer]]

The Sorrowed Weald is a [[All Regions of The West|region]] of [[The West]], dominated by colossal, blue trees and inhabited by [[Monsters]] reliant on their ambush tactics. The Sorrowed Weald is constantly damp, with water circulating from the water-logged soil up to the tops of the great [[Weeping Titans]], the aforementioned giant trees, and pooling back down in droplets to the muddy earth below. Dense vines cart off massive segments of the region, massive, gnarled roots make for natural fortifications that hide horrors of the forest and the very earth itself hides flesh-eating magics.

The [[Weeping Titans]] are the crux of this ecosystem- their massive canopies and sprawling roots sheltering the land below from the angry sun above. Their leaves constantly drip with excess water- creating a constant rainfall that cleans out the soil and drowns any small insects that leave their hovels. The region is densely guarded by its inhabitants, who seem attracted to the sounds of logging efforts, or the coughing fits of adventurers as they choke on the glowing spores in the area.

Indeed the [[Monsters]] of The Sorrowed Weald are perhaps the best at catching [[Adventurers|adventurers]] off-guard, with their skills of camouflage being almost entirely unmatched. When venturing in The Sorrowed Weald, a careful step and a watchful eye serve a man well. A group of Dwarves, who also discovered and named the region, have made quite the profit selling out survival guides for just this region alone- and though it comes at a hefty price, its well worth it.
