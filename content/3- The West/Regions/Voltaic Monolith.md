The Voltaic Monolith is the name given to the [[All Regions of The West|region]] of [[The West]] surrounding the artifact of the same name. This pillar of stone- a [[Sites of Power|Site of Power]]- sits at an estimated mile to mile and a half tall, roughly seven thousand feet tall at the most.  This is the largest standing structure within The West, though still obviously shorter than the wall of diamonds known as [[Wyrmscale Reaches|The Wyrmscale Reaches]]. The Voltaic Monolith earns its name from its constant attraction of lightning from storm clouds that perpetually sit above its tip. This energy is sent via large metal cables to [[Forlorn Graveyard|The Forlorn Graveyard]], being the only 'path' in the west that can be reliably followed between two regions.

The Voltaic Monolith's surrounding area is constantly plagued by random lightning strikes as excess energy makes its way from the central pillar to supplementary lightning rods that soak up what would have been wasted energy. Despite this, some bolts still miss the auxiliary poles, and strike the dirt floor of the area, creating large pillars of molten glass as the mass of earth flings up and cools rapidly. These chunks of glass are well tempered, being more than capable of shattering a solid steel blade if enough force is applied. With their sheer presence acrost the region, and the rarity of these missed strikes- being roughly once per week according to reports- it would seem that the Voltaic Monolith might in fact be the oldest standing structure within [[The West]], possibly even older than the only other contender, [[Forlorn Graveyard|The Forlorn Graveyard]].

With the constant overcast skies, the Tyrannical Sun's wrath cannot pierce the clouds often. This has allowed limited flora to occur and evolve under the rule of the Monolith. These plants all are completely reliant on the [[Electromancy|electromancy]] produced by the region- as they are without sun to generate standard energy. The plant life here is largely without leaves, instead relying on the Vermaus [[Magic Patterns|Pattern]] to attract the electromancy in the area. For those unfamiliar with magic patterns, they are naturally occurring formations of rock that occur near certain Sites Of Power, these patterns increase the attraction of their particular elemental force. The Vermaus Pattern, named by its discoverer Rannick Vermaus, is a series of overlapping square shapes, rotating forty five degrees in smaller and smaller series of rows.

The plants in the area attempt to gain a large surface area with numerous crevasses and giant flat 'wings' like those of a Mammoth's ears. While these plants could theoretically sustain themselves forever in a perfect environment, the fluctuation of the potency of magic during full and new moons means that some plants may grow too large and starve after repetitively smaller moon phases. These floral deaths revitalize the soil and allow what little water content remains to be sustained despite the harsh environment, allowing more plants to sprout even in such hostile earth. If not for the Voltaic Monoliths presence, this patch of desert would still be nothing more than sand and dead cacti.

The only monsters, if you can even call them that, that are found within this region are small insects that feed off of the plant life in the area, or from the other herbivorous insects. Nothing here is larger than a closed fist, and nothing here poses a threat to adventurers in the slightest save for a rash if bitten. The plant life here is still great for alchemical reagents, meaning that its never truly a wasted trip.
