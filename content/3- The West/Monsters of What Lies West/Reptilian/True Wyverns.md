True Wyverns are [[Legendary Monsters]] of [[The West]] that can be found fouling the skies of any region- no matter how corrupted by [[Magic]], blighted by [[Eldritch Energy]], or completely mundane. These are the origin for the name 'wyvern', all others are pretenders- false-wyverns.

True Wyverns are visually like no other living creature- they are gargantuan beasts of myth, born from the blood of the Wyrms. Some call them lizards or reptiles- others beasts, but the wise call them horrors. They are unbeaten, they are inexorable, they are inevitable. Wyverns are ninety feet long- sporting a pitch black scale coloration acrost their bodies. On their backs, a series of natural chimneys spew forth smog and soot into the air.

During youth, a True Wyvern is a weak foe- unable to spit fire nor use their brawn as they begin life as lone hunters no bigger than an owl. As they grow, however, True Wyverns will generally remain close to [[The Warbling]], where they bathe in the oceans of lava to begin accumulating their own mobile [[Sites of Power|Site of Power]]- their heart. A True Wyvern's heart is shielded with primal heliomancy, allowing it to withstand temperatures powerful enough to melt steel. The blood of a True Wyvern inherits this heat- being spewed out by the wyvern as a scorching liquid that lights clothes and tinder aflame.

The chimneys on a True Wyvern's back release the byproducts of their own cooking flesh- burnt muscle tissue, immolated scales, and desiccated organs. These beasts possess incredible natural regeneration which allows them to cook their bodies so haphazardly.

The immense arcane power of a Wyvern also creates several environment altering spells around them. The sky within a two mile radius of a True Wyvern will begin taking on a red hue as their mobile site of power corrupts the air around them. The stories of their terror have also empowered them- with an aura of arcane dread following them throughout their travels. Perhaps acting as a gift from [[Orpheus]] himself to warn his children to the danger that draws near.

Their blood, as mentioned before, is as hot as white-flame. Melting iron and setting forests ablaze in seconds. Getting caught in a torrent of the burned ichor is an almost certain death- skin will slough from bone, eyes will melt in their sockets, and soul will beg for mercy- being lost forever in the arcane fire. This is not exaggeration; a person who dies at the might of the fire blood cannot be resurrected- their death is permanent, unless a spell exists to defy the law of reality.

Combat against a True Wyvern, if escape is not possible, is a long, and difficult travail. They must first be knocked out of the sky- which can be done with either a well placed shot to the wing joint, or by immense arcane firepower. Then, one must take great care to avoid the face of the beast- as its bites and blood sprays are enough to instantly slay any lineage. Their scales are incredibly resilient, with only the most enchanted of weapons being capable of piercing their natural plate mail. Finally, even grievous injuries can be recovered from if cryomancy is not used to seal the wound- a spellcaster or an artifact that creates ice is absolutely necessary to defeat a True Wyvern, else it recover from almost any blow.

True Wyverns, as adults, are at the utter peak of the food chain. Nothing, not even [[Ossuary Titans]] are safe from their predation, though the latter is the most capable of defending themselves if a True Wyvern makes an attempt. In their youth, however, True Wyverns are easy prey for even adventurers- as their scales have not hardened nor their blood spray heated to extremes. True Wyverns have heavily declined in population as their scales fetch for a high price, and adventurers are constantly on the hunt for their lone young.
