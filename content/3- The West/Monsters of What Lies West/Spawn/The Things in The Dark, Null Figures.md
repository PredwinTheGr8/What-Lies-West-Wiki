### [[Monster Callsigns]]-

- Visible presence, no other means have been found.

'The Things in The Dark', as named by a dead man, are a new form of [[Eldritch Energy|eldritch]] [[Monsters|monster]] of [[The West]] that appeared two years ago after [[The Unfeathering]]. The Things in The Dark, or as they are more often called, Null Figures, are large, pitch black humanoid figures with no visible eyes or orifices. They silently wait in the corners of ones vision, or peeking around corners, perhaps under a tree a mile or so away. They posses absolute size manipulation of their forms- growing or shrinking as they see fit, though most often they prefer to use their size to intimidate their 'obsession'.

A Null Figure's 'obsession' is the person who first caught a glimpse of them, most often a party's vanguard. The obsession will be the most likely to see additional manifestations of The Thing in The Dark- and is therefor in far more danger than other members of their party.

Looking vaguely in the direction of a Null Figure is cause for nosebleeds, tremors, eye-bleeding, nausea and fainting spells. Direct eye contact with the visible form of a Null Figure, though, is to suffer an instant death, at least in most cases. The physical form of one of The Tings in The Dark is completely aberrant: meaning that it is completely comprised of [[Eldritch Energy]]. Looking at their body, whether it be the feet, the torso or the face, will cause a persons head to... pop. 

Of all encounters with Null Figures, two out of the thirty three have resulted in instantaneous death. The only survivors felt as if their heads were going to explode, but instead went unconscious and dreamt of an 'impossible castle.' Both survivors have never had contact with one another, and both described the same dream, lending credence to their claims. The two survivors, to this day- three and six months later each- claim they see 'extra things' compared to their companions. Lurching figures in doorways, colossal insects with a million limbs on the sides of buildings, ancient soldiers with holes for faces following towns guard. Scholars believe they may be seeing into another world that mirrors our own... only eldritch power rules instead of magic.

Whatever the case, combat against a Null Figure is currently impossible as they are immune to all known armaments- silver, adamantine, mithril, even eldritch spells. The only escape from one of these aberrations is to leave the ruin you found it in, and pray it doesn't follow you home.

Null Figures have no place in an ecosystem, only interacting with adventurers and their own animal companions.
