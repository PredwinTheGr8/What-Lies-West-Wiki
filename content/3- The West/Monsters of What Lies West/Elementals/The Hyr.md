"Walk twixt bramble and bough without guidance. Only when you trust the woods the most, or when you fear it more than anything else. That is when you would meet them- The Hyr."

-Rz'dahl Who Writes All, [[Recorders|recorder]]

The Hyr are enigmatic entities that are said to reside within large, uninhabited forests such as [[The Great Swamp|Moulugg]]'s southern edges in [[Koross]] and the ever ominous [[The Blackwood|Blackwood]] of [[Balphora]]'s north. There are many folktales around The Hyr, ranging from happy fairy tales to bitter sagas of heroes trying in vain to conquer forests- only to end up hanging from trees with their innards splayed like ribbons at a carnival.

Stories- the only real evidence of their existence- describe The Hyr as towering oaken creatures that walk without sound. Likening to an [[Ents|Ent]], the Hyr are said to hide amongst trees and wait for interlopers to draw near, before silently reaching down and plucking them like a crop from the ground.

The Hyr are known in lore to prey on wandering children particularly. It is said that the entities prefer the meat of innocence, and perhaps may take interest in the flesh of virgins for that reason too. The Hyr have many stories surrounding children seeing another youth running into the forest, ushering them to follow behind. The child is lured into a meadow in which a Hyr waits to devour them. This fae-like behavior has led to many cultures seeing The Hyr as nothing short of evil creatures.

In a handful of stories, a person survives an encounter with a Hyr by striking a deal with the entity, offering the monster their name in exchange for their life. A Hyr will gladly take such a transaction, and will use their bound servant to lure in even more victims. According to legend, you can identify a person controlled by a Hyr by watching their behavior closely- they will allegedly avoid stepping on any plant life whenever possible, and will act violently if any around them trample any flowers or other such foliage.

Ever the arrival to Balphora, the world seems to have dulled down on the folktales and fae stories, with The Hyr being a long lost memory nowadays. Folk are too concerned with The West to be making up monsters on their own.
