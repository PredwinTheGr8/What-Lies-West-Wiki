Jeweled Fish are Aeronautical Osteichthyes- commonly referred to as flying fish- from [[The West]], found within [[Shelled Trenches|The Shelled Trenches]], [[True Desert]], [[Shattered Divide]] and rarely within [[Blasted Basin|The Basin]]. Jeweled fish males range between four to seven inches long at adulthood and females between ten to fifteen inches long. Males are most commonly a blue-green color, whilst females take on a crimson hue. Males possess a slightly higher sail-to-body ratio than females, females possess large bony protrusions on their heads used to concuss attackers or prey.

Jeweled fish are a constantly seen, but rarely interacted with '[[Monsters|monster]]' of The West. Though that word- monster- simply means a creature of the west mutated by magics, I feel as if it loses meaning when speaking of creatures that for the most part could not harm a mortal even if they wanted to. Regardless, massive schools of Jeweled fish can be seen flying about their territories, often consisting of well over a hundred members in areas with plenty of food. The males, rarer of the two sexes, are kept near the middle of their schools, while the females keep to the edges. A school of Jeweled Fish is a fairly common sight in areas they appear, often spied blotting out the landscape in the distance, or perhaps flying above out of the reach of any firearms or magics.

Jeweled Fish only disperse or lower their altitude when they feed- with their diet consisting of largely sessile creatures such as Infantile [[Inverters]], though with time its possible a school could rend even an adult asunder. The smell of [[Graviturgy]], often described as petrichor, also causes Jeweled Fish to lower in altitude. This is most likely a survival instinct used to avoid being thunderstruck or otherwise, as jeweled fish scales are mostly comprised of iron- a magnetic material that attracts electricity- magic or not. I considered leaving this tidbit in, but given all of your... intellects I figured it best to tell you that, yes indeed, lightning is attracted to metal. You're welcome.

While Jeweled Fish can be consumed safely, the value in slaughtering one comes from their offal- specifically their "well bladder", with the 'well' part referring to the tiny 'site' of power they keep within- allowing them to fly indefinitely. This organ is a primary component in many elixirs and the material component for a handful of powerful Graviturgy spells. If one can manage to harvest an intact bladder it fetches a high price, or could obviously be used as is by a skilled mage... not that many of you are, in fact, skilled mages.

Jeweled fish prey largely on smaller monsters and creatures when found, such as [[Aberrant Jerboa]], [[Aberrant Isopods|Aberrant Isopod]] nymphs, some Whimseys- namely [[Vibrant Whimseys]], and just plain old, regular Jerboa. Jeweled Fish are preyed upon by [[Dunewakers]], [[Sky-Rippers]], [[Haymaker Snails]], [[True Wyverns]], and to be frank, many more monsters that would take far to long to name. They are a common and integral food source in the regions they appear. Dunewakers in particular require many Jeweled fish to sustain their own power. Without them, the monsters would go extinct.
