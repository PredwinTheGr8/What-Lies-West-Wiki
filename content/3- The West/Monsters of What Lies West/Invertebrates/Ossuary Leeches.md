"Blasphemous to the law of size. The young of titans, the spawn of true giants. A parasite of the bone- to be bitten is to wail in pain as secretions of bile melt flesh and dig through marrow. Steel thyself, then. In both perception and tolerance for squeamish things. Thy foe is small, unnoticed until it attacks."

-Ashkram Who Speaks Strange, [[Scholars|scholar]] and [[Adventurers|adventurer]]

Ossuary Leeches are small, parasitic [[Monsters]] of [[The West]] that reside naturally within the bounds of [[Ossuary Reach|The Ossuary Reach]], though [[Adventurers]] sometimes inadvertently carry them outside of the west and they may be found within butcher shops. Ossuary Leeches are small, pinky-finger sized worms with flat backs covered in spines used to deter predators from making a meal of them, and to stop a living host from attempting removal. Ossuary Leeches earn their name from both the locale in which they originate, and for their dietary choice of solely consuming the marrow found within bones.

Ossuary Leeches are unable to subsist themselves on flesh alone, requiring the dense protein of bone marrow to bring themselves into their next life stages. Eventually cascading in a snowball of size into the largest living things in the world- [[Ossuary Titans]]. To get past the sturdy outer bone layer, the mouth of a leech unfolds into a network of fleshy vines that corrode through the layer, and once the marrow is secured, lesser acids break down the substance into a slurry that is then ingested.

Their primary deceased hosts are other Leeches- though the adult form of their species of course. Ossuary Titans, wanderers as they are, can still sense the end of their lives coming. They will always attempt to return to the Ossuary Reach, where they will yield to age and perish lying down. If the Titan had secured a mate, their young will hatch inside of their now rotting corpse and begin their feast.

Leaving the rest of the decomposition of a Titan for [[The Rotting of a Titan|it's own archival]], we can continue on with the threats posed by the Leeches.

Sometimes, an Ossuary Leech will be displaced from its carcass host- whether it be an Ossuary Titan or one of the far older beasts that have no living members. When this happens, the Leech will find a high point- sometimes resulting in finding a new dead host- and will begin to lie in wait. Once a living creature either walks under or near the Leech, it will spring forth, expending great energy. If it misses, the Leech is almost sure to die, but if it makes its mark, it will wait for the creature to fall asleep.

An attached leech will crawl slowly to the neck or cranium of a creature, being careful to not wake their host by accidentally spiking them or disturbing their ears. Once they locate a major artery, they will immediately release their flesh net and burn a hole through flesh to get to it. On most wild Monsters, this is surely fatal. On most adventurers, though, a simple cast of healing serves to close the wound and stop any major damage from occurring. The burning sensation, though, persists for a great time. Described as an immeasurable heat that will not fade. If a skilled enough Cleric is present, a lesser restoration spell can instantly alleviate the spell. Otherwise, a fortnight of constant burning is in store for any survivors.

Ossuary Leeches are preyed upon by juvenile [[Ossuary Titans]], [[Bone Dogs]], [[Pricks]], [[Gnolls]], [[Maws]], [[Arcanosaurs]], [[Slimes]], [[Rot-winged Wranglers]], and young [[Dunewakers]]. In turn, Ossuary Leeches can easily catch any of these monsters off guard and return the favor.
