Void Bisons are [[Monsters|monsters]] of [[The West]] found within its [[All Regions of The West|region]] of [[The Void Dunes|The Void Dunes]]. Void Bisons are twenty feet tall, 15 feet wide, and 32 feet long at adulthood, with black, dense fur on their backs that easily falls off when pulled, likely to help Void Bisons escape from the claws of their primary predator- [[Wyverns]]. This fur can get upwards of six feet thick, and can prove to be a problem for any who seek to skin and butcher the monsters, as the wiry hair quickly dulls a blade. The mouth of a void bison is full of baleen, like that of a whale, used to filter out sand from their primary food source, [[Umber Grass]]. 

These teeth fold back on hinge-like muscles to make room for a giant, prehensile tendril of flesh with barbed hooks at its apex. This mass is used to attack and maim predators by slashing or grappling them. Void Bison see from two separate clusters of goat-irised eyes, roughly 12-15 in each cluster. This gives the [[Monsters|monsters]] a massive field of view, so long as their fur doesn't get in the way. Tamed Void Bisons, domesticated by the [[Bath'kah]], will regularly have the fur around their eyes sheared to allow them to see better and respond to predators while the Bath'kah, or their pets the [[Warp Beasts]] are otherwise preoccupied.

Also present on a Void Bison Bull's head, is their horns. These horns are immensely heavy, and a single swing of them is more than enough to break the ribs, or rather the entire body of the average person. If one can manage to fell a Bull, which are usually larger than other Void Bisons, the horns fetch a mighty price.

Void Bisons are relatively tame around most [[Adventurers|adventurers]], though larger folk like [[Minotaurs]] and [[Centaurs]] can easily spook them into defending themselves. Void Bison, despite naturally accumulating in herds, will not actively seek to help their fellows unless they are a bonded pair of mates, or parent and child, though most Bulls will investigate any distress in the herd and may act with violence if they're seemingly in the mood for it.

When in combat against a Void Bison, keep to its rear- as despite the size of its legs, they cannot rapidly kick backwards, or hardly at all. If you can manage it, severing their mouth-tendril can turn the balance of power into your favor, as the veins within the mass are numerous and will cause the Bison to bleed out... eventually.

Void Bison are herbivores, though they will suck on the bones of any other creatures they find to get precious nutrients. They are preyed upon by Larger [[Wyverns]], [[Behemoths]], Packs of [[Warp Beasts]], [[Dunewakers]] and Larger [[Inverters]]. They are kept as cattle by the [[Bath'kah]].
