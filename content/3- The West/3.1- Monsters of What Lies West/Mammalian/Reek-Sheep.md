## [[Monster Callsigns|Callsigns]]-

- Foul smell that wracks the soul
- Calls
- Tracks and dung

Reek-Sheep are [[Monsters|monsters]] of [[The West]] primarily found inhabiting [[The Creeping Peninsula|The Creeping Peninsula]]. Reek-Sheep, despite their name, closely resemble the common goat, with long black hair doused in their... musk- that acts as a primary form of defense against their predators. The musk of a Reek-Sheep is foul, so foul that [[scholars]] believe that it is arcane in origin as surely nothing could ever naturally smell as completely horrendous as a Reek-Sheep's oily excretions. Reek-Sheep range between six to twelve feet at the shoulder when adults.

Reek Sheep, though monstrous as they are, can be tamed and brought back to [[The City of Wyrms]] where they are made into fine pack animals and food sources to take on expeditions. With their smell, an adult Reek-Sheep makes for bad prey- and their oily fur also allows them to escape the clutches of even [[Inverters]]- hunters notorious for their lethality. Domestic Reek-Sheep are far less rotten smelling unless in distress, suggesting that the musk has something to do with their mood- the happier they are the better they smell, though a pocket full of posies will still be required if you plan on spending a large amount of time with them.

Reek-Sheep meat and cheese is prized among foodies like the [[Catfolk]] for their unique flavor profile and alternative effects- as [[Eldritch Energy|eldritch]] spawn, though not as corrupted as most others, Reek-Sheep ingredients host powerful eldritch "flavors" that to the common man are barely noticeable, but for the brilliant and well studied, may arise a few seconds after swallowing as an indescribable palate.

Reek-Sheep are easy foes, being felled by most weapons if a vital organ is hit such as the lungs or head. If the bowels are hit, however, run for cover and bring a wet cloth to your throat. Reek-Sheep bile rapidly aerosolizes into a fog of unbreathable poison- which if one desires can be purchased in jars within [[The City of Wyrms]] to use in combat.

Reek-Sheep are clever prey animals, and though many predators may try, their foul smell and sharp horns, combined with their large herds means that few can make a meal out of them- with only the scorching heat of a [[True Wyverns|True Wyvern]] capable of neutralizing their rot.
