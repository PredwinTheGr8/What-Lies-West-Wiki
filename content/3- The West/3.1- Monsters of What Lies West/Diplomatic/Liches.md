### [[Monster Callsigns|Callsigns]]-

- Stench of Necromancy- commonly perceived as the smell of posies
- Raised corpses shambling about- oft called 'zombies'
- Smell of tallow, burning candles
- Sense of dread, though mild

Liches are [[Monsters]] of [[The West]] that can be found inhabiting ruins that show signs of significance to [[The Old Empire of The West]]- namely ancient libraries and desolate workshops. Liches visually resemble large, humanoid corvids. Their feathers, often measuring two feet long, are black at the base, and shift into a deep brown at the tips in a gradient pattern. These feathers are often coated with alchemical reagents from the Liches spells and mundane rituals.

Liches largely concern themselves with repetitive tasks that otherwise seem purposeless. They will endlessly reorganize books on a shelf, or needlessly toil away on a perfect blade until it is weathered away to dust- before grabbing another and starting again. Liches will only take pause for one of two reasons;

**One**, to raise a minion. Liches do not hunt for themselves, nor do they offer much resistance alone as they are quite sluggish. Instead, within a liches territory they will reanimate and control up to twenty different 'zombies'. Most often, these zombies are the animated husks of common, but strong, [[Monsters]] such as [[Arcanosaurs]] or [[Maws]]. Sometimes, a Lich may reanimate a deceased mutant such as a [[Gnolls|Gnoll]] or perhaps a [[Killfreaks|Killfreak]]. These animated corpses will patrol the Lich's territory and find any prey items, which they will then slaughter and return to their master for its consumption.

**Second**, to meditate. Liches require frequent meditations to sustain their holdover all their puppets. A dead, or incompetent Lich will lose control over their undead servants, resulting in their number going completely mindless- pursuing and hunting anything that moves, even each other.

Liches, and by extension their minions, do not care in any way about the presence of [[Adventurers]]. A group of adventurers is perfectly safe walking near or with a zombie controlled by a competent Lich. Some adventurers even identify a Lich's minions as a sign that the area is safe- meaning many will take the opportunity to eat or sleep the moment they see a patrolling, fetid carcass. Some adventurers claim that they have awoken from such a slumber to find one or more zombies waiting near the edges of their encampment, before departing once the group awakens. 

Despite the above behavior of the zombies themselves, Liches seem to either ignore adventurers outwrite, or cannot perceive them in some way. Liches, so long as they are not accosted, will simply continue doing their tasks if they are ever discovered by a group of adventurers. On that note, finding a Lich, even if you know one is in the area, is incredibly difficult. It takes the skilled use of clairvoyant magics to find them in their lair- or even just to find their lair. You see, Liches themselves are clearly masters of the arcane as well, and with their skill they will cloak the doors and other thresholds of their home to keep it hidden. Many attempts have been made to commune with Liches, but they seem to not care in teh slightest for the words of any who approach them- speaking in an ever changing tongue that most cannot fathom- Ley-Speak.

If one must combat a lich or its minions, [[Heliomancy]], the power of [[Orpheus]], works the best at dismantling undead forces. While the minions are almost never grouped up together, if they are making sure you avoid being surrounded is clearly the best choice.

Liches are apex predators, their minions can coordinate to take down many monsters, and the bigger the monster, the bigger the Liches next minion. Though, one group of adventurers revealed the lair of a Lich while being stalked by [[The Annihilator]]. The fiend was said to immediately fire a beam of energy at the lair, destroying it utterly and loosing the undead unto the world with the death of their master.
