## [[Monster Callsigns]]-

- Frog-like croaks
- Heavy footsteps- often heard from half a mile away
- Smell of rotting fish
- Increased eldritch and arcane power in area
  
The "Fog Men" are [[Monsters]] of [[The West]] that only inhabit the fog-coated region of [[The Creeping Peninsula|The Creeping Peninsula]]. A Fog Man possesses the same silhouette as a [[Humans|human]], though they have far larger shoulders and necks to support their heads. Fog Men average at sixteen feet tall, though elders may reach upwards of twenty if well fed. Across their bodies, a single, massive mouth stretches vertically up and down a Fog Man's chest, with numerous, bright green, glowing eyes lining the "lips" of the mouth. Two larger compound eyes sit at the Fog Man's head, giving it complete area vision.

The hands of a Fog Man are extremely calloused from years of scratching themselves- starting out as bloody wounds and hardening into rock hard scabs. These hands are used to grip and pull the small trees of the region out of the ground- used for territorial displays or fights, and to kill their prey. Despite what one would think, the trees have adapted to this constant assailment from the Fog Men, and are now reliant on being displaced to spread their seeds.

The Skin of a Fog Man is a light greyish blue, which closely matches the hue of the fog within The Creeping Peninsula. This allows Fog Men to remain still when their prey approaches them from the same angle as the sun, sitting in wait until their meal gets close enough to notice them- close enough for it to be far too late.

In combat, Fog Men are smart enough to recognize and prioritize spellcasters and sharpshooters, bashing and killing any hooded- staff wielding members of a party first to weaken the rest. This doubles as part of their dietary needs, as arcane power keeps their eldritch energy from overpowering their body and consuming them- thus Fog Men may retreat with the corpse of one or two mages as a suitable hunt. Clerics of [[Orpheus]], though, are particularly sought after by Fog Men, as the light of Orpheus is far more powerful a force against eldritch energy.

Some particularly smart Fog Men have learned to cripple a cleric of Orpheus instead of eating them- dragging the cleric to the beast's den and keeping them to repetitively cast healing spells on them to hold back their corruption. Though, as one knows, being crippled and lost within [[The West]] is the first stage to becoming a [[Mutants|mutant]].

When fighting a Fog Man, shielding casters and smaller members of a party is paramount, as Fog Men aren't in the fight to kill an entire party- only to feed themselves. If they cannot safely secure a kill they may retreat, or worse, if they can manage to grab a party member only the fastest of adventurers can keep up with a Fog Man running off with their meal.

Fog Men prey upon [[Maws]], low-flying [[Plaguewings]], [[Screechers]], young [[Inverters]], [[Mimics]], lone [[Bath'kah]] that stray from [[The Void Dunes|The Void Dunes]], [[Warp Beasts]], [[Arcanosaurs]], mutants such as [[Ghouls]] or [[Gnolls]], and [[Reek-Sheep]]. They are only preyed upon by other Eldritch Spawn such as [[Charnel Wyverns]], stray above-ground [[Abominations]], and other, larger Fog Men.
