### [[Monster Callsigns|Callsigns]]-

- Visual Distortions
- The [[Song of The Annihilator]], otherwise known as the Eldritch Lullaby
- Smell of curses, described as vinegar
- Frequent Mind-Whispers
- Nosebleeds
- Sense of dread
- Quiet whispers in the tongue of [[The Deep]]

Monoliths are [[Eldritch Energy|Eldritch]] Spawn of [[The West]] that hold no primary residence in any one [[All Regions of The West|region]]. Like [[Grolles]], a monolith can be difficult to detect, as like a Grolle a Monolith is nearly invisible itself. Instead, patches of dark smoke often accompany the main body of a Monolith, and are the only tell one may be present. These dark patches are common in certain delves though, to be sure one may check that these anomalous clouds do not leave cracks or broken objects in their wake. Monoliths, despite appearances, weigh several tons and often break floor tiles and wooden walkways beneath them. Despite this they make no noise when moving and can levitate or even move through walls.

As aberrant spawn Monolith can be barred entry from a room by sealing off the threshold inside with salt. Despite being able to move through walls, salt in only a doorway will stop a monolith from entering the chamber completely unless it can find another mundane threshold to enter via. Monolith possess poor object permanence, and unless one can see you through the threshold it will eventually leave, given that it's psionic radar that produces [[Monster Callsigns|mind whispers]] cannot pierce a salt-guarded room.

Monoliths are the most common form of the so called 'true spawn' of [[The Deep]]. True spawn are characterized by pitch black bodies or by completely intangible, they are also never made in [[Maernos]], but instead earn their namesake by being literally created and birthed out of a rift into The Deep. This includes [[The Things in The Dark, Null Figures|The Things in The Dark]], [[Perfections]], [[Immolators]], [[The Knights in Yellow]], and some others. Half Spawn are instead creatures made by True Spawn or Eldritch interference. Included are [[Abominations]], [[Grolles]], [[Shamblers]] and [[Rejects]] to name a few.

Monoliths possess the greatest opportunity for change among Spawns. Most vanish within a week as they fail to learn how to sustain themselves in the material plane. But the ones that can learn to last? Those are the most dangerous, the most vomit inducing of horrors. As Monoliths persist they learn, they adapt and calculate. There are several paths one of these creatures might pursue, and all are grim to encounter.

A Monolith that comes to favor violence might search for better tools to do such. They will become more solid, and will infiltrate an empty suit of armor to pilot it around- becoming a [[The Knights in Yellow|Knight in Yellow]]. A Monolith that takes kindly to ambushes may find the game of hide and seek fun. Their form will greatly solidify to limit the smoke of their body from wandering, they will become more human-shaped and learn to manipulate their bodies- becoming a [[The Things in The Dark, Null Figures|Null Figure]]. Another option is one that actively hunts mortals, a clawed demon that clings to ceilings and hides in doorways- a [[Visitors|Visitor]]. One might get enamored with mortal speech and cadence, and seek to mimic it. They will learn to talk themselves, and will seek out the attention of mortal kind to make sure they fit in- becoming a [[Perfections|Perfection]].

Finally, the most horrid of mutations occurs out of pure, unrelenting hate. If a Monolith has survived deadly encounters with [[Adventurers|adventurers]], where the explorers used iron and salt to beat the monster back it may become wrathful and in constant pain. It will relentlessly hunt down Adventurers and consume their souls to quell its anger in vain. It will douse its raging flame with blood, it will quench its thirst, its malice, with screams.

It will become an [[Obliterator]]. An impossible foe that will have to wait its turn to be spoken of, but know that as of my expedition... I have known one's wrath personally.

In combat against a Monolith iron and or salt is a necessity. They cannot bleed without eithers presence, and will never die unless one or the other is used to snuff out its life. Do not get grabbed by the creature, lest it drag you away screaming. Do not let its words bury themselves in your mind, lest you laugh with madness. Do not let one so much as touch you, only a grazing claw is all it takes to become marked.

Monoliths do not belong in [[Maernos]]. Kill every last one and let their disappearing form bring a smile to your face, for you have done well.
