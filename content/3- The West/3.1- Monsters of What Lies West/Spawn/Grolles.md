"Grolle. Just the name says it all huh? I can't imagine them being named anything else. I don't know who came up with it but its perfect- they're so slimy and squealy and just all kinds of disturbing."

-Amelia Harrow, [[Adventurers|adventurer]]

### [[Monster Callsigns|Callsigns]]-

- Moderate Mind Whispers
- Distorted walls, floor, lighting- visibly described as "shifted"
- Nose bleeds
- Squealing sounds- described as swine adjacent
- Trails of finger nails and mucous.

Grolles are [[Monsters]] of [[The West]] that primarily reside in mundane ruins of [[The West]], though they can be found anywhere with moisture condensation and have a particularly high population within [[The Forlorn Graveyard|The Forlorn Graveyard]]. Grolles are visibly incomprehensible, possessing near-completely corrupted bodies comprised of [[Eldritch Energy]], though unlike the harrowing [[The Things in The Dark, Null Figures|Null Figures]] of two years ago, Grolles appear to have once been a naturally occurring animal that somehow, miraculously adapted to the presence and power of [[The Deep]]. Grolles, while visibly incomprehensible, can be physically interacted with to reveal certain pieces of their shape via touch. A surface of ever shifting nails, like those found on the hand, make up a large, nearly flat surface on the underside of a Grolle. These nails regularly shed off as the creature moves throughout the ruin, and each Grolles nails are very different in both shape and size to one another- leading to an idea that they serve to mark territory.

Grolles also are undeniably in possession of numerous tendrils, which serve as their only form of locomotion. Each tendril is tipped with some sort of barbed orifice, suggesting these appendages are where the Grolle keeps its mouths. When alive, the form of a Grolle is far harder to notice while it moves quietly inside the dark corridors of its home. Luckily, they posses an incredibly telling behavior- their squeals. Grolles will, without fail, break out into a shrill, pig-like cream when they see a creature- predator or prey. Scholars like myself believe that this serves entirely as an intimidation display, used to force prey into corners or to make a predator retreat. Grolles also will fake charges, bluffing numerous times to fake out their prey or to disturb their predators.

For a regular mortal, looking at a Grolle causes nausea, nosebleeds and fatigue. [[Risen Ones]] will still experience extreme nausea, but their lack of stomachs makes them susceptible to "phantom vomit" spells. This also can be used to find a Grolle, as the head throbs upon direct eye contact with a Grolle one might use it to locate any hidden in plain sight. If one doesn't burn a Grolle though, their hide is the active resource in making Cloaks of Incomprehensibility.

When fighting a Grolle, spear thrusts and conflagratory weaponry works best, with the latter often causing the Grolle to flee. Grolles are also incapable of passing salt lines, further betraying their presence if they accidentally bump into one and the salt burns them. Lighting a Grolle on fire results in a completely alien looking sight, with the fire latching onto fourth dimensional limbs otherwise non-interactable by humans and the like. This still increases their visibility though, and can thus be used to keep track of them during combat. Holy water will cause a Grolle to violently explode. While this instantly dispatches them, their blood and flesh can cause extreme [[Mutants|mutations]], and the shrapnel from whatever bones they have can cause severe bleeding. Blunt weapons do nothing to harm the blubbery flesh of a Grolle.

Grolle's will attempt to eat many Ruin-Based fauna, including small [[Thunderjaws|Thunderjaw]] nymphs, [[Verdant Whimseys]], [[Snitches]], and even stray [[Arcanosaurs]]. Still, [[Thunderjaws]] and [[Arcanosaurs]] often make the Grolle their prey as well, with both creatures being the only non-eldritch beasts that can consume the flesh of the Grolle. [[Abominations]] as always are not fans of anything with a pulse, including Grolles, but [[The Knights in Yellow]] can strangely be seen tracking the movements of a Grolle but not acting upon them. [[Chromatic Sentinels]] will smash any Grolles they see immediately.
