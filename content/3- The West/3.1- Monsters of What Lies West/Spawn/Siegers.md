[[Build Togethers|404]], [[index|What Lies West?]]

"We only let it happen once, back when [[Adventurers]] still had to help build the city and expeditions weren't authorized. We saw them out in the wastes, gangly arms, lanky torsos, rusted, scrappy weapons. We had figured it a trick of the sun back then- no one had ever gone west yet, not in such number at least. They were so far away that it seems illusionary, so we ignored em. But, under the light of [[The Many Named Moon]]'s fullness, a war horn sounded off. Over two thousand of us died that day. Never again."

-Elzroy Ishvey, Guard Captain of [[The City of Wyrms]].

Siegers are large humanoid [[Monsters]], or perhaps [[Mutants]]... or even [[Eldritch Energy|eldritch]] spawn... Foes. They are foes from [[The West]], only appearing within close proximity to [[The City of Wyrms]]. Siegers, named for their only definable actions against The City- attacking it, are nine foot tall, humanoid figures with a melted wax-like armor covering their entire body, except for any joints. The unarmored parts reveal blood-red skin and muscle. The arms and legs of a Sieger are twice the length of their torso, making them very intimidating to face, though a blow to the legs is almost certain to shatter them.

Siegers first appeared eighty-three years ago a week after [[The Unfeathering]]- a point in time when [[The City of Wyrms]] was still being refurbished before expeditions began in true capacity. They began amassing on the horizon in massive number, but since they only came out in the devastating heat of day, few guards actually saw them, and even fewer thought they were anything more than a smudge of black illusion from the Tyrannical Sun.

Rumors of 'an army of demons in the distance' were dismissed, causing panic while so many civilians were without homes was a surefire recipe for disaster, after all. But, as we know eighty years later it was no farce. The Siegers descended upon [[The City of Wyrms]] in a tide, armor scrapping on armor, orange rusted weapons screeching against sand grains- and howling mouthless screams raging across the dunes.

The Defense was successful, but several dozens of Siegers broke through the at-the-time weak walls. Hundreds of Civilians met their end, and even more of the heroic towns guard and would-be adventurers died in their effort to save the unarmed. The Siegers cackled and laughed with extreme mirth at their own bloodshed, demoralizing many in their presence. It was long, a week or so of battle, but in the end every last Sieger was felled.

Dissection of Siegers reveals that they possess no organs, blood lines, bones or even a brain. They are completely comprised of a clay-like flesh. It should be noted however that Siegers that lose pieces of their armor will lose motor control over the limbs underneath, suggesting some sort of [[Magic|arcane]] external skeleton. Examination of the undersides of plates suggest this as well- with numerous as of yet undeciphered glyphs glowing dimly underneath.

The towns guard who often take it upon themselves to cull Sieger numbers have gotten quite good at it- finding that blunt weaponry and phalanx tactics work best- creating long, rounded clubs that can be jabbed forth from behind shields. Though it should be noted that this only immobilizes Siegers, as they have no internal organs or anything fatal to hit, you must bludgeon them to absolute smithereens to completely 'kill' them.

When in combat against a Sieger, sweeping the legs and destroying joints like shoulders and knees works best to nullifying the threat. Siegers prefer overhead attacks that have great force, keeping light on your feet- or if you're strong enough- blocking the attack will help one stay alive. Siegers also never start fights outside of [[The City of Wyrms]], even if a group of [[Adventurers]] walks among their number. Meaning that if you wanted, you could simply ignore them altogether- though the towns guard politely ask that all adventurers 'show em what for.'

Sieger flesh CAN be consumed safely, and as such [[Ironback Reapers]], [[Arcanosaurs]] and the stray [[Inverters|Inverter]] will happily make a meal from a lone Sieger, though a large enough group of them is an obvious threat to any monster that they would encounter.
