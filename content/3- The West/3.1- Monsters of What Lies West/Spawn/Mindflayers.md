### [[Monster Callsigns]]-
*Symptoms of infection

- Hysteria
- Increased aggression
- Increased sadness, laziness
- Drastic mood swings
- Itchy spine
- Hand tremors
- Seizures
- Hallucinations- audible, visible, tactile
- Nightmares

Mindflayers are [[Eldritch Energy|eldritch]] [[Monsters|monsters]] of [[The West]] that can be found inhabiting any region, other than the most extreme locales such as [[The Warbling]] and [[The Godless Maw|The Godless Maw]]. Mindflayers are six branched, circular eldritch spawn similar in appearance to certain sea stars- with large thorns protruding from their plant-like limbs. Mindflayers, according to the few scholars who have gotten to experiment on one, feed off of negative emotions such as anger, sadness and disappointment. To get their food, a Mindflayer MUST find and latch onto a living, intelligent host.

Mindflayers will use their power to meld with a host's spine and latch onto their brain. They will glide through the skin without leaving so much as a scratch, leaving their host clueless to their new occupant until the Mindflayer finally starts warping their personality. A Mindflayer victim will suffer from extreme mood swings- flipping from sobbing into their hands to fuming anger in seconds, followed up with bouts of laziness and a lack of will to do anything harder than breathing.

These emotions start off barely noticeable, but as time goes on they will intensify into an undeniable corruption on flesh and spirit. Nightmares of a massive moon of thorns will haunt the host- visions of dark, infinite eyed beasts will follow them when awake and the sounds of a  massive maw crunching on bone will torment their hearing. Bugs will crawls 'neath their skin and moths will flit in and out of their mouth as they speak. But none of it is real... hopefully. These symptoms are similar to what survivors of [[The Things in The Dark, Null Figures|The Things in The Dark]] experience- visions of a parallel world where [[Eldritch Energy]] rules supreme.

If one remains infected for an extended period of time, eventually their sleep will last longer and longer, until eventually they never wake again. Victims that arrive at this stage will constantly twitch and periodically wail in their eternal slumber, suggesting that they are experiencing constant nightmares. If one get's lucky before this stage however, there is hope for recovery.

Mindflayers evidentially existed before the apocalypse of [[The Old Empire of The West|The Old Empire]] occurred- with some ruins possessing machines of immeasurable complexity that can safely remove a Mindflayer from its host. Finding one, on the other hand, is a challenge all on its own though.
