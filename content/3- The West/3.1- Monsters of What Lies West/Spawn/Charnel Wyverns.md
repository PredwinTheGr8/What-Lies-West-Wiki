### [[Monster Callsigns|Callsigns]]-

- Nosebleeds
- Constant Mind Whispers
- Smell of Curses, often described as vinegar
- Smell of necromancy, often described as posies
- Roars- described as a hundred souls crying for help
- Clear visual presence
- Large number of other spawn in the area

Charnel Wyverns are [[Eldritch Energy|eldritch]] spawn of [[The West]] that can be found in any [[All Regions of The West|region]]. Charnel Wyverns- also called corpse-drakes, melders, hierophants or sky terrors- can be seen flying through the skies constantly searching for adventurers to consume. Charnel Wyverns *only* hunt Adventurers, I cannot stress this enough, if you see one, it's hunting for *you* and your party.

Charnel Wyverns can detect sapient thoughts, though they cannot precisely pinpoint them. To evade a Charnel Wyvern, focusing on deep breaths or delving underground will help lose the spawn's lock on you, though its never certain it will leave. As true spawn, Charnel Wyverns do not need to eat, sleep or drink to survive. They exist solely to incorporate more flesh into themselves, and will not stop until utterly obliterated. Though they primarily hunt Adventurers, Charnel Wyverns will not bypass significant amounts of biomass to absorb. Dead monsters of considerable size, such as an [[Arcanosaurs|Arcanosaur]] for instance, make for excellent supplements to a below-weight Charnel Wyvern.

Dead creatures are resuscitated when absorbed, no matter how grievous the injury. If you've lost a fellow adventurer in The West, always burn their body if you cannot bring it with you, lest they may be awoken to a suffering without end.

Visually, Charnel Wyverns are simply masses of combined flesh- the bodies of their victims. These souls are forced to remain attached to each other until the monstrosity is destroyed. Their thoughts are believed to mingle with one another, only being allowed to speak when the Spawn roars- during which time pleas for mercy or screams of agony can be heard beneath the terrifying roar of the Wyvern. The silhouettes of Charnel Wyverns can sometimes be mistaken for the [[True Wyverns|real deal]], making them one of two [[Wyverns|false Wyverns]]. With the other being of course the [[Plaguewings]].

In combat against a Charnel Wyvern, holy magic and silver weapons work best to destroying the 'soul' of the monstrosity. If felled thus, the souls within are allegedly freed from [[The Deep]]. Though no study can support this, hope is better than the alternative reality. Avoiding the mouth of a Charnel Wyvern is key, as being swallowed is to be instantly assimilated- or rather it can be, as adventurer Girtz Ribmangler once clawed his way out of a Charnel Wyvern, suggesting mental or physical resilience can protect oneself.

As stated above, Charnel Wyverns do not belong in the food chain, they only serve to disrupt it. Kill them if you are capable, fear them if you are not.
