Taunting all who would hear them in the tongue of [[The Deep]], Immolators are[[Legendary Monsters|Legendary]] [[Eldritch Energy|Eldritch]] Spawn of [[The West]] made 'flesh' directly from [[The Annihilator]]. Visually, Immolators resemble The Annihilator's own body- this form is covered by a blinding light that sears the flesh of mortals to ash; hence their name. Immolators, or as they call themselves 'The Ones Reborn' in Deeptongue, are a currently massive threat to the whole ecosystem of [[The West]], with small pockets of Immolators working to completely destabilize the entire ecosystem starting at the roots.

Since their recent appearance some months ago, Immolators have wiped out seventeen unique species of monster, destroyed an estimated 13 square kilometers of land, killed and [[Soul Rending|rended]] 245 [[adventurers]], besieged [[The City of Wyrms]] twenty two times and broke through the defenses five of those times. Immolators are imposing, difficult foes that often defy physics when not acted upon by [[To kill a Creature older than the very word.|salt or iron]] to stabilize them- warping light around them and leaving shifting holes that peer into [[The Deep]]. Immolators often lead other spawn into battle or throughout their destructive actions against The West. Most often seen following Immolators are [[Shamblers]], [[Grolles]], [[The Knights in Yellow]], [[Monoliths]] and [[Kharahziaizi, Psycho Mantises|Kharahziaizi]]. For Grolles and Psycho-Mantises they seem to be under a great deal of mind control- and are sometimes struck down for annoying an Immolator.

Immolators are the most clear and concise servants of [[The Annihilator]], with many other spawn keeping their distance from any signs of The Sun Scourge and those following Immolators around doing so likely out of what could be called fear- or perhaps they too are controlled. Immolators are not kind to other spawn- especially [[Mutants|mutants]] caused by The Deep such as [[Death Knights]] and [[Abominations]]. Immolators indeed at a first glance seem to despise anything other than The Annihilator itself, with more cases witnessing an Immolator disintegrating a [[The Things in The Dark, Null Figures|Null Figure]] than cooperating with one.

The appearance and actions of Immolators bring to light something rather under looked in the Eldritch Hierarchy; that being that there are vaguely defined factions within the Spawns' numbers. Null Figures and [[Monoliths]] do not often collaborate, [[Grolles]] and [[Abominations]] attempt to kill one another, [[Mindflayers]] will infect both mortals and monstrosities. Some True spawn clearly attack one another while others walk past each other- and some directly collaborate. There are certain spawn that are used by many others like [[Shamblers]] which seem designed on a deep level to be obedient to anything possessing eldritch power.

Immolators, though, possess the most unclear allegiances to the other spawn I've seen- PERSONALLY seen. Immolators, as stated above, *have* gotten into [[The City of Wyrms]] on five separate occasions in only the past few months. It seems that once their goals are right within their grasp- whatever those goals even are- they begin to get brash, foolhardy and more likely to destroy everything around them- including the very minions they forced into their service.

In combat against an Immolator Iron and Salt are a necessity. They cannot be harmed without first being materialized and are completely immune to magics of all schools, it takes cunning, strength and teamwork to destroy one and you are expected to. Immolators are not only a threat to [[The West]] but the entire world, and [[Ser Mallorin Berns of Harush]], director of the Adventurer's Guild has placed an absolute kill order on any Immolator encountered, with hefty rewards of course. It is the duty of any able bodied warrior to drive an iron spear into the voidform that is an Immolator and forever remove its stain from our world of [[Maernos]].
