Gnolls are [[Mutants|mutant]] [[Monsters|monsters]] of [[The West]] that mostly inhabit the ancient graveyards of [[The Ossuary Reach|The Ossuary Reach]]. Gnolls were once mortals of [[The Plain Three]], but after prolonged exposure to the [[Magic|magics]] of the desert, the [[Cardinal Sickness]] turned them into lanky, boney creatures that can only laugh at their tormented forms. Gnolls will often continue to wear the gear they equipped before their transformation, though fitted items like mail and cloths might fall lose or become torn as the person mutates beyond recognition.

Gnolls appear most often in [[The Ossuary Reach|The Ossuary Reach]], as their current predicament is easily caused by [[Ossuary Leeches]] siphoning a persons energy and leaving them immobile under the evil sun of [[The West]]. Gnolls can appear in other [[All Regions of The West|regions]], of course, but they also seem to be drawn in great number to The Ossuary Reach. Why this is so, and how they can now perfectly navigate the west after their mutation, is completely unknown- though with most questions, [[Magic|magic]] is a good answer until the truth is found, as boring and repetitive such an answer is.

Gnolls are relatively easy to bring down, with no particular weaknesses or strength. A good bash to the head, a shot to the chest, or a gaping stomach wound will kill them just as easily as any normal mortal. Though one might be able to avoid a fight altogether if you remain in a group and stay calm- as though they are mad, they are decidedly NOT stupid, remember that.

Gnolls are near the bottom of local food chains, hunting opportunistically to take down any meals they come across- like [[Arcanosaurs]], [[Ironback Reapers]], or other, common desert prey items for regular adventurers.
