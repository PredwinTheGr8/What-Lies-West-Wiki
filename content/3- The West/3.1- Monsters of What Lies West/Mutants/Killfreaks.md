"You'll probably find one in the ruins- one of the cold, empty hallways- when you least expect it. The sounds of scraping feet, the whimper of a lost mind, the sparking, slashing wails of metal claws on stone walls. Then, you'll see it... or them, rather. Gaunt eyes, patchy hair, a weary expression- the face of someone trapped in the body of a murderer."

-Amelia Harrow, [[Adventurers|adventurer]]

Killfreaks are [[Mutants|mutant]] [[Monsters|monsters]] of [[The West]] that were once mortal men and women of any lineage. They have been twisted by the [[Cardinal Sickness]] into vile monsters that wander the desolate halls of sunken ruins within The West, aimlessly slaughtering any they find and shoveling the gore into their forever-hungry mouths. Killfreaks are massive, grotesque mirrors of their former selves. Arms and legs grow in length and bulk, facial features become more pronounced as all fat leaves their cheeks and necks. Their hair or scales will begin sloughing off too, but the most gruesome, and fatal of their transformations occurs on the hands.

The fingers of a Killfreak will grow in length- becoming imbued with metals as the magics that corrupt them transmute their flesh. Their hands become a dozen swords, constantly grinding away at the walls of their home, creating dull, brutal weapons that kill slowly, leaving massive lacerations from barely-sharp blades. With enough time, and with enough eldritch presence, a Killfreak may further mutate into an [[Abominations|Abomination]].

Killfreaks, unlike [[Gnolls]] or other [[Mutants|mutants]] can be saved from their predicament with restorative spells, revealing that the person who fell to the [[Cardinal Sickness]] was awake and thinking within their own body- just unable to control it. Many an [[Adventurers|adventurer]] who cannot restore a Killfreak to their former selves will go out of their way to mercy kill any they come across, rather than risk the person being trapped forever when they return to [[The City of Wyrms]] and cannot find their way back.

Killfreaks are dangerous opponents, they move quickly, irrationally, and violently. They flail their arms around constantly when in combat, leaving any who engage in melee at constant risk of catching a stray blow. When in combat with a Killfreak, blows to the head can disorient the [[Mutants|mutant]] and leave it vulnerable to a fatal strike.

Killfreaks will attack anything they see, and attempt to eat it afterwards- scholars believe it is a result of the victim desperately trying to end their own lives, with their will barely turning into action. [[Monsters]] rarely attack Killfreaks, as their claws and incessant flailing are a great danger to any beasts they would encounter in the ruins. [[The Knights in Yellow]], however, will exterminate any [[Mutants|mutants]] they find.
