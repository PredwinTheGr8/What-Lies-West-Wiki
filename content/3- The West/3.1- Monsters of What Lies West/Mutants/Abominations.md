"We were in a quiet- *dead quiet*- sunken ruin within [[The Forlorn Graveyard|The Forlorn Graveyard]]. Ripped apart suits of armor, ravaged corpses of [[Monsters|monsters]], and the stench of decay and dung. We entered a large chamber with a large stone in its center. Once we got close, the stone took a deep breath and rose up- a writhing, heaving mass of tendrils and teeth. It screamed with such force that we went deaf for a moment. Twenty three reduced to five in three minutes."

-Olluk The Bull, [[Adventurers|adventurer]]

Abominations are [[Mutants|mutant]] [[Monsters|monsters]] of [[The West]] that are not particular to any one region, though they only reside in sunken ruins. Abominations are characterized by their lack of form- being a roiling mass of shifting muscle and mouths with barely recognizable legs supporting a weight of upwards of three tons. Abominations are inherently extremely violent- and their massive frames allow them to act very efficiently as killers, being able to go toe to toe with anything that can be found in the ruins. [[The Knights in Yellow|Knights in Yellow]], [[Grolles]], [[Thunderjaws]]; all of these monsters stand a near zero chance of survival against Abominations.

If the Callsigns suggest that an Abomination might be present, the safest option is to leave the ruin, lest you wake the demonic creature and meet your end unless you're extremely prepared. Abominations are extremely durable creatures, taking numerous blows to cripple, and tens more to fell. Without any proper weak spots, as they often lack a visible head and their organs are buried deep under their muscle, the only hope of a cornered party is to keep attacking the beast with everything they have until the Abomination falls, and even then a minute or two more of attacks will ensure the monstrosity is dead.

Abominations are highly territorial, and therefor luckily often reside alone in their ruins. Though, horrifyingly, an abomination may venture out from its den to find another to reproduce with, being the only [[Mutants|mutants]] capable of birthing more of their kind.

When in combat against an Abomination, staying out of reach and using ranged weaponry like bows or flintlocks works best to avoiding becoming a red paste. If enough damage is sustained, an Abomination may attempt to run and hide, but you should not follow it- Abomination dens are heavily corrupted by [[Eldritch Energy]], and are deadly to the flesh of man just by proximity.

Abominations are at the top of the food chain where they appear, even in regions such as [[The Void Dunes|The Void Dunes]] where they can sometimes be seen looming over the corpses of several [[Bath'kah]] who thought their might was better.
