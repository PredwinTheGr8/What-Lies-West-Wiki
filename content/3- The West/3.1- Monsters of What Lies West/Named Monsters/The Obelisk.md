"The Obelisk" is a singular, highly possessive [[Eldritch Energy|Eldritch]] Spawn that holds immense obsession with biological men of any lineage. Eye witness accounts started off difficult to deal with, as anyone other than the 'possessed' individual accepting the existence audibly that The Obelisk is real will result in the Entity immediately acting with aggression, lifting up and consuming the individual in large, bloody bites. To those unpossessed, they will not be able to see, hear or smell any traces of The Obelisk save for a few Callsigns.

Proximity to The Obelisk causes discomfort, to say the very least. Women will experience abdominal cramps and men will begin to feal a persistent sensation of fear. Those "blessed" by [[Mortacious, Father of Curses]] will feel extreme pressure on their "marks" left by the curse-god. With prolonged exposure causing open lacerations to form. One of The Obelisk's victims was one such individual, and his death was a gruesome sight to behold as the curse was ripped out of his body, leaving him but a contorted husk of a lizardfolk.

Within an as of yet undefinable radius, creatures and monsters will become immobile. Monkeys would fall from trees, birds plummet down from the sky, and horses mid stride fall over. There is no upper limit to a creatures size, with one single case of even an [[Ossuary Titans|Ossuary Titan]] paralyzing when The Obelisk manifests somewhere.

Another prevailing feeling is one of doom. Other than the possessed individual all mortals within an, again, as of yet undefined radius will sense their end rapidly approaching. This makes it particularly difficult to speak with the possessed, as that would mean also being in proximity to The Obelisk when it manifests- therefor meaning the entire discussion you feel as if at any moment a flintlock will fire at your head.

Finally, several hallucinations that can only be described as disturbing will occur. These glimpses of the supernatural are brief as a blink, faster even. Without paying attention you may miss them, the visual ones at least. Images include hanging humans with their bellies cut open. From my glimpses it looks as if the women have had their uteruses gouged out and left hanging, and the men have had something... alive shoved inside them. One may also see discarded infants and fetuses covered in blood on the floor, some still wailing for the brief moment the hallucination is visible. Finally, rooms may become filled with blood or overtaken by raging flames.

Audible Hallucinations are quiet, but include distant cries of babes and a lullaby that frankly chills me to the core. I cannot help but shrink into my chair when it happens during an interview.

Finally, the last hallucination that can be seen and heard at the same time... *It*.

The Obelisk itself.

I have interviewed a grand total of thirty eight victims. I had always braced myself that one day I would see The Obelisk myself, but it took nineteen years and twelve victims before I caught a look. I understood it, then, why everyone was so horrified at the invisible thing standing right behind me in the cell. Its as this letter describes it. Pale, radiant white skin with blood dripping from the shoulders onto the stone floor. Two, beady black eyes with vibrant red veins snaking away from them. And that mouth... full of teeth and exaggerated, pale red flesh that reveal a hole large enough for a [[Minotaurs|Minotaur]] to fall into. I barely see her, and I'm never prepared for when it happens, but every time I only hear her groaning noises of discomfort. It, I mean... Its important not to call it a Her; it isn't, not anymore at least.

The Obelisk, The White Lady... I have only known this fear when I was forced into the [[The True Desert|Desert]] by that abomination of light. At least the "Lady" gave me some preparations long before the [[Arrival of The Annihilator]].

From witness accounts The Obelisk seems to be after them in some sort of sexual way, at least they seem to all unanimously think so. They said it caresses their chest at night, that it drags a single, knife like finger down their back and cuts them, that it tries in vain to seduce them. The thought alone disturbs me... and with the imagery from the hallucinations I believe they may be correct. Victims will rave about The Obelisk so, so much that their voices will give out completely, as is what happened to my dear friend last year...

Out of the thirty eight victims, I have guided twelve to safety. It takes an extreme amount of willpower, mental fortitude and physical resilience to purge The Obelisk from a host creature. To start, the possessed must also refuse to acknowledge The Obelisk, and also refrain from thinking or vocalizing that The Obelisk is a "woman" in any way. Thinking or vocalizing such encourages the entity into further latching onto the host. My assistant for these prolonged years of thwarting The Obelisk, Screak Whisper-Whisker, was possessed. Luckily, through our combined knowledge Screak had long since known the solution to avoiding The Obelisk. He simply locked himself up in a small room for a week or so, and then came out un-possessed after an hour of not hearing The Obelisk's horrid vocalizations as it attempted to find him like a lost puppy.

I know, deep down that it's only a matter of time before I meet her- it, I mean... and though I know that I know how to defend myself I am not at all sound of soul at the thought of seeing it for more than a fleeting moment in the corners of my vision... I abhor the thought of hearing it try to talk to me... I shudder at the visualization of it's bloody shoulders dragging on my roof... I'd rather be Annihilated.
