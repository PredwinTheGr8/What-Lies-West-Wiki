"I'm telling you! It's real! It's real! It swooped down from the tyrannical sun itself! It unfurled into a thousand edges! Its eyes were bottomless! It had no mouth but it spoke in to tongue of madness! It unfurled again and again until a screaming beam of light seared my party to dust! It Annihilated them! Please! Please you don't have to-"

The last words of Hugo Edwards during his execution under penalty of murdering his party members. Three years later, he was proven right.

The Annihilator is a unique... entity of [[The West]]. What first started as the literally unbelievable ramblings of a man destined for the chopping block turned into tall tales of a distant floating cloud that 'felt wrong.' Then it became stories of a booming voice and an unfolding square of doom that spoke in a tongue unknowable- incomprehensible. It spoke it the spaces between words, behind the silence of dawn, the end of a sentence. There are many who would be called crazy for such a thing normally, but these tales seemed to be almost [[Magic|bizarrely]] unbelievable, as if it was the work of a spell rather than mere suspicion.

As such, not a single story was ever believed. Luckily even in my own ignorance I still kept some notes, though mine was for comparing the notes between the reports- which at first didn't line up but two years ago during [[The Unfeathering]] I suddenly could see the truth... They had never been contradicting, they had never once changed between accounts. But it was too late.

Two years ago, on the very day of The Unfeathering, The [[Bells and Whistles]] grew so close to [[The City of Wyrms]] that it could be heard even in the Prairie beyond the eastern walls. And then, like a tide of destruction, The Annihilator descended from the sky. It was as they said- an indiscernible entity of light that despite my best attempts I could not truly understand. That same voice was there- or at least I think it was. I remember a feeling of a memory of a conversation with a... with an empty chair? A hidden man in a room that whispered just loud enough for me to know he was there but not enough to understand.

I remember feeling its movements crawling on me, despite how far away it was... or was it close to me the whole time? It was impossible to understand how large it was. Nevertheless its a real entity, we know that now. If only it didn't cost so many to finally understand. Bells chimed in our ears louder than harpy screams. Beams of energy destroyed houses, melted streets and turned people into solid water. Not ice. Not. Ice. I know its difficult to understand that, it must be. It was water, but as if it was moving glass- cool to the touch and forever in the shape of the taken.

Some of them are still around. Sitting still in a rocking chair outside a pub, or some other cruel monetary position to draw in visitors... The Annihilator came for us and proved itself real- and ever since [[The West]] has grown crueler. [[The Things in The Dark, Null Figures|The Things in The Dark]] showed up the same year. The [[Maws]] started coming out of their natural habitat. It seems to me like The Annihilator might be some kind of organizer- a master of chess and the monsters are pieces and it gets to make up its own rules while we have to play around with the normal ones.

It still gets seen sometimes. Watching from miles away. Its impossible shadow blocking the sun. I've heard tales of many monsters, of countless dangers and fiends. But this thing... this Annihilator is the first I've ever seen. Every time I hear about it it reminds me of the devastation, of its earning of its namesake...

Are we in over our heads?
