"LITTLE FREAKERS! WEE BASTARDS! TINY, ITTY-BITTY BUG EYED CUTIES. DAMNIT! I HATE HOW MUCH I LIKE THEM! ... I- I wanna pet one I think. Can I pet Dave? Please? Pretty please? Damn."

-Markus Krauler, [[Adventurers|adventurer]]

Carrion Thieves are [[Monsters|monsters]] of [[The West]] that solely reside within [[The Shelled Trenches|The Shelled Trenches]]. They resemble octopi at a glance, but are in study more similar to squids than any other mollusk. Carrion Thieves possess thirteen tentacles, all of which are used to propel themselves through their home or to manipulate objects. Carrion Thieves earn their name from their scavenger way of life- following powerful predators such as [[Sky-Rippers]] or even just [[Adventurers]] at a distance and waiting for them to fell prey. The Carrion Thieves to not waste time waiting for the predator to leave, and will instead take rapid bites from carcasses while avoiding the creature's killer. Adventurers do not like this one bit, as all too often does a Carrion Thief take off valuable scales, or run off with a precious organ- or even flee with a shiny carving knife they mistook for food, before dropping the tool from a great height when they cut themselves on it. This latter part happens quite frequently apparently. Ahh, to see such whimsey myself- and I *have!*

I can now say that I have interacted with a living monster, and not just any monster! My very own monster! That is to say, Harold brought an injured Carrion Thief back to [[The City of Wyrms]] and entrusted it's care to me. At first, our relationship was very... complicated. The little beast, which I have dubbed 'Dave', frequently knocked over valuable equipment whenever he got loose from his cage- which was quite frequent, mind you. Eventually though, with time, he has grown attached to me... very firmly. He gravitates around me even as I write this document, and given the elasticity of his body it is currently impossible for me to hide from him with my current door gaps- perhaps I will need a refit done soon.

With a living monster in my home, I have been able to learn much about Carrion Thieves. Dave has done very well to allow me to examine his behavior. They spend much of their time in hovering just above the ground to conserve energy. While flying, they exude the smell of [[Graviturgy]], described most commonly as the smell of rain- petrichor- which at the dead of night when your ready for bed is quite calming, at least to myself. While Carrion Thieves DO travel in packs, they do not interact much with one another, and seem to prefer bonds with only one or two others of their kind rather than the whole 'annoyance'- the name for a group of Carrion Thieves coined by none other than Girtz Ribmangler.

Dave represents this perfectly I believe. While he *does* refuse to leave my side- even though I leave the windows open during summers- Dave does not seem interested in anyone else, even my closest colleagues that frequently visit such as Rakrak and Ashkram are nary even approached by Dave. He will over ever get close if one of them offers food, but that's the only situation where he ever leaves my personal space.

Normally, a Carrion Thief is crepuscular, meaning that it is most active during the twilight hours of the day- dawn and dusk. Dave has grown accustomed to my own sleep... 'schedule' if one could call it that. He simply remains awake when I am myself, and sleeps when I sleep. I must admit the comedy in him sleeping longer than me on occasion and making to find I have already left to begin writing. Wild Thieves will make calls to locate one another- a sort of high pitched babble made by exhaling air and flapping their tendrils in their breath. Dave regularly uses this noise when he is looking for me. Perhaps the most unsettling part is no matter how quite my snicker he always hears it and flies right over. He did it this morning before I wrote my document on [[Haymaker Snails]]. Currently, as I write this, he is playing with a bottle cork, throwing it repeatedly at my free hand so that I may toss it back to him. Annoying, but how could one resist the charms of such a free spirited creature?

Carrion Thieves are widely regarded as pests by adventurers, but as one may assume I don't match their sentiment. As with many monsters, they are simply misunderstood. Be it Whimseys, [[Mire Hulks]], [[Goodfellows]] or even [[Snitches]], there are plenty of 'monsters' within [[The West]] that are more than welcome. At least in my eyes, that is.

Carrion Thieves do not hunt, and only scavenge the leftovers- or rather left befores- of other monsters such as [[Sky-Rippers]] and [[Vorpal Lancers]]. They are preyed upon by anything that can match or exceed their own cunning- a difficult feat no doubt. Namely [[Vorpal Lancers]] are fast enough to catch an unwary Thief that goes in for a swipe.
