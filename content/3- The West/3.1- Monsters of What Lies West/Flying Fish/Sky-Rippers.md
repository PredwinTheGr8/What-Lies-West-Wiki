## [[Monster Callsigns|Callsigns]]-

- Faint, flat purple splotches of air

- Very clear visible presence- "seriously," so says Markus Krauler. He wanted it quoted.
- High number of [[Carrion Thieves]] in the area
- Increased air moisture
  
Sky-Rippers are [[Monsters]] of [[The West]], being a rare sight inside of [[The Shelled Trenches|The Shelled Trenches,]] though they are known to wander other regions such as [[The Ossuary Reach|The Ossuary Reach]] as well. Sky Rippers are forty foot long, shark-like monsters that use [[Magic|graviturgy and conjuration]] to move about their territories. They possess a blueish underside- a poor attempt to conceal their presence given their massive size, and a color-changing top that they use to mirror the ground beneath them- likely a tool used to hide from their predators- of which there are only two real candidates given their own size.

Sky-Rippers earn their namesake from their mastery of conjuration- being able to open large rifts at will, which the Ripper uses to quickly travel between two distant points or make a quick attack at a distant or adjacent target such as an adventurer on their flank side. Adventurers aren't fond of Sky-Rippers, not because they're particularly dangerous- in fact their bite force isn't particularly strong for a beast their size. No, their annoyance comes from just how slippery such a massive monster can be. Sky-Ripper contracts are common bounties in [[The City of Wyrms]], as their fins are believed to have medicinal properties- specifically for... fertility. Regardless, Adventurers regularly return empty handed as Sky-Rippers know well enough when they're bound to lose, and will flee via portal.

Markus Krauler, a known Sky-Ripper expert claims that he has lost thrice as many Sky-Rippers as he has felled. He has expressed- NUMEROUS TIMES- that Sky Rippers are, in fact, "bastardly cowards of monster kind." Many [[Adventurers]] share his sentiment, though despite this underestimation on behalf of hunters Sky-Rippers can easily turn the tables if they feel completely cornered- specifically if Silence has been cast. Sky-Rippers with nowhere to run flip like a switch once they realize they can no longer flee, thrashing about and biting with feral strength as they fight for their lives.

Personally, I feel as if Sky-Rippers could just be altogether avoided given the stories I've been told. It seems as if the monsters want next to nothing to do with adventurers and would rather be left well enough alone. Markus Claims they always flee, Harold claims he's never so much as been looked at wrong by one, and TRAILA- who provided my quote, mind you- SHOT IT FIRST. By Orpheus, sometimes you just have to remember that these people aren't scholars- hell two thirds of them, maybe more, sign their names with chicken-scratch.

With that out of the way, fighting a Sky-Ripper seems, according to- again- adventurers recounting of their fights altogether easy enough. Slash the stomach, bludgeon the face and poke out the eyes. Killing one, however seems quite the travail and triumph. A spell caster with silence under their repertoire will serve well.

Sky-Rippers prey upon everything they can fit in their mouths- except seemingly adventurers- namely including [[Haymaker Snails]], [[Jeweled Fish]] schools and [[Vorpal Lancers]]. Sky-Rippers are only preyed upon by [[True Wyverns]] and [[Ossuary Titans]]- on that latter part it seems entirely the fault of the Sky-Ripper to be eaten by a giant lumbering titan when it can fly and teleport- but it WAS a one time case and it came from the mouth of Markus, so who knows.
