### [[Monster Callsigns]]-

Weeping Sprites are Elemental [[Monsters]] of [[The West]], being found within [[Sorrowed Weald|The Sorrowed Weald]], though theoretically they could appear anywhere a powerful [[Hydromancy]] [[Sites of Power|source]] is located. Weeping sprites are half foot across glowing blue motes of water, humming just audibly above the constant leave-fall of The Weald. While at first their behavior seems unguided- with them bumbling into trees and bushes aimlessly. On closer inspection- an inspection done by my colleague Ashkram Who Speaks Strange- Weeping Sprites seem to be the crux of the Weald's ecosystem.

Weeping Sprites are rampant decomposers. They wander throughout The Weald searching for decaying plant and monster matter, before latching onto the source of rot and dissolving it within their bodies. Weeping Sprites, being water elementals, do not exactly possess a need for rotting matter. There are theories that [[The Old Empire of The West]] specifically designed the Sprites *for* the Sorrowed Weald, knowing well in advance the amount of rot that would be created by such a massive and dense biosphere. Regardless, Weeping Sprites do in fact dissolve rotting matter, and whether we understand why or not, they and they alone keep The Weald clean and healthy.

This consumption and refinement of decaying matter means that, despite their actual bodies being completely comprised of magic, the contents within a Sprite are edible- at least to desperate monsters. Some plants also exude a rotting smell to attract Sprites- such as the [[Surgin' Surgeon]], a towering, electric plant with spines covering its exterior that can pop a Sprite and cause it to deposit its contents before reforming.

Weeping Sprites are of no harm to [[Adventurers]], with their small bodies and harmless exteriors being of no threat to even the smallest [[Kobolds|Kobold]]. If one cannot find suitable water for drinking, Weeping Sprites can be popped to drop their contents, then once they reform can be drunk straight out of the air without consequence.

Weeping Sprites are only actively eaten by [[Maws]], which are invasive within The Weald. There are concerns that the Maws may inadvertently collapse the entire ecosystem of The Weald and perhaps other regions if they are not controlled in population. Sprites do not prey on any creatures, as is their nature as elementals.
