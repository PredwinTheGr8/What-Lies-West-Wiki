### [[Monster Callsigns|Callsigns]]-

- Smell of Pyromancy- often described as sulfur
- Tracks, Dung, Calls
- High arcane presence, Fae Rifts
- No local eldritch energy presence
- Pitch-black smoke columns
- Increased ambient temperature
- Scorched Regrikgruki Shells
  
Chrozhran Destroyers are [[Legendary Monsters]] of [[The West]] that inhabit both [[The Warbling]] and [[The Devastated Mesas]]. They are some of the largest carnivores inside of The West- with adult specimens reaching one hundred and twenty feet long and upwards of 60 feet wide. They closely resemble the physiology of the ancient Wyrms, though their back wings have devolved into massive arms that the Destroyer uses to support its massive midsection. The scales of a Destroyer are often a deep red- used to camouflage (ineffectively) against the same red cliffsides of The Mesas. These scales are also incredibly heat resistant- allowing the Destroyer to remain suspended in molten lava for an indefinite amount of time.

Like their cousins, the [[True Wyverns]], destroyers need to refuel their "furnace" at sites of incredible heat, such as [[The Warbling]]. Chrozhran Destroyers use their living furnace to utilize pyromancy at devastating levels of power. Destroyers have earned their name fairly: being completely capable of leveling [[The City of Wyrms]] if they were without attrition. The fiery beam of pyromancy produced by a destroyer turns flesh to ash, stone to searing gas, and adamantium into nothing but slag. To be caught in the path of this inferno is to suffer a short, but agonizing death.

Destroyers are incredibly important to the natural health of the entire ecosystem. Their massive size and mobile Site of Pyromancy at adulthood banishes local presence of Eldritch energy. Stray Destroyers that travel across [[The West|The West's]] other [[All Regions of The West|regions]] leave rich ground and blooming flowers in their wake. Water purifies in their presence and eldritch spawn are sent back from whence they came- explaining the complete lack of any spawn in their natural habitats. The dens of Destroyers, though, are more comparable to The [[Phrazmollograggra Mountains]], as the idleness of the Destroyers within means that the magic they produce accumulates fast.

Destroyers are almost completely uninterested in the presence of [[Adventurers|adventurers]]. Ashkram Who Speaks Strange compares adventurers to "insects" next to a Destroyer, and after all "would you pay attention to an insect?" Personally, yes. I would certainly notice a spider crawling next to my face. But still he makes a good point as to why Destroyers don't often attack adventurers- it doesn't make sense calory wise for them, and they aren't a threat to an adult specimen.

Chrozhran, the adventurer who became the namesake of the [[Monsters|monster]], faced off against a younger, smaller Destroyer outside of its den, where it felt threatened enough to attack. Chrozhran made a brave stand to allow her companions to escape, but lost her life in the process. Her sacrifice has been immortalized in the name of the creature, which were previously named Khalkrazdumah, the dwarven name for Wyrm-born.

In combat against even the largest of Destroyers, proximity is key. Destroyers are sluggish and their melee attacks can easily be dodged. Their fire beam possesses the power to damage them as well, meaning that they won't attempt to use it on any close range combatants. Attacking the area around the middle limbs serves best to felling a Destroyer, as the area surrounding the devolved wings was once more flexible to allow movement. Now, the scales here are the weakest and the flesh the thinnest- making for a great target to deal serious damage. The immense heat of the scales makes them susceptible to temperature shock from cryomancy as well, further weakening the defenses of a Destroyer. Despite their size compared to [[True Wyverns]], the grounded nature of Destroyers means that far more of them have been killed compared to the ever cunning Wyverns. With absolutely zero confirmed True Wyvern kills compared to the three adult Destroyer kills.

Destroyers feast on adult [[Regrikgruki]], [[Chasm Crawlers|Chasm Crawler]] Matrons, low flying [[True Wyverns]] and their [[Wyverns|fakes]], stray Alpha [[Maws]] of extreme size, [[Bloat Kings]] and even [[Ossuary Titans]]. They are in turn preyed upon by [[Ossuary Titans]], other Destroyers, the most massive of [[Inverters]], [[Dunewakers]]- and one has been seen receiving the "judgment" of [[The Annihilator]].
