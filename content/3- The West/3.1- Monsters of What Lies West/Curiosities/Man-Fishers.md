"An explosion- mighty and loud. A harpoon- accurate and barbed. A scream- of terror and agony. A quiet- of realization and fear. A trek- one man short, one man dead. Woe, unto ye who might experience such a thing first hand. Woe, even more, should thou be the one struck."

-Ashkram Who Speaks Strange, [[Scholars|scholar]] and [[Adventurers|adventurer]]

## [[Monster Callsigns|Callsigns]]-

- Luck: the creaking of a branch, the flutter of a bird, the moment of breath before the fireball.

Man-Fishers are [[Monsters]] of [[The West]] that solely inhabit [[The Sorrowed Weald|The Sorrowed Weald]]. Visual accounts of Man-Fishers are rarely recorded, but the few sightings are differed and argued upon. The only commonality is a harpoon-like instrument somewhere along the body, and even the silhouette is argued.

Man-Fishers earn their name from that harpoon- a barbed, bony instrument that more closely resembled something found on a whaling vessel rather than any natural weapon. Survivors of Man-Fishers- those with keen teammates that cut the harpoons tendon-reel and had healers nearby- recount that there is a paralytic poison on the barb that numbed their senses and confused them for a short while even after receiving healing magic.

Man-Fishers are unmatched in camouflage- so adapted to hiding that even scrying [[Magic|magic]] cannot detect them. The most daunting, though, of their adaptations is their mastery of pyromancy. All stories of Man-Fishers involve some sort of explosion before the harpoon struck a party member. Scholars, such as Ashkram, believe that this is the complex use of magic in the form of a fireball spell being used to propel the harpoon.

Adventurers discount this, as many monsters that use magic cannot use such powerful spells, and not as quickly back to back as a Man-Fisher would have to. They also say things along the line of "If it can cast fireball, why doesn't it just use that to kill us?" Admittedly, that makes a good case against the Man-Fisher mastering pyromancy. Nonetheless, scholars stand by their decision.

Combat against a Man-Fisher is decided in seconds. If the Man-Fisher skewers and reels a party member away, the monster will flee into the dark undergrowth of the [[Weeping Titans]]. If the harpoon misses, it may try again unless the connecting tendon is cut away, when it will once more retreat immediately. 

Depending on their size, Man-Fishers may be the apex predators of [[The Sorrowed Weald|The Sorrowed Weald]], preying on almost all of its denizens. Whether or not any monsters, such as perhaps a [[Creeping Toads|Creeping Toad]], prey upon a Man-Fisher is, again, up to the size and agility of the monster. Until a dead one is recovered its impossible to say where it truly lies on the food chain. It is safe to say, however, that [[Walking Bastions]] are immune to their harpoons, suggesting that creatures of such a massive size are not safe from predation. But again, speculation can only go so far.
