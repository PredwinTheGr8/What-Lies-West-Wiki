### [[Monster Callsigns|Callsigns]]-

- None

Man-Fishers are [[Legendary Monsters]] of [[The West]] that solely reside within [[The Sorrowed Weald]]. They are perfect killers that in 60 years of adventuring have had not as much of a glimpse of a carapace or a scrap of hair found. Nobody knows what the creatures look like, what they smell like, what they sound like- save for their method of bringing death. Man-Fishers are, perhaps, the smartest non-spawn creatures in all of The West, even rivaling the much more agreeable [[Mire Hulks]] from [[The Blasted Basin]] and their cousins the [[Swamp Hulks]] inside the very same [[All Regions of The West|region]] the Man-Fishers hunt in.

Man-Fishers earn their namesake from their method of hunting. Groups trekking through [[The Sorrowed Weald|The Weald]] may suddenly hear an explosion, like that of a ship cannons, and turn to see one of their number skewered through by a large boney harpoon. This and this alone is the only 'part' of a Man-Fisher that has ever been catalogued. If a party doesn't respond quickly the Man-Fisher will quickly reel in their catch at roughly 10 feet per second. It only takes a blink for someone to disappear into the canopy, and only another few seconds for their remains to drop down.

Some think that Man-Fishers *are* spawn, as many records of Man-Fisher attacks state the creatures maim and kill an adventurer before dropping the uneaten corpse from the trees. While horrifying, it simply seems that Man-Fishers sometimes hunt entirely for sport, as can be seen with numerous other monster corpses within [[The Sorrowed Weald|The Weald]]. [[Ouberinth]], [[Riphawgs]] even [[Death Knights]] can be found skewered and left to rot. Some of these corpses do still have the harpoon inside the flesh, suggesting either the creature managed to get away before dying or that the Man-Fisher used the creature to dispose of a dull or faulty harpoon. The latter option seems likely as from the corpses I found during my first expedition all had misshapen or broken harpoons inside, with the tendon connecting the harpoon still dangling from the bone of the harpoon.

But Man-Fishers *do* still eat to survive. They prefer to string up their prey in large nets of vile, green ichor and vines. Sometimes these corpses will rest near or directly underneath the trap that is a [[Rippervines|Rippervine]]. A show of cunning that may trap nosey creatures like [[Riphawgs]] or [[Arcanosaurs]] that come to find what's rotting, only to be eaten by the Man-Fisher later.

But one creature, above all others, seems to have gained the Man-Fishers interest... or ire, pick whichever you like most. The [[Walking Bastions]], mountains of armor and defenses like an organic flechette cannon, but you can find more on that in the Bastion's own document. The two creatures seem to be evolving to counter one another. One has excellent eyesight and immense armor, the other perfect stealth and a natural naval cannon. Walking Bastions are the only creature in The Weald that actually stand a good chance at killing or at least maiming a Man-Fisher. Adult [[Creeping Toads]] also likely pose some kind of threat, as they *are* the most poisonous [[monsters]] in [[The West]] and consuming them cannot be good for any creatures digestion.

Man Fishers only seem to have developed this long range disjointed weapon in order to properly predate upon [[Walking Bastions]]. Without such a rival its impossible to tell what Man-Fishers would look like today, or vise versa.

Combat against a Man-Fisher is all up to fleeing. While I cannot directly recommend putting yourself in danger- smaller or weaker members of a party are the usual target of a Man-Fisher, with Mages, [[Kobolds]] and [[Goblins]] being prime targets. Man-Fishers have learned to leave corpses in trees for Clerics of [[Orpheus]] to find- only to trap or kill the priests. While none have lost their lives yet, the foul beasts have tried regardless.

Man-Fishers predate, or at least hunt, everything- or at least they try to. Several [[Ossuary Titans]] have been seen with a harpoon or two... or two dozen hanging from their torsos and necks. As for normal prey, anything the size of a [[Walking Bastions|Walking Bastion]] or smaller is fair game for the creatures. And sometimes even that might not stop them as with the above Titans and even in one case a dead [[True Wyverns|True Wyvern]] with a harpoon found impaling its throat, aimed perfectly at the triangular weak spot near its collarbone- a weak point no adventurer has ever managed to hit. With not a single corpse ever being found, some believe that Man-Fishers have no predators, and are only kept in check by their slow breeding or perhaps infighting. It is speculated that like Mammoths or [[Harush|Harushian]] Elephants, Man-Fishers have a collective graveyard they go to when they die. The more horrifying answer is that Man-Fishers *don't* die, and can live forever.
