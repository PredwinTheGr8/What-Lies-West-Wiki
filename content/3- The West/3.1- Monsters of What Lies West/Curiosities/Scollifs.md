### [[Monster Callsigns|Callsigns]]-

- Angelic singing
- Worm-like tracks in the sand
- Cold sands

Scollifs are peaceful, docile [[monsters]] of [[The West]] that primarily reside within [[The True Desert]], though they can be found in any [[All Regions of The West|region]] exposed to the sun with a sandy floor. Visually, a standard Scollifs resembles a large sea jelly, though with an oblong, ovular shape instead of a circular one. Blue 'worker' Scollifs are the most common, though any Scollifs is a rare sight in The West. These Scollifs average at two and a half feet long, and usually hover roughly three inches above the sands. Next up in size are the 'guard' that only appear in Scollifs 'Harmonies' with a Queen. Guard take on a yellow coloration, average five and three quarters of a foot long and hover at a foot above the sand- allowing them to see over their smaller fellows. Guard will remain near the center of Harmonies closer to the groupings Queen.

The Queen is solely responsible for the reproduction of more Scollifs. A speculated fourth type of Scollifs is attributed to being the 'male' of the species, though no visual  confirmation has been made on any subjects. Scollifs Queens average twelve feet long with a bright red coloration, hovering an average of three inches above the ground, the same as 'worker' Scollifs. It is speculated that, given the solidarity and lack of resource movement between individual Scollifs, their numbers are solely for safety. An individual Scollifs, other than the Queen, gets no benefits from its fellows. Each Scollifs is responsible for their own food, water and therefor survival. The Queen only receives the defense of the toxin-filled stingers of her Guard, and the promise that her own children will be eaten long before she will.

Some Harmonies can be found without a Queen. [[scholars]] believe that these Harmonies are perhaps victim to an unseen predator specialized to hunting a Scollifs herd. Another potential, more boring option, is that Scollifs are not particularly intelligent, and that parts of a Harmony simply split off and lose their parent Harmony. Whatever the case, some groups are doomed to die out one by one.

More onto the individual physiology of a Scollifs, then?

Scollifs are host to a dense outer layer of glass tinted by chemicals within their body. This glass like shell is, in fact, quite dense and capable of resisting rather extreme damage. During the day, Scollifs will accumulate [[Pyromancy]] from the sands using complex tendril structures protruding from their 'belly' region. These tendrils are incredibly hot due to the nature of their arcane contents, being capable of searing flesh at a mere touch, even through mail. The top of a Scollifs is rather cool though, comparatively at least- glass under the sun is never a smart thing to sit on. At the front of a Scollifs, two large clusters of 'eyes' sit, though they lack any organic components. These structures react to light and shadows, but can be focused to identify individual lights and colors.

This allows, although crudely, for the Scollifs to identify one another and remain in a group. Perhaps crudely is the wrong word though. Its unfair to suggest that Scollifs are anything less than a perfectly adapted organism to [[The West]]. Nothing else has it as easy as a Scollifs, even [[True Wyverns]] can starve- but no matter the path trodden by a Scollifs, the road is always laden with their food. Regardless, the existence of Scollifs puts into question whether they naturally came about after the calamity that destroyed [[The Old Empire of The West]] or were instead created before the fall. Whatever the case, Scollifs are an absolute curiosity without real rhyme or reason for existing.

Scollifs are rarely predated upon, only consumable by other Pyromantic creatures such as [[True Wyverns]] or [[Chrozhran Destroyers]]. [[Arcanosaurs]], [[Ironback Reapers]] and idiotic [[Maws]] will consume Scollifs, with the latter two often dying as a result, though Arcanosaurs are capable of withstanding the heat. The inside of a Scollifs is the only nutritious part, being comprised of a protein dense super-organ that science has yet to provide a purpose of. Scollifs will absorb any small creatures that get in their way, but this serves no purpose to giving them energy, as the flesh of, per say a Jerboa, is instantly reduced to useless ash on contact.

The 'liver' organ of a Scollifs is a primary ingredient in countless potions and concoctions, namely [[Healing Potions]]. Handle with care while it cools.
