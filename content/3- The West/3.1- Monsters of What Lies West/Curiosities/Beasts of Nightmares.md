### [[Monster Callsigns|Callsigns]]-

- Negative emotions and pain become dulled
- High pitched 'bark' noises
- Easier time catching one's breath

"Beasts of Nightmares", or as I will call them 'Lapdogs' are '[[monsters]]' of [[The West]] native to [[The Creeping Peninsula|The Creeping Peninsula]], though they are found wandering [[The True Desert|between regions]] as they hop between their natural environment and [[The Swallowed City|The Swallowed City]]. Lapdogs visually resemble nothing on our world that is born of nature- being foggy masses of dark energy no larger than a chicken. The form of a Lapdog is that of a quadrupedal creature, with two motes of bright red light for eyes. The 'skin' of a lapdog is incredibly soft, impossibly so some say.

Lapdogs earn their name- the name *I've* given them- from their behaviors surrounding sapient creatures, namely [[adventurers]]. Once a lapdog detects a weary adventurer or other intelligent creature- an ability with a roughly mile wide radius. Once it finds a 'lap-host', the Lapdog will attempt to maintain a close proximity to the creature. At this point, many knowledgeable adventurers will reach down and pick up the Lapdog for a short while- roughly thirty minutes to an hour- as they travel. Lapdogs weigh barely anything, and with frequent swaps between arms can be carried indefinitely by the average adventurer.

While maintaining contact with a creature, the Lapdog saps away negative energy and pain. This action seems to be the primary, and thus far only, food source of a Lapdog. Adventurers what maintain prolonged contact with a Lapdog report that all aches, pains and negative or exhausting thoughts vanish. While a Lapdog can help to negate it, fatigue will not vanish when touching a Lapdog.

Most often, the slow pace of a Lapdog means that they will not arrive next to their lap-host until their party goes to sleep for the night, meaning that nine times out of ten the lap-host only finds out there's a Lapdog when they wake up to see the creature laying on their chest or curled up near their body. Those who awaken to find a Lapdog on their person report that they had the best sleep of their lives, waking up refreshed and without so much as a mild ache in any joint.

Lapdogs are seemingly capable of curing most illnesses that come with old age, and may even revert some of the aging process with prolonged exposure- though they cannot revert decades of time on a person. Notably though, arthritis can often be cured outright by a Lapdog- though of course [[Healing Potions]] and [[Heliomancy]] can accomplish the same.

Lapdogs are not present in a larger food chain, being unpalatable by most other [[monsters]]. [[Seamsters]] often have multiple Lapdogs inside of their dens, suggesting a very important mutualistic relationship between the creatures.
