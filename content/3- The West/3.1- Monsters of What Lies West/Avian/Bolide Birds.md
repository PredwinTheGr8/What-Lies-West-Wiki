### [[Monster Callsigns|Callsigns]]-

- Static charge
- Increased local gravity
- Crude Graviturgy glyphs
- Frequent mind whispers

Bolide Birds are [[Monsters|monsters]] of [[The West]] that primarily reside within [[The Devastated Mesas|The Devastated Mesas]]. They distantly resemble crows and other corvids from The East, mixed heavily with other flying animals such as bats and pseudodragons. They often possess a wingspan of twelve feet, and range between five and seven feet tall. The primary characteristic of a Bolide Bird is its adept use of Graviturgy- with the creatures largely possessing a rudimentary understanding of basic primordial glyphs. Bolide Birds use these crude glyphs and innate spellcasting knowledge to trap and kill their prey, which often includes incautious [[Adventurers|adventurers]].

Bolide Birds will typically fly far above the ground during the early morning and late evening, when prey is the most active and they can avoid burning in the light of the Tyrannical Sun. Once a Bolide Bird spots prey it earns its namesake as it puts its arcane prowess to the upper limit. The Bird will swoop down and engage its spell- greatly increasing its own gravity to an utterly insane degree and shielding itself with thaumaturgy to cushion its fall. If one were to look up, they would see a shooting star of fire... heading straight towards them.

Several, inexplicable booms will shake the ears of any within miles of the Bird as it speeds up, before finally making impact with a giant ball of fire. Survivors of a Bolide Bird attack often report that even after the Bird had fled to recover, they thought they were already dead. Their ears ringing, their body shaking, their hair scorched, its hard not to understand.

Bolide Birds show some notable weaknesses that can be exploited- namely that they fear water, as their additional weight combined with wet feathers is a bad combination for making a quick getaway. Its safest to stay near the small channels between the Mesas in case a Bolide Bird has its eyes on you.

Bolide Birds prey on anything within their home, as their hunting tactics are perfect for punching through the armor of the giant beasts that call the Mesas home, though if they mess up their shot the tables easily turn.
