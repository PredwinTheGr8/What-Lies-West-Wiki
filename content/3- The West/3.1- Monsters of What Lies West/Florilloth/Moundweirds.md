"And then we- we uh-... Did you hear that? No, no I'm not kidding Andrich. Harold wake up Traila. We got a- A MOUNWEIRD!"

-Girtz Ribmangler during my first, and frankly awful, expedition to [[The West]].

### [[Monster Callsigns|Florillith Callsigns]]-

- Smell of rot
- Strung up, organ-less carcasses
- Scraping sounds, dragging noises- described as a soggy towel on sand

Within [[The Crimson Expanse]] of [[The West]] many horrid, voracious [[Florillith]] lurk in underneath the [[Vampire Weed]] or sit dormant under the blood red tyrant son. One of them, though- above all others and even some flesh and blood monsters.

Moundweirds, [[Legendary Monsters|Legendary]] Florillith of The West.

Moundweirds are hulking masses of plant matter- shambling mounds of rotting leaves hiding a center of spindly roots from the "real" plant. Moundweirds widely vary in size acrost all specimens, but the oldest are consistently between twenty and forty feet tall, with an estimated weight of seventy to ninety tons. Visually, Moundweirds live up to their name as strange, nearly shapeless masses of plant life. The only consistent parts are legs to move and a "head" like structure at the top, being the fruiting body of the true Moundweird beneath all of the infested plant matter on its surface.

Moundweirds are hard to discern from their surroundings at times. Most flora within [[The Crimson Expanse]] does not rot and holds a black or dark purple color. Moundweirds are covered in rotting, green-blue flora, meaning that at some point, or at multiple points more likely, a Moundweird will migrate to [[The Sorrowed Weald]], where it sources rotting or living plant life. The rotting of the plant life creates heat, which in turn energizes a Moundweird, it also creates water to protect a Moundweird from direct sunlight from The Tyrant.

Particularly old Moundweirds are nearly entirely fire-retardant, as the moisture stored in their fake flesh is too vast and too cool to be caught fire efficiently, with any fires only going a few feet before stopping. The dense, numerous layers of plant matter also make impacts with jabbing or blunt weapons rapidly lose momentum. Only rapid slashes can make any meaningful damage against the dense flesh of a Moundweird, with even some magics being near-useless as they can even fail to target the Moundweird as its living parts can be so buried beneath the flesh.

Onto their actual interactions with mortals, then.

Moundweirds need biological matter for... something. Be it plant life- the husk of an [[Arcanosaurs|Arcanosaur]] or an entire [[Monster Group Names|Glimpse]] of Grolles, Moundweirds actively hunt down sources of energy without rest. Fortunately Moundweirds aren't particularly smart, and an [[Adventurers|adventurer]] or other creature in proper hiding won't be found. Those huddled around a campfire though, or galivanting through the sands will be easy to detect as the Moundweird does posses rudimentary tactile, visual and hearing senses.

Moundweirds gather this bio matter for as of yet unknown reasons. Its possible it could be solely for nutrition but some Moundweirds go far past simple accruement of resources. Theories pose that at one point Moundweirds were made artificially to find and accumulate sources of rot similar to the [[Weeping Sprites]] of [[The Sorrowed Weald]]. After recent... dreams I think its possible that Moundweirds were meant to clean out the dead during the fall of [[The Old Empire of The West]]. Whatever the case, now Moundweirds bludgeon mortals to death and shove their corpses into their walking compost bin of a body.

The "true" body of a Moundweird is a dense, wiry matt of roots and sensory structures that mostly occupy the central region of the body, similar to a mortal's skeleton. Small pockets of sensory structures will breach the flesh slightly, giving the Moundweird its senses to explore the world. At the top, a horrid mass of compound eyes sits. While they cannot see very far, they offer complete vision all around the Moundweird. These eyes are still from that of the true plant beneath, meaning that these are actual examples of plant-made eye structures- incredible, really.

As I hinted towards, these notes came from my first ever excursion into [[The West]]. Were I not physically present for examination after Girtz, Harold and Traila, quite frankly brutalized the Moundweird they said that it would have rotted into pulp within a day. I asked to confirm this but they wanted to keep moving, so I simply took a large sample along with us. And true to their statement it rapidly began to lose mass and water content. After examination I found a chemical solution being spewed from the true body of the Moundweird. This solution wasn't particularly corrosive to my own skin, nor the small chunks of meat within the Moundweird, but to plant matter was a catastrophically acidic compound. I suspect that within [[The Crimson Expanse]] dead Moundweirds are what create the medium sized pockets of forestry in the region comprised of non-native plants, as hardy seeds can survive the acid and are left surrounded by already processed fertilizer many seeds get a rapid head start when a Moundweird is felled.

In combat against a Moundweird swords and axes are most effective, though the latter may become jammed as the plant matter compresses around it. As a fight with a Moundweird drags on it well get faster and faster as it losses chunks of the heavy plant matter weighing its parts down. Finishing a fight quickly is key to avoiding exhaustion with larger Moundweirds. Once parts of the true body have been exposed, targeting them causes immense physical reactions with the Moundweird- visibly it resembles pain reception. Attacking the pale-white roots when possible will deter the Moundweird and even cause it to flee with sustained attacks.

Moundweirds are preyed upon only by accident from creatures that mistake them for something living- and of course having the required force to actually kill a Moundweird. This includes [[monsters]] like [[Chrozhran Destroyers]], [[Plaguewings]], [[Errikxgizes]] and other large monsters. Moundweirds themselves make a "meal" of anything smaller than them. Within their native habitat of [[The Crimson Expanse]] this normally includes [[Aberrant Jerboa]], but outside of it they may consume [[Maws]], [[Mire Hulks]], [[Bone Dogs]] or any other medium-sized Monster.
