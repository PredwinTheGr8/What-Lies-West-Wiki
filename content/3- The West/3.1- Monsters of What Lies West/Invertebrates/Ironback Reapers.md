### [[Monster Callsigns|Callsigns]]-

- Oily, slightly sulfuric smell, like that of a disturbed cockroach or earwig.
- Faint clicking noises
- Carcasses with untouched eyes

Ironback Reapers are the smallest members of [[The Reaper Family]], consisting of [[Mithrilback Reapers|Mithrilback]] and [[Adamant Reapers|Adamant]] Reapers as well. As [[monsters]] of [[The West]], Ironbacks reside mostly within [[The True Desert]] although they can be found in any area with significant sand coverage like [[The Blasted Basin]], [[The Void Dunes|Void Dunes]], [[The Ossuary Reach|Ossuary Reach]] and [[The Devastated Mesas]]. Ironback Reapers earn their name for their off-white backs, which take on a smelted iron-like coloring, and from their strangely shaped stingers. Ironbacks and their cousins you see, are scorpions, at least at one point in time they were. They posses two sets of compound eyes and two pairs of standard eyes at the ends of stalks. These stalk-mounted eyes are used to take brief and infrequent glimpses at the surface, as Reapers are subterranean dwelling ambush hunters.

Ironback Reapers and their cousin the Mithrilback are the only ones to possess these binocular stalks, and use them to determine whether a series of tremors is from prey or predator before they make the mistake of trying to pin down something like an [[Arcanosaurs|Arcanosaur]], which could easily scorch a Reaper into a husk. Another common threat to the health of an Ironback is the consumption of a [[Scollifs]]. Scollifs levitate off the ground through magics, but the heat of their body often injures a buried Reaper, causing the arthropod to emerge and attack the Scollifs out of self defense- usually also resulting in a scorched shell being the only thing left of the Reaper.

 Ironbacks measure roughly five feet long with an additional two from their tail. The claws of males are usually one foot long as well, while females are often one and a half to two feet long. At largest, accounting claws and tail, an Ironback Reaper may become nine feet long or so. Their stinger- the reason for the 'reaper' part of their name- unlike the mundane members of the order Scorpiones have stingers that face inwards to their body, and are made entirely of metals like copper or iron- sometimes both. This creates a large, sharp scythe that gives them the title of reaper. Ironback reapers are non-venomous, and the only threat they pose comes from direct injury rather than any poison.
 
 Adult Reapers are often the target given to rookie [[adventurers]], who's mentors direct them to one in order to test their metal. As some of the weakest monsters in [[The West]] Ironbacks are a perfect target for newcomers to ensure they're ready for what comes next. A group of Ironback Reapers isn't called a [[Monster Group Names|Tutorial]] for nothing after all. Their tomalleys- the liver and pancreas of many arthropods- are harvested and steeped with water to create a horrid, though fortitude inducing, tonic known as [[Tomalley 'Tea']].
 
 In combat against an Ironback Reaper severing or crushing the claws and then neutralizing the tail leaves the creature defenseless and easy to cull. They are a simple foe to bring down and can feed roughly 6 or so people once slain, making them a valuable target for [[adventurers]] to take on.
 
 Ironback Reapers prey on normal and [[Aberrant Jerboa]], infant [[Inverters]], [[Shamblers]] on their way to [[The City of Wyrms]], [[Gnolls]], Injured [[Arcanosaurs]], stray [[Thunderjaws]], grounded young [[Plaguewings]], [[Skrin]] and other small roamers like lone [[Maws]]. They are in turn preyed upon by [[Arcanosaurs]] most regularly, with very few other predators being able to actually locate them- [[Inverters]] may be a suspect but given the sessile nature of both monsters it's doubtful it happens often. [[Scollifs]] only ever kill Ironback Reapers on accident and obviously cannot eat their kill given their nature.
