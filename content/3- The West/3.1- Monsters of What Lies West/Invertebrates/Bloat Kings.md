### [[Monster Callsigns|Callsigns]]-

- "Slurping" noises
- Presence of rot
- 'Hellishly' rancid smells

Bloat Kings are [[monsters]] of [[The West]] that roam between [[The Ossuary Reach]], [[The Devastated Mesas|Devastated Mesas]] and [[The Sorrowed Weald|Sorrowed Weald]]. Bloat Kings closely resemble massive flies, with adult specimens who found stable sources of food weighing nearly three tons according to scholarly adventurers like Skriek and Ashkram. Harold Green's party once reported a Bloat King three times the record size, though obviously cannot exactly prove it with the creatures size.

Bloat Kings are primary decomposers for regions they appear in. With the Bloat Kings increasing in size between certain regions following their migration to adulthood. Starting as nymphs, Bloat 'Princes' begin their lives within [[The Devastated Mesas]]. Here, they primarily collect the pollen of [[Canyon Lilies]] and feast on dead monsters such as [[Regrikgruki]], [[Chasm Crawlers]] and maybe if they're lucky a dead [[Chrozhran Destroyers|Chrozhran Destroyer]]. Moving past their infantile stage, the Bloat Prince migrates to [[The Sorrowed Weald]], where its larger size allows it to avoid carnivorous plant life such as [[Vissouvois]] and [[Surgin' Surgeon|Surgin' Surgeons]]. Here, it likely takes the opportunity to ingest more [[Magic|magic]]-rich monsters such as [[Walking Bastions]], [[Creeping Toads]] and even perhaps the ever illusive [[Man-Fishers]].

Finally, after reaching sizes large enough to question how it maintains flight at all, the Bloat King will migrate to [[The Ossuary Reach]], where its now immense size means it can anchor itself in place onto, under, or inside of one of the giant ever-rotting carcasses within The Reach. Upon reaching this size, nothing native to The Reach can kill a Bloat King, though many may take a bite or two in passing it will not result in great harm to the Bloat King, as they keep a dense layer of foam between their skin and organs. This foam is noxious even to the natives of The Reach such as [[Bone Dogs]]. Finally, after a massive amount of time feasting and feasting on flesh, the Bloat King will burst apart into a cloud of their nymphs that will migrate back to The Devastated Mesas.

Bloat Kings play too massive a role in the ecosystems they find themselves in to name all of their predators, but namely they are prized by [[Chasm Crawlers|Chasm Crawler]] Hunters, [[Creeping Toads]] and [[Chrozhran Destroyers]] when fully grown. Sometimes a Bloat King will consume something infected by [[The Deep]] and[[Mutants|mutate]] into a [[Devourer]].
