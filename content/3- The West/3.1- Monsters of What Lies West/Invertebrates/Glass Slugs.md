(From Kendamarania)

"Once, when we were traveling through [[The Crimson Expanse|The Crimson Expanse]], our mage, Humphrey, started getting real sluggish like. He had trouble casting even the most basic of his spells. Finally, after he collapsed we pulled his clothes off and found them- dozens of them all over his body. Glass Slugs had sapped him dry."

-Rem'ka Who Scours Wastes, [[Adventurers|adventurer]]

### [[Monster Callsigns|Callsigns]]-

- Visible presence on the ground
- Long, serpentine trails
- dizziness, fatigue
- inability to cast high tier magics

Glass Slugs are [[Monsters|monsters]] of [[The West]] that primarily inhabit [[The Crimson Expanse|The Crimson Expanse]], though they can accidentally be carried to other [[All Regions of The West|regions]]. Glass Slugs earn their name from their semi-translucent body, allowing one to see their inner organs and veins. The slugs hunt using primal magics to sense prey, before latching on around the lower legs and feet and siphoning [[Magic|magic]] from their host.

Glass Slugs remain largely sedimentary until a host is detected, a survival instinct to hide from the mobile roots of the region. Glass Slugs also exude a corrosive slime, that burns through flesh and leather, but also doubles as protection from any would-be florous carnivores. Despite the corrosive slime, the numbing agents within prevent most discomfort felt by the acids, allowing the glass slug to remain attached for long periods of time before getting noticed by their host.

While attached, either to a living or dead host, Glass Slugs will siphon off and subsist on the mana produced or left behind from their host. A person with only a handful of Glass Slugs on their body may take days to notice them- as alone, they don't consume much mana. A person with many, however, will begin to experience nausea, dizziness, and a lack of arcane control. To safely remove a Glass Slug, a sheet of corrosive resistant material, such as- ironically- glass or steel can be slid underneath the slug, then it can either be tossed away or stomped on.

**To avoid a Glass Slug entirely, paying attention to the ground for their contrasting bodies against the sand, or dousing one's feet with saltwater can help one avoid or dissuade the creatures.**

Glass Slugs are parasitic, not predatory in nature and thus do not prey on any other creatures, though they can on accident kill hosts with numerous Slugs all siphoning from them. They are preyed upon by [[Aberrant Jerboa]] and [[Scrawlers]]. Every predatory plant within [[The Crimson Expanse|The Crimson Expanse]] also often makes a meal of them.
