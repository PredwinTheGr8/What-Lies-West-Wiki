### [[Monster Callsigns|Callsigns]]-

- Visual presence, buzzing sounds

Vren are '[[monsters]]' of [[The West]] found within [[The Sorrowed Weald]]. At a cursory glance they resemble eastern bees, at a closer one though they are revealed to be far removed from the standard bee 'pattern' as it were. Vren possess circular, oscillating mouths, allowing them to cut, bite and even pollinate with their fuzzy little tongues. Their bite is noted as "painful, but not deadly." Vren are opportunistic decomposers, being primarily pollinators and light predators. Colonies of Vren, being upwards of three hundred or so members strong, can be found in the only dry places within The Weald- the undersides of [[Corsair's Balconies]]. These nests are made from processed plant fibers, namely comprised of [[Madman's Requiem]], though the chemicals responsible for delirium are long since inert.

Instead, the smell of a dead Requiem attracts other insects to the nest, which are either consumed by the Vren or incorporated into the hive if the attracted insect is a [[Vell Beetles|Vell Beetle]]. Vell Beetles and Vren 'Bees' are mutualistically symbiotic- a term that means that both creatures benefit from one another. I really need to stop using such big words don't I? Anyways, Vell Beetles, as their name suggests, are [[Electromancy|electromancers]]. Vren will feed Vell Beetles and offer them shelter, in exchange the Vell is expected to defend the colony from more resilient predators like [[Creeping Toads]] when they appear.

While Vren do possess stingers, many creatures from The Weald have actually adapted a mucous lining to resist the insects' barbs. The bond with Vell Beetles ensures that despite this, the colony remains defended from large predators. This sting is still painful for [[adventurers]] to experience, though obviously [[Risen Ones]] are immune to pain, and [[Lizardfolk]] are resistant to the sting as well. Instead, this poison is used to paralyze other invertebrates- creatures without spines- the same size as the Vren. Dead insects are then brought to the colony and made into food or used to reinforce key chambers in the hive.

As opportunistic decomposers, Vren can be found abuzz surrounding dead [[Monsters]]. Though they have competition in [[Bloat Kings]], who also prize rotted flesh, the sheer number of Vren means they often come out successful with giant meat chunks, even if they lose a handful to a Bloat King.

Vren are actively preyed upon solely by [[Creeping Toads]], who hold invertebrates as their sole food source during their youth. When adults though, Creeping Toads prefer more... warm-blooded prey. Some Vren Colonies have been found scorched and impaled by a sharp object, suggesting that [[Man-Fishers]] hold distain for the little insects.
