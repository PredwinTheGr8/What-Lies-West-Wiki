### [[Monster Callsigns|Callsigns]]-

- Trails of slime
- Smell of [[Transmutation]], often described as ammonia
- Snapping noises- like that of distant gunfire
- Bony, harpoon-like protrusions embedded in soft terrain
- Predators, such as [[Vorpal Lancers]], found dead via blunt force trauma, acid burns or blood loss, caused by aforementioned harpoons
- Large shed off snail shells
- Higher ambient temperature, smell of [[Pyromancy]], often described as sulfurous

Haymaker snails are invertebrate [[monsters]] of [[The West]] that reside within both [[The Shelled Trenches]] and [[The Blasted Basin]]. Haymaker Snails are semi-sessile devout herbivores that use their radula to scrape off the moss growing on the cliffsides of The Trenches, or to collect and swallow up the dense [[Mire Weed]] of The Basin's many oases. Visually, Haymaker snails are, to a point, very large snails, at least in male specimens who's colors are very vibrant regardless. Male Haymakers possess large, spiral shells that are used primarily to house fatty tissues. Normal snails of course instead use their shells to protect their organs, meaning either some gruesome mutations have occurred here, or regular snails and the Haymaker snails have a distant, pre-shell ancestor. Interesting to contemplate either way I suppose.

Most of a Haymakers 'non-vital' organs are kept close to the end of their tails. These organs are coated with 'propionate,' a name coined by fellow [[Scholars|Scholar]] Ivan Von Conrad of course. This compound, according to his research, grants a sense of satiety in those who consume it, meaning that if any creatures take a bite from a Haymaker Snail they may begin to feel 'full' even if they're near starvation. Organs in this area of the Snail include; Liver, kidneys, gallbladder, [[Pyromancy|pyromancy]] bladder (in females) and the [[Transmutation|transmutation]] bladder (in males).

One might say, 'But these organs sound incredibly vital, how on earth could they be described in the inverse of such an importance.' And to that I would say keep reading and you'll find out you impatient little gnome. The answer is astonishing though really; another organ is kept deep behind the fatty tissues in the snail's shell. This organ reproduces damaged or lost organs, and the cells within the snail *move* out of the organs path before it is reattached. I had my own doubts, but using arcane methods I was granted the opportunity to study one up close during my first expedition into [[The West]].

And, I must say it's simply just a truth of this creature that it can regenerate organs and replace them. But, I'm sure that the average reader, likely an [[Adventurers|adventurer]], isn't reading this to find out how they can 'walk' off grievous injury, but instead to find out how that 'damned haymaker' happens. At the base of the shell, a massive muscle is wound up tight, and then released. That's it- simple really. When the *male* specimen is threatened you can hear this muscle 'winding up' so the speak as microscopic tension pockets pop, making loud snapping sounds that almost resemble a close miss from a rifle shot. The shell is then released, shooting out thanks to elastic tendons that connect it, making contact with the aggressor and causing massive blunt force trauma.

Male Haymaker snails will only use this if approached and aggravated, as this action uses significant amounts of energy to repeat, and with repetitive use can even wear down the connection between shell and snail- causing the two to separate and resulting in the snail dying within hours from both infection and trauma. I realize now as I write this that it *is* strange that there are different genders between Haymaker snails as regular ones obviously do NOT have differing genders and instead are hermaphroditic. Oh well, as if [[Adventurers|adventurers]] care about the smaller details.

Male Haymakers use primal [[Transmutation]] [[Magic]] to enhance their muscles and repair minor damage. This magic is likely what allows them to regrow and move around organs as well if I had to assume. Doubling back to that point it is rare to find a male Haymaker missing some of their organs, as usually their other defense is more than enough to dissuade predators.

Both male and female snails also possess the ability to vomit their stomach acid to blind predators or attackers. This substance is enhanced by high oxygen presence and rapidly burns through flesh on contact. It can be neutralized by rinsing with water or by coating in copious amounts of sand if water is not on hand. Sanitizing the wound is highly recommended afterwards to avoid infection. Moving on then, *female* Haymakers are by far the most striking in appearance.

Females are larger, and more of a sallow shade compared to the bright and colorful male. Their shells are a spiral cone covered in pores that house large bony spines, though they are made of keratin, and not calcium. Female's shells are far larger compared to their bodies when measured up against males. Males have a 20-30% of their mass in their shell, whereas females have 60-70% of their mass within theirs. It should be noted that even without the shell, females would still be thrice the size of a normal male- with males measuring between 6-9 feet and females 11-15 feet at adulthood.
Females still posses the organ-making organ in their shells, but also house smaller organs that produce the spines that jut out from their shells. These spines are moved via the same mechanism by which the male specimen moves their organs, though the female lacks any bladders that house transmutation magic. This suggests that males don't use their [[Transmutation|transmutation]] magic to move their cells around, but I'm not ready to think about that right now and until I have the willpower and coffee in bulk to go back out into [[The West]] again I am *NOT* trying to finding out.

Instead of a [[Transmutation|transmutation]] bladder, female Haymakers possess a [[Pyromancy|pyromancy]] bladder. This organ supplies conflagratory fumes to the base of each spine. Adding two and two together you can assume what happens when one of these sacks is set alight- the spine is shot out. This, as with the males 'haymaker' special, is how the female defends herself from threats. The cone can be turned and angled all around the top of the female haymaker, meaning no safe angle for attack other than from straight below the snail is completely safe.

[[Adventurers]] are rather annoyed with the presence of Haymaker Snails, as they often find themselves resting in damp crevasses or the undersides of caves- or most often in the doorway leading to a ruin. It is a party frequent and irksome task to remove a snail from these thresholds safely- most often meaning that the snail is culled to make way. I am here to tell you that violence need not be enacted on such beautiful creatures. Simply locate fresh greenery from nearby, wave the foliage in front of the Snail's eyes and olfactory system and then guide the creature away. They aren't nearly as slow as regular snails, and can easily be moved without harm within roughly a minute or so.

While I cannot personally recommend it, for fear of harm befalling you from my 'advice' I found that if you wish to many Haymakers will accept gentle hand running down their neck while they eat- or at least they don't notice it when they're preoccupied. I absolutely don't recommend doing this if one of your companions is a very jealous [[Carrion Thieves|Carrion Thief]], as you may be nipped in a demand for redirected attention.

If combat against a Haymaker Snail cannot be avoided, using ranged attacks works best on males, and [[Cryomancy|cryomancy]] best on females. Severing the neck from the rest of the body will not immediately kill the creature, but it will stop it from using its shell to attack you. Their flesh is supple and can be stewed or roasted over a fire after being patted dry. Season with copious amounts of salt or sauce.

Haymaker snails are devout herbivores, and thus do not consume prey. They are however predated upon by [[Sky-Rippers]], [[Vorpal Lancers]], [[Cane Crabs]], [[Pink Dazzlers]], [[Coral Things]], [[Mire Hulks]], [[Kharahziaizi, Psycho Mantises|Kharahziaizi]], [[Chitterbacks]] and the odd [[Ossuary Titans|Ossuary Titan]] that passes by and spots one at eye level in [[The Shelled Trenches]]. [[The Things in The Dark, Null Figures|Null Figures]] can sometimes be seen in the corner of one's eye looming over and staring at Haymaker Snails, though nothing obvious ever seems to happen these snails.
