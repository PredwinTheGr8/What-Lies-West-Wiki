"I was alone the first time I met one. Girtz had me off gathering the [[Mire Weed]] in a nearby oasis. I noticed it a few times- a patch of the weed was getting closer to me, at least it seemed like it was. I was green then, I didn't pay it any mind- the one creature in the whole of [[The West]] perfect for teaching that lesson without killing you. I heard a burbling growl, then. I turned and saw the patch right next to me, I looked down into the chest-high water and saw three pairs of eyes looking up at me. I haven't screamed that loud ever again in my life."

### [[Monster Callsigns|Callsigns]]-

- Brackish Water
- Uneaten, circular patches of Mire Weed
- Low grumbling noises
- Stoneless beaches

Mire Hulks are [[Monsters]] of [[The West]] that primarily reside within [[The Blasted Basin|The Blasted Basin]]. They are large, rounded crustaceans with a primarily muddy-brown shell with white splotches on top, and boast a beautiful, opal white underside. Mire Hulks reach sizes of fourteen feet across and ten feet long front to back. Their shells, from belly to top, are roughly eight feet thick, with three of those being the top part of the dense, spongey carapace.

The upper portion of a Mire hulks shell is very soft, used by the Mire Hulk to plant [[Mire Weed]] on its back to grow. Mire Hulks will use this growth to attract their prey, and also [[Adventurers|adventurers]], though they aren't a target of predation, or anything else malicious.

Mire Weed is a key ingredient, alongside [[Scollifs]] liver and [[Ossuary Leeches|Leech]] blood, for [[Healing Potions]]. [[Adventurers]] in [[The Blasted Basin|The Blasted Basin]] are constantly on the lookout for ripe stalks of Mire Weed, but other Monsters- namely [[Quilbeasts]]- also actively seek and consume Mire Weed to sustain themselves. Most Mire Weed growths are likely to have been recently fed upon, all except the ones growing on the backs of Mire Hulks. In fact, Mire Hulks are such successful hunters that with time, they may become completely consumed by their Mire Weed and become unable to breath or reproduce as they live long enough to die by the very tool that kept them fed.

That is, unless they find an [[Adventurers|adventurer]]. Adventurers wading around the various murky oases might find themselves getting bumped into by one of these great beasts- but not out of territorial protection or a desire to hunt- the Mire Hulk comes for 'scritches'.

Mire Hulks seem to greatly enjoy having their excess Mire Weed growth being ripped out by the roots. They groan in satisfaction and blow bubbles to express their satisfaction in the relief adventurers give them. Mire Hulks are perhaps among the most beloved of all monsters within [[The West]], just alongside the aforementioned [[Scollifs]] and other, smaller curiosities found in the dunes. Some adventurers have reported that they felt safer in the murky, brown waterways than in the dense palm trees within [[The Blasted Basin|The Basin]], as numerous Mire Hulks follow them around in the water and form defensive circles around them.

Though, it should be noted that young Mire Hulks aren't quite as 'in the know' about the usefulness provided by adventurers, and may attempt to eat them as they would any other prey item. Its always a good idea to practice caution in the brackish water- the safest option is of course to stay out of it, but there are certain precautions one can take to keep a Mire Hulk at bay.

- Aversion to the smell of Necromancy
- Fear of high pitched noise
- Immobile during sandstorms

Mire Hulks primarily prey upon [[Quilbeasts]], and once adults have no natural predators.

Additionally, Mire Hulks do not like small stones, and will often bury them under the sand. Why this is is unknown but it may be a remnant of artificial behavior.
