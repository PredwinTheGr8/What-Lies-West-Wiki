Ossuary Titans are [[Monsters]] of [[The West]] that can often be found walking among the desiccated carcasses of [[Ossuary Reach|The Ossuary Reach]] while they're young. Ossuary Titans are the largest living creatures in The West, towering above at an estimated height of 130 feet on average, though some are clearly larger, being upwards of 300 or so according to some adventurers. Their bones pierce their skin and grow into dense plates of armor upwards of eight feet thick. On their back, several translucent sails vent heat from their bodies and draw in magic for consumption.

Ossuary Titans begin their lives in [[Ossuary Reach|The Ossuary Reach]] as the [[Ossuary Leeches]], which are sometimes barely bigger than a [[Humans|Human's]] finger. Leaving the life stage of the Leech for their own archive, once they've fulfilled their nutrition goals, they burrow into the sand and later immerge much closer in appearance to their adult form.

From there, the young Titan will scrounge around [[Ossuary Reach|The Ossuary Reach]] looking for anything smaller than itself to consume with it's mouth full of razor sharp rows of fangs. As always, prey and predators of the Ossuary Titans will be placed in the addendum. This does include [[Ossuary Leeches]] that have yet to burrow, seeming to be a way to wipe out future competition, or just mindless consumption of anything with protein.

Eventually, an Ossuary Titan will grow so large that nothing can sustain it- this stage marks the beginning of their status as a true 'titan'. The Titan will leave the [[Ossuary Reach|Reach]] and begin mindlessly wandering [[The West]]. Their sails, coined by an orcish adventurer as 'flesh vents', release the heat from their bodies during the night, and draw in ambient magics to fuel the creatures body. The Ossuary Titans seem to be the first, and only, [[Monsters]] to be capable of sustaining themselves only off of magic.

While young, Ossuary Titans prey upon [[Ossuary Leeches]], young [[Rot-winged Wranglers]], [[Arcanosaurs]] and [[Bone Dogs]], though adults of these prey items can easily flip the tables. Adolescent Ossuary Titans are vulnerable to predation from [[Mimics]], [[Dunewakers]], [[Inverters]] and [[Wyverns]].
