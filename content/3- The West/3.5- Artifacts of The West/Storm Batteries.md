([[Build Togethers|372]], [[What Lies West]])

Storm batteries are ancient artifices of [[The West]] that are often found buried in the sands around [[The Voltaic Monolith|The Voltaic Monolith]], idly sitting in the halls of [[The Forlorn Graveyard|The Forlorn Graveyard]], or powering the contraptions therein. Storm Batteries, as their name suggests, harvest the electromancy produced by The Voltaic Monolith and are used to grant motion to the idle machinations within the ancient hallways of The Forlorn Graveyard.

Storm Batteries are highly sought after by both [[Scholars|scholars]] and merchants, who value them for historical significance and monetary value respectively. Storm Batteries are quite rare, and are more often than not guarded directly by [[Metal Skeletons]], which will fight to the last to ensure that an [[Adventurers|adventurer]] doesn't nab the battery. Removing a Storm Battery from a machine may be incredibly dangerous, as certain machines seem to absorb latent eldritch energy and prevent it from corrupting the structures around the artifice.

Removing the battery from such a machine will result in a short window of safety before the restraints fail, and the energy within will poor out into the halls. Needless to say, being caught in such a wave of energy isn't fatal, but instead a fate worse than death.

Currently, no machines have been made that can withstand the energy produced by Storm Batteries, and several attempts have levelled entire buildings in blue explosions.
