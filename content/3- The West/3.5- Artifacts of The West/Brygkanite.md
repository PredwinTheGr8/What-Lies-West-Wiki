([[Build Togethers|546]], [[index|What Lies West?]])

Brygkanite, also named "West Gold," is an incredibly rare alloy comprised of Gold, Adamantine, Silver and an as of yet unknown final element that stabilizes the compound- as normally Gold and Adamantine react in a volatile explosion when melded with heat. The unknown final element, which seems to disappear when re-heated (resulting in an explosion as the adamantine and gold mix when hot) does not conduct electricity, does not rust, maintains a certain harness and durability between every single sample and glows a faint yellow color when exposed to [[Graviturgy]].

This fourth element, boringly named 'The Fourth Element', is perplexing to all who examine it. There is a very real possibility that this material- which IS metallic- comes from [[The Deep]]. Without the means to enter The Deep, at least not safely, it's impossible to know the compounds origins fully. The Fourth Element, in particularly dense chunks of Brygkanite, nullifies magic within a certain area, scaling up with the alloy's mass.

Within [[The Forlorn Graveyard]], a single, lone door stands at the end of a well-marked path inside the Region. This door is completely comprised of Brygkanite- and weighs an estimated 30 tons. The material is impervious to all damage, all magic (on account of being made of Brygkanite) and further dispels magic within 100 feet. The chamber within is suspected to also be comprised of Brygkanite, as arcane teleportation cannot pierce well behind the door, though its possible that [[adventurers]] have simply overestimated the chamber's vastness within.

[[Scholars]] and [[Recorders]] both hold value in Brygkanite for different reasons, with Scholars seeing the material as historically significant, and Recorder's whittling down the metal into scalpels that can cut through magic barriers used by some monsters- even in death.

Most adventurers will never get a look at Brygkanite in their entire lives, with thus far only seven samples being found in 40 years, other than the giant door within The Graveyard, of course.
