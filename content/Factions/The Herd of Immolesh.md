([[Build Togethers|333]], What Lies West?)

"Even the little ones pal, even the little ones! I saw a [[Goblins|Goblin]] in a tavern a fortnight ago with the bleeding mark on her arm. Some [[Elves|Elf]] git slid over next to her and she told 'im to buzz off. When he kept trying ta talk to her, at least twelve fellas stood up and walked over, all with the same mark on their arms... not ONE of em was a horned bloke. The Herd is bleedin' everywhere these days, best not take any chances you ask me."

-A sentence from one brigand to another

The Herd of [[Immolesh]] is a giant mercenary faction, emphasis on 'giant'. While there isn't a true total, estimates are in the upper tens of thousands, possibly more, with more and more joining up each day- sometimes other guilds entirely. As the name suggests, the leader of the 'herd' is Immolesh, The Fire Tamer. The name was coined by his followers, who even after they found peace in the south, in [[Koross]], stuck with Immolesh and pledged their lived to him. Immolesh's herd grew and grew, eventually drawing the eyes of even other peoples such as [[Dwarves]] and [[Ratfolk]], the entrepreneurial sort of folk.

Nowadays, the Herd is so massive that every city has at least 3 members hidden in plain sight- though if their arms were to be exposed, one would see the black brand of Immolesh on their upper bicep. The mark is permanent, though those who leave the Herd, either voluntarily or by banishment, will have a second brand placed over the first, marking them as no longer part of the group.

Still, even a "pack-mate" that has been banished or has left will still be looked upon fondly by their previous companions, at least the [[Minotaurs]], who cherish old bonds, regardless of how sour the relation was. As can be expected, the Herd is very close-knit, with Immolesh and his [[Minotaur Elders|Elder]] banner bearers encouraging members to learn of one another, and protect each other if danger would befall them.

The general morale of members of the Herd is quite high, their 'second family' keeping them safe in distant lands, be it [[Harush]] or, of course, [[The West]] and [[Balphora]]. Bar fights that start with a slug to a pack-mates face often end in utter chaos as other members of the herd that don't even know the one who was assaulted gang up on their assailant like wasps.. wasps with big clubs and even bigger muscles. Indeed, it is a wise choice to let the Herd do as it chooses, lest you face their stampede.
