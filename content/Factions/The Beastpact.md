After the [[Orc Revolution]], many of the races 'managed' by [[The Plain Three]] sought shelter from the wrath of their lords. Some fled to [[Harush]], others sought asylum in [[The Great Swamp]], but those who remained in [[Koross]] would not cower, they would not flee.

The Beastpact was made- a unified guild of freedom fighters who actively argue for the rights of the people who were used by [[House Thaddeus]] and [[Moradesh]]. The Beastpact was created by [[Ser Mallorin Berns of Harush]], who we will simply call Mallorin. Mallorin is a [[Catfolk]] who's history was rather long, but we can speak of him [[Ser Mallorin Berns of Harush|another time]].

Today, The Beastpact's power is used by many to escape the grip of slavers, with their own brand or visa being enough to deter any would be slaver from kidnapping those under its protection. Indeed, their power is so well known, and their wrath even more so that across the [[Ocean Magnamous]] in [[Balphora]] just saying your affiliated with them might be enough to deter attackers.

Speaking of Balphora, the Beastpact has recently begun construction of a headquarters in [[The City of Wyrms]] to get the funds they need for their constant political battles.
