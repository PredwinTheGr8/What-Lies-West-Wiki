Cresthram is the southern most country within [[Koross]], taking up a small section of                      [[Frigid South|The Frigid South]]. Its capital city, Rvestravough, is the only trade haven in the country, with many other cities having long since lost contact with the wider world as the storms raging from the southern [[Polar Gates|Polar Gate]] have intensified.

In times of old, Cresthram's people were unified with the [[Ogres]]. Their religion, cities and sometimes even homes were shared.
