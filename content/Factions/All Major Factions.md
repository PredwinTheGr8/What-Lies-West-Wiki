There are many different factions in both [[Balphora]] and [[Koross]], some comprised of only a single race, others a guild of multiple, but almost all of them want whatever is waiting in [[The West]].

The Factions are as follows:

## [[Humans|Human]] Only Factions-

- [[House Thaddeus]]: 
A family of nobles that are the cause for [[Orcs|Orcish]] slavery and a major spread of corruption in the morals of those associated with them. Many races outside [[The Plain Three]] despise house Thaddeus and those associated with them- especially the head of the family [[Maximillian Thaddeus]].

- [[The Conrad Family]]: 
A noble family funded by [[House Thaddeus]] that seeks to rule [[The West]] by either buying out or dominating the other factions within. The Conrad Family follows the values of house Thaddeus- seeing other races outside [[The Plain Three]] as lesser creatures to be extorted.

- [[Church of Orpheus]]: 
A Theology that worships an ascended mortal named [[Orpheus]], who was said to vanquish giants. The Church sees themselves as just rulers that will unite the world under one banner. Most races outside [[The Plain Three]] sought asylum from The [[Orc Revolution]] inside the southern territories controlled by The Church, and were granted it. Only humans are allowed into the upper ranks of the church, but other races can still climb quite high.

- [[Hands of Raknarous]]: 
A warrior cult secluded beyond the desert of [[Harush]]. They preferred to be left alone, but once [[The Calling]] rang across the world many of them began to march west. These warriors worship [[Raknarous The Bloody]], a spider [[Gods|goddess]] that is fueled by bloodshed. Though the nature of their worship is violent, the Hands of Raknarous are quite calm people, and are still welcome in society so long as their sacrifices continue to remain livestock only.

## [[Elves|Elf]] Only Factions-

- [[Moradesh]]: 
An Elven Kingdom with close ties to the [[Humans|Human]] [[House Thaddeus|House of Thaddeus]]. The people of Moradesh look down on every other race, only slightly less so to Humans and [[Dwarves]]. Though some have broken away from the prejudice of their fellows, most elves from Moradesh are extremely full of themselves and wish for nothing but to sip wine and eat grapes.

- [[The Woodborn]]: 
Forest Elves that reside in [[The Great Swamp]] alongside the [[Lizardfolk]]. The two peoples have close ties to one another and work to ensure nobody trespasses on their ancestral grounds. Woodborn rarely leave the swamp, but those who obey [[The Calling]] do so for a yearning to protect more than just their swamp.

- [[The Marked Ones]]: 
A conclave of Elves who worship [[Ol'moordurad]], [[The Old Ones|Great Old One]] of solitude. The Marked ones have dark, purplish skin and prefer to keep to themselves and their ancient underground citadels. Though with [[The Calling]] many have left to silence whatever is bringing them out from their burrows.

## [[Dwarves|Dwarf]] Only Factions-

- [[Great House of Horadrin|The Great House of Horadrin]]: 
The Largest Dwarven Clan in [[Koross]], The Great House has a long history with the other members of [[The Plain Three]], being the face of their kind in the treaty. The Great House favors unity and ancestral ties- with objects of great age being worshipped like [[Gods|gods]] in their massive holds. The Great House has sent many kinsmen out to obey [[The Calling]] in an effort to reclaim the numerous, empty holds in [[Balphora]].

- [[Sons of Y'zrold]]: 
Radical Dwarven Templars that worship [[Y'zrold Acktraz, The Mage-Eater]]. They seek to wipe out all sources of [[Magic]] and [[Eldritch Energy]] in the world, whether it be living creatures or ancient artifacts. The Sons of Y'zrold are particularly fervent in their conquests to [[The West]], as it is absolutely littered with arcane curious.

- [[The Mightlongs]]: 
The best craftsmen in the world, they call themselves- the Mightlongs pride themselves in being the Dwarfiest Dwarves around. They get their name from one such fixation- their Beards, which are longer and more well kempt than that of any other clan. The Mightlongs fancy themselves as artisans and mercenaries, obeying [[The Calling]] out of a craving for more materials and a Dwarves favorite metal- Gold.

## [[Kobolds|Kobold]] Only Factions-

- [[Da Tame Ones]]: 
The Kobolds who were "domesticated" by the other races. They seek friendship, family, and lots and lots of shinnies. They head west to accompany their friends and to explore the rest of the world.

- [[Da Wild Ones]]:
The Kobolds who remained "wild". They rarely approach other races and try to keep to themselves. They are reactive, feral creatures that don't think very much before they act. Food and drink can appease them easily and be used to control their masses. They rarely ever go to [[The West]] as they don't feel [[The Calling]] and don't have much reason to go there- almost exclusively arriving on accident.

## [[Goblins|Goblin]] Only Faction-

- [[Ramradga]]:
A Goblin Kingdom rules by [[The Great One]]- a hereditary position inherited time and time again by the current Great One's oldest child. The current Great One is [[Razzmakka Manhandler]]. A Goblin Queen with very particular... interests regarding humans. Ramradga holds allegiances with [[Great House of Horadrin|The Great House of Horadrin]], the [[Church of Orpheus]] and [[The Herd of Immolesh]]- though they are generally friendly to all who would have them, as is a goblins nature.

## [[Risen Ones|Risen One]] Only Factions-

- [[Coalition of Corpses]]:
A group of Risen Ones that have chosen safety in numbers against those who would curse their existence like the [[Sons of Y'zrold]] and [[House Thaddeus]]. The Risen Ones of this faction hold secret meetings and hidden seances to converse with each other away from prying eyes to secure their safety- and hope that [[The West]] holds answers for this as well.

- [[Gravewalkers]]:
A small group of Risen Ones that have accepted their label of monsters, and seek to bring down the other races of [[Koross]] to their level through use of the mindless ones and reanimation of even more dead. Though their numbers are small currently it will not remain so forever.

## [[Lizardfolk]] Only Factions-

- [[Moulugg Protectorate]]:
The "Official" name given by [[House Thaddeus]] to the largest of the Lizardfolk factions- a term to describe the numerous tribes devoted to the safeguarding of [[The Great Swamp]] aka "Moulugg" as they call it. The Lizardfolk are not fond of intruders, nor of leaving the swamp. As they don't feel [[The Calling]] the Lizardfolk of this "group" that go to [[The West]] do so solely out of a yearning for adventure, a very unpopular idea in Lizardfolk society.

- [[Westwander Scalebacks]]:
A united Lizardfolk guild that has adopted the ways of [[Humans]] to better control their members in expeditions to [[The West]]. This faction is holed up in [[The City of Wyrms]], and holds quite a sizable chunk of the city. Some of their number returned to [[Koross]] for a festival in [[The Great Swamp]], and are making their way back to The West.

## Non-Restricted Factions-

- [[The Herd of Immolesh]]:
A massive mercenary group completely loyal to [[Immolesh]], The Fire Tamer. [[Humans]], [[Goblins]], any other race even, can be found within the ranks of Immolesh's mercenary group. Those affected by [[The Calling]], and even others that are deaf to it, journey to [[The West]] to get their payday.

- [[The Beastpact]]:
A unity between [[Catfolk]], [[Ratfolk]], [[Kobolds]] and other beast races that acts as a mortal rights committee, fighting for their rights against [[House Thaddeus]] and [[Moradesh]]. While adventurers sometimes align themselves with the Beastpact for renewing licenses, mort of the true members are scholars and war veterans tired of the oppression constantly dealt to them. True members of this group will venture [[The West|West]] to find power to use as leverage for their sovereignty.

- [[The Waywards]]:
A group of bandits, vagabonds and brigands who fled to [[The West]] after their crimes caught up with them in [[Koross]]. While some of them may have been falsely charged, and most of them *are* good people, one should still watch their purse when walking through their cities like [[Three Purses]] and [[Freestuff]].

- [[Rvestravough]]:
The south-living peoples near the [[Polar Gates|Polar Gate]] of frost. During the [[Orc Revolution]] they accompanied their ancient companions, the [[Ogres]] in fighting for the Orc's freedom. While mostly [[Humans]] reside within Rvestran cities, after the Revolution's end other lineages found their tact and loyalty to old bonds appealing, and many immigrants made their way south. Though, the violence and devastation they wrought even against the giant [[Minotaurs]] has given them a grim reputation as marauders to the northerners and they have been largely cut off as of late- meaning some communities have been forced to begin raiding the lands of The [[Church of Orpheus]], further vilifying them.
