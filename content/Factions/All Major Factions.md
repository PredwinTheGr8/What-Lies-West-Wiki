([[Build Togethers|446]], [[index|What Lies West?]])

There are many different factions in both [[Balphora]] and [[Koross]], some comprised of only a single race, others a guild of multiple, but almost all of them want whatever is waiting in [[The West]].

The Factions are as follows:


- [[House Thaddeus]]: 
The perpetrators of nearly every woe that has befallen [[Koross]], [[Iradeen Thaddeus]] conquered most of Koross before his death. The [[Orc Revolution]] destroyed most of what he had built, though, and today House Thaddeus has grown desperate to regain their former glory- led now by Ira's son, [[Maximillian Thaddeus]].

- [[The Conrad Family]]: 
A noble family funded by [[House Thaddeus]] that seeks to rule [[The West]] by either buying out or dominating the other factions within. The Conrad Family follows the values of house Thaddeus- seeing other races outside [[The Plain Three]] as lesser creatures to be extorted. The Conrad Family has a great, but not absolute, monopoly over [[adventurers|adventurer]] contracts.

- [[Church of Orpheus]]: 
A Theology that worships an ascended mortal named [[Orpheus]], who was said to vanquish many of [[The Old Ones]]. The Church sees themselves as just rulers that will unite the world under one banner. Most races outside [[The Plain Three]] sought asylum from The [[Orc Revolution]] inside the southern territories controlled by The Church, and were granted it. Only humans are allowed into the upper ranks of the church, but other races can still climb quite high. They believe answers to divinity may lay deep inside of [[The West]], and must be destroyed to preserve the glory of their god.

- [[Hands of Raknarous]]: 
A warrior cult secluded beyond the desert of [[Harush]]. They preferred to be left alone, but once [[The Calling]] rang across the world many of them began to march west. These warriors worship [[Raknarous The Bloody]], a spider [[Gods|goddess]] that is fueled by bloodshed. Though the nature of their worship is violent, the Hands of Raknarous are quite calm people, and are still welcome in society so long as their sacrifices continue to remain livestock only. Raknarous has demanded many of her followers to go [[The West|West]] in search of something- something they will "know when witnessed."

- [[Moradesh]]: 
An Elven Kingdom with close ties to the [[Humans|Human]] [[House Thaddeus|House of Thaddeus]]. The people of Moradesh look down on every other race, only slightly less so to Humans and [[Dwarves]]. Though some have broken away from the prejudice of their fellows, most elves from Moradesh are extremely full of themselves and wish for nothing but to sip wine and eat grapes. Many have sought out travel to [[The West]] solely for the riches and alleged power within.

- [[The Woodborn]]: 
Forest Elves that reside in [[The Great Swamp]] alongside the [[Lizardfolk]]. The two peoples have close ties to one another and work to ensure nobody trespasses on their ancestral grounds. Woodborn rarely leave the swamp, but those who obey [[The Calling]] do so for a yearning to protect more than just their swamp. [[The Calling]] is the only reason any of them ever go [[The West|West]], with some expressing hopes to end the 'annoyance' once and for all.

- [[The Marked Ones]]: 
A conclave of Elves who worship [[Ol'moordurad]], [[The Old Ones|Great Old One]] of solitude. The Marked ones have dark, purplish skin and prefer to keep to themselves and their ancient underground citadels. Though with [[The Calling]] many have left to silence whatever is bringing them out from their burrows. Like [[The Woodborn]], they only ever leave to answer [[The Calling]].

- [[Great House of Horadrin|The Great House of Horadrin]]: 
The Largest Dwarven Clan in [[Koross]], The Great House has a long history with the other members of [[The Plain Three]], being the face of their kind in the treaty. The Great House favors unity and ancestral ties- with objects of great age being worshipped like [[Gods|gods]] in their massive holds. The Great House has sent many kinsmen out to obey [[The Calling]] in an effort to reclaim the numerous, empty holds in [[Balphora]].

- [[Sons of Y'zrold]]: 
Radical Dwarven Templars that worship [[Y'zrold Acktraz, The Mage-Eater]]. They seek to wipe out all sources of [[Magic]] and [[Eldritch Energy]] in the world, whether it be living creatures or ancient artifacts. The Sons of Y'zrold are particularly fervent in their conquests to [[The West]], as it is absolutely littered with arcane curious.

- [[The Mightlongs]]: 
The best craftsmen in the world, they call themselves- the Mightlongs pride themselves in being the Dwarfiest Dwarves around. They get their name from one such fixation- their Beards, which are longer and more well kempt than that of any other clan. The Mightlongs fancy themselves as artisans and mercenaries, obeying [[The Calling]] out of a craving for more materials and a Dwarves favorite metal- Gold.

- [[Ramradga]]:
A Goblin Kingdom rules by [[The Great One]]- a hereditary position inherited time and time again by the current Great One's oldest child. The current Great One is [[Razzmakka Manhandler]]. A Goblin Queen with very particular... interests regarding humans. Ramradga holds allegiances with [[Great House of Horadrin|The Great House of Horadrin]], the [[Church of Orpheus]] and [[The Herd of Immolesh]]- though they are generally friendly to all who would have them, as is a goblins nature. [[Goblins]] are highly susceptible to the power of [[The Calling]] as they have a sensitivity to [[magic]]. Though they often go just to find kindred spirits or to leave with friends also affected by the curse of [[The West]].

- [[Coalition of Corpses]]:
A group of Risen Ones that have chosen safety in numbers against those who would curse their existence like the [[Sons of Y'zrold]] and [[House Thaddeus]]. The Risen Ones of this faction hold secret meetings and hidden seances to converse with each other away from prying eyes to secure their safety- and hope that [[The West]] holds answers for this as well.

- [[Gravewalkers]]:
A small group of Risen Ones that have accepted their label of monsters, and seek to bring down the other races of [[Koross]] to their level through use of the mindless ones and reanimation of even more dead. Though their numbers are small currently it will not remain so forever. They must seek out the secrets of Necromancy in order to preform their plot of perilous post-mortem pernicious-ness.

- [[Moulugg Protectorate]]:
The "Official" name given by [[House Thaddeus]] to the largest of the Lizardfolk factions- a term to describe the numerous tribes devoted to the safeguarding of [[The Great Swamp]] aka "Moulugg" as they call it. The Lizardfolk are not fond of intruders, nor of leaving the swamp. Though they remain resistant to [[The Calling]], newborn Lizardfolk within the last century have grown yearnful of adventure, and have gone [[The West|West]] to satiate it.

- [[Westwander Scalebacks]]:
A united Lizardfolk guild that has adopted the ways of [[Humans]] to better control their members in expeditions to [[The West]]. This faction is holed up in [[The City of Wyrms]], and holds quite a sizable chunk of the city. Some of their number returned to [[Koross]] for a festival in [[The Great Swamp]], and are making their way back to The West.

- [[The Herd of Immolesh]]:
A massive mercenary group completely loyal to [[Immolesh]], The Fire Tamer. [[Humans]], [[Goblins]], any other race even, can be found within the ranks of Immolesh's mercenary group. Those affected by [[The Calling]] journey to [[The West]] to get their payday.

- [[The Beastpact]]:
A unity between [[Catfolk]], [[Ratfolk]], [[Kobolds]] and other beast races that acts as a mortal rights committee, fighting for their rights against [[House Thaddeus]] and [[Moradesh]]. While adventurers sometimes align themselves with the Beastpact for renewing licenses, mort of the true members are scholars and war veterans tired of the oppression constantly dealt to them. True members of this group will venture [[The West|West]] to find power to use as leverage for their sovereignty.

- [[The Waywards]]:
A group of bandits, vagabonds and brigands who fled to [[The West]] after their crimes caught up with them in [[Koross]]. While some of them may have been falsely charged, and most of them *are* good people, one should still watch their purse when walking through their cities like [[Three Purses]] and [[Freestuff]].

- [[Cresthram]]:
The southern territories beyond [[Church of Orpheus|The Church of Orpheus']] own land, hidden deep within [[Frigid South|The Frigid South]]. Cresthram is constantly besieged by the arcane forces of the southern [[Polar Gates|Polar Gate]], resulting in near-constant blizzards that blot out the sky and choke the lungs of any who breath the icicle strewn air. The Cresthrans are a strong people, though after they assisted their ancient friends the [[Ogres]] and by proxy the [[Orcs]] in their [[Orc Revolution|revolution]] they haven't been trusted. Many have fled to [[Balphora]] to earn new lives without the prejudice against their ancestors.

* [[Scholam Wyrmras]]:
A powerful guild of [[Scholars]] in direct opposition to The [[Sons of Y'zrold]] who would rather burn all artifacts of [[The West]] away. The Scholam was one of the first buildings risen during the colonization of [[The City of Wyrms]]. They sponsor expeditions to find both new monsters and ancient lore to be catalogued, being the primary source of income for many adventurers. This has sparked massive strife with some other factions who despise the Scholam's hoarding of [[adventurers]].
