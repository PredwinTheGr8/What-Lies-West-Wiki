(318, What Lies West?)

"All born under the light of Orpheus are his children, those who have lost their parents and caregivers may look to us, to Orpheus, for their safety- prosperity."

-[[Vicar Friedrich Von Müller]], Head of the Church of Orpheus.

The Church of Orpheus is old, perhaps the oldest of any [[Humans|Human]] [[All Major Factions|Faction]] in [[Koross]]. Their religion is built around unity, and a continued force of cooperation between all races. The [[Orc Revolution]] caused a lot of destruction, and when thousands of children lost their parents, the Church of Orpheus were the ones to take them under their wings.

Whether it be *human*itarian aid or the care of orphaned children, the Church of Orpheus has built up a longstanding trust between them and the other races within [[Koross]].

The Church of Orpheus has sent numerous among their number to [[The West]] for several reasons:

- To fund their efforts
- To give aid to the oppressed people of the area
- To directly oppose the prejudice of [[The Conrad Family]] and [[Sons of Y'zrold]].
- To get rid of ''Bad Apples"
  
With their political power, the Church has worked tirelessly to ensure that all will be equal- there will be no nobles or kings, only those smart enough to lead justly and the education to lift people onto that pedestal. Though, their goals require immense wealth and power... power that hopefully lies West.
