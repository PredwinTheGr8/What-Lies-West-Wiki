The Coalition of Corpses is a group of [[Risen Ones]] who have unified within the borders of [[Balphora]] for their own safety, as other factions such as the [[Sons of Y'zrold]] see their existence as witchcraft or other forms of dark [[Magic|magic]]. While the Coalition has no headquarters in Balphora, some still suspect that [[The Lawless North]] of [[The Waywards]] might serve as their "home" in the west. Speaking of cardinal directions- there are a select few who believe that the Coalition might have used their undead nature to survive in [[The West]] and perhaps even make a kingdom in the sands, though such things are unfounded.

When the Risen Ones first awoke in [[Koross]], it was in fact both [[Immolesh]] and [[Razzmakka Manhandler]] who recommended the bone folk flee from the land to the West and obey [[The Calling]] and though their peoples are completely intertwined a Risen One is certainly more likely to listen to a [[Goblins|Goblin]] or [[Minotaurs|Minotaur]] than any other persons.
