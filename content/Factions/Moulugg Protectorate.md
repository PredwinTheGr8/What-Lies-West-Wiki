The main group of Lizardfolk who 'reign' over [[The Great Swamp]] also called Moulugg. The 'protectorate' part of their name comes from [[House Thaddeus]], who named their 'country' to insinuate that they rule over the swamp when in fact that could not be further from the truth. The Lizardfolk don't use this name to re-present their people often, instead calling themselves 'the kin' rather than 'Moulugg Protectorate'.

The Protectorate is very, very protective of their lands. Early in history they were welcoming to visitors, but after [[Humans]] of House Thaddeus and [[Moradesh|Moradeshian]] [[Elves]] tried to set up logging camps, everything went south fast. The Lizardfolk, and their allies in [[The Woodborn]] immediately and viciously slaughtered any who would harm their trees. House Thaddeus for their part withdrew their workers immediately, but Moradesh's hunger for industry made them hesitant to leave.

Moradesh worked in secret to harvest wood using magics to silence their work and to hide their presence. This continued for years, the Lizardfolk suspected something was wrong when they found large swaths of their forests stripped of trees, but they could never find a culprit to blame it on. Moradesh's efforts granted them metric tons of wood from the swamp, all of it burned as fuel, or used to make their vain cities.

This continued until the [[Orc Revolution]], when one of the groups of Orcish slaves who had been harvesting the wood surrendered themselves to the Protectorate, and while asking for mercy they told them of who made them chop their trees.

The Lizardfolk and Woodborn mobilized for war that day, and though the war has long since ended, the Swamp is never to be entered by any of [[The Plain Three]] ever again.
