(292, What Lies West)

"[[Balphora]] needs a strong hand to guide it! It needs knowledge, power, and an entire army at its back! It *needs* the Conrad Family!"

-Alyssa Conrad, 3rd Daughter of the Conrad Family.

The Conrad family is the longest running and most powerful noble house in Balphora. Their ties to [[Koross]] in [[House Thaddeus]] have allowed them to take a massive political hold over both the guild system and taxing of Balphora. They claim themselves to be the undeniable rulers of the region, yet many deny their claim despite the long history the family has with The West. Most people who ventured west did it to escape the nobles and lords back home, and seeing the Conrad Family parading themselves around is salt in the wound.

The Conrad family seems oblivious to the hatred directed at them from the masses, a dangerous thought, given just how many of these people are veterans of [[The Wars of Balphora|massive battles]] throughout the area, or are even professional monster hunters alone. Certainly, if the Conrad family can't keep control of these people than disaster is all but inevitable.
