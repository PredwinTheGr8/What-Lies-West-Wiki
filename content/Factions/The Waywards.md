A ragtag group of people who willingly or unwillingly left society- they are not builders, nor poets- no. They are scum, the bottom of the barrel and while some of them may extend a helping hand from time to time most of them are out for themselves. You never know if theirs a bounty on your head and who's here to claim it until its too late, so its best to play it safe unless you know you've never wronged anybody, and that's impossible in [[Balphora]].

The Waywards are trying to build up an ungoverned society, an impossible task, really that has no clear end in sight. While some call them hopeless, they fancy themselves as entrepreneurs- great inventors of a lifestyle- take what you want, give what you want. Though not every bandit aligns themselves with the Waywards as a group, the term is still used for any vagabonds who've made their way to [[The West]].
