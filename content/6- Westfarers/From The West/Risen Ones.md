"Sun's Scourge's pawns, they say. Perchance they may be correct- that we will sneak into your homes and slit your throats for it is our deepest need... That we may blow out your torch on a dark road and drag you away into the night howling in pain. But I raise you a different question of motives- if we were made to kill, then why reanimate us so weak of will and confused? I don't know who I was, who my family could have been. I rose from an unmarked grave on the hillside of a backwater abandoned city long after its denizens had answered [[The Calling]]. I rose alone, I live alone, I sleep alone. I am alone. And yet, I am content, we all are- The 'Risen Ones' crave solitude and wish not for bloodshed. So then, I ask you. If we were made to kill, why not make us better at it?"

-Krell of Thousand Steps, Risen [[Humans|Human]] [[Hands of Raknarous|Hand]]

The Risen Ones are an unfortunate people, I gather. In all their history they have been dejected, turned away and forbidden entry. They have rarely ever been welcome- with to my knowledge only one city holding a great deal of tolerance, being of course [[The City of Wyrms]]. The Risen Ones suffer a unique origin, with [[The Unfeathering]] being the source of their animacy. When the very first Unfeathering rung out acrost [[Maernos]], it's [[The Calling|Calling]] was so powerful it clenched the souls of the dead and pulled them back into their mortal shells. Peasants, Vicars, Kings- none were too weak or too strong of will to be spared.

Risen Ones possess no memory of their time alive, nor do they hold any memories of [[Yonder, Beyond The Gate|Beyond The Gate]]- the slang term used to describe the afterlife of course. Depending on where they surface, and when, a Risen One may face many or few trials, of which I have some examples;

* The [[Dwarves]] revere the old ways, and the ones who orchestrated them. Many Dwarven Risen Ones are not vilified, and are instead treated as pieces of holy work- with even The [[Sons of Y'zrold]] holding their tongue in the presence of an ancient dwarven lord made 'flesh' again. Still, with their memories long gone a great deal of even the most ancient Dwarven Lords and Ladies leave their holds and journey [[The West|West]] to answer the Call Occidental, as despite all the love they still feel as if they do not belong.

- [[Humans|Humanity]] as a whole has feared the Undead, even before they knew for a fact that they really, truly existed. When The Risen Ones appeared they were often burned at the steak or shattered to pieces with mauls and hammers. It took examples from both The Dwarves and the Eastern [[Hands of Raknarous]] to hammer home that The Risen Ones are simply people without flesh, but the damage was done, and it was deep. 'Newborn' Risen Ones are taught by their elders that Humans are scared creatures that would sooner strike a Skeleton down rather than speak with them, as such many Risen Ones avoid Human-majority Cities like [[Luminarch, The City of Lanterns|Luminarch]] and [[Three Purses]].
  
- The Eastern Nations- governed by [[Lizardfolk]], [[Centaurs]], [[Catfolk]] and [[The Hand Of Raknarous]]- all from the start treated the Risen Ones with a deal of respect, if not out of reverence like The Kellissk, then out of fear or taboo like The Catfolk and Hands. The Centaurs specifically seemed to just go on with business as usual, only with skeletal companions riding along side them as they charged down mighty Bisons and Boars. The Hands of Raknarous believe that becoming Risen is Punishment from their Goddess- or rather an opportunity to earn her favor once more. Risen Hands will keep their [[The Hand Of Raknarous|Mark]] upon their bicep, or rather where their bicep was- meaning that despite the mind forgetting origins, the soul does not. The Catfolk treat their Risen with pity rather than reverence or fear. They are saddened by their amnesiac state and lack of culture despite being from such a powerfully spoken peoples. There are more mortally driven Catfolk who see the existence of the Risen as a truth that Death Strips away all that is you, and that you merely join the faceless masses in a place of dull reality forever. A... sinister thought to say the least.
  
- The [[Elves]] all have had very... conflicting views on The Risen Ones. With the so called 'High Elves' of [[Moradesh]] purging them on mass, a fact of my history I am not proud of as I personally hunted down some when we were still befuddled as to their origins and motives. This resulted in a similar situation with Elves and the Risen as with Humanity and the Risen. [[The Woodborn]] and [[The Marked Ones]] both acted without much care to their Risen companions. The Marked Ones already act with solitude, so the Risen Ones found solace within their hidden citadel. The Woodborn usually cannibalize their dead, and the few bones left out in the wilderness that animated simply went west as there was nobody around to guide them back home.
  
- [[Cresthram|Cresthrans]] in particular do NOT like Risen Ones as they resemble creatures from their folklore- which you can be sure the Cresthrans are very attached to within their culture. Risen Ones are turned away at constant, vilified and blamed for crimes they had no possibility of committing. The [[Gravewalkers]], a coalition of Risen terrorists and cultists have origins from Cresthran neglect and violence, with their leader once being a legendary Jarl- who of course lost his memories-  but delved into his history and learned of the power he should have wielded even after death. After a brief conflict, the Gravewalkers fled to [[Balphora]] were they now plan their dastardly plot. You know what they say about Cresthrans- you can't teach them anything.

As of yet, no Risen [[Minotaurs]], [[Goblins]], [[Kobolds]] or [[Ratfolk]] have made an appearance. With time I'm sure it is inevitable, but I'm sure that they'll receive a warmer welcome than their 'cousins' did.

The Risen Ones have adopted pieces of other cultures and come together to make their own. While I haven't personally had any dealings with a Risen One, I am told that they prefer to keep to themselves and rarely deal with outsiders. The older a Risen's corpse was before waking, the less emotion they have- with particularly old Risen Ones acting with a cold precision as they hold no empathy or joy in their hearts.

In my own opinion the Risen Ones should be cured of their condition in the correct way- by ending the spell over them and allowing them to rest once more with a fulfilled purpose. Such a duty may prove difficult, and will require a great deal of effort answer their own question;
