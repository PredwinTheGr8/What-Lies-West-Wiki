When [[The Calling]] rang out across [[The Wider World]], [[Koross]] included, many dropped their jobs and sought out this mighty 'song' of [[The West]]. For a short time, mortals all over Koross from the edge of [[The Northern Wastes]] to [[Harush]] and [[The Great Swamp]] flocked to the docks against the [[Ocean Magnamous]]. But, a month or so after the ships first set sail, a time of great terror befell the entirety of the eastern factions.

The Undead- The Risen Ones.

Some of them crawled out from their graves as mindless creatures that walked into the oceans, others wandered in hordes in the countryside, but most of them would come back aware, sentient, and heeding The Calling.

The rulers of the lands they rose had differing reactions to the dead coming back. The [[Dwarves]] hold close ties with their ancestors, and though the Risen Ones hold no memory of their past lives the living Dwarves essentially worshipped the ground they walked on.

The [[Humans]], largely of [[House Thaddeus]] rejected any Risen Ones who came to their door, sometimes burning them in pyres as mobs of scared citizens acted out of fear. Though some Human factions like the [[Hands of Raknarous]] have religious dies to their dead as well, and they were welcome wherever their tents propped up even if they were not of their ilk. And of course, The [[Church of Orpheus]] tried their best to be welcoming.

The Risen Ones souls are not completely intact upon resurrection, with their emotions being all out of sorts as a result. The "youngest" carcasses among them are more in control of themselves, but the longer a body has been dead the more likely it is that they reanimate without their wits, indeed the mindless corpses are the ones without any souls at all.

All of this has led to the [[Sons of Y'zrold]] declaring every last Risen One an abomination of dark magic that should be exterminated, but with the [[Great House of Horadrin]] and [[The Mightlongs]] both supporting their existence they need to act carefully unless they wish to anger their kin. The Risen Ones tried their best to fit in with their fellows, but with their loss of memory, the activities they cant share anymore like eating and sleeping, most felt alienated from even their direct family, and chose to leave.

Many of them still remain in [[Koross]], but most of their number went West to make their own destinies- forging their [[Coalition of Corpses]], a name agreed upon because it rolled off the tongue well enough and fit their needs. The Coalition serves to protect those within, for there is safety in numbers. The Coalition is located in the west but they have no official headquarters or city to meet in, instead their meetings are given in a secret tongue only the dead can understand to keep their leaders and people safe.

The members of the Coalition hope that whatever Lies West, it will cure their affliction... one way, or another...
