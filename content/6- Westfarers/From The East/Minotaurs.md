Legends say that from the wasted, volcanic forges of the [[Phrazmollograggra Mountains]], a god of beasts forged the Minotaurs, and sent them into the world to be its voice- its will. Minotaurs are accustomed to the heat of a forge, the smell of gunpowder, and the thrill of bloodshed. In the early days of [[Koross]], the Minotaurs were said to have ravaged the lands around their mountain valley. Hamlets, citadels and even entire armies that were built or wandered too close were subject to massive raids conducted by these 12 foot tall behemoths. 

Food stores taken, munitions stolen, armor repurposed- hundreds of thousands of gold pieces were lost due to the unwavering ferocity of the Minotaurs, and it would have continued if not for the assistance of the [[Dwarves]], specifically the [[Great House of Horadrin]]. The kinsmen of the Great House built a massive, heavily fortified wall that blocked off the Minotaurs access to the south- and though the Minotaurs put in great effort to stop construction of the wall, the ingenuity and firepower of the Great House, and the numbers of the [[Humans]] who assisted them, stopped the largely uncoordinated beastmen from making any semblance of a proper attack on the construction site.

Once the wall was built, a constant vigil was kept upon it for well over a hundred years, but the Minotaurs learned after only a handful of attempts that breaching the wall would be impossible. 78 years of silence, looking out into the northern wastes to see nothing but dead grass and acacia trees. Eventually, slowly, the forces sent to guard the wall diminished year after year- with there only being three-dozen or so Human soldiers left to guard it at the end.

Then, one day, the earth began to shake.

Soldiers looked to the north, and saw a sea of black- the dark, ashen fur of the Minotaurs, thousands of them, tens of thousands... all of them. The soldiers panicked, ringing the bell and lighting the fires, but there was nothing to be done against such a massive force of monsters. The force marched in unison, grunting and chanting like an actual, real army would. Each minute that passed more and more showed themselves on the horizon, before finally coming to a stop a mile or so away from the wall- cannons, riflemen, and dominated monstrosities dotted through their ranks.

One of their number, the largest Minotaur any man had ever seen, walked alone towards the wall. His name was [[Immolesh]], The Fire Tamer, and his words echoed to the ears of those who cowered on the wall. He demanded an audience with the kings who had cut his people away from the world.

At the time of Immolesh's demand, the Humans, [[Elves]], and Dwarves had been in the midst of a 12 year long war against the [[Orcs]] and [[Ogres]] to the south, as well as the [[Lizardfolk]] of the east. To provoke the Minotaurs into a war when their coordination had finally been achieved would invite complete destruction to their empires. And so, on the night of a full moon, four leaders met under its light.

Immolesh had seen the future in the stars, he said. He spoke that the war was a blessing for his people, and for theirs. For he had an offer they could not possibly refuse- to allow his kind, The Minotaurs into their society, and in exchange they would crush any who would oppose them, for a price of course.

And it was thus.

The Minotaurs, under the pay and supply of their new allies, used their hulking forms, artisan weapons and tact minds to halt all momentum [[The Plain Three]]'s opponents had made. The Orcs and Ogres, the Lizardmen, and even small bandit factions within the realm all met the might of the Minotaurs for the first time, and they could hold no defense against it. Armies were crushed, cities raised, and territories reclaimed. In six short months, the forces who had once opposed them were brought to their knees by their own ancient foe.

123 years later, the Minotaurs wander the world in mercenary herds, taking jobs from those willing to pay them, and always coming back with their contracts fulfilled. The Minotaurs go west in [[The Herd of Immolesh]] and other small mercenary companies to find work, and to of course answer [[The Calling]].
