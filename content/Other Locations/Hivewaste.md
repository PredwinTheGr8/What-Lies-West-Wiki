The furthest any mortal has gone East, Hivewaste is a brutal, insect ridden region that encompasses what could be described as the Prime Meridian of [[The Wider World]]. Hivewaste is overseen by [[Raknarous The Bloody]], who ensures that no mortal trespasses on her lands, and if they do, her children shall swarm them and leave nothing but bones behind.

While only a handful of expeditions have been sent into The Hivewaste, even fewer have actually made it back to [[Koross]]. Life within the waste is built on the massive number of insects within, some a normal, tiny size and other towering higher than castle walls. Outside The Hivewaste, the [[Hands of Raknarous]] patrol to keep trespassers away, and will enact their worship if necessary to dissuade any from even attempting to come close to The Hivewaste.

Many believe that there is a land beyond The Hivewaste, and that even more races may lie across it, but no true signs have been identified, and it will likely never be proven.
