# WIP

On a white desert's night, born under the light of an eclipse, during the festival of [[Akmaban]], a [[Catfolk|cat-man]] was born, a cat-man who would change the very fate of his people, or so they all say. His name? Mallorin. Ser Mallorin Berns of [[Harush]].

Mallorin's early life was like that of all others born under the gaze of [[Generous Harush]]. He was well fed, he was taught the ways of survival in the pale desert, he was surrounded by like minded people... like minded drones... No, Ser Mallorin was not happy with his peoples lack of progression, their lack of foresight. Mallorin spent his early teen years trying to scratch an itch in the back of his head- his people were doomed, by what, and when it would occur, Mallorin did not know, he only knew it was inevitable.

Some of his family, he told this suspicion. They asked questions time and time again, and in the end Mallorin simply told them his thought had gone away just to keep them from pestering him again and again.

Mallorin worked to climb the ranks of the Catfolk's government. He spent years building a reliable reputation, hoping that in a position of power he might find the source of their doom and snuff it like a candle. While Mallorin did not become a ruler, his efforts landed him as one of the most important nobles in all of Harush. His political power, though not of the highest kind, was enough for most citizens to recognize his seal, and the work he had to do to bring his family to such a pedestal.

He used his newfound wealth to buy out as many trading companies as he could, as many food stalls as he could find and thus make even more money. He used his vast coffers to fund expedition after expedition into [[The Wider World]], they found a [[The Great Swamp|swamp]] with 'less than accepting' residents within, folk with scales on their skin and tongues the length of an arm- the so called [[Lizardfolk]] of Moulugg and their allies [[The Woodborn]].

Mallorin did his best to increase their relations with their new neighbors, but they seemed more inclined to push everyone away from them.
