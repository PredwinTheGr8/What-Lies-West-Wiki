# WIP

On a white desert's night, born under the light of an eclipse, during the festival of [[Akmaban]], a [[Catfolk|cat-man]] was born, a cat-man who would change the very fate of his people, or so they all say. His name? Mallorin. Ser Mallorin Berns of [[Harush]].

Mallorin's early life was like that of all others born under the gaze of [[Generous Harush]]. He was well fed, he was taught the ways of survival in the pale desert, he was surrounded by like minded people... like minded drones... No. Ser Mallorin was not happy with his peoples lack of progression, their lack of foresight. Mallorin spent his early teen years trying to scratch an itch in the back of his head- his people were doomed, by what, and when it would occur, Mallorin did not know, he only knew it was inevitable.

Some of his family, he told this suspicion. They asked questions time and time again, and in the end Mallorin simply told them his thought had gone away just to keep them from pestering him again and again.

Mallorin knew that to make change he would need favors, and to earn favor he would need wealth. Its hard to become wealthy in Catfolk society, not because any higher ups would try to stop him, but because their own law forbade hoarding resources. Mallorin decided that the best way to gain power would be to organize other Catfolk's businesses, whether by buying them out with hides or by working for free. Eventually, his letter seal was well recognized, with many understanding the quality of his work- the diligence of his archives.

He used his newfound fame to collect as many trading companies as he could, as many food stalls as he would find and thus make even more of a name for himself. He used his vast stores to fund expedition after expedition into [[The Wider World]], they found a [[The Great Swamp|swamp]] with 'less than accepting' residents within, folk with scales on their skin and tongues the length of an arm- the so called [[Lizardfolk]] of Moulugg and their allies [[The Woodborn]].

Mallorin did his best to increase their relations with their new neighbors, but they seemed more inclined to push everyone away from them, but why? With time, Mallorin had his answer. The light feet of Catfolk easily escaped the eyes and ears of the Lizardfolk guardians. As they travelled west they found their reason- massive swathes of their forest had been cut down. The swamp folk were simply wary of loosing their woods to the ones who lied west.

Mallorin came up with an ingenious plan to earn the favor of the Lizardfolk and Woodborn, or at least he hoped it was. But first, he would need to earn their favor enough to warrant them even bothering to hear him out. Mallorin tried many different attempts at earning the favor of the Lizardfolk.
