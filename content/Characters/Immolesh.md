[[Build Togethers|316]], [[index|What Lies West?]]

Immolesh is the spiritual leader of the [[Minotaurs]], though his name has not always been this. The Minotaurs had been struggling to survive when Khail was born, the great wall The [[Great House of Horadrin]] had made a decade prior choking out their only true food source. The [[Phrazmollograggra Mountains]] had choked out the soil- with their constant ash fall making it nigh impossible to grow anything.

Khail's life as a calf was rough, there was little water, even less food and beyond that barely any kinship between his fellow Minotaurs. Khail barely made it to his teens, his mother and father being forced to do unspeakable things just to feed him and themselves. His life was paved with those he had to consume to survive... and he hated it. He hated all of this violence, the oppression from the great herds- the slavery and murder directed at his own people from those who thought they were better.

Even worse, he understood why the southlings had done what they did- cutting them off from the world. The [[Dwarves]], [[Humans]] and all the rest had the right to fear his people and cut them off from their green pastures and flowing wheat fields. Thus, he would force them to welcome them... if only he had the strength to do so. Despite all the effort his parents put into keeping him alive, meat and meat alone is not good for a bovine creature like a Minotaur. His body could hardly move outside of short bursts of energy and his breathing grew weaker and weaker each year from that cursed smog.

One day, however, Khail's family became a victim of the violence they had put onto others- cannibalism. Two dozen brutes descended on their homestead near the base of the mountains and while they held the door shut with all their might, Immolesh's parents screamed at him to flee- to run and live. Khail looked at them with tears in his eyes, then bolted out the back door, running as far as his legs could take him, not stopping until his lungs could not bear the pain any longer.

Collapsing in the ashes, he looked forward to see his proximity to the violent, fire spewing mountains that cursed the Minotaurs land. The earth was breathing beneath him- flowing magma beneath the earth that pulsed and writhed like the deep functions of a mighty beast. Catching his breath, Khail stood up and searched the area around him- scorched trees, rivers of magma... the bones of giants. But distantly, pressed against the rocks was a mighty stone entryway- carved into the very face of the mountains.

Something deep inside his spirit told him to go there- a yearning... a [[The Calling|Calling]].

Walking into the cold, stone corridor, Khail's muscles and skin twitched at just how cold this hall was. In the shadows as he walked, he spied desolate, flesh-stripped bones of his kin, though some still had little giblets of skin and fur left on their ribs that small beetles ate away at. Walking past one of the closer corpses to his path forward, Khail instinctively reached out for one of the bloated grubs and tossed it into his mouth... juicy and filling.

His waltz brought him through desolate, massive caverns with lava falling from the ceiling. Distant homes with black windows and scorched walls from the lava flowing past them. He walked across a massive bridge that stretched over the city below. This place had not been abandoned long... no it seemed recent, in the last hundred years or so. The corpses deeper in still had flesh, though it was foul and green. Their wounds were of black powder weapons- flintlocks, arquebuses and the like. Still, despite the carnage around him he pressed on, the citadel in the distance was calling to him.

It loomed in the distance, a hulking structure that betrayed just how far away it truly, truly was. Built against a massive column of rock that was three cities across, easily. This cavern is truly, unfathomably massive. While there was plenty of lava and giant pits all over, the homes built here are rarely near them or in the way... what had forced everyone out? The defenses here are impeccable.. why do the [[Minotaur Elders|elders]] not speak of this place? It hasn't been that long, clearly, so why?

He kept pondering everything he was seeing when, before he knew it with his face down he slammed into the gate of the citadel. As he fell onto his ass, he looked up to see the gate slowly open from his impact, swinging inward so gracefully that one could mistake it for magic- not even a squeak from the hinges as this monolithic wooden entryway gaped.

Stepping inside, that gut feeling became stronger- guiding him through hallways, doors and secret passages before finally he was led to a dead end- a room. The heat was immense- a massive pillar of magma flowing from the ceiling. Around him, discarded axes that seemed perfect, all with a slot for some sort of gem near their heads. And lo, that gem was on a pedestal near to a green anvil.

As he approached, the anvil thrummed and pulsed with each step he took, carrying the vibrations to its head and glowing with [[Magic|arcane]] pulses. Next to it, a hammer made from a different, blueish metal rested on the ground. Behind all of it, nestled in the magma was a forge of immense heat. The rumblings of the volcano around him, and the feeling in his gut... and the axe... He was granted an idea.

Without much thought, he began to work the forge- smelting down an ingot of that same, green metal and working it into the head of a halberd. His fingers and wrist moved like that of a master artisan practicing their favorite craft. It was easy, second nature to Khail as he shaped out a mighty weapon.

Then, as it cooled, he reached for the gem and placed it into the slot he had made for it. Bending the strange metal to lock it inside. But nothing happened... nothing glorious at least. There was no wood for a handle, so Khail sat holding the head of the weapon, looking around for something... something to wield it with. But then, the feeling came back again. Without putting any thought into his movements, the weak- barely standing Minotaur walked to the falling column of magma behind the forge, and reached to touch it.

In a flash of fire- Khail was consumed by the flames. The pain was immense, but it did not last long. The halberd's head glowed with a vibrant, red light- rescuing Khail from the pain of the magma. He stepped away, his fur charred black... and his height? He was towering above the forge now, his arms were dense with muscles, his hunger and weak lungs sated. And as he held up the weapon, magma flowed into the handle slot- straightening and hardening into a mighty, basalt handle.

Turning to the exit, Khail was dead, and Immolesh took his steps.

He walked out under moonlight, back to his home. Looking to the stars in the sky... they said something with their flashes. War would come for [[The Plain Three]], and in the middle of it his people would be granted their freedom.

Immolesh began his war of unity then and there. He marched to the tents of the Elders, the war camps of the largest of his kind, yet still smaller than him. Some cowered from him and pledge fealty instantly, others dueled him and lost in three strokes of his weapon. It took decades, but Immolesh had single handedly conquered what thousands of humans could not- [[The Northern Wastes]] were under his command alone.

And right on time, for the wall guarding the south was at its weakest...
