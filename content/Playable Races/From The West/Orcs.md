Legends say that the orcs were created from foul sorceries, that their existence is built on black magic, and that they are inherently evil. For as long as the Orcs can remember, they have been oppressed by [[The Plain Three]] and forced to work as slaves- they don't recall their own origins, nor their history at all- all of it being lost at some point so long ago even it goes unremembered.

While the [[Dwarves]] stayed out of true slavery against the Orcs, the other two- [[Humans]] and [[Elves]]- used them in every aspect of life as if they were tools. This had lasted for untold millennium, and perhaps it would have continued if not for one, simple, discovery.

In [[The City of Wyrms|The City of Wyrms]], the building techniques of orcs, and murals of their existence, proved their origins as nothing heinous, but as normal. Slates written by unknown figures told of how the Orcs were spawned from the gullet of a beast, and growing restless in their prison of flesh they cut themselves free- that beast was a Dragon- a Wyrm. The Orcs, according to this figure, are born of the might and nobility of the ancient creatures, and thus are mighty and noble themselves- they were born...

# To Rule.

One Human scholar, who already had thought the treatment of orcs was poor, made it his life's goal to tell every last orc he could of this, and urge them to break their chains. And thus, the War of Freedom was started.

It went well for a time, the orcs were larger, and just as smart as their oppressors. They had been the ones building and farming, not the pathetic Humans. They had been the ones defending the walls and clearing the fae-ish forests, not the Elves. They had been taught what the others had forgotten- how to **survive**. They made an allegiance with the [[Ogres]] of the southeast, who had also been slighted by The Plain Three and wanted their own revenge. What started as a boycott for several months eventually turned sour though, as the Humans wanted their workers back and would stop at nothing to get them.

What they hadn't anticipated, was that the Orcs foresaw this, and prepared accordingly. They knew how humans fought- spears and shields, muskets on occasion, and made the perfect weapon against their entire fighting philosophy- an extremely armored Ogre. The [[Orc Revolution]] would be very, very bloody.

Ogres were so big, so strong, that they could easily carry armor three, maybe four inches thick to battle. No spear nor musket ball could penetrate their mail, and with the Orcs supporting their charges it was impossible for the humans to win. On top of this, the Elves had trespassed on the ancestral grounds of the [[Lizardfolk]], and had earned their ire as well.

Indeed, it seemed that the Orcs would flip the table on their previous oppressors, and would be the iron boot on **their** necks. But, as some might know, the Humans, Elves and Dwarves found a new ally that outmatched the Orcs and Ogres- the [[Minotaurs]]. For every Ogre there were 3-6 Minotaurs, and it only took two of them to wrestle down a bulky, armored Ogre. The Minotaurs could send Orcs onto their backs with only a single swing from their clubs and falchions.

Rapidly, the entire Orc force lost their leadership and cohesion, and were forced to retreat. But not all was lost- [[Immolesh]], the leader of the Minotaurs demanded the Humans, and the Elves, to throw away their hatred to the greenskins- that possibly unity may be achieved, despite the coin in it were he to command their slaughter.

Without a strong military, on their last legs and with a beast man breathing down their necks, The Plain Three signed a treaty to free the Orcs, and welcome them into society as true members of it.

With the discovery of their ancestral home, many orcs- also feeling [[The Calling]] to [[The West]], seek to uncover the history of their people, or just want to make a name for themselves. Indeed, they make for excellent warriors, and there is no finer creature to watch a mans back than an orc, for their bonds run deep. Now, 120 years later they are largely accepted by most, though pockets of puritans still spit on their feet at any opportunity.
