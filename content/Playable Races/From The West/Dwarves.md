[[The West]] was their kingdom, the Dwarves say over and over again. Make no doubt, their citadels lay abandoned all across [[Balphora]], even in the deserts themselves. And so, again and again [[Sons of Y'zrold|The Sons of Y'zrold]], [[Great House of Horadrin]] and even some members of [[The Mightlongs]] argue that all other races, barring perhaps the Orcs and Goblins, should just turn around and leave.

But their recorded history starts nowhere near Balphora, instead near the [[Phrazmollograggra Mountains]]' southern edge.

[[Humans]] recount that both Dwarves and [[Elves]] have been around... well forever basically. With the Dwarves being the first to approach them with promises of unity and kinship. From here society between the two, and eventually the Elves, boomed into a revolution of gunpowder, cogs and freedom... freedom...

The [[Orcs]] were found, and against the wishes of the Dwarven factions the Humans and Elves enslaved them to the last- hundreds of thousands of greenskins all locked away in chains for the crime of being useful to keep around.

This marked the birth of [[Sons of Y'zrold|The Sons of Y'zrold]], who took it upon themselves to hunt down the so called "dark magic" that created the orcs. Though we've spoken of them before... The other Dwarves largely locked themselves away in their mountain holds, avoiding most contact with the major Faction behind the enslavement of the Orcs- [[House Thaddeus]], who had been pushing pro- enslavement ideals onto other humans around them, eager to open a massive slave trade while the money was hot.

As many know though, the Orcs fought against their chains, and though they lost the battle due to the interference of the [[Minotaurs]], they would win the war thanks to [[Immolesh]]'s... "encouragements" to House Thaddeus.

Once the war was over, The [[Great House of Horadrin]] and [[The Mightlongs]] both began speaking with the humans who had at least attempted to avoid slavery, but the Elves, who all unanimously agreed on slaver supremacy, never saw a dwarven diplomat again- even to this day relations between the two peoples have never, ever improved.

Dwarves who feel [[The Calling]], or even those who don't, will go to The West to reclaim their ancestral holds and kick out any squatters that have taken residence. Some however, are still in it to explore, or make a quick buck, or even to escape the law as countless others have.
