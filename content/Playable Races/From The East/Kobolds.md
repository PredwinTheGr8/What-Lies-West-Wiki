Kobolds have been around since the beginnings of Civilization as a whole. Early in [[Koross|Korosian]] history [[Humans]] told stories of tiny lizard folk hiding in the forests and caves. Many didn't believe these stories, but there were still some who never stopped looking for them, eager to prove their existence. The Kobolds were indeed eventually proven to exist, but not from the discoveries of an explorer, nor the collected hide of a hunter- but instead from the shock and awe of a man on his way from the fields, screaming in terror at a child sized bi-pedal lizard eating a rat in an alleyway.

At first, people chased away Kobolds as pests- but they found that where Kobolds hunted, true pests were few and far between. Indeed the Kobolds ate so many rats and small insects that cities housing a Kobold "den" had more bountiful harvests, and made far more money than their neighbors due to their success. Eventually, slowly, Humans all over began to appreciate Kobolds, and even began to feed them time to time themselves. This all led to the Feral, ravenous Kobolds to become... "cute", in a way to some, and even capable of speech.

Indeed, what was first called mimicry was quickly proven to be a rapid evolution to ascend to human level intellect. Kobolds worldwide became capable of speech, counting and problem solving- in other words: sapient. Kobolds adopted the habits and some physical features of the races they spent the most time around.

Kobolds that spend time around humans become posh and full of themselves.

Kobolds that spend time around Lizardfolk become independent and efficient.

Time with Minotaurs made them opportunistic and violent.

Catfolk gave them a taste for cooking.

And so on and so on...

As mentioned they also inherited some physical features from the ones they hung around the most. Minotaurs gave them beards, and fur along their backs. Lizardfolk gave them spines all across their bodies, humans made their scales smaller and smother. Of all the races who had trials and hardship in their history, the Kobolds alone remained comfortable, for almost everyone likes a Kobold. While the other races waged war and killed each other, the Kobolds waited on their comfy beds in their "master's" homes, taking naps and eating bowls of fruit like little parasites... cute parasites.

When [[The Calling]] to [[The West]] began, many Kobolds went out of a want to stay with those close to them- their "owners" and friends. Some go out independently, searching for new companions, others just want to experience the world and the West is the most "new" to explore ever.

While their intellect isn't the best, they're still more than smart enough to formulate plans and make traps- making them excellent sappers on the battlefield or in dungeons all across the deserts. Groups with kobolds in their midst are bound for success, after all...
