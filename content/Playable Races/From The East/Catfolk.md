The Catfolks' history is full of strife. Their history begins in the far east, across [[The Great Swamp]] in the southeastern lands of [[Harush]]. Here, food is difficult to come by and must last for as long as possible. This pushed the Catfolk to cooking extremes: making as much use of a carcass as they could, and trying their best to make it taste good.

Centuries of this led the Catfolk to become the most culinarily strong group in [[Koross]]. [[Dwarves|Dwarven]] traders that got past the [[Lizardfolk]] found the Catfolk, and after getting along well enough and tasting their food, the Dwarves made the decision to petition the elders to invite them into the West (not Balphora) to join their great allegiance- consisting at the time of the Dwarves, [[Humans]], [[Elves]], [[Ratfolk]] and [[Goblins]]. [[Orcs]] still being enslaved, the Lizardfolk still secluding themselves and the [[Minotaurs]] currently walled off.

The Catfolk accepted their request, excited to see and taste the wider world- but were met with a grim, boring palate instead. The Catfolk were disgusted at the treatment of the Orcs, sullen at the state of Goblins, and bewildered by the "ownership" of [[Kobolds]]. They found a society alien to theirs, and thought they tried their best to acclimate it was a difficult road.

Many Catfolk found work in the kitchens of nobles, food carts on the street, and inns across the countryside. Their at the time unique cooking methods drew in hundreds- spiced meats, jerky, and their stew-making methods all appeased the masses and nobles alike. Though they had found success most of the Catfolk were still unsure if they were comfortable with the treatment of most other races beneath the rule of [[The Plain Three]], and some began to speak of returning to Harush.

Before a significant number of them had chosen to leave, however, the [[Orc Revolution]] began- and against the call of both the Humans and Elves, the Catfolk abstained from the conflict, though they cheered for the Orcs from the sidelines. Some Catfolk, though, refused to sit idly by while the Greenskins fought for their freedom, and took up arms against their oppressors as well, with at least a hundred or so of their peoples joining in the war.

These individuals found that conflict suited them- the finesse of rapiers, the twists of daggers, the recoil of an arquebus, all of it was liberating, divine. The Catfolk who joined the orcs were key forces in destabilizing the chain of Human and elven command. Catfolk, you see, make for excellent assassins and rogues, their nimble figures and healthy bodies allowing them to scale walls and murder the generals inside without being seen.

Indeed, the few Catfolk who joined up managed to cause more mayhem than the entirety of the Orcish army- disrupting supply lines mostly. This left the Humans and Elves unable to defend themselves as even their own soldiers abandoned the war out of either fear or even pity to the Orcs.

But as is known, the Orcs did not win this war, for the Minotaurs stomped their army into dust. Though not all was lost...

It was the Catfolk who approached [[Immolesh]] and pleaded with him to enforce the freedom of the Orcs, and though they promised him something, what that was is unknown to this day by any but the most well known Catfolk, and they aren't prone to sharing it either.

The Catfolk venture West out of a need for adventure, not just to answer [[The Calling]]- or to get away from the nobles back home- escape judgment for a crime, or to search for more ingredients to put in their foods. No team is complete without a cook, after all.
