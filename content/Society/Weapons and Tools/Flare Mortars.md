([[Build Togethers|428]], [[index|What Lies West?]])

Flare mortars are large, floor deployed metal canisters used to launch heavy, bright red flares. Flare mortars fire their payloads high up into the sky, using [[Magic|graviturgy]] to achieve greater heights by lowering the effective gravity of the flare. Flare mortar payloads can be seen from up to twelve miles away, allowing for long range requests, even in area's that would typically defy traversal, such as [[The West]].

[[Adventurers]] in particular carry at least three flares on their person at a time- despite how large The West is many parties actually bump into each other or just barely miss one another. This means that flares can be used to signal for assistance, and there's a fairly good chance that someone will in fact come to one's aid.

It is common courtesy to pay out any who comes to your aid when you've used a flare mortar. A gratuity of ten percent of your current earnings is the most common, but parties often find suitable ways around paying the full price, or even at all depending on what else they may have on hand.
