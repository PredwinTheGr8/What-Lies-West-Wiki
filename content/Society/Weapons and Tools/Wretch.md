### [[Potion Composition|Composition]]-

- One part [[Scollifs]]' liver
- Two parts [[Vissouvois]]
- One part drinking water

"Wretch" be a concoction more than a potion. Tis a vile brew what makes you do its namesake- buke yer guts out! Now, me-knows that sounds right asinine, t'why'd someone e'er want to quash something vile? Well yesee, the brew saps magic outta ye like no t'marrow. If'n you're feeling the effects of [[Mutants|mutation]] and you feel 'sif you're loosin yesself, need simply slug it back and wait a few seconds then wham! All that troubles ye is a puddle of sick on the floor er dunes now!

Maybe I should figure out somethin to make it taste a wee bit better? Sugar? Though only sugar ye'd get round [[The West]] is [[Thunder-Jam]], an we know that'd only make it taste a bit worse. An'wey- I shall endeavor, I know fancy words look at me, to find a suitable taste enhancer what so its not as vile as tis r'now. If'n its been a while and you want an updated recipe or what have you, you know damn well where to find me oh loyal customer- Seamus' Cauldron, corner of Breggan Street and [[The Onyx Archway|Onyx Arch]] Way. Be seein you!
