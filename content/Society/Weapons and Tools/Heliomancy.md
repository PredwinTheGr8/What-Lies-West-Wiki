"Praise be to his might- to his gilded glowing hand stitching wounds closed. For his power is the power of life! The strength to raise armies from the surgeon's tents! To invigorate both body and soul!"

Heliomancy is a school of [[magic]] that originates directly from the realm of [[Orpheus]]. Heliomancy is widely regarded as the most pure form of magic, being the only school that cannot cause [[Mutants|mutation]] with repetitive use, and even capable of reverting mutations under certain circumstances. Practitioners of Heliomancy, known widely as 'healers', can be found in every corner of the world, though Heliomancy- unlike other magic schools- cannot simply be learned to use its power.

Being blessed with the power to wield Heliomancy is a gift given directly by [[Orpheus]]. It is a rare gift, with very few individuals being granted the honor of wielding it. [[Church of Orpheus|The Church of Orpheus]] covets and collects those blessed by their [[Gods|god]]. Many times a year, templars of Orpheus will spread throughout [[Koross]] and now [[Balphora]] to search for those with his blessing, and take them back to the Capital City of [[Luminarch, The City of Lanterns|Luminarch]].

Heliomancy is prized by [[Adventurers|adventurers]], as it remains one of the the best solutions to grievous injury in [[The West]]. Though again, with the sheer rarity of Healers they are in high demand. As of yet, only three Clerics of Orpheus partake in expeditions into The West; Amelia Harrow, Urridin of [[Harush]] and Rakxgrakx the Gleamer. Urridin and Rakxgrakx are both not of the human people, showing well and truly that Orpheus does not play favorites. Truly he is most benevolent.

Amelia Harrow is the only of the three to not be officially ordained by The Church- as she preferred to have her own 'solidarity' against the church.
