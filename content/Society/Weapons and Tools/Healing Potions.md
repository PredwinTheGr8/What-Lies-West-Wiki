([[Build Togethers|398]], [[What Lies West]]?)

"Ahh! A most powerful concoction, made from the carcasses of two and the flesh of one. Ware, though, for improper consumption of such aberrant applications may not only renew your vitality, but bring forth new flesh as well. Drink responsibly, and caringly."

-Ashkram Who Speaks Strange, [[Scholars|scholar]] and [[Adventurers|adventurer]]

Healing potions are a rare commodity in [[The West]], powerful reagents that stitch wounds shut, bring forth new blood, and re-energize those who ingest it. Healing potions are made from three key ingredients- [[Scollifs]]' Liver, [[Ossuary Leeches|Ossuary Leech]] Blood and [[Mire Weed]] husk. Other ingredients, such as the blood of powerful monsters can be added to increase the potency of the potion. 

Healing potions can easily seal many gruesome injuries, but they are not to be treated as a cure all. If the situation has calmed down, stitching a wound normally is a far better option than using a healing potion, as they do not come without downsides. The ingredients of a healing potion are heavily corrupted by [[magic]], and though mortals do have innate resistance to it, that's no reason to overexpose oneself. [[The West]] is practically drowning in magic, the substance is in the very air itself. Drinking a healing potion on top of that may just be the final push to contracting the infamous [[Cardinal Sickness]].

If a larger wound is present, one that goes to the bone, or one over six inches across, arcane spells are a far better solution for healing- as Healing potions become more and more dangerous the larger a wound is. Larger wounds may result in extra flesh manifesting- such as benign tendrils, extra teeth, eyes, scales, hair, horns and even entire mouths capable of making sound. These symptoms aren't always benign, though. Sometimes they are the beginnings of becoming a [[Mutants|mutant]].

Perhaps the worst part is that healing potions, thanks to the properties of the [[Scollifs]]' liver as their main ingredient, are addictive. While it may take a few back to back drinks, it only takes around four or so within an hour to suffer from arcane withdrawals. Symptoms include a bubbling sensation on the skin, being able to 'feel' ones bones within their body and increased paranoia. The slums of [[The City of Wyrms]] are full of ex-[[adventurers]] who succumbed to their addiction, and now beg for coin with horrid, mutilated forms.

Still, despite the dangers, Healing Potions save lives, and though you're always one mistake away from a living hell, isn't that better than dying altogether? I'm not sure personally. But then again I'm no adventurer, hell I've never even *seen* a healing potion before.
