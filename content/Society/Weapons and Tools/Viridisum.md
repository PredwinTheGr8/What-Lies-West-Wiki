Viridisum, named by it's creator Emmerick Von Conrad, thirty second in line to [[The Conrad Family]] title, is a somewhat common tool used by adventurers within [[The West]]. Viridisum is a gaseous substance with a bright green hue, though when stored it is completely clear. Viridisum is stored in small, fragile tubules of quartz glass, in a separate inner tubule another, secret liquid reagent is stored, that when combined with the acting substance of Viridisum greatly increases the volume of the cloud.

Breaking the seal, either by throwing the tubule or by hitting it with a projectile, the two substances will combine and expand to fill an area of roughly sixty cubic feet. The gas is significantly heavier than air, often stopping just below the knees of average adventurers, though small pockets still thrash about the air above the more obvious line.

Symptoms of Viridisum poison are gruesome and painful. The gas is harmless to skin for a long period, but an extended amount of time standing in the cloud will result in rashes- which will then progress into open lacerated wounds as the gas eats away at flesh faster and faster. When inhaled, though, the poison becomes far more dangerous, eating away at the soft flesh and causing nausea, vomiting, internal bleeding and hallucinations.

The eyes are vulnerable to the poison as well, as such there are certain precautions one must take to protect themself when Viridisum is deployed. Goggles to protect the eyes, and a watered down cloth to protect the lungs. Viridisum is highly soluble by volume, a wet cloth can absurd up to roughly 10~ minutes worth of Viridisum before it becomes unsafe to remain in an area any longer. This part of the gas' weakness is purposeful, meant to be used by adventurers so they don't need to wait for the gas to naturally diffuse.

Despite dangers, Viridisum remains a powerful tool for adventurers that don't want to round a corner into a thousand, hungry scarabs, or even worse, a dense nest of [[Corpse-Thicket]].
