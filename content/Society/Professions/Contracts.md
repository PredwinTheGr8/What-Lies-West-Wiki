Salutations, I am the [[Adventurers|Adventurer's]] Guild's vice commander Ser Mallorin Berns, though you may think of me only as Mallorin. This note is in regards to updates concerning contracts, expedition authorization and return-taxation. If you know of a fellow who remains illiterate we ask that you either read this document aloud to them or point them to a kiosk where an employee of the guild might instead. Thank you.

# Contract Updates

Contracts are now required to leave the Onyx Archway into [[The West]]. Contract sources are only recognized if they have a major seal immediately visible on the exterior of the scroll after acquisition. To acquire a seal bring the contract to its listed employer and or its current handler. It is the duty of the handler or employer to place the seal, asking an adventurer to preform it themselves is illegal. This law is in place to protect adventurers from fraud- as clarifying that the handler or employer never placed the seal themselves means that they never directly authorized its start.

There is currently no current ranking system that prevents adventurers from taking on higher level missions if they are unprepared. While a solution is being worked on the Guild simply recommends not punching above your weight class, as it were. If a contract is made with the express intent to cull a monster or other entity's presence inside of [[The West]] and its [[All Regions of The West|regions]] then it is to be approved directly by majority vote during a [[Council of Wyrms|Council]] meeting. If approved the Adventurer's Guild will increase reward amounts appropriately to increase morale regarding the contract.

Adventurer parties must all sign their individual names on Contracts now. Failure to do so will result in the reward becoming void and the party being fined ten percent of the rewards value.
Failure to complete a contract upon return or within the granted time window will result in a one week period of complete deauthorization to embark on expeditions. If repetitive failures occur all members of a party are liable to become banned from entering [[The West]] from as little as one month to indefinitely depending on severity of failure.
# Expedition Authorization Updates

Expeditions can no longer be authorized without a contract, see the above section to clarify further information. One literate and skilled writer must be present in every party above four members. It is the job of this persons to catalogue curious findings and collect any small samples they may come across. Success in these tasks is grounds for increased rewards sponsored by the guild or other handler, direct transport to The [[Scholam Wyrmras]] will result in further increased rewards.

Any sightings of legendary monsters such as; [[The Knights in Yellow]], [[Chrozhran Destroyers]], [[True Wyverns]], [[Death Knights]] or [[Seamsters]] as well as sightings of [[The Annihilator]] are to be reported in the following manor. Region>Landmarks>Time of Day> Number of Party Members.

If the cataloguer is art-capable anatomical sketches are welcome and offer increased pay as well. Drawings of the landscape are also sought after but as of current do not offer higher pay than notes of the same vista(s).
