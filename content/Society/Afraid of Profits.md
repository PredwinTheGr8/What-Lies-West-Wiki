([[Build Togethers|346]], [[What Lies West]]?)

From the deepest recesses of [[The West]], to the most decorated of Mesas in [[Harush]], people will always have their opinions. But two groups of people, the [[Catfolk]] and [[Ratfolk]] are almost constantly under pressure from travelers, poets and scholars for their 'alien' societies. Both [[Humans]] and [[Elves]] have societies built around industry, farming and more importantly, their nobles.

Nobles are important according to these peoples- they 'guide' those beneath them to build a better world, a world that just so happens to have the nobles at the top. As of late, with the power gap after the [[Orc Revolution]], many noble families have been buying up land- farms, riversides and other agricultural acres to secure their fortunes. At the same time, this massive movement from dozens of noble families has left many peasants and other lower class folk with nowhere to retire.

Nobles in [[Koross]] have bought up so much land that only one twentieth of the farmable land in Koross is owned by the common man. On the opposite, the 'lower classes' of [[Harush]] and [[M'rikki]] are living better than some nobles- for their societies run off a so called 'perfect communism'. Though a Koross noble would never call it perfect.

[[Ratfolk]] society is built around usefulness- so while not every single citizen is treated equally, they remain heavily encouraged to look out for their friends and family. [[Catfolk]] society is built around their community- their one-ness in the arid environments of [[Harush]]. Both of these peoples have been encouraged by their environment, or religions, or both to look after one another and ensure that everyone does their part. Ratfolk Merchants and Catfolk Chefs are both as renowned in their home towns as a pottery maker or a painter. Everyone knows everyone else in their cities- both because having friends in their worlds is important and because the way they settle areas leads to a lower density of individuals.

A [[Humans|Human]] noble may oversee hundreds of workers, and only know a handful of their names- while a [[Ratfolk]] Overseer might have half that number, but they know each of them personally. An [[Elves|Elven]] Lord might tithe thousands in taxes every year and brew discord in their citizens- while a Catfolk 'noble' doesn't even know what the word 'tax' means.

The [[Elves]] and [[Humans]] are so opinionated on these two other lineages because it seems that they are 'afraid' of making money, when its their societies reliance on community that pushes them to not exploit their fellows.

The [[Koross|Korosian]] treatment of their workers has gotten so unbelievably horrid that it seems [[Humans]] and [[Elves]] are doomed for an economic crash in the coming years, with their citizens either going [[The West|West]] to find opportunities, or immigrating to [[Harush]] and [[M'rikki]] to earn a proper living.
