([[Build Togethers|369]], [[What Lies West]])

"Yesss! Make food, good food that no get bad and icky! Very good for body, and other things too! Rashuns! Rashuns! Rashuns!"

-Klink, famous [[Kobolds|Kobold]] [[Adventurers|adventurer]] and chef


Rations come in all sorts of shapes and sizes- [[Catfolk]] prefer dried meats or hunting their own meals for their party, [[Humans]] prefer their hardtack and jerky, and [[Elves]] their dried fruit. But among all of these perfected styles there is one that remains incredibly... experimental.

Kobolds have always been a curious bunch, their aptitude for learning is much higher than other races, and though they often remain dim-witted from the perspective of outsiders, a kobold is in fact always thinking- what of, though? Well, whatever their currently fixated on- most often an inconvenience they repeatedly encounter. Kobolds that venture to [[The West]] frequently have to subsist off of dry, flavorless meats and breads, not the kind of food a kobold typically enjoys.

One Kobold, Klink, decided that she would fix this frequent atrocity on her palate. She began to experiment with magics of all kinds, graviturgy, hydromancy, heliocraft. All of these had less than favorable results- explosions, burning rooftops and miniature floods. But, eventually, she came across the perfect school of magic to preserve her food- chronomancy- the magic of time.

She had a long adventure to find the artifacts she would need, and faced many travails to acquire such trinkets, but eventually she made her way home to [[The City of Wyrms]] with a cart full of mystic machines that she scrounged from the sunken ruins... alone.

Klink set up shop in a stall at first, using re-wired machines to freeze the advancement of time on food, keeping them perfectly warm and juicy in a suspended field. These 'Rashuns' quickly became incredibly popular among adventurers, and even in the eyes of scholars who desperately wanted to acquire her machines for their own studies.

Klink had her machines stolen on eight separate occasions, but with her vast wealth from selling her rations each time she was able to hire out [[The Herd of Immolesh]] to find her livelihood again, and each time the [[Scholam Wyrmras]] got a little bit more destroyed. Today, Klink has made enough money to permanently hire guards for her stall, though they likely wouldn't go even if she couldn't pay them- free food and the company of a kobold are good for the soul, after all.
