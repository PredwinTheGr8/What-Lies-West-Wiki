([[Build Togethers|354]], [[What Lies West]]?)

Balphora is the mapped region of the western continent, that's name has yet to be agreed upon as of yet. It borders the only entryway into [[The West]], accessible from the largest city in the world, [[The City of Wyrms]]. Desolate [[Dwarves|Dwarven]] holds, empty [[Orcs|Orcish]] bastions, and other, void cities are sprinkled throughout the region of Balphora. It seems that long, long ago many of the folk found in [[Koross]], across the [[Ocean Magnamous]], are originally from Balphora. Balphora, other than the ancient cities within its borders, is characterized by its vastly different ecosystems in its northern half. Dense, pine tree rainforests, monolithic ocean cliffsides and even a desert or two are all things you can find in Balphora's northern half.

## The Landcleaver

 Speaking of halves, Balphora is home to the largest river in the known world- [[The Landcleaver River]], often just called the Landcleaver. This river, at its widest point, would take four days to walk acrost. The Landcleaver's bank is so large that at many points it seems completely flat across the horizon, despite how irregularly it moves when mapped.

The Landcleaver is the life blood of many towns upon its embankment- with the largest 'new era' keep in Balphora being upon its eastmost point on [[The Eternal Delta]]- [[Delta front]]. This city, and many others on The Landcleaver can easily trade with one another, as the calm waters on the great river are easy to navigate. In fact, The Landcleaver currently houses more ships in its waters than anywhere else in the world combined- an estimated 200 cargo ships.

The Landcleaver hold many records, in fact. Longest river, widest river, most tributaries, largest swamp, most commonly mistaken for an ocean. On that ladder point, many newcomers to Balphora that don't come into the region from the River sometimes get turned around when they see it- 'Wasn't I going north? Why is the ocean here?" is a common question to guards posted on river-side hamlets.

## The Blackwood

On the topic of getting turned around, to the northern-most region of Balphora lies [[The Blackwood]]. The Blackwood is the largest 'mundane' forest in the known world, not counting [[The Great Swamp|Moulugg]] because its less dense, and a mire not a true forest.  The Blackwood is home to the undeniable tallest trees in the world, however. At their tallest these massive trees reach heights of 900, sometimes over a thousand, feet tall. Their only competition, the [[Weeping Titans]] of [[Sorrowed Weald|The Sorrowed Weald]] come close, but the immense heat of [[The West]] forces them into complacency.

The trees of The Blackwood have had millennia to grow, perhaps they once reigned across the [[Wyrmscale Reaches]], before whatever apocalypse occurred destroyed everything, but nowadays they are lonely giants with nothing else like them. The Blackwood is remarkably flat for its proximity to the Wyrmscale Reaches, with only small hills and dips being the only variance in elevation within the forest. It makes it easy to lose your way- and the tall trees that block the sky out prevent the use of stars to navigate. Many people choose to ignore the Blackwoods, choosing to remain in proximity to [[The City of Wyrms]] as its the only known route into The West. The same goes for why folk don't venture south, that and the lack of resources.

## K'bass Kah

Moving south, bordering [[The Landcleaver River]], K'bass Kah is the name given to the ash choked regions of [[K'bass Vould]] ('Ash Valley') and [[K'bass Veld]] ('Ash Prairie'). K'bass Kah has been choked out by west-blowing winds carrying the smog of the [[K'bass Kaulldour]] ('Ash Mountains'). Though the damage isn't as extreme as the blight caused by the [[Phrazmollograggra Mountains]] back in [[Koross]], K'bass Kah's ('Ash Place') is still the last place you want to be caught in the elements. Crops cannot grow, birds cannot breath- man and more are not meant to live in this place. Only two cities exist within this realm, and both are cloistered, ancient dwarven holds.

Still, the K'bass Kaulldour draw in plenty of miners willing to risk their lungs for the ore seams dancing across their surface. Iron, silver, and rarer magic metals like mithril and adamantine are more than worth the danger, it seems.

## Highborn Lowlands

Named by those who live above [[The Landcleaver River]], the regions true name, 'Western Koross', is almost entirely ignored by any outsiders that talk about the place. The Highborn Lowlands is ruled largely by [[The Conrad Family]], who work directly under orders from [[Maximillian Thaddeus]]. The highborn lowlands are comprised of vast, flat fields that stretch on seemingly forever. They make a great home for cattle, and have been perfect for building massive cities over the last century that folk have immigrated [[The West|West]] to [[Balphora]]. 

South of this region, the fields stretch on seemingly forever, getting hotter for a while as they meet the equator, but nobody has been further south than that. Within the fields are a species of heavy-feeding grass that greedily claims all the nutrients- the reason for the lack of trees. There are rivers, but they never get wider than a carriage. The soil isn't great for growing crops, either, forcing folk to remain where they are- lest they risk starvation.

The Lowlands are mostly home to small game, like rabbits and fowl that can easily hide in the man-tall grasses of the region that a person ca easily get lost in during the morning and evening. Some pilgrims have gone south to find places to build, but none have sent letters back. Whether they found what they were looking for or not, it will take centuries to find out as the focus of everyone is drawn to The West.

## Green Country

The mid-eastern region of [[Balphora]], Green Country is where many [[Orcs]] have migrated to after the [[Orc Revolution|revolution's]] end a hundred years back. Green Country is welcoming to outsiders, but the folk here don't suffer thieves or bandits- if you are blamed you are guilty, at least until the Orcs can figure their laws out. They're new to this whole government thing, y'know? Anyways, their kingdom, officially called "Orsrule", is obviously new, but with the Orcs doing many of the construction and logging jobs back in [[Koross]], they are perhaps the best people for making a kingdom from scratch out of anyone else.

The country's proximity to The [[Ocean Magnamous]] has led to a surprising amount of trade- despite the Orcs being... well orcs, the other two factions bordering the Ocean aren't popular either. To the north, [[The Waywards]] are just as likely to rob you as they are to actually do trade, and to the south, representatives of [[The Conrad Family]] will tax you into oblivion. The Orcs have earned a great deal of favor with traders, then, for they offer fair tax and safe travel on their half of [[The Landcleaver River]]. This has led to a strong unity between Orcs and [[Ratfolk]] merchants, who have entered a longstanding agreement of trade.

## Landcleaver Basin

The Landcleaver Basin holds within its cradle many [[Dwarves|Dwarven]] Holds- all of which have been reclaimed by the various clans that have come to [[Balphora]] in search for answers to their ancient evacuation. The largest Dwarven Clan, The [[Great House of Horadrin]], has spent hundreds of thousands of gold shillings on funding expeditions into the southern parts of [[K'bass Kaulldour]], though they have sent a handful into the northern parts surrounding the Dwakes of [[K'bass Vould]]. The Dwarven clans are largely 'at war' with the small pockets of bandits that have taken their ancient holds for themselves, namely [[The Waywards]] who have taken two entire great holds within the K'bass Vould.

The Basin itself is characterized by its constant mist and frequent drizzling from light rainstorms. The basin makes for bad farming, but frequent visits from migrating birds and the vast numbers of fish inside of [[The Landcleaver River]] and its tributaries in the area more than make up for the lack of food. The Dwarves also released many boars into the area, who's population has boomed as of late- allowing the Dwarves, or anyone else in the area, to hunt them year round without risk to their disappearance.

## Prairie of Wyrms

Largely associated with [[The City of Wyrms]] due to proximity, the 'Prairie' of Wyrms is anywhere you can 'still see the city, no matter how barely you manage it.' Essentially, the Prairie of Wyrms ends as soon as the colossal city disappears over the horizon. The Prairie of Wyrms doesn't have any 'new age' cities within its bounds, as all who have come here have only come for The City of Wyrms.

The Prairie of Wyrms though, unlike the southern-most fields of [[Balphora]], possesses extremely high soil quality- full of phosphorus and nitrogen. The outskirts of [[The City of Wyrms]] is covered with fl0owing wheat and vegetable fields owned by various guilds within, though [[The Conrad Family]] owns most.

## Westmarch Hills

The Westmarch Hills are the second smallest region- just above the Prairie of Wyrms- and are home to many small villages- namely along the northern half of [[The Landcleaver River]]. The Westmarch Hills don't possess any 'ruler', though [[The Waywards]] and a high number of [[Risen Ones]] make their homes here.

The Westmarch Hills are home to many wild bison and mammoths like most of [[The Lawless North]] and [[The Blackwood]]. This has made room for settlers to tame some of the beasts and use them to plow fields and drag carts.

#### All of these regions are constantly under extreme traffic as more and more [[Adventurers|adventurers]], farmers, smiths and [[Scholars]] make their way across the [[Ocean Magnamous]] to [[Balphora]]- or rather, to [[The West]].
