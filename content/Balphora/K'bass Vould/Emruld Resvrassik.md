[[Build Togethers|302]] [[index|What Lies West?]]

'Twixt the choking valleys of [[K'bass Kaulldour]], there is a unique and ancient artifice that has changed the entire valley- the so called "Resvrassik". A mighty, stone and iron wall in the valley that choked the river eons ago, before the [[Dwarves]] left Balphora and forgot their history. This 'reservoir', in the tongue of man, is one of the few Dwarven ruins still left standing after all this time- still functioning as a clockwork generator that powers a now retaken hold to the east in the [[K'bass Kaulldour]] against the artificial lake. Inside the lake, a strange variation of algae filters out the ash and grime the active volcanoes of the mountains produce, in times of old this was likely used as an effort to control the pollution and allow fish to live peacefully, but without any oversight for thousands, possibly hundreds of thousands of years, the algae has grown uncontrollably, smothering the reservoir and its connected lakes in the [[K'bass Vould]] and preventing any life from propagating in the rotting, green waters.

The area surrounding the hold and the reservoir have both been taken by [[The Waywards]], a legion of vagabonds that hold illicit power over [[Balphora]]- with the Dwarves of all factions being very unhappy that their ancestral halls are being pillaged and defaced by mere bandits, though they can't do anything about because, well, their ancestors did too good a job fortifying the holds, and the Bandits can simply wait out any armies that show up, or unleash all hell with the decrepit, but still very functional, defenses inside the holds.
