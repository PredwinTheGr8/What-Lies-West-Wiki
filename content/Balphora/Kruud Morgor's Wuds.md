[[Build Togethers|303]] [[index|What Lies West?]]

On the eastern coast of [[Balphora]], in the territories dominated by [[The Waywards]] there lies an inconspicuous wee forest, that contains within a bounty of mushrooms and fruits ripe for foraging. But alas, for any who go into these woods without permission from its master will meat a most grim fate- for they will come face to face with that master of the forest- Morgor.

Morgor, you see, is a very articulate Ogre, a student of higher commerce and intellect- he fancies himself a lord, and the forest he inhabits his land. When The Waywards first made their buildings on the [[The Coast of Countless Corpses|True Coast]] in the port of [[Freestuff]] Morgor made his first appearance to ensure they knew who's land it really was- His. The small militia and "governor" of Free Stuff met Morgor in the fields outside the forest, where the Ogre loomed over them- at least 16 feet tall. His body covered in wooden armor and briars.

The ramshackle guard were quivering in their boots when Morgor made his offer unto them, seeing an opportunity to finally have subjects to call his own.

"THESE WOODS AND THIS COAST IS **MINE** SNIVVLERS! YOU WANTS TA LIVE 'ERE THEN YOU GOTS TA PAY THE TOLL!"

His booming voice shook the nerve out of any who would hear him, and carried on over the mountains to be heard by all in their territories, or so they say. Needless to point out- a handful of ruffians and vagabonds stood no chance against "Kruud" Morgor, and the city of Free Stuff would rather stay where they are- looting the waters to the east. So, with a hit to their pride as thieves and bounty hunters the men and women of Free Stuff agreed to Kruud Morgor's terms, and yielded his 'Wuds' to him, never to enter without his majesties permission.
