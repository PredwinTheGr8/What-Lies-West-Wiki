(308, What Lies West?)

[[Balphora]] is old, so old that for the past several millennia the races that came from Balphora originally seem to have forgotten their ancestral home- [[Dwarves]], [[Kobolds]], [[Orcs]] and so on. These peoples all have signs of their previous inhabitation scattered throughout both Balphora and even [[The West]] itself, but the greatest evidence of any previous civilization is where the dunes meet the [[Wyrmscale Reaches]]- **The City Of Wyrms**.

The city earns its name from its discovery, where the first to find the mighty citadel found the carcasses of five ancient dragons, reduced to nothing but bone, resting across the city in strange positions- as if they were caught fleeing from something and swiftly killed. The structures within the city are at a glance not originative of any one lineage. At a deeper level of investigation, this holds true, as the sigils, construction and other identifiable patterns come from the styles of all kinds of peoples- namely the strong rocky holds of Dwarves and the brutalist, hardy walls of Orcs. Though looking closely reveals that the sewers were designed by Kobolds, and the interiors still left intact having goblin furniture styles.

Indeed, it seems that The City of Wyrms was a group project between the ancient ancestors of some lineages within [[Koross]]. Why they fled, or even why there are no records of their previous inhabitation is unknown. To this day, despite the rampant destruction caused by the cities current inhabitants, scholars still try to put together what exactly happened, to no avail.

The City of Wyrms is a decrepit, yet still mighty monument of marble, basalt and stone. It is to this day, the largest city ever, and archeologists suspect it has held that title for well over 20 thousand years. This city serves as a major checkpoint to The West, acting as a rest stop and return point for adventurers that desire to venture out into the sands. The city itself has been claimed by the adventurers that reside here- opulent marble buildings being besmirched with rudimentary wooden refitting and scaffolding. Indeed, currently most of the city is hidden behind "convenient" wooden walkways, demolition efforts, and the destructive pillaging done by vagabonds looking for artifacts. The City of Wyrms seems doomed to crumble away as more and more is taken from it, while nothing is ever given.
