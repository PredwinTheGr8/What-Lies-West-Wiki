(299, What Lies West?)

The Coast of Countless Corpses, also known as the True Coast, is a location on the shores of [[Balphora]], around the center of the mapped region. The True Coast, as scholars and nobles often call it, is where the first ever fleet of ships entered Balphora... to great casualties. A fleet of over 120 ships was destroyed by [[Coral Spines]] under the waves that were completely unavoidable. The formations ripped apart the bottoms of the ships' hulls and the waves slashed their occupants against the razor-sharp coral. Over 4 thousand souls were lost in 12 minutes, and to this day the wreckage of those ships can be seen beneath and above the waves, their spoils and soldiers all lost forever... unless you have a smaller ship of your own to venture between the spines.
