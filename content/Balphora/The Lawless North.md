A region of [[Balphora]] bordering the [[Ocean Magnamous]], owned by [[The Waywards]] that includes the [[The Blackwood]], [[Warbling Desert]], [[K'bass Vould]] and [[The Coast of Countless Corpses|The True Coast]]. The 'Lawless' North is garrisoned by people who left [[Koross]] specifically to get away from nobles, and have united under the very concept of freedom.

The Lawless North has existed ever since the first man destined for the noose fled acrost the [[Ocean Magnamous]]. The cities of this realm of [[Balphora]] are ramshackle at best- with only a few *real* builders making their homes here it may take a very, *very* long time for buildings to start resembling the ones back in [[Koross]]. The Lawless North borders [[Green Country]]
